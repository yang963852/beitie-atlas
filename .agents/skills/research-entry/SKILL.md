# Prepare one knowledge contribution

Open [the library](../../../knowledge/README.md) and the [evidence workflow](../../../docs/atlas-workflow.md). Identify the subject of the requested change and the single editable source. A person can submit the same material in the Chinese Issue form without a local checkout.

1. Record exactly which work, physical witness, person, relationship or observation the source identifies. Look for a stable ID and edition or collection number. For holding and provenance changes, edit the catalogue data and refresh its generated reading copy. For original prose, edit the authored Markdown linked by the library.
2. Find the institution or author, work title and passage or print edition and page. Connect each assertion to the part that supports it. A teaching-use claim also needs an identifiable teaching resource and evidence of its use. Record unresolved disagreements with the relevant locators.
3. Identify the author and edition behind a reading note. Point to a character, line or leaf that the reader can inspect. For a person-to-person relationship check both endpoints, the relationship type and direction, using the same cited relationship on both pages.
4. Preserve Chinese originals and aligned translations. Use the article's metadata and links, retain contributor credit, and give item-level rights evidence for media. Check the rendered article and pass the project checks for implementation or dataset edits.

Hand off the Issue or PR, subject IDs, citations and outstanding editorial questions. Only an authorized reviewer can change publication approval.
