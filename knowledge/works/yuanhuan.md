# 远宦帖

## 背景

### 中文

王羲之写给益州刺史周抚的一封信，亦称《省别帖》。存世一本为双钩廓填，卷上有宋徽宗瘦金书签题与内府印记，又有金章宗「群玉中秘」「明昌御览」及元、明、清收藏印。唐《十七帖》刻本也收此札，馆方认为刻本线条较呆板，不如墨迹本传神。

### English

A letter Wang Xizhi wrote to Zhou Fu, governor of Yi province; it is also known as the Xingbie modelbook. The surviving sheet is a double-outline tracing copy, carrying Emperor Huizong's slender-gold title slip and palace seals, then the Jin emperor Zhangzong's seals and Yuan, Ming and Qing collectors' seals. The Tang Seventeen-Day Letters engraving also carries this text; the museum considers its lines stiffer and less revealing than this ink copy.
