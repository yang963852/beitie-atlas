# 峄山刻石

## 背景

### 中文

秦始皇巡行峄山，刻石纪事。碑文所记的秦代巡游，与后来传世各本的制作年代，各有其时。今存者为北宋淳化四年（993）郑文宝据南唐徐铉摹本重刻于长安的“长安本”。

### English

The Qin inscription commemorates the First Emperor’s visit to Mount Yi. The recorded imperial tour and the making of the surviving versions belong to different dates. What survives is the “Chang’an version”, recut in 993 by Zheng Wenbao from a copy attributed to Xu Xuan of the Southern Tang.
