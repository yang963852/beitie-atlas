# 真草千字文

## 背景

### 中文

《真草千字文》传为南朝陈、隋年间僧人智永所书，真书、草书两体逐行间写。故宫说明记其可靠传本主要有三种：唐代传入日本的墨迹本、北宋大观三年（1109）薛嗣昌据长安崔氏所藏真迹摹刻的「关中本」刻石、南宋《群玉堂帖》四十行残本；馆方称三本风格一致。本库为墨迹本、关中本原石与故宫藏宋拓本册分别建档：墨迹本今在日本，文化厅登记为唐、作者阙名、京都府个人藏；关中本原石八石（两面刻八栏），现藏西安碑林博物馆；宋拓本册二十七开，朱翼盫旧藏后捐故宫。

### English

The Thousand Character Classic in regular and cursive script is attributed to the monk Zhiyong of the Chen–Sui years. The Palace Museum names three reliable recensions: an ink manuscript that reached Japan in the Tang, the "Guanzhong" recension cut in 1109 by Xue Sichang from a manuscript held by the Cui family of Chang'an, and a forty-column fragment in the Southern Song Qunyutang tie; the museum says the three agree in style. This library records the ink manuscript, the Guanzhong stones and the Palace Museum's Song rubbing separately. The manuscript is now in Japan, registered by the Cultural Affairs Agency as Tang, author unnamed, in private custody in Kyoto Prefecture; the Guanzhong stones are held at the Xi'an Beilin Museum; the twenty-seven-leaf Song rubbing was Zhu Yizhen's and later donated to the Palace Museum.

## 待考

### 中文

- 三种传本的制作关系无证据连接：没有材料表明日本墨迹本即薛嗣昌摹刻所据的「长安崔氏藏本」，本记录不建此关联。
- 墨迹本的真伪（智永真迹说与唐摹说）与关中本碑石尺寸、行款的两套官方口径，分别存于各实物记录的待考。
- 南宋《群玉堂帖》残本的今藏情况未考，未建档。

### English

- No evidence links the recensions' making: nothing shows the Japanese manuscript is the "Cui family manuscript" that Xue Sichang traced, and no such relation is recorded here.
- The manuscript's authenticity dispute and the stones' conflicting official measurements are kept on each object's own record.
- The Qunyutang tie fragment's present holding has not been researched, so it is not catalogued here.
