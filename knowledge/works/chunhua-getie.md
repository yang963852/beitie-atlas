# 淳化阁帖

## 背景

### 中文

王著奉敕编次内府所藏墨迹，摹刻为十卷。临王羲之，可从卷六读起。

### English

Wang Zhu arranged manuscripts from the imperial collection into ten engraved volumes. The selection attributed to Wang Xizhi begins in volume six.
