# 迴元观钟楼铭

## 背景

### 中文

这方横石记一座唐代道观的旧事。历代金石书中未见著录，直至1986年才在西安出土。馆方定其为柳公权晚年书迹。

### English

A horizontal slab inscribed with the history of a Tang abbey, unearthed in Xi’an in 1986 and previously unknown to epigraphic catalogues. The museum treats it as a late Liu Gongquan autograph.
