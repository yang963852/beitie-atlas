# 牛橛造像记

## 背景

### 中文

北魏太和十九年（495），长乐王丘穆陵亮夫人尉迟为亡息牛橛造弥勒像，题记刻于龙门古阳洞。研究院官网刊布的论文把它系于太和十九年，列为古阳洞最早的一批像龛之一；官网介绍页记古阳洞在西山南段，碑刻题记八百余品，「龙门二十品」十九品出于此洞，此记即其中之一。国家文物局《第一批古代名碑名刻文物名录》第339行录此记，收藏单位为龙门石窟。所引来源未记书写者。

### English

In 495 (the nineteenth year of Taihe under the Northern Wei), Lady Yuchi, wife of Qiumuling Liang, Prince of Changle, dedicated a Maitreya image for her deceased son Niujiao, with the dedication carved in Guyang Cave at Longmen. A paper published on the research institute's site dates the niche to 495 and counts it among the earliest in the cave; the grottoes' own page places Guyang Cave on the west hill, records more than eight hundred carved inscriptions there, and counts nineteen of the famous Longmen Twenty among them, this dedication being one. The national register of famous steles, row 339, lists the dedication under the Longmen Grottoes. The cited sources name no calligrapher.
