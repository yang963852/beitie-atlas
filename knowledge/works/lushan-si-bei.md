# 麓山寺碑

## 背景

### 中文

李邕叙长沙麓山寺自晋至唐的沿革。文、书、刻素有“三绝”之称。元丰三年（1080），米芾来此学书，名字留在了碑阴。

### English

Li Yong’s account of a Changsha monastery from its Jin-dynasty founding to the Tang, praised as a “three excellences” stele for text, calligraphy, and cutting. Mi Fu studied it in 1080 and left his name on the reverse.
