# 松风阁诗

## 背景

### 中文

崇宁元年（1102），黄庭坚罢太平州，改“管勾洪州玉隆观”，溯江西上，本欲卜居荆南，九月停船樊口，却在武昌（今鄂州）盘桓下来。鄂州市博物馆记他寓居西山古寺，“沉醉于满山参天的古松，听松涛与雨声而眠”，又游寒溪、菩萨泉、钓台、怡亭，就在这一年九月写成《松风阁诗帖》。诗为七言，起句“依山筑阁见平川”，中间写夜雨会饮“夜雨鸣廊到晓悬”，末段“东坡道人已沉泉，张侯何时到眼前”——苏轼已死，张耒未到。同一年同一地，他还与张耒、晁补之合书李深之夫妇墓志，被当地称作“三绝”。[鄂州市博物馆，苏门三绝展](https://ezbwg.com/ReadNews.asp?NewsId=1736)

墨迹纸本，据《鄂州日报》所记，纵三十二点八厘米、横二百一十九点二厘米，二十九行。卷后有宋元以来十五家跋语，钤贾似道、大长公主、项元汴、孙承泽、安岐、卞永誉诸家印，又有乾隆、嘉庆、宣统“御览之宝”；著录于《钤山堂书画记》《清河书画舫》《庚子销夏记》《墨缘汇观》《石渠宝笈续编》，并刻入《三希堂法帖》等丛帖。原迹今藏台北故宫博物院。[鄂州日报，黄庭坚与松风阁](https://ga.eznews.cn/ezrb/2021/20210121/20210121_005/20210121_005_1.htm)

递藏一线可以当履历读：宋时归向氏，后入贾似道；元归大长公主祥哥喇吉；明入项元汴；清经孙承泽、安岐、卞永誉，终入内府。一件夜宿山寺写成的诗稿，从私人手里一路走到皇帝的书架，每一次转手都留一方印。[鄂州日报，黄庭坚与松风阁](https://ga.eznews.cn/ezrb/2021/20210121/20210121_005/20210121_005_1.htm)

### English

In 1102, the first year of Chongning, Huang Tingjian was removed from his post at Taiping and given the sinecure of supervising the Yulong Abbey in Hongzhou. He went up the Yangzi intending to settle near Jingnan, but stopped at Fankou in the ninth month and stayed on at Wuchang, today’s Ezhou. The Ezhou Municipal Museum records that he lodged in an old temple on West Mountain, “lost in the ancient pines that fill the slopes, sleeping to the sound of wind in the pines and rain”, and visited the Cold Stream, the Bodhisattva Spring, the fishing terrace and the Yiting pavilion; there, in the ninth month of that year, he wrote the Poem on the Hall of Wind and Pines. It is a seven-character poem, opening “a pavilion built against the hill looks out on the plain”, turning on the night rain and drinking, “rain sounding in the corridor till dawn”, and closing “the man of Dongpo has already sunk into the spring, when will Lord Zhang appear before my eyes” — Su Shi was dead, Zhang Lei had not come. In the same year and the same place he joined Zhang Lei and Chao Buzhi in writing the epitaphs of Li Shenzhi and his wife, which locals called “three excellences”. [Ezhou Municipal Museum, the Su family exhibition](https://ezbwg.com/ReadNews.asp?NewsId=1736)

The work is ink on paper. According to the Ezhou Daily it measures 32.8 by 219.2 cm and runs to twenty-nine lines. Fifteen colophons follow, from the Song and Yuan onward; the scroll carries the seals of Jia Sidao, the Grand Princess, Xiang Yuanbian, Sun Chengze, An Qi and Bian Yongyu, along with the Qianlong, Jiaqing and Xuantong “imperial inspection” seals. It is recorded in the Qianshan tang shuhua ji, the Qinghe shuhua fang, the Gengzi xiaoxia ji, the Moyuan huiguan and the second series of the Shiqu baoji, and was carved into the Sanxitang fatie and other modelbooks. The original is now in the National Palace Museum, Taipei. [Ezhou Daily, Huang Tingjian and the Hall of Wind and Pines](https://ga.eznews.cn/ezrb/2021/20210121/20210121_005/20210121_005_1.htm)

The chain of ownership can be read as a curriculum vitae: the Xiang family in the Song, then Jia Sidao; the Grand Princess Sengge Ragi in the Yuan; Xiang Yuanbian in the Ming; Sun Chengze, An Qi and Bian Yongyu in the Qing, and finally the imperial collection. A poem written overnight in a mountain temple travelled from private hands to the emperor’s shelves, leaving a seal at every change of hand. [Ezhou Daily, Huang Tingjian and the Hall of Wind and Pines](https://ga.eznews.cn/ezrb/2021/20210121/20210121_005/20210121_005_1.htm)
