# 大智禅师碑

## 背景

### 中文

唐开元二十四年（736）为慈恩寺僧义福所立。西安碑林博物馆藏品页记碑为螭首龟趺，高 345 厘米、宽 114 厘米，隶书 32 行、满行 61 字；中书侍郎严挺之撰文，隶书名家史惟则书写，碑阴《大智禅师碑阴》亦出其手。与汉隶摩崖对读，可见盛唐隶书的另一种整饬面目；本库已收唐玄宗《石台孝经》，同属唐隶。

### English

Erected in 736 for the monk Yifu of Ci'en Temple. The Xi'an Beilin Museum records a stele with a dragon head and tortoise base, 345 cm high and 114 cm wide, thirty-two lines of clerical script with up to sixty-one characters per line. The text is by the vice director of the Secretariat Yan Tingzhi and the writing by the noted clerical-script calligrapher Shi Weize, who also wrote the inscription on the reverse. Read against Han cliff inscriptions it shows the ordered face of High Tang clerical script; this library also holds the Stone Terrace Classic of Filial Piety by Emperor Xuanzong, another Tang clerical work.

## 待考

### 中文

- 馆方页面未给文物号、陈列位置与碑阴刻石年份；碑身与通高的量法未说明，不据其他著录换算。
- 史惟则生卒、籍贯与官职，所核馆方页面未记载，留空。
- 清人「开元第一」的评价出自馆方转述，本条不作艺术史定论。

### English

- The museum page gives no inventory number, gallery location or date for the reverse inscription; it does not explain whether the height is measured with head and base, so no conversion from other catalogues is made.
- Shi Weize's dates, origin and offices are not stated in the audited page and are left open.
- The Qing connoisseurs' "first of the Kaiyuan era" judgement is quoted by the museum; it is not adopted here as an art-historical verdict.
