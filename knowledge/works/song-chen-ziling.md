# 送陈子龄会试诗

## 背景

### 中文

王宠为门人陈子龄赴会试所作的三首诗，纸本，册页，纵二十三点二厘米，横三十六点三厘米，楷书十八行。款署「雅宜山人王宠。书似□湖尊兄先生。求正」，钤「太原王宠」「王履吉印」。文中「陈子龄」为王宠门人。[故宫博物院，送陈子龄会试诗页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229599.html)

馆方说此页「极尽涩拙之态，每一笔画均似在牵掣中运行，绝无流动率意之痕，结构亦不求平整」，按风格定为晚年之笔。徐邦达认为它「秀媚中有欹倾拙逸态」，是「小楷书中代表作品」；刘九庵说它「结构不求平整，多似漫不经，实则寓含险绝之势」。两个人的话合起来，正好说清这页的难处：看起来松，其实处处在较劲。[故宫博物院，送陈子龄会试诗页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229599.html)

### English

Three poems Wang Chong wrote for his disciple Chen Ziling, who was leaving for the metropolitan examination. Ink on paper, an album leaf, 23.2 by 36.3 cm, eighteen lines of regular script. It is signed “Yayi shanren Wang Chong, for the venerable Hu of ... to correct”, with his two seals. Chen Ziling was one of his students. [Palace Museum, poem leaf for Chen Ziling](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229599.html)

The museum calls the leaf “awkward to the last degree: every stroke seems to move against a drag, with no trace of fluent ease, and the frames do not aim at levelness”, and dates it by style to his late years. Xu Bangda found in it “a leaning, awkward ease within grace”, and called it a representative work in small script; Liu Jiuan said its structure “looks careless but holds a dangerous steepness”. Together the two remarks explain what is difficult here: it looks loose, and is straining everywhere. [Palace Museum, poem leaf for Chen Ziling](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229599.html)

## 待考

### 中文

- 页无年款，馆方以风格定晚年，非著录年份。
- 三首诗的释文未录，诗题未见于馆方页面。
- 上款「□湖尊兄」有一字被挖去，其人未考。

### English

- The leaf is undated; the late date rests on style, not on a record.
- The three poems are not transcribed, and their titles are not given on the museum page.
- One character of the dedicatee’s name has been cut away, and the person is unidentified.
