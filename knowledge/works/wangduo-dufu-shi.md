# 草书唐人诗卷

## 背景

### 中文

《草书唐人诗卷》纸本，卷装，今藏上海博物馆，馆方记其原名《草书杜甫诗卷》。卷中录杜甫诗七首，王湾、祖咏、綦毋潜诗各一首。款识「铎年五十五」，钤「王铎之印」「烟潭渔叟」。馆方系于清顺治三年（1646），王铎时年五十五，并称其用笔凝练俊健，使转纵横。[上海博物馆，清 王铎 草书唐人诗卷](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/m/article/id/CI00166295)

### English

The Scroll of Poems by Tang Poets is ink on paper, in handscroll form, and is now in the Shanghai Museum, which records its original title as Wang Duo’s Cursive-Script Scroll of Du Fu’s Poems. It carries seven poems by Du Fu and one each by Wang Wan, Zu Yong and Qiwu Qian. The inscription reads “Duo, aged fifty-five”, with the seals “Wang Duo’s seal” and “Fisherman of the Misty Pool”. The museum dates it to 1646, the third year of Shunzhi, when Wang Duo was fifty-five, and describes the brush as condensed and vigorous, the turns free and sweeping. [Shanghai Museum, Wang Duo’s cursive-script scroll of Tang poems](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/m/article/id/CI00166295)
