# 散氏盘

## 背景

### 中文

西周晚期，夨国侵散国后割田赔偿，铭文记定界、盟誓与地图，三百五十余字。书风奇逸跳宕，结字欹侧而姿态横生，被推为篆书的极致；与《毛公鼎》的庄重对读，可见西周晚期金文的两种面貌。

### English

In the late Western Zhou, the Ze state compensated San with land after an attack. The inscription records the boundaries, the oaths and a map in just over 350 characters. Its writing is restless and oblique, each character tilted into a new posture, and has been praised as the extreme of seal script. Set beside the grave Mao Gong Ding, it shows two faces of late Western Zhou bronze writing.

## 待考

### 中文

- 字数有三百五十字与三百五十七字两说，馆方页面作三百五十字。
- 器名旧称“散氏盘”，铭文自称“散”“夨”，定名与族属仍有讨论。

### English

- The count is given as either 350 or 357 characters; the museum page says 350.
- The vessel is conventionally called after the San clan, while the text names both San and Ze; the naming and clan relations are still discussed.
