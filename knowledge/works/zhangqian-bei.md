# 张迁碑

## 背景

### 中文

东汉中平三年（186），为县令张迁立碑。泰安市博物馆的活动记录将原石列于馆藏。

### English

An Eastern Han stele dated to 186 for the magistrate Zhang Qian. A Tai’an Museum activity report identifies the stone among its holdings.

## 导读

### 中文

东汉中平三年（186），为县令张迁立碑，立石者为故吏韦萌等。碑文没有署撰文者与书丹者姓名；作品条目把立碑者、刻工与撰书者分开记录。[中华珍宝馆竹云阁帖著录](https://g2.ltfc.net/work/SUFA/67d64a1899a6320270c19860)

碑文记“賃師孫興，刊石立表”，即刻工为孙兴；碑阴另列捐资题名四十一人。立碑者与刻工都没有被写成书丹者，读碑时也不应替它补一位名家。[国家图书馆《北京图书馆藏石刻叙录（六）》](https://www.nlc.cn/upload/attachments/2026-04-23/187a80e0.pdf)

原石今为泰安市博物馆馆藏。2025 年的馆方活动报道把它列入馆藏，但没有说明具体陈列位置；本站的[张迁碑原石](../catalogue/zhangqian-bei.md#stele-zhangqian)据此记录收藏，不据此判断今日是否展出。[泰安市博物馆活动报道](https://tsgw.taian.gov.cn/art/2025/5/19/art_48253_10316874.html)

另据碑帖著录，此石明初出土，但著录没有给出年份、地点与依据；出土、入藏与今日陈列状态，所核来源都没有记载。这些空白保留在实物条目的待考里，不用“相传”补足。[中华珍宝馆竹云阁帖著录](https://g2.ltfc.net/work/SUFA/67d64a1899a6320270c19860)

### English

In 186, the third year of Zhongping, the stele was erected for the magistrate Zhang Qian; the former subordinates Wei Meng and others put up the stone. The inscription names neither its author nor its calligrapher, and the record keeps sponsors, engraver and writers apart. [Zhu Yun Ge album note](https://g2.ltfc.net/work/SUFA/67d64a1899a6320270c19860)

The inscription says that the donors “hired the craftsman Sun Xing to cut the stone”, so Sun Xing is the engraver; the reverse lists forty-one donors. Neither the sponsors nor the engraver are presented as the calligrapher, and no famous hand should be supplied for them. [National Library of China, stone inscription survey](https://www.nlc.cn/upload/attachments/2026-04-23/187a80e0.pdf)

The original stone is now a Tai’an Museum holding. A 2025 museum activity report lists it among the collection but does not state where it is displayed; this site’s [original stone](../catalogue/zhangqian-bei.md#stele-zhangqian) records the holding on that basis and makes no claim about current display. [Tai’an Museum report](https://tsgw.taian.gov.cn/art/2025/5/19/art_48253_10316874.html)

An album note says the stone was unearthed in the early Ming, but gives no year, place or evidence; the audited source records neither the find nor the accession. These gaps stay open in the object record rather than being filled with tradition. [Zhu Yun Ge album note](https://g2.ltfc.net/work/SUFA/67d64a1899a6320270c19860)
