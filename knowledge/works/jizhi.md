# 祭侄文稿

## 背景

### 中文

唐颜真卿书《祭侄文稿》。此卷曾藏北宋宣和内府，《宣和书谱》著录内府所藏颜书二十八件，此其一；靖康之变后流出，辗转为越州石邦哲所得。至元二十一年（1284），鲜于枢在杭州重装此卷。

### English

Yan Zhenqing’s Draft Requiem for a Nephew. The scroll once belonged to the Northern Song Xuanhe imperial collection; the Xuanhe Shupu lists it among the twenty-eight Yan works held there. It left the palace after the Jingkang catastrophe and passed to Shi Bangzhe of Yuezhou. Xianyu Shu remounted it at Hangzhou in 1284.
