# 张猛龙碑

## 背景

### 中文

碑文颂张猛龙治郡之绩，其中尤记兴学之事。碑高280厘米、宽123厘米，额题“魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑”十二字，碑阳二十六行、满行四十六字，碑阴为题名。

### English

The stele commends Zhang Menglong’s local administration and promotion of education. It is 280 cm high and 123 cm wide; its heading reads “Stele in Praise of Prefect Zhang of Lu Commandery” in twelve characters, and the obverse carries twenty-six lines of forty-six characters, with names on the reverse.

## 待考

### 中文

- 碑阳行数与尺寸各源有异说：或记二十四行，或记二十二行；尺寸或记通高228厘米、宽91厘米。本库并列，不取其一。

### English

- Sources differ on the obverse line count (twenty-four or twenty-two) and on the size (280×123 cm or 228×91 cm). Both are kept, neither is chosen.
