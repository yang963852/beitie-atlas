# 乙瑛碑

## 背景

### 中文

东汉元嘉三年（153），碑记鲁相乙瑛请为孔庙置守庙人之事。原石藏于曲阜汉魏碑刻陈列馆。

### English

An Eastern Han stele of 153 recording the request by the chancellor of Lu, Yi Ying, to appoint a keeper for the Confucius Temple. Held in the Qufu Han–Wei Stele Gallery.
