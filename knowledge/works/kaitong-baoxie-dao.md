# 鄐君开通褒斜道摩崖

## 背景

### 中文

东汉永平年间为纪念石门隧道与褒斜道开通而刻的摩崖。汉台区政府页记永平六年至九年（63—66）凿石开路，并说郡守命人把开通经过刻于山崖；国务院侨务办公室转载的汉中市博物馆名誉馆长梁中效访谈记此刻为东汉永平九年（66）所刻，并称它是「中国文字书体由篆书向隶书过渡时期的重要物证」。上世纪七十年代初因修建水库，包括此摩崖在内的十三品石刻被凿迁至汉中市博物馆，专室陈列，世称「石门十三品」。

### English

A cliff inscription cut in the Yongping era of the Eastern Han to commemorate the opening of the Shimen tunnel and the Baoxie road. The Han tai district government dates the engineering works to 63-66 and says the governor had the account carved on the cliff; an interview with Liang Zhongxiao, honorary director of the Hanzhong Museum, republished by the State Council's Overseas Chinese Affairs Office, dates the carving to 66 and calls it important evidence for the transition from seal to clerical script. In the early 1970s a reservoir project led to the thirteen celebrated inscriptions, this among them, being cut from the cliff and moved to the Hanzhong Museum, where they are shown together as the Thirteen Shimen Pieces.

### 中文

- 两个来源分别给出「永平九年（66）刻石」与「永平六年至九年（63—66）开凿工程」，所指不同；本条以刻石年份入结构化日期，两种原文写法并存。
- 撰文者与书丹者姓名，所核两处页面均未记载。
- 原石的行数、存字与尺寸，所核两处页面均未给出，不据其他著录补写。

### English

- One page gives 66 for the carving, the other gives 63-66 for the road works. The structured date records the carving year; both source wordings are kept.
- Neither page names the author of the text or the calligrapher.
- The number of lines, surviving characters and measurements of the cliff are not given in either audited page and are not supplemented from other catalogues.
