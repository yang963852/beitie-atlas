# 祀三公山碑

## 背景

### 中文

东汉元初四年（117年），常山相冯君到官，时值羌寇蝗旱之后，吏民祷祀三公山神，甘雨屡降、年谷丰登，遂立石纪事。碑文十行，行字多寡不等，末列长史、五官掾、令、丞及刊石者姓名。人民网记此碑刻得极深，多数字至今清晰，是汉代碑刻中极有名的篆书，后世临摹整碑、集字为联者甚多。原石长期流传于封龙山一带，今存封龙书院汉碑亭，与《白石神君碑》并为河北境内仅存的汉代碑刻。

### English

In 117, Feng, Governor of Changshan, took office after Qiang raids, locusts and drought; the officials and people prayed to the god of Mount Sangong, rain fell repeatedly and the harvests returned, and a stone was set up to record it. Ten lines of unequal length end with the names of the officials and the cutter. People's Daily notes that the characters were cut deep and remain mostly clear, and that the stele is among the most famous seal-script Han monuments: later generations have copied it whole or gathered its characters into couplets. The stone stayed in the Fenglong Mountain area and now stands in the Han stele pavilion of the Fenglong Academy, one of the two Han steles surviving in Hebei, with the Baishishenjun stele.
