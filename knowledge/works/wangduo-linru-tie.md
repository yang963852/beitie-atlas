# 行书临汝帖轴

## 背景

### 中文

《行书临汝帖轴》绫本，纵237厘米，横53.4厘米，今藏故宫博物院。所临为《汝帖》中晋孝武帝司马曜《汝昨来帖》、陈永阳王伯智《热甚帖》、魏阮籍《欻尔帖》三帖。馆方指出，此作并非一味摹袭，对原帖有脱字，而笔法、结构、布白、墨法既渊源有自又自出匠心，可见王铎学书的方法。[故宫博物院，行书临汝帖轴](https://ggzl.dpm.org.cn/pages/exhibit_works/details?id=12680)

馆方又系此轴于清顺治三年（1646）八月，即其降清后授官之时，并认为它透露出王铎当时闲适平和的精神状态。[故宫博物院，行书临汝帖轴](https://ggzl.dpm.org.cn/pages/exhibit_works/details?id=12680)

### English

The Hanging Scroll of a Copy of the Ru Modelbook is ink on silk, 237 by 53.4 cm, now in the Palace Museum. It copies three pieces from the Ru modelbook: the “Ru zuolai tie” of Emperor Xiaowu of Jin, Sima Yao; the “Reshen tie” of Chen Bozhi, Prince of Yongyang; and the “Chuer tie” of Ruan Ji of Wei. The museum notes that the copy is no mere imitation: characters are omitted from the originals, while brush method, structure, spacing and ink are both rooted in the sources and the writer’s own, showing Wang Duo’s way of studying calligraphy. [Palace Museum, hanging scroll of a copy of the Ru modelbook](https://ggzl.dpm.org.cn/pages/exhibit_works/details?id=12680)

The museum dates the scroll to the eighth month of 1646, the third year of Shunzhi, after Wang Duo’s surrender and appointment under the Qing, and reads it as revealing a relaxed and peaceful state of mind at the time. [Palace Museum, hanging scroll of a copy of the Ru modelbook](https://ggzl.dpm.org.cn/pages/exhibit_works/details?id=12680)

## 待考

### 中文

- 馆方展品信息栏朝代作「明」，简介则称「清王铎」并系于顺治三年八月；本库按书写年份系于清，两说并存。
- 所临三帖的脱字位置，馆方未逐字标出。

### English

- The museum’s fact panel labels the scroll “Ming”, while its commentary calls it “Wang Duo of the Qing” and dates it to the eighth month of 1646; this library files it by writing year under the Qing, keeping both readings.
- The museum does not mark the omitted characters in the three copied pieces.
