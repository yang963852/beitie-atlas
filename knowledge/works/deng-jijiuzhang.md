# 章草书急就章卷

## 背景

### 中文

本卷书写史游《急就篇》，由邓文原以章草书之。故宫博物院藏本为纸本手卷，纵23.3厘米，横368.7厘米，文物号故00002791；文本作者与书写者分列，不把《急就篇》的著作权归给邓文原。[故宫博物院，邓文原章草书急就章卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

卷后邓文原自识：“为理仲雍书于大都庆寿寺僧房。”年款“大德三年三月十日”（1299），时年42岁。馆方说明理仲雍名熙，为于阗国（今和阗）人，曾为吴郡判官，亦见袁华跋；庆寿寺在元大都（今北京）西长安街，即双塔寺，今废。[故宫博物院，邓文原章草书急就章卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

馆方称本帖为邓文原早年书，“用笔圆转劲健，流露锋芒，有法度，甚合古人遗意”。所引元张雨跋则说：“素履斋书此，早年大合作，中岁以往，爵位日高，而书学益废，与之交笔研者，始以余言为不妄。殆暮年章草，如隔世矣，信为学不可止如此。”这是张雨的评语；馆方页未给该跋年月，本记录不补年。[故宫博物院，邓文原章草书急就章卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

故宫所藏本卷引首有弘历行书“草圣”二字，卷尾有董邦达所画松柏竹石图。本幅后有元代石岩、杨维祯、张雨，明代余诠、袁华、道衍、陈谦题跋。本幅钤“邓文原印”“巴西邓氏善之”“素履斋”；鉴藏印有“袁华私引”“姜绍书印”“二酉”及项元汴、乾隆、宣统内府诸印。《式古堂书画汇考》《珊瑚网书跋》《平生壮观》《大观录》皆有著录。[故宫博物院，邓文原章草书急就章卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

### English

This scroll copies Shi You’s *Jijiu pian* in Deng Wenyuan’s clerical cursive script. The Palace Museum’s paper handscroll is 23.3 cm high and 368.7 cm long, under inventory number 故00002791. The text author and calligrapher are credited separately; authorship of the *Jijiu pian* is not assigned to Deng.[Palace Museum, Deng Wenyuan’s clerical-cursive Jijiu Zhang](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

Deng’s self-note reads: “Written for Li Zhongyong in a monk’s room at Qingshou Temple in Dadu.” It is dated 大德三年三月十日 (1299), when he was forty-two. The museum identifies Li Zhongyong as Xi, a native of the kingdom of Yutian (present-day Khotan) who served as a prefectural judge in Wu Commandery and also appears in Yuan Hua’s colophon. It places Qingshou Temple on West Chang’an Street in Yuan Dadu (present-day Beijing), identifies it with the Twin Pagoda Temple, and says it is now lost.[Palace Museum, Deng Wenyuan’s clerical-cursive Jijiu Zhang](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

The museum calls this an early work by Deng, with “rounded, vigorous brushwork, exposed edges, discipline and a manner closely fitting the ancients”. It quotes Zhang Yu’s Yuan colophon: “Written in the Sulü Zhai, this is a great work of his early years. Thereafter, as his rank rose day by day, his calligraphy declined; those who shared ink and inkstone with him began to see that my words were not wild. His late-life clerical cursive seems from another age—truly, study must not stop.” This is Zhang Yu’s judgment. The page gives no date for his colophon, so none is supplied here.[Palace Museum, Deng Wenyuan’s clerical-cursive Jijiu Zhang](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

The Palace Museum scroll has a frontispiece with the Hongli emperor’s running-script words “草圣” (“Sage of Cursive”), and a painting of pines, cypresses, bamboo and rocks by Dong Bangda at the end. Colophons follow the calligraphy by Shi Yan, Yang Weizhen and Zhang Yu of the Yuan, and Yu Quan, Yuan Hua, Daoyan and Chen Qian of the Ming. The calligraphy bears “邓文原印”, “巴西邓氏善之” and “素履斋”; collector seals include “袁华私引”, “姜绍书印”, “二酉”, and seals of Xiang Yuanbian, the Qianlong emperor and the Xuantong imperial household. The scroll is recorded in the *Shigutang shuhua huikao*, *Shanhu wang shuba*, *Pingsheng zhuangguan* and *Daguan lu*.[Palace Museum, Deng Wenyuan’s clerical-cursive Jijiu Zhang](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

## 待考

### 中文

- 元明诸跋的署年与先后次序未录；张雨跋只作署名评语，不补年份。
- 入藏日期、递藏经历及现裱、展室未记。
- 尺寸、印鉴与题跋名单依馆方目录，未据原件复核。

### English

- The Yuan and Ming colophons have no recorded dates or sequence here; Zhang Yu’s colophon is kept as an attributed judgment without an invented year.
- The accession date, custody history, present mounting and gallery are not recorded.
- Measurements, seals and the colophon list follow the museum catalogue and have not been checked against the object.
