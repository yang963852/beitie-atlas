# 治平帖

## 背景

### 中文

纸本行书，纵二十九点二厘米，横四十五点二厘米。信写给治平史院主与徐大师两位：先问久别思念、法眷安好、佛阁是否建成、数年念经度得几人徒弟，末了托付石头桥、堋头两处坟茔照管，又嘱程六之事「惟频与提举是要」，署「八月十八日」。全篇没有一句谈书法，都是日常的托付与问候。[故宫博物院，苏轼行书治平帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231008.html)

馆方据卷后赵孟頫、文徵明、王穉登三家的跋，定此帖为苏轼熙宁年间在京师所作，时年三十余岁；又说它「笔法精细，字体遒媚」，与苏轼早年书风相合，并引赵孟頫跋中「字划风流韵胜」一语。卷前引首有明人所画苏轼像和释东皋妙声所书《东坡先生像赞》；卷末三跋之外，还有「商丘宋荦审定真迹」「吴江张基德载图书」两方鉴藏印，并见于《平生壮观》卷二、《装余偶记》卷二、《盛京书画录》第二册。一封托人照看坟茔的信，后来被装成卷、配上像与赞，读法便从「一封信」变成「一件苏轼」。[故宫博物院，苏轼行书治平帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231008.html)

### English

Ink on paper, 29.2 by 45.2 cm. The letter is addressed to the head of the Zhipping cloister and to Master Xu: it asks after a long separation and the health of the community, whether the Buddha hall has been completed and how many disciples have been ordained over the years, and it ends by entrusting the graves at Shitou Bridge and Pengtou to their care, with a word about Cheng Liu — “only keep pressing him, that is what matters” — signed “the eighteenth of the eighth month”. Not one sentence in it is about calligraphy; the whole sheet is ordinary errands and greetings. [Palace Museum, Su Shi’s letter to Zhipping](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231008.html)

From the colophons of Zhao Mengfu, Wen Zhengming and Wang Zhideng at the end, the museum dates the letter to Su Shi’s years in the capital under the Xining reign, when he was in his thirties; it describes the brush as “fine and precise, the characters fluent and graceful”, consistent with his early manner, and quotes Zhao Mengfu’s phrase “the strokes have the elegance of a fine manner”. The frontispiece carries a Ming portrait of Su Shi and a eulogy of the Dongpo portrait written by the monk Donggao Miaosheng; after the three colophons come the collectors’ seals “examined as genuine by Song Luo of Shangqiu” and “from the library of Zhang Ji of Wujiang”, and the letter is recorded in the second volume of the Pingsheng zhuangguan, the second volume of the Zhuangyu ouji and the second fascicle of the Shengjing shuhua lu. A letter asking friends to look after a grave was later mounted as a scroll and furnished with a portrait and a eulogy; the sheet now asks to be read as a work of Su Shi, not only as a letter. [Palace Museum, Su Shi’s letter to Zhipping](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231008.html)

### English

- The year is inferred from the colophons; the letter itself is undated and no year is entered here.
- The museum says only that a Ming artist painted the frontispiece portrait and that the monk Donggao Miaosheng wrote the eulogy; it gives neither name nor date.
- The inventory number 故00002887 and the measurements follow the museum’s catalogue page and have not been checked against the object.
