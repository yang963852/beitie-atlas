# 黄州寒食诗帖

## 背景

### 中文

北宋苏轼书《黄州寒食诗帖》。此卷纸本，书《寒食雨二首》，卷后有黄庭坚跋。国立故宫博物院记其于1987年购藏。

### English

Su Shi’s Cold Food Poems. The scroll is paper and carries the two Cold Food poems; a colophon by Huang Tingjian follows them. The National Palace Museum records its purchase in 1987.
