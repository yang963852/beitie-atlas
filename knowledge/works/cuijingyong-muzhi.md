# 崔敬邕墓志

## 背景

### 中文

北魏《崔敬邕墓志》，熙平二年（517）十一月二十五日刻，楷书二十九行、行二十九字。上海图书馆展览说明记：清康熙十八年（1679）在河北安平县出土，康熙三十年（1691）被县令陈宗石砌入乡贤祠，嘉庆年间罢任时携去，石旋佚，故拓本流传极稀。本条收录该馆藏端方旧藏浓淡墨拼合初拓本，前半淡墨拓八开、后半浓墨拓四开，列国家一级文物；册中有王士祯致朱彝尊手札及王瓘、张祖翼、端方、罗振玉、褚德彝等题跋。

### English

The Northern Wei Epitaph of Cui Jingyong was carved in 517, twenty-nine lines of twenty-nine characters in regular script. The Shanghai Library's exhibition note records that it was unearthed at Anping, Hebei, in 1679, was built into the local worthies' shrine by the county magistrate in 1691, was carried away when a later magistrate left office, and soon disappeared, so rubbings are extremely rare. This entry covers the library's composite album of an early impression in light and dark ink, formerly Duanfang's, listed as a first-grade cultural relic, with a letter from Wang Shizhen to Zhu Yizun and colophons by Wang Guan, Zhang Zuyi, Duanfang, Luo Zhenyu, Chu Deyi and others.
