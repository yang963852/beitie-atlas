# 爨宝子碑

## 背景

### 中文

刻于东晋义熙元年（碑署大亨四年），清乾隆四十三年（1778）在曲靖城南杨旗田出土，后湮没，咸丰二年（1852）重新出土并移置城内武侯祠，1937 年移入曲靖中学（今曲靖一中）碑亭。1961 年列为首批全国重点文物保护单位。碑以方整古拙的书体著称，是研究隶楷之变的重要实物。


## 待考

### 中文

- 碑自署「大亨四年」，省文旅厅与曲靖市政府均读作 405 年，云南省文物考古研究所页面作 404 年；两说并存，不作取舍。
- 公布宽度有 0.68 米（省、市页面）与 0.86 米（云南宣传网页面）两种，结构化尺寸采用两个政府页面一致的数字。
- 后世有著作称撰书者姓名，所引政府页面未载，故不主张作者。

### English

- The inscription's own date, 大亨四年, is read as 405 by the provincial culture department and the municipal government; a provincial archaeology institute page gives 404. Both readings are recorded.
- Published widths differ: 0.68 m (provincial and municipal pages) and 0.86 m (a provincial publicity page). The structured dimensions use the figure the two government pages agree on.
- Some later accounts name a writer; the cited government pages do not, so this record asserts no hand.

## 导读

### 中文

碑自署“大亨四年”。云南省文化和旅游厅与曲靖市政府把这一年读作 405 年，云南省文物考古研究所的页面作 404 年；作品条目把两说并列，不替读者选一个。[云南省文化和旅游厅答复函](https://dct.yn.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/qtgk/zxtablqk/202404/t20240410_2381763.html)[云南省文物考古研究所页面](https://www.ynkgs.cn/view/ynkgPC/1/248/view/1514.html)

此碑清乾隆四十三年（1778）在曲靖城南杨旗田出土，后一度湮没；咸丰二年（1852）重新出土，移入城内武侯祠，1937 年再移入曲靖中学（今曲靖一中）碑亭。1961 年，它被列为首批全国重点文物保护单位。本站记录的是[爨宝子碑原石](../catalogue/cuan-baozi-bei.md#stele-cuan-baozi)。[曲靖市政府《爨文化博物馆》](https://www.qj.gov.cn/html/2020/csmp_0417/83745.html)[云南省文化和旅游厅答复函](https://dct.yn.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/qtgk/zxtablqk/202404/t20240410_2381763.html)

书体方面，作品条目记为“隶书向楷书过渡”，这是为检索与阅读设置的分类，不是对某一笔画的描述。公布宽度另有 0.68 米与 0.86 米两说，条目并列而不合并；引用时先说明依据的是哪一说。[云南省文化和旅游厅答复函](https://dct.yn.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/qtgk/zxtablqk/202404/t20240410_2381763.html)[云南省文物考古研究所页面](https://www.ynkgs.cn/view/ynkgPC/1/248/view/1514.html)

碑上未署撰书者；后世虽有著作称撰书者姓名，所引政府页面未载，故本条目不主张作者。[曲靖市政府《爨文化博物馆》](https://www.qj.gov.cn/html/2020/csmp_0417/83745.html)

### English

The stone dates itself to the fourth year of the Daheng era. The Yunnan Provincial Department of Culture and Tourism and the Qujing municipal government read that year as 405, while a Yunnan institute of archaeology page gives 404; the record sets the two readings side by side and does not choose one. [Yunnan culture department reply](https://dct.yn.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/qtgk/zxtablqk/202404/t20240410_2381763.html) [Yunnan archaeology institute page](https://www.ynkgs.cn/view/ynkgPC/1/248/view/1514.html)

The stone was unearthed at Yangqitian south of Qujing in 1778, then lost again; it was recovered in 1852 and moved into the Wuhou shrine in the city, and in 1937 it was moved into the stele pavilion at the Qujing school, now Qujing No. 1 Middle School. In 1961 it was listed among the first group of nationally protected cultural sites. This site records [the original stone of the Cuan Baozi stele](../catalogue/cuan-baozi-bei.md#stele-cuan-baozi). [Qujing municipal government, Cuan Culture Museum](https://www.qj.gov.cn/html/2020/csmp_0417/83745.html) [Yunnan culture department reply](https://dct.yn.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/qtgk/zxtablqk/202404/t20240410_2381763.html)

For script, the record classifies the stele as a transition from clerical to regular script; this is a classification for retrieval and reading, not a description of any single stroke. Published widths also differ, 0.68 m and 0.86 m; the record keeps both rather than merging them, and a citation should say which figure it follows. [Yunnan culture department reply](https://dct.yn.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/qtgk/zxtablqk/202404/t20240410_2381763.html) [Yunnan archaeology institute page](https://www.ynkgs.cn/view/ynkgPC/1/248/view/1514.html)

The stone names no writer or calligrapher. Later works do give names, but the cited government pages do not, so this record asserts no author. [Qujing municipal government, Cuan Culture Museum](https://www.qj.gov.cn/html/2020/csmp_0417/83745.html)
