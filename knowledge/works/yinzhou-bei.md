# 尹宙碑

## 背景

### 中文

国家图书馆「碑帖菁华」著录《尹宙墓碑》，年代作东汉熹平六年（177），书体为隶书，刻立地河南省鄢陵县；碑额篆书横题「從銘」。该馆所藏为清乾隆拓，一张，192×92 厘米，顾千里、瞿镛旧藏，丁惠康捐赠，馆藏号「顧專78」。著录中的拓本与原碑是两件事，尺寸与题跋属于拓本。

### English

The National Library of China catalogues the Stele of Yin Zhou under the Eastern Han year 177, in clerical script, carved in Yanling County, Henan, with a seal-script heading reading "Cong ming". Its holding is a Qianlong-era rubbing, one sheet, 192 by 92 cm, once owned by Gu Qianli and Qu Yong, donated by Ding Huikang, with the shelf mark Gu zhuan 78. The rubbing and the stone are separate things: the measurements and collectors' seals belong to the rubbing.

## 待考

### 中文

- 「4月24日卒」的「卒」字所指未明，页面未说明是碑主卒日还是立碑纪年；不据此推定月日。
- 原碑今日是否存世、存于何处，所核页面未记载。
- 撰文者与书丹者均未署名，馆藏著录作「[佚名]撰」。

### English

- The word "卒" in the catalogue date is unexplained; it is not taken as evidence for a specific month or day.
- Whether the original stone survives and where it is held are not stated in the audited page.
- Neither the author nor the calligrapher is named; the catalogue gives "[anonymous]".
