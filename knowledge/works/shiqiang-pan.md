# 史墙盘

## 背景

### 中文

西周恭王时，微氏史官墙作盘，内底铸铭十八行二百八十四字，前半颂文、武、成、康、昭、穆及恭王，后半记微氏六代家史。铭文排列整齐、笔势流畅，是西周中期金文由雄浑转向整饬的代表，也是研究西周史与微氏家族的第一手材料。

### English

Under King Gong of Zhou, the scribe Qiang of the Wei clan made this basin. Its inner base carries eighteen lines and 284 characters: the first half praises the kings from Wen to Mu, the second records six generations of the Wei family. Evenly spaced and fluently written, it represents the shift from the vigorous early Western Zhou manner to a more ordered middle-period style.

## 待考

### 中文

- 铭文所记家史可与《史记》对读，但微氏高祖的封地仍有异说。
- 盘铭的“恭王”为学界据世系推定，铭文本身未书王名。

### English

- The family history can be read against the Shiji, but the original fief of the Wei clan remains disputed.
- The attribution to King Gong rests on the king list, not on a royal name in the text.
