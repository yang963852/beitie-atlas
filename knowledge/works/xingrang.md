# 行穰帖

## 背景

### 中文

唐代双钩摹本，馆方认为应是原信的前半。文字最早著录于张彦远《法书要录》，后入北宋宣和内府；北宋亡后，金人王庭筠刻入《雪溪堂帖》。明代经项元汴、韩世能、董其昌鉴藏，董其昌以此帖换得李唐《江山小景图》卷，友人吴廷收得后刻入《余清斋法帖》。今藏普林斯顿大学艺术博物馆，John B. Elliott 遗赠。

### English

A Tang double-outline tracing copy, which the museum takes to be the first half of the letter. The text is first recorded in Zhang Yanyuan's ninth-century Fashu yaolu and entered the Northern Song Xuanhe collection; after the dynasty fell, Wang Tingyun engraved it in the Xuexitang modelbooks. In the Ming it passed through Xiang Yuanbian, Han Shineng and Dong Qichang, who traded it for Li Tang's River Scenes; his friend Wu Ting then had it engraved in the Yuqingzhai modelbooks. It is now in the Princeton University Art Museum, the John B. Elliott bequest.
