# 鸭头丸帖

## 背景

### 中文

唐摹本，纸本。经北宋宣和内府收藏，现存部分宣和裱。晚明为吴廷族兄吴希元所藏，董其昌与王肯堂因此得观并题跋。馆方以「外拓」用笔与连绵相属的「一笔书」说明王献之在父书之外的变革。

### English

A Tang tracing copy on paper. It passed through the Northern Song Xuanhe collection, and part of the Xuanhe mounting survives. In the late Ming it belonged to Wu Xiyuan, a cousin of Wu Ting, which gave Dong Qichang and Wang Kentang the chance to see it and add colophons. The museum explains Wang Xianzhi's departure from his father's manner through his outward-turning brush and the linked momentum later called one-stroke writing.
