# 曹全碑

## 背景

### 中文

碑为东汉曹全而立，今藏西安碑林。碑上留下了受颂者的姓名，执笔者却未署名。

### English

An Eastern Han stele for the official Cao Quan, held at the Xi’an Beilin Museum. No scribe is recorded on the stone.

## 导读

### 中文

《曹全碑》是东汉为曹全所立的碑，今藏西安碑林。碑上留下了受颂者的姓名，却没有署撰文者与书丹者的姓名。[西安碑林博物馆条目](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=145)

年代有两说：西安碑林[现行条目](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=145)与[故宫条目](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228534.html)作 185 年，碑林[另一条目](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2996&c=View&lang=en&m=home)作 186 年。条目把两说并列，不替读者选一个。

读碑时可以先看碑阴所列捐资立碑的属吏王敞、王毕等；他们是立碑人，不是撰文者或书丹者。原石与故宫明初拓本要分开读：本站记录的是[曹全碑原石](../catalogue/cao-quan-bei.md#stele-cao-quan)，纸上的墨色不能当成石面的特征。[故宫明初拓本条目](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228534.html)

### English

The Stele of Cao Quan is an Eastern Han stele erected for Cao Quan, now held at the Xi’an Beilin Museum. The stone preserves the name of the man it honors, but it records neither the author of its text nor its calligrapher. [Xi’an Beilin Museum entry](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=145)

The date has two readings: the current Xi’an Beilin [entry](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=145) and the [Palace Museum entry](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228534.html) give 185, while [another Beilin entry](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2996&c=View&lang=en&m=home) gives 186. The record sets the two readings side by side and does not choose one for the reader.

When reading the stone, begin with the donors listed on the reverse, the subordinate officials Wang Chang, Wang Bi and others; they paid for and erected the stone, and they are not its author or calligrapher. Read the original stone and the Palace Museum’s early Ming rubbing as two separate things: this site records [the original stone](../catalogue/cao-quan-bei.md#stele-cao-quan), and ink on paper cannot be treated as a feature of the stone surface. [Palace Museum early Ming rubbing](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228534.html)
