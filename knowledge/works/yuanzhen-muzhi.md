# 元桢墓志

## 背景

### 中文

刻于北魏太和二十年（496），志石高七十厘米，宽七十厘米。志主元桢（447—496）为北魏宗室，皇兴二年（468）封南安王。他以孝行著称，却恃宠而骄，因敛财枉法被削爵夺职；后随孝文帝南征，在宗室重臣反对迁都时襄助孝文帝，促成迁都洛阳，不但恢复爵位，还成为镇北大将军、相州刺史。[西安碑林博物馆，元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)

馆方评此志书法：「以雄奇之气势、恣肆之意态、朴拙之质感、刚劲之笔力，成为书法艺术转型时期的代表作。」志文所记的削爵、复爵与迁都，也可当作北魏改革时期统治阶层的一段侧写。[西安碑林博物馆，元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)

1926 年洛阳出土，1938 年于右任捐藏西安碑林。[西安碑林博物馆，元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)

### English

Carved in 496, the twentieth year of Taihe, the stone measures 70 by 70 cm. Its subject, Yuan Zhen (447–496), was a prince of the Northern Wei imperial clan, enfeoffed as Prince of Nan’an in 468. He was known for filial conduct but, relying on favour, grew arrogant and was stripped of rank for corrupt enrichment; later, on the southern campaign, he supported Emperor Xiaowen’s plan to move the capital to Luoyang against the opposition of the clan and senior ministers. He recovered his title and became General of the North and governor of Xiangzhou. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Epitaph of Yuan Zhen](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)

The museum describes the calligraphy as “a representative work of the period of transition in calligraphic art, with a masculine and extraordinary momentum, an unbridled bearing, a plain and blunt texture, and a hard, strong brush”. The record of demotion, restoration and the move of the capital also reads as a side-light on the ruling class during the Northern Wei reforms. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Epitaph of Yuan Zhen](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)

It was unearthed at Luoyang in 1926 and given to the Beilin by Yu Youren in 1938. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Epitaph of Yuan Zhen](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)
