# 草书五言联

## 背景

### 中文

上海博物馆将此作定为近现代于右任草书楹联，纸本，纵138.4厘米，横33.7厘米。款识「民国三十九年十一月于右任」，题跋「万里先生法正」；馆方简介明确说书于1950年，时年七十二岁。民国三十九年即1950年，款识记至十一月，未记具体日。[上海博物馆，藏品字段与简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160765)

「万里先生法正」是题跋中的献词，不是联语；所引页面没有转录五言联正文。馆方评其「笔力雄强深厚，以魏碑之法作草，气势磅礴」，这是馆方的书艺评价。[上海博物馆，题跋字段与简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160765)

### English

The Shanghai Museum catalogues this as a modern paper couplet in cursive script by Yu Youren, 138.4 cm high and 33.7 cm long. The inscription reads “民国三十九年十一月于右任,” and the colophon reads “万里先生法正.” The museum’s introduction states that it was written in 1950, when Yu was seventy-two. 民国三十九年 corresponds to 1950; the inscription gives November but no exact day. [Shanghai Museum, catalogue fields and introduction](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160765)

“万里先生法正” is a dedication in the colophon field, not the couplet text. The cited page does not transcribe the five-character couplet. The museum describes the brushwork as forceful and profound, using the method of Wei steles in cursive writing, with majestic momentum; this is the museum’s artistic evaluation. [Shanghai Museum, colophon field and introduction](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160765)
