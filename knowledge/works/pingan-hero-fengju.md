# 平安何如奉橘三帖

## 背景

### 中文

一卷三札。〈平安〉报平安，行书兼草书；〈何如〉问候友人并告近况；〈奉橘〉原是〈何如〉的附书，北宋时独立成帖，三帖最晚在明末合裱为一卷。卷中摹出南朝至隋鉴书人的押署，并有开皇十八年（598）三月廿七日柳顾言、诸葛颖、释智果题记。馆方数位典藏称三帖为「唐代以前」的摹本，南部院区展件说明则称「唐人双钩廓填」。

### English

Three letters on one scroll. Ping'an reports that all is well, in running script mixed with cursive; Heru greets a friend and gives news of himself; Fengju began as a postscript to Heru and was separated into its own piece in the Northern Song. The three were joined into a single scroll by the late Ming at the latest. The sheet also reproduces the half-signatures of Southern Chen to Sui authenticators and a colophon dated the twenty-seventh day of the third month of Kaihuang 18 (598), signed by Liu Guyan, Zhuge Ying and the monk Zhiguo. The digital archive calls the copies pre-Tang; a Southern Branch exhibition note calls them Tang double-outline tracings.

## 待考

### 中文

- 摹写年代有「唐代以前」与「唐人双钩廓填」两说，本记录不填年代。
- 装裱横长，馆方数位典藏作 46.8 厘米，文化部古物公告作 47.3 厘米，不取一数。
- 〈平安〉失去末两行，馆方文稿记此事而未记失去的时间。

### English

- The copying period is given as either pre-Tang or Tang; no date is recorded here.
- The mounted width is 46.8 cm in the museum record and 47.3 cm in the 2012 designation; neither figure is chosen.
- The museum essay notes that Ping'an lost its last two lines but does not date the loss.
