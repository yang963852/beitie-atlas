# 武威仪礼简

## 背景

### 中文

1959年武威磨嘴子汉墓出土，四百六十九枚，抄录《仪礼》九篇，墨书成熟汉隶。结体扁平、蚕头燕尾，字距疏朗，是汉代写本隶书的典范；与《熹平石经》的刻隶对读，可见墨迹与刻石的不同。

### English

The Wuwei tomb at Mozuizi yielded 469 slips in 1959, carrying nine chapters of the Yili in mature Han clerical script. Flat forms, silkworm-head and swallow-tail strokes, and generous spacing make it a model of Western Han manuscript clerical script. Set beside the carved Xiping stone classics, it shows what the brush does that the chisel does not.
