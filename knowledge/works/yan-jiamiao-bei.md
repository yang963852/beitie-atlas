# 颜氏家庙碑

## 背景

### 中文

颜真卿于建中元年（780）为其父颜惟贞立此家庙碑，三年后遇害。碑阳、碑阴各二十四行、行四十七字，两侧各六行、行五十二字；螭首龟趺，高三百三十八厘米。唐室离乱后碑仆于郊野，北宋太平兴国七年（982）移入府城文庙重立，为后来移入今碑林最早的碑石之一。

### English

Yan Zhenqing made this shrine stele for his father Yan Weizhen in 780, three years before his death. Both faces carry 24 lines of 47 characters; each side carries 6 lines of 52. The stone is 338 cm high with a chi-dragon head and a turtle base. Having fallen in the fields during the disorders of the late Tang, it was re-erected in the prefectural Confucian temple in 982, and it is among the earliest steles moved to the site of the present Beilin.

## 待考

### 中文

- 馆方只记高三百三十八厘米，未记宽厚，故仅录高度。
- 982 年为碑阳宋人题记所记重立之年，据馆方概述；非立碑之年。

### English

- The museum gives the height as 338 cm and does not give width or thickness, so only the height is recorded.
- The 982 date is the re-erection recorded in the inscription's own Song colophon, as summarised by the museum; it is not the date of the stele.
