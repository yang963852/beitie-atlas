# 大秦景教流行中国碑

## 背景

### 中文

唐人在长安大秦寺立碑，追记景教使团于贞观九年（635）入唐，继而叙其后一百五十年间的兴替。馆方将此石列为世界著名碑刻之一。

### English

A Tang stele erected at the Daqin monastery in Chang’an, recording the arrival of the Nestorian mission in 635 and its fortunes for the following century and a half. The museum calls it one of the four famous steles of the world.
