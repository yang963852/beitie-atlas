# 石台孝经

## 背景

### 中文

玄宗亲为《孝经》作序、注解并以隶书写经，李亨篆额。三十五方石合为一碑，下承三层石台，故名《石台孝经》。

### English

The emperor’s own preface, commentary, and clerical-script text of the Classic of Filial Piety, assembled from 35 blocks into a stele with a three-tiered base. The heading in seal script is by Li Heng (Emperor Suzong).
