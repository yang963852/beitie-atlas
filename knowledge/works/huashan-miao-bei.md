# 西岳华山庙碑

## 背景

### 中文

东汉延熹八年（165）立于华阴西岳庙，碑文记历代封禅祀典与弘农太守袁逢重修华岳庙之事，字为隶书。故宫展览页列举四种可信的传世拓本：长垣本、华阴本、四明本、顺德本；其中四明本与华阴本今藏故宫博物院。四明本整纸装裱一轴、保留碑额两旁唐人题名，华阴本剪条装裱三十八开，两本的装裱、尺寸与递藏各自独立，不能互相顶替。本库把它们分别记为两件实物。

### English

Erected in 165 at the temple of Mount Hua in Huayin, the stele records the history of imperial sacrifices and the rebuilding of the temple by the governor Yuan Feng; its script is clerical. A Palace Museum exhibition record names four accepted transmitted rubbings - Changyuan, Huayin, Siming and Shunde - of which the Siming and Huayin versions are held by the Palace Museum. The Siming version is an uncut mounted scroll preserving Tang inscriptions beside the heading; the Huayin version is a cut-and-mounted album of thirty-eight leaves. Their mounting, measurements and custody histories belong to each copy separately, and this library records them as two objects.

## 待考

### 中文

- 原石何时、因何毁佚，以及今日是否尚有残石，所核两处馆方页面均未记载，留空。
- 碑末「郭香察书」名款是否为书丹者署名，馆方页面只记名款存在，未作判定；异说并存，本条不取其一。
- 长垣本、顺德本的今日收藏地，所核馆方页面未给出，不推定。

### English

- When and why the original stone was lost, and whether fragments survive, are not stated in either museum page and are left open.
- The museum records the "Guo Xiangcha shu" signature at the end of the text but does not decide whether it names the calligrapher. The competing readings stand side by side.
- The present holdings of the Changyuan and Shunde versions are not given in the audited pages and are not inferred.
