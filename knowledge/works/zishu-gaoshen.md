# 自书告身帖

## 背景

### 中文

《自书告身帖》是颜真卿从吏部尚书转任太子少师的任免文书，卷藏日本东京台东区立书道博物馆。上海博物馆「丹青宝筏」大展数字展厅记：时代唐代，创作年份建中元年（780），纸本，收藏单位为东京台东区立书道博物馆；简介称「相传是由颜本人所书，故称『自书告身帖』」，卷尾有蔡襄、米友仁和董其昌题跋；递藏经南宋内府、贾似道，明徐守和、清梁清标与安岐入乾隆内府，后为恭亲王奕䜣藏，传至溥儒。2018 年该卷借展上博「丹青宝筏」大展。

### English

The Self-written Certificate of Appointment records Yan Zhenqing's transfer from Minister of Personnel to Junior Tutor of the Heir Apparent; the scroll is held by the Calligraphy Museum of Taito Ward, Tokyo. The Shanghai Museum's digital exhibition room for its 2018 Dong Qichang show dates it to 780, notes it is on paper and records the holding institution; its text says the certificate is "said to have been written by Yan himself, hence the title", lists colophons by Cai Xiang, Mi Youren and Dong Qichang, and traces custody through the Southern Song court and Jia Sidao, Xu Shouhe in the Ming, Liang Qingbiao and An Qi into the Qianlong court, then Prince Gong and Pu Ru. The scroll was lent to that Shanghai exhibition.
