# 三坟记

## 背景

### 中文

《三坟记》刻于唐大历二年（767），李季卿撰文，李阳冰篆书，记其三位兄长迁葬之事。碑今存西安碑林，螭首龟趺，碑身中部横断，两面刻篆书。李阳冰承李斯《峄山碑》的玉箸笔法，线条匀细而结体修长，此碑是唐篆复兴的代表，也是学小篆从秦碑过渡到唐碑的常用范本。

### English

Record of the Three Graves was carved in 767, with text by Li Jiqing and seal script by Li Yangbing, to record the reburial of Li Jiqing’s three elder brothers. The stone is now in the Beilin Museum in Xi’an: a monument with a damaged chi-dragon head and a tortoise plinth, broken across the middle, with seal script on both faces. Li Yangbing worked from Li Si’s jade-chopstick manner in the Yishan inscription, with even, fine strokes and tall, balanced characters. The stele represents the Tang revival of seal script and is a common model for moving from Qin to Tang seal writing.

### English

- The museum dates the carving to 767. Another account says the original was lost and the present stone is a Song recutting; its date and evidence remain unchecked.
- The stone is damaged. Line and character counts follow the museum entry, and some characters are missing.
