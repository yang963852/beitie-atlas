# 篆书急就章轴

## 背景

### 中文

清赵之谦篆书《急就篇》节录轴，纸本，纵112.4厘米、横46.4厘米，今藏故宫博物院（文物号新00094049）。轴录史游《急就篇》四句，末识“史游急就篇，益斋仁兄正。赵之谦”，钤“赵之谦印”。故宫记其书宗邓石如篆法，又融入魏碑笔意，折笔由圆变方，笔势浑厚，而肥扁多于圆劲，结字严整、张弛有度，全幅凝重沉劲。赵之谦篆书存世不多，此轴是他把邓派篆法与北碑笔意打通的一个实例。

### English

This hanging scroll, ink on paper, 112.4 by 46.4 cm, is a seal-script excerpt from Shi You's Jijiu pian by Zhao Zhiqian of the Qing, now in the Palace Museum (no. 新00094049). It quotes four lines of the text and is signed “史游急就篇，益斋仁兄正。赵之谦”, with his seal. The Palace Museum describes his manner as Deng Shiru's seal script worked over with Wei-stele brush habits: turns squared where they would be round, the strokes heavy rather than roundly springy, the characters strict and well poised. Zhao's seal script rarely survives, and this scroll shows how he joined the Deng school to northern stele brushwork.

## 待考

### 中文

- 具体创作年代不详，馆方仅据书风推测为晚年之作。
- 受书人“益斋”，故宫据考可能是上海人陈以谦，字益斋，工诗文词；此考非定论。

### English

- The exact date is unknown; the museum dates it only by style, to Zhao's late years.
- The recipient “Yizhai” is thought by the Palace Museum to be Chen Yiqian of Shanghai, courtesy name Yizhai, a poet and orchid painter; the identification is not certain.
