# 王铎行草书自书诗卷

## 背景

### 中文

此卷书王铎自作诗六首，纸本，作于明崇祯十二年（1639）正月，王铎时年四十七，今藏故宫博物院。行草笔势连绵而字间连带不多，靠行气贯通，是他中年书风形成期的面貌。王铎由钟繇、二王、颜真卿、米芾上溯，以行草著称，此卷正可看作其风格的起点。[故宫博物院，王铎行草书自书诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228265.html)

据故宫著录，卷纵二十六点九厘米、横一百六十六厘米。释文起首作“君宣年家兄翁判南康，赠以六首”，末款“己卯正月弟王铎具草”，左钤“王铎之印”“烟潭渔叟”。近人马宗霍说：“明人草书，无不纵笔以取势者，觉斯则纵而能敛，故不极势而势若不尽，非力有余者，未易语此。”这卷正可拿来验这句话：笔势一路连绵，却始终没有放尽，收在行气里。[故宫博物院，王铎行草书自书诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228265.html)

王铎（1592—1652），字觉斯，号嵩樵、烟潭渔叟、痴仙道人，孟津（今洛阳孟津县）人。明天启二年（1622）进士，累官礼部尚书、东阁大学士。书法师钟繇、二王、颜真卿、米芾，尤得力于《淳化阁帖》，既浸淫古法又有所创新，最擅行、草，刻有《拟山园帖》《琅华馆帖》，《清史稿》有传。[故宫博物院，王铎行草书自书诗卷·王铎](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228265.html)

### English

This scroll carries six of Wang Duo’s own poems. It is ink on paper, written in the first month of 1639, when Wang was forty-seven, and is now in the Palace Museum. The running-cursive brushwork runs on continuously without many joined characters, held together by column rhythm; it shows the manner of his middle years as his style took shape. Wang traced his lineage through Zhong Yao, the two Wangs, Yan Zhenqing and Mi Fu, and became known for running and cursive script. This scroll can be read as the starting point of that style. [Palace Museum, Wang Duo’s scroll of his own poems](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228265.html)

The museum gives its size as 26.9 by 166 cm. The text opens “for my elder brother of the same examination year, Judge of Nankang, six poems presented”, and closes “respectfully drafted by your younger brother Wang Duo, first month of jimao”, with the seals “Wang Duo’s seal” and “Fisherman of the Misty Pool” at the left. The modern scholar Ma Zonghuo wrote: “Ming men’s cursive all stretch the brush to seize momentum; Juesi stretches and can also hold back, so he never exhausts the momentum and yet it seems inexhaustible — this is not something a man of less than full strength could say.” The scroll tests that sentence: the brush runs on and on, yet never quite lets go, held inside the column rhythm. [Palace Museum, Wang Duo’s scroll of his own poems](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228265.html)

Wang Duo (1592–1652), courtesy name Juesi, style names Songqiao, Fisherman of the Misty Pool and Chixian Daoren, was a native of Mengjin, modern Mengjin county in Luoyang. He passed the examinations in 1622 and rose to minister of rites and grand secretary of the East Pavilion. In calligraphy he studied Zhong Yao, the two Wangs, Yan Zhenqing and Mi Fu, drawing most on the Chunhua ge tie; steeped in the old methods, he also innovated, and was strongest in running and cursive script. His carvings include the Nishan yuan tie and the Langhua guan tie, and the Qing History carries his biography. [Palace Museum, Wang Duo’s scroll, biography of Wang Duo](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228265.html)

## 待考

### 中文

- 本幅无藏印，亦未见著录，早期流传不明。
- 卷首上款所涉人物，馆方未作考订。
- 馆方条目朝代作“清”，而款识为己卯（明崇祯十二年），本库按书写年份系于明。
- 馆方说明记上款作“君宣”，同页释文作“群宣”，一字之异未详孰是。

### English

- The scroll carries no collector’s seals and is not recorded in early catalogues; its early transmission is unknown.
- The museum does not identify the dedicatee named at the beginning of the scroll.
- The museum catalogues the scroll under the Qing, though its colophon is dated jimao, the twelfth year of Chongzhen in the Ming; this entry dates it by the colophon.
- The museum’s note gives the dedicatee’s name as Junxuan while its transcription of the text reads Qunxuan; the one-character difference is left unresolved.
