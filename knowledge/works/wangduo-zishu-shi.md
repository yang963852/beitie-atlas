# 王铎行草书自书诗卷

## 背景

### 中文

此卷书王铎自作诗六首，纸本，作于明崇祯十二年（1639）正月，王铎时年四十七，今藏故宫博物院。行草笔势连绵而字间连带不多，靠行气贯通，是他中年书风形成期的面貌。王铎由钟繇、二王、颜真卿、米芾上溯，以行草著称，此卷正可看作其风格的起点。

### English

This scroll carries six of Wang Duo’s own poems. It is ink on paper, written in the first month of 1639, when Wang was forty-seven, and is now in the Palace Museum. The running-cursive brushwork runs on continuously without many joined characters, held together by column rhythm; it shows the manner of his middle years as his style took shape. Wang traced his lineage through Zhong Yao, the two Wangs, Yan Zhenqing and Mi Fu, and became known for running and cursive script. This scroll can be read as the starting point of that style.
