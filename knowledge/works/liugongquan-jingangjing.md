# 柳公权书金刚经

## 背景

### 中文

法国国家图书馆藏敦煌拓本《金刚般若波罗蜜经》（Pelliot chinois 4503），题名「柳公權書」，著录年代为九世纪。卷尾题记记长庆四年四月初六（824年5月8日）柳公权为右街僧录準公书，末行记强演、邵建和刻；BnF 著录并引《两唐书》柳公权本传所记其书《金刚经》碑事为旁证。拓本裱于一卷，28.5×1166.6厘米，2022年完成高清数字化。馆藏著录转述题记内容，本身不断言柳氏真迹。

### English

The BnF holds a Dunhuang rubbing of the Diamond Sutra (Pelliot chinois 4503), titled "written by Liu Gongquan" and dated by the catalogue to the ninth century. The trailing colophon, as recorded in French, gives the sixth day of the fourth month of Changqing 4 (8 May 824), with Liu Gongquan writing for the monk-official Zhun, and a final line naming Qiang Yan and Shao Jianhe as engravers; the record also points to the account of Liu's Diamond Sutra stele in the two Tang histories. The roll of rubbings measures 28.5 by 1166.6 cm and was digitized in high definition in 2022. The catalogue relays the colophon's wording without asserting an autograph.
