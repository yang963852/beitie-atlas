# 郙阁颂

## 背景

### 中文

全称《汉武都太守李翕析里桥郙阁颂》，东汉建宁五年（172）刻，记武都太守李翕重修郙阁栈道之事。国家图书馆碑帖菁华著录为隶书，责任者作「（漢）[仇靖]撰；（漢）[仇紼]隸書」，刻立地记「陝西省略陽縣白崖」，并藏一件明末清初拓、梁启超旧藏的「校致」未损本。国务院侨务办公室转载的访谈把《郙阁颂》与《石门颂》《西狭颂》合称「汉三颂」；本库已收后两颂。陕西日报署名报道记 1979 年原刻凿迁至略阳灵岩寺博物馆，并记南宋田克仁仿原刻形制在灵岩寺崖壁另刻一本。原刻、南宋重刻与传世拓本须分开著录。

### English

The full title is Eulogy of the Xili Bridge and Fuge Gallery Road by Li Xi, Grand Administrator of Wudu. Carved in 172, it commemorates Li Xi's repair of the Fuge gallery road. The National Library of China catalogues it as clerical script, credits (with brackets) Chou Jing as author and Chou Fu as clerical-script writer, gives "Baiya, Lueyang County, Shaanxi" as the place of carving, and holds a late Ming or early Qing rubbing once owned by Liang Qichao. An interview republished by the State Council's Overseas Chinese Affairs Office groups it with the Shimen Song and Xixia Song as the "Han Three Eulogies", the other two of which are already in this library. A signed Shaanxi Daily report records the 1979 removal of the original cliff to the Lingyan Temple Museum and a separate Southern Song recarving by Tian Keren on the temple cliff. The original, the recarving and the rubbings are distinct witnesses.

## 待考

### 中文

- 原石尺寸、现存字数与凿迁经过仅见于署名报道，未见灵岩寺博物馆自己的藏品页，故只作报道转述，不写成馆藏著录。
- 南宋重刻的确切年月，所核页面只记「南宋时期」，不换算、不补年号。
- 国家图书馆在仇靖、仇绋姓名外加方括号，表示著录者的存疑标记；本条照录，不把方括号当成定论。

### English

- Measurements, the surviving character count and the 1979 relocation come only from a signed newspaper report, not from a museum object page; they are reported as press statements rather than catalogue data.
- The Southern Song recarving is dated only to "the Southern Song period" in the audited sources; no reign year is inferred.
- The library brackets the names Chou Jing and Chou Fu as a cataloguing qualification; the brackets are preserved rather than read as a settled attribution.
