# 东山松帖

## 背景

### 中文

纸本行草，为一通信札的断片，有四字磨灭。无款署与题跋；钤南宋「绍兴」连珠印、「内府书印」，以及明文徵明、刘承禧、吴廷、清曹溶等印，原有清乾隆内府诸印与乾隆题语，已被刮去。宋《宣和书谱》《中兴馆阁录》、明董其昌《容台集》、清孙承泽《庚子消夏记》、安岐《墨缘汇观》著录，刻入明吴廷《馀清斋法帖》、董其昌《戏鸿堂法帖》与清《三希堂法帖》。

### English

A paper leaf in running-cursive, a fragment of a letter with four characters effaced. It carries no signature or colophon. Its seals include the Southern Song Shaoxing interlocking seal and the Palace Library seal, then Ming collectors' seals of Wen Zhengming, Liu Chengxi and Wu Ting and the Qing collector Cao Rong; the Qianlong palace seals and imperial inscription were scraped away. It is recorded in the Xuanhe Calligraphy Catalogue and the Zhongxing guange lu, in Dong Qichang's Rongtai ji, and in Sun Chengze's and An Qi's catalogues, and was engraved in the Yuqingzhai, Xihongtang and Sanxitang modelbooks.
