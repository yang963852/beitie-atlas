# 雁塔圣教序

## 背景

### 中文

唐褚遂良书《雁塔圣教序》。本条收录慈恩寺圣教序拓本。原碑永徽四年（653）刻，分两石刻，嵌于唐慈恩寺大雁塔南门左右龛内；左龛为唐太宗所撰序，右龛为太子李治所撰记，均由褚遂良书，万文韶刻石。碑高148厘米、宽69厘米；序碑楷书二十一行、行四十二字，记碑楷书二十行、行四十字。

### English

Chu Suiliang’s Preface and Record at the Wild Goose Pagoda. This entry covers a rubbing of the Da Ci’en Temple version. The original was carved in 653 on two stones set into the left and right niches of the pagoda’s south gate: the preface by Emperor Taizong on the left, the record by the crown prince Li Zhi on the right, both written by Chu Suiliang and cut by Wan Wenshao. Each stone is 148 cm high and 69 cm wide; the preface runs twenty-one lines of forty-two characters and the record twenty lines of forty characters.
