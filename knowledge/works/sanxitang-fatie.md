# 三希堂法帖

## 背景

### 中文

清乾隆年间宫廷编刻的大型丛帖，收三国至明历代法书，分三十二卷，刻于艾叶青石。乾隆十二年（1747）上谕编刻，十五年（1750）大臣刻跋，前后相衔。帖名取自养心殿西暖阁「三希堂」所藏三件王氏墨迹；刻成后帖石嵌于北海阅古楼壁间。

### English

A court compendium of calligraphy from the Three Kingdoms to the Ming, carved in 32 volumes on 艾叶青石 by order of the Qianlong Emperor. The 1747 order and the 1750 colophon bracket the work. It is named after the three Wang treasures kept in the Hall of Three Rarities beside the Hall of Mental Cultivation, and the finished stones were set into the walls of the Yuegu Lou pavilion in Beihai Park.

## 待考

### 中文

- 故宫拓本条目称收入精品「三千四百余件」；报道则记一百三十五位书家、三百四十件作品、刻石四百九十五方。两处总数相差一个数量级，均不入结构化字段。
- 此帖为丛刻，非单一作品：所记年代是编刻之年，非所收书迹之作年。
- 帖石在北海原址；故宫所藏清拓是另一件实物，本记录不并列。

### English

- The Palace Museum's rubbing entry says the compendium takes in more than 3,400 pieces; the press feature counts 340 works by 135 calligraphers and 495 stone blocks. The two totals are an order of magnitude apart and neither is asserted in a field.
- The work is a compilation, not a single composition: its reading date is the date of the compilation, not of the works it transmits.
- The stones are in situ at Beihai; the palace's own rubbing in the Palace Museum is a separate object and is not recorded here.
