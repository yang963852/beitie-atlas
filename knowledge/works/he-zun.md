# 何尊

## 背景

### 中文

西周早期贵族何所作青铜尊。内底铸铭十二行一百二十二字，记成王营建洛邑（成周）、训诰宗小子，以及武王克商后告天“余其宅兹中国”之语；“宅兹中国”是迄今所见“中国”一词最早的文字记录，宝鸡青铜器博物院亦作此表述。此尊1963年出土于宝鸡贾村，一度流入废品站，1965年由宝鸡市博物馆工作人员购回；1975年马承源除锈时发现铭文，因器主名“何”而定名何尊，是西周早期金文的又一重要样本。

### English

A bronze wine vessel cast by He, a noble of the early Western Zhou. Twelve lines and 122 characters inside the base record King Cheng's building of the eastern capital Chengzhou, his charge to the young kinsmen, and King Wu's words to Heaven after conquering Shang: “Let me dwell here in Zhongguo, the centre of the land.” That phrase is the earliest known written appearance of “Zhongguo”, as the Baoji Bronze Ware Museum also states. The vessel was found at Jiacun, Baoji, in 1963, passed into a scrap yard, and was bought back by the Baoji museum in 1965. In 1975 Ma Chengyuan removed the corrosion and discovered the inscription, naming the vessel after its maker He. It is another key sample of early Western Zhou bronze writing.
