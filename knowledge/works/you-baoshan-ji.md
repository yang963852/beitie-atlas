# 游包山集

## 背景

### 中文

正德十五年（1520），王宠游昆陵包山，作游诗数首，结为《包山集》。嘉靖五年（1526）冬，他过鸿溪，宿友人补庵居士处，居士索书；次年他便把八年前的旧作用小楷抄录二十二首寄去。纸本，纵二十一点六厘米，横三百二十三点五厘米。文嘉见后称「天才妙绝」，后人普遍推为王氏小楷代表作。[上海博物馆，历代书法馆换展](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/I00004231)

苏轼说「大字难于结密而无间，小字难于宽绰而有余」，这卷的结体正是「宽绰而有余」：字距拉开，行气疏宕，笔笔不肯放快，看去拙，其实每一步都算过。刘九庵对此卷是否出自王宠之手有疑问，馆方两说并存，没有替他定案。[上海博物馆，历代书法馆换展](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/I00004231)

### English

In 1520 Wang Chong visited Mount Bao near Kunling and wrote a set of poems about the trip, gathered as the Baoshan ji. In the winter of 1526 he stopped at Hongxi with his friend the layman Bu’an, who asked him to write them out; the following year he copied twenty-two of the old poems in small regular script and sent them. Ink on paper, 21.6 by 323.5 cm. Wen Jia called it “genius beyond compare”, and later writers have generally taken it as Wang’s masterpiece in small script. [Shanghai Museum, rotation of the calligraphy gallery](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/I00004231)

Su Shi said that large characters are hard to keep dense without gaps, and small ones hard to leave roomy with something to spare; this album is roomy with something to spare — wide spacing, loose column rhythm, not one stroke allowed to hurry. It looks awkward, but every step was calculated. Liu Jiuan doubted that the hand was Wang’s, and the museum keeps both views without settling the question. [Shanghai Museum, rotation of the calligraphy gallery](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/I00004231)

## 待考

### 中文

- 所抄首数，来源作二十二首，另有二十五首之说，未据原卷核对。
- 刘九庵的疑问只见于换展说明的转述，未见其论证。
- 卷后题跋与鉴藏印未录。

### English

- The sources give twenty-two poems, with another count of twenty-five; neither has been checked against the scroll.
- Liu Jiuan’s doubt survives only as a sentence in the museum notice, without his argument.
- The colophons and collectors’ seals are not recorded.
