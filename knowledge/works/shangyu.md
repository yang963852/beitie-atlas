# 上虞帖

## 背景

### 中文

硬黄纸本，唐摹本。前隔水有宋徽宗瘦金书「晋王羲之上虞帖」月白绢签，钤南唐「集贤院御书印」半印、「内合同印」，又有北宋「政和」「宣和」、双龙、「内府图书之印」等印记。曾藏南唐内府、北宋宣和内府，明代经晋王府、韩逢禧，清代经梁清标鉴藏。

### English

A Tang tracing copy on hard-yellow paper. The front mounting carries Emperor Huizong's slender-gold title slip, "Wang Xizhi of Jin, Shangyu Letter", on moon-white silk; the sheet bears a half-seal of the Southern Tang Academy of Worthies and the Neihetong seal, along with Northern Song Zhenghe and Xuanhe seals, a double dragon and the Imperial Library seal. It passed through the Southern Tang and Northern Song palaces, the Ming Prince of Jin's household and Han Fengxi, and in the Qing through Liang Qingbiao.
