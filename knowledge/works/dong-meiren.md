# 董美人墓志

## 背景

### 中文

隋《董美人墓志》。本条收录清拓本。志为隋文帝第四子蜀王杨秀所撰，开皇十七年（597）刻；原石清嘉道间出土于陕西兴平县，咸丰三年（1853）毁，故传世皆拓本。

### English

The Sui Epitaph of Dong Meiren. This entry covers a Qing rubbing. The text was composed by Yang Xiu, fourth son of Emperor Wen of Sui and Prince of Shu, and carved in 597. The stone was unearthed in Xingping, Shaanxi, between the Jiaqing and Daoguang reigns and destroyed in 1853, so only rubbings survive.

## 待考

### 中文

- 出土地点有陕西兴平与西安两说，清代拓本递藏的人名亦有陆庆勋、陆剑庵异文，均并存不改。

### English

- The findspot is given as Xingping or Xi’an, and the Qing rubbing’s custody is recorded under Lu Qingxun or Lu Jian’an; both variants are kept.
