# 妙严寺记

## 背景

### 中文

《湖州妙严寺记》为牟巘撰文、赵孟頫书的手卷，纸本，馆方定年约 1309–1310。普林斯顿大学艺术博物馆藏品页记：书法本幅 34.2×364.5 厘米，题跋 34.6×206.7 厘米；卷后有姚绶四跋（1468、1471、1474、1482）及张震、张谦（1909）、王同愈（1932）等题跋；递藏记录记 1998 年由 John B. Elliott 遗赠入藏，藏品号 1998-53。妙严寺在赵氏家乡湖州附近，馆方描述称记文即该寺的历史。

### English

The Record of the Miaoyan Monastery is a handscroll with a text by Mou Yan written out by Zhao Mengfu, ink on paper, dated by the museum to about 1309–10. The Princeton University Art Museum page gives the calligraphy as 34.2 by 364.5 cm and the colophon section as 34.6 by 206.7 cm, records four colophons by Yao Shou (1468, 1471, 1474, 1482) and others, and traces the provenance to a bequest by John B. Elliott in 1998, under object number 1998-53. The monastery stood near Zhao's hometown, and the museum describes the text as a history of that monastery.
