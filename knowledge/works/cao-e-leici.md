# 曹娥诔辞

## 背景

### 中文

《曹娥诔辞》卷为表彰东汉烈女曹娥而作的诔辞抄本，绢本小楷，辽宁省博物馆藏。馆方展览说明记：书心有东晋升平二年（358）署款，故又名「升平帖」；自宋至清对作者一直有王羲之与东晋佚名两种观点，今取后者；书心的结构和书写方法体现了东晋今楷趋于成熟的风尚；书心有唐怀素等题名和观款，卷后有南宋高宗赵构、元赵孟頫、乔篑成等十余人题跋。辽博简介页称其为馆藏现存世界最早的楷书墨迹——此为馆方自述，照录不断言。

### English

The Elegy for Cao E is a small-regular-script copy on silk of the elegy for the Eastern Han maiden Cao E, held by the Liaoning Provincial Museum. The museum's exhibition text records a signed date of 358 (the second year of Shengping) in the main sheet, hence the alternative title "Shengping piece"; that from the Song onward the authorship was debated between Wang Xizhi and an anonymous Eastern Jin hand, and that the museum now takes the latter; that the structure and manner of writing reflect the maturing of regular script in the Eastern Jin; and that the sheet carries viewing inscriptions by the Tang monk Huaisu and others, with more than ten colophons including Zhao Gou, Zhao Mengfu and Qiao Kuicheng. The museum's own introduction calls it the earliest extant regular-script ink manuscript in its collection; that wording is the museum's and is quoted, not asserted.
