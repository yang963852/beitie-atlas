# 十七帖

## 背景

### 中文

此为致益州刺史周抚的一组书信，前后约十四年。唐内府将王羲之书迹以「一丈二尺为一卷」装裱；张彦远《法书要录》记《十七帖》长一丈二尺、一百七行、九百四十三字。原墨迹已佚，今以刻本流传，据展览报道以唐弘文馆「馆本」一系最存原貌。

### English

A group of letters to the 益州刺史 周抚, written over about fourteen years. The Tang imperial collection mounted Wang Xizhi's letters into scrolls of one 丈二尺; 张彦远《法书要录》 records a 十七帖 of 107 lines and 943 characters. The original manuscript is lost, and the text survives through rubbings, the 弘文馆 system being the most faithful according to the exhibition report.

## 待考

### 中文

- 「一百七行、九百四十三字」是张彦远所记唐内府卷的情况，不是在世拓本册的清点结果。
- 馆方以起首语「十七日」题名；本项目以《十七帖》为作品名，馆方题名作为实物别名记录。
- 该册是否具足一百七行，未据图版核对，故不主张完缺。

### English

- The 107-line, 943-character figure describes the Tang palace scroll as recorded by 张彦远; it is not a count made on the surviving album.
- The museum titles its album by the opening phrase (On the Seventeenth Day); the project keeps 十七帖 as the work title and records the museum title as an alias of the object.
- Whether the album carries the full 107 lines has not been checked against a plate, so no completeness claim is made.
