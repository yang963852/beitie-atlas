# 快雪时晴帖

## 背景

### 中文

东晋王羲之书《快雪时晴帖》。本条收录传世摹本，一般认为是唐代精摹本。乾隆十一年，此本与王献之《中秋帖》、王珣《伯远帖》合称“三希”，藏于三希堂。

### English

Wang Xizhi’s Clearing after Snow. This entry covers a surviving manuscript copy, generally considered a fine Tang tracing. In 1746 it was grouped with Wang Xianzhi’s Mid-Autumn and Wang Xun’s Boyuan as the “Three Rarities” and kept in the Hall of the Three Rarities.
