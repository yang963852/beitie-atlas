# 苏孝慈墓志

## 背景

### 中文

《苏孝慈墓志》为隋代持节大将军苏孝慈的墓志。国家文物局《第一批古代名碑名刻文物名录》第490行录「隋持节大将军苏孝慈墓志」，收藏单位为蒲城县博物馆。渭南市政府报道记该志入选名录；陕西师范大学历史文化资源库记：苏孝慈仁寿二年（602）卒于交州任所，年六十四；清光绪十四年（1888）夏因大雨地陷，墓志石刻出土，知县张荣升携至城内嵌于尧山书院墙壁；志石与跋文刻石现藏县博物馆石刻室西廊。所引来源未记刻石年份与书者。

### English

The Epitaph of Su Xiaoci marks the Sui general Su Xiaoci. The national register of famous steles, row 490, lists the stone under the Pucheng County Museum; the Weinan government's report records the register inclusion, and the university history-resource page records that Su died in 602 in Jiaozhou aged sixty-four, that the stone was unearthed in the summer of 1888 after heavy rain collapsed the ground, that the magistrate Zhang Rongsheng brought it into the city and set it into the wall of the Yaoshan Academy, and that the stone and its colophon block are now kept in the county museum. The cited sources give neither the carving year nor a writer.

## 待考

### 中文

- 刻石年份无出处：通行作仁寿三年（603），仅见不可引用线索；结构化年份留空。
- 石面尺寸、行款与字数未见于所引来源。
- 康有为所记「光绪十三年出土」与通行「光绪十四年」的差异未核实。

### English

- No citable source states the carving year; the common third year of Renshou (603) appears only in non-citable leads, so the structured year stays empty.
- Dimensions, line count and character count are absent from the cited sources.
- The discrepancy between Kang Youwei's "unearthed in the thirteenth year" and the common "fourteenth year" has not been resolved.
