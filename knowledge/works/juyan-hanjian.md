# 居延新简·隧长焦永死驹劾状

## 背景

### 中文

1974年出土于内蒙古额济纳旗甲渠候官遗址，十六简，释读四百余字，记东汉建武三年调查驿马死亡一案。全篇章草，收笔带燕尾，字字独立而气脉贯通，是汉代章草的典范，也是边塞文书书写的实物。

### English

Found in 1974 at the Jiaqu frontier office in Ejina, this sixteen-slip document of 27 CE records an inquiry into a post-station horse’s death. The whole text is in zhangcao, with swallow-tail exits, each character separate yet the column continuous. It is a model of Han cursive and a direct sample of how frontier offices wrote.

## 待考

### 中文

- 简主名有“焦永”与“侯永”两种录文，本记录题名不取其一。
- 十六简的编连次序依整理本，原编绳已朽。

### English

- Reports transcribe the official’s name as either Jiao Yong or Hou Yong; the title here avoids choosing.
- The order of the sixteen slips follows the edited text; the original cords have rotted away.
