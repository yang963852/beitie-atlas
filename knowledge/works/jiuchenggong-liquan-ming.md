# 九成宫醴泉铭

## 背景

### 中文

魏徵在九成宫撰铭，颂辞中寄有规谏之意，欧阳询为之书丹。今日寻访此碑，仍可在九成宫遗址内见其原石的著录。

### English

Wei Zheng’s remonstrance, written at the emperor’s summer palace and transcribed by Ouyang Xun. The stone still stands inside the protected palace site; the bureau lists it among the site’s surviving monuments.
