# 九成宫醴泉铭

## 背景

### 中文

魏徵在九成宫撰铭，颂辞中寄有规谏之意，欧阳询为之书丹。碑高2.44米、宽1.18米，楷书二十四行、行五十字，唐贞观六年（632）六月立于陕西麟游九成宫。原石今存，但经剜凿，损泐过多，已非原貌。今日寻访此碑，仍可在九成宫遗址内见其原石。

### English

Wei Zheng’s remonstrance, written at the emperor’s summer palace and transcribed by Ouyang Xun. The stone is 2.44 m high and 1.18 m wide, with twenty-four lines of fifty characters; it was set up in the sixth month of 632 at the Jiucheng Palace in Linyou, Shaanxi. The original survives, but recutting and damage have left it far from its first state. It still stands inside the protected palace site.
