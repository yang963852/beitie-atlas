# 玄秘塔碑

## 背景

### 中文

唐会昌元年（841）立碑。裴休撰文，柳公权书丹，又以篆书题额。西安碑林藏其石，故宫另藏明初拓册，石与纸各有来历。

### English

A Tang stele of 841, composed by Pei Xiu and written and titled in seal script by Liu Gongquan, held at the Xi’an Beilin Museum.

## 导读

### 中文

唐会昌元年（841）立碑。裴休撰文，柳公权书丹，并以篆书题额；刻石者为邵建和、邵建初兄弟，二人是唐中书省玉册官，专办御用刻石。[西安碑林博物馆条目](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=148)

原石今藏西安碑林；故宫博物院另藏一册明初拓本，二者是两件不同的实物。原石有自己的刻立与收藏经历，拓本有自己的拓制年代与收藏，不能把拓本上的墨色当成石面今日的状态。原石见[玄秘塔碑原石](../catalogue/xuanmi-ta-bei.md#stele-xuanmi-ta)，拓本见[故宫明初拓册](../catalogue/xuanmi-ta-bei.md#dpm-xuanmi-ta-bei-026)。[故宫博物院《明初拓大达法师玄秘塔碑册》](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/259370.html)

读的时候先分清四种角色：裴休撰文、柳公权书丹并篆额、邵氏兄弟刻石，以及后世拓本的拓制者。碑文作者与书写者不是同一人，刻工也不是书丹者；把这几层混在一起，就会把馆方没有说的归名补进去。[西安碑林博物馆条目](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=148)[故宫明初拓册](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/259370.html)

馆方条目没有说明原石何时入藏碑林、今日是否展出；这些空白留在实物条目的待考里。[西安碑林博物馆条目](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=148)

### English

The stele was erected in 841, the first year of Huichang. Pei Xiu composed the text, Liu Gongquan wrote it in regular script and added a seal-script heading; the brothers Shao Jianhe and Shao Jianchu cut the stone, both being jade-tablet officers of the Tang Secretariat who handled imperial commissions. [Xi’an Beilin Museum entry](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=148)

The original stone is held at the Xi’an Beilin Museum; the Palace Museum holds a separate early Ming rubbing album, and the two are different objects. The stone has its own history of carving and keeping, the album its own date and holding; ink on paper cannot be treated as the stone’s present surface. The original stone is recorded as [the Xuanmi Pagoda Stele original stone](../catalogue/xuanmi-ta-bei.md#stele-xuanmi-ta), and the rubbing as [the Palace Museum’s early Ming album](../catalogue/xuanmi-ta-bei.md#dpm-xuanmi-ta-bei-026). [Palace Museum, early Ming rubbing album](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/259370.html)

When reading, separate four roles: Pei Xiu as author, Liu Gongquan as writer and heading-writer, the Shao brothers as engravers, and the later maker of the rubbing. The author and the calligrapher are not the same person, and the engraver is not the calligrapher; merging these layers invites attributions the museum does not make. [Xi’an Beilin Museum entry](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=148) [Palace Museum album](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/259370.html)

The museum entry does not state when the stone entered the Beilin or whether it is on display today; those gaps remain in the object record. [Xi’an Beilin Museum entry](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=148)
