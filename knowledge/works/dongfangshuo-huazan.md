# 东方朔画赞碑

## 背景

### 中文

唐《东方朔画赞碑》，赞文为西晋夏侯湛所作，颜真卿书，自署天宝十三载（754）十二月立。国家文物局《第一批古代名碑名刻文物名录》第590行录「唐"汉太中大夫东方先生"画赞碑」，收藏单位为陵县文博苑。德州广播电视台报道记：碑俗称「颜子碑」，现藏于陵城区文博苑；碑阳碑阴各15行、左右侧各3行、满行30字，四面刻辞，全碑共1073字；学习时报报道记碑高2.6米、宽1.03米、厚0.22米。所引来源未记书体。

### English

The Tang Stele of the Eastern Shuo Painting Eulogy carries a text by the Western Jin writer Xiahou Zhan written out by Yan Zhenqing, self-dated to the twelfth month of 754. The national register of famous steles, row 590, lists the stele under Lingxian's cultural relics garden. A broadcaster's feature records the common name, the present holding in the Lingcheng garden, the layout of fifteen lines per face plus three on each side of thirty characters, and 1073 characters in all; a newspaper feature gives its size as 2.6 m by 1.03 m by 0.22 m. The cited sources state no script classification.
