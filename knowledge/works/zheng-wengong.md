# 郑文公碑

## 背景

### 中文

郑道昭为父亲记功，两山各留一刻。上碑在平度天柱山，下碑在莱州云峰山。

### English

Zheng Daozhao commemorated his father in two cliff inscriptions, the upper on Mount Tianzhu and the lower on Mount Yunfeng.
