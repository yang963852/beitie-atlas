# 三门记

## 背景

### 中文

此为苏州玄妙观重修三门所立碑之文稿。玄妙观始建于西晋，元代重修三门并立碑；记文由牟巘撰写，赵孟頫誊写并篆额。馆方称其运笔以唐李邕为法。

### English

Zhao Mengfu's draft for the stele recording the repair of the three gates of the Xuanmiao Abbey in Suzhou, a Daoist temple founded in the Western Jin. Mou Yan wrote the text and Zhao Mengfu copied it, adding a seal-script heading at the front. The museum notes that the writing follows the manner of the Tang calligrapher Li Yong.

## 待考

### 中文

- 此卷为碑文原稿，非碑石。本记录不主张碑石存世。
- 「四十九至五十六岁」是国家数据库据碑文与赵氏官职所作的推断，非著录年月，故不换算为公元纪年。
- 馆方未记尺寸，实物大小从缺，不取二手数据填补。

### English

- The scroll is the draft, not the stele. The record does not claim that the stone survives.
- The 49–56 age bracket is the national database's inference from the text and Zhao Mengfu's offices; it is not a recorded date and is not converted into years.
- The museum gives no dimensions, so the object's size stays empty rather than being taken from a secondary account.
