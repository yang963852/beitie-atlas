# 三门记

## 背景

### 中文

此为苏州玄妙观重修三门所立碑之文稿。玄妙观始建于西晋，元代重修三门并立碑；记文由牟巘撰写，赵孟頫誊写并篆额。馆方称其运笔以唐李邕为法。

### English

Zhao Mengfu's draft for the stele recording the repair of the three gates of the Xuanmiao Abbey in Suzhou, a Daoist temple founded in the Western Jin. Mou Yan wrote the text and Zhao Mengfu copied it, adding a seal-script heading at the front. The museum notes that the writing follows the manner of the Tang calligrapher Li Yong.

## 待考

### 中文

- 此卷为碑文原稿，非碑石。本记录不主张碑石存世。
- 「四十九至五十六岁」是国家数据库据碑文与赵氏官职所作的推断，非著录年月，故不换算为公元纪年。
- 馆方未记尺寸，实物大小从缺，不取二手数据填补。

### English

- The scroll is the draft, not the stele. The record does not claim that the stone survives.
- The 49–56 age bracket is the national database's inference from the text and Zhao Mengfu's offices; it is not a recorded date and is not converted into years.
- The museum gives no dimensions, so the object's size stays empty rather than being taken from a secondary account.

## 导读

### 中文

这是苏州玄妙观重修三门所立碑文的原稿，不是碑石。记文由宋末元初文人牟巘撰写，赵孟頫誊写，并在卷首以篆书题额；馆方称此卷为赵孟頫所书碑文原稿，正文楷书。[东京国立博物馆藏品记录](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=TB1461)[ColBase 记录](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/TB-1461?locale=zh)

书写年份没有著录。馆方只把此卷定在元代 14 世纪；日本国家数据库据碑文与赵孟頫的官职，推断书写时年四十九至五十六岁。这是推断，不是著录年月，作品条目因此不换算成公元年。[东京国立博物馆](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=TB1461)[ColBase](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/TB-1461?locale=zh)

馆方另称其运笔以唐李邕为法；这是馆方的说法，本项目照录，不把它改写成自己的风格判断。读此卷时，可把“牟巘的记文”“赵孟頫的誊写与篆额”“馆方的评价”分开记。[ColBase 记录](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/TB-1461?locale=zh)

此卷为纸本墨迹，馆方未记尺寸，条目留空；碑石是否存世、原石在何处，本条目不主张。要了解玄妙观与立碑本事，只能回到记文本身与馆方说明，不用后出的地点或尺寸补足。[东京国立博物馆](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=TB1461)

### English

This is the draft of the inscription recording the repair of the three gates of the Xuanmiao Abbey in Suzhou, not the stele itself. Mou Yan, a literatus of the late Song and early Yuan, wrote the text; Zhao Mengfu copied it and added a seal-script heading at the front, and the museum describes the scroll as Zhao’s draft for the stele, written in regular script. [Tokyo National Museum record](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=TB1461) [ColBase record](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/TB-1461?locale=zh)

No writing year is recorded. The museum dates the scroll only to the fourteenth-century Yuan; the Japanese national database infers, from the text and Zhao Mengfu’s offices, that he was forty-nine to fifty-six when he wrote it. That is an inference, not a recorded date, and the record does not convert it into a year. [Tokyo National Museum](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=TB1461) [ColBase](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/TB-1461?locale=zh)

The museum also says that the writing follows the manner of the Tang calligrapher Li Yong; this is the museum’s statement, recorded here as such and not rewritten as the project’s own judgement of style. When reading the scroll, keep “Mou Yan’s text”, “Zhao Mengfu’s copy and heading” and “the museum’s assessment” apart. [ColBase record](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/TB-1461?locale=zh)

The scroll is an ink manuscript on paper; the museum gives no dimensions, so the record leaves size empty. It does not claim that the stele survives or say where a stone might be. To understand the abbey and the commission, return to the text and the museum’s account rather than filling the gaps with later locations or measurements. [Tokyo National Museum](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=TB1461)
