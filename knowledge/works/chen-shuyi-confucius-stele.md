# 陈叔毅修孔子庙碑

## 背景

### 中文

馆方将此碑列入隋代藏石。碑题所记，是修孔庙一事；陈列馆详页记碑文二十一行、行四十七字，仲孝俊撰文，并明言「碑隶书，结体方正，峻严方饬」。碑额为篆书，与正文书体不同。

### English

The stele is listed in the gallery's Sui holdings under its temple-repair title. The gallery's full entry records twenty-one lines with up to forty-seven characters, names Zhong Xiaojun as the author, and states that the text is in clerical script, square-framed and severe. The heading is in seal script, a different hand from the main text.

## 待考

### 中文

- 书体另有异说：详页所引清《集古求真》称此碑「似楷似隶，饶有意致」；与馆方正文「碑隶书」并列，不取其一。
- 书丹者姓名，所核页面未记载；碑末残字的释读有「监上石」「□□书」两说，页面未作定论。
- 英文标题为本项目为检索所拟，非来源所用题名。

### English

- A second characterisation is recorded: the gallery quotes the Qing Ji gu qiu zhen calling the writing "between regular and clerical, full of interest". It stands beside the gallery's own "clerical script" statement; neither is preferred.
- No calligrapher is named in the audited pages, and the damaged final words have two proposed readings that the gallery does not settle.
- The English title is a project rendering for navigation, not a title used by the source.
