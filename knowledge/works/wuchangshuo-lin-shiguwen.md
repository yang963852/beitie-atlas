# 吴昌硕临石鼓文轴

## 背景

### 中文

此轴临《石鼓文》第三鼓“田车”篇，纸本，书于1915年，吴昌硕时年七十一，今藏故宫博物院。他把石鼓的横势改为纵势，结字参差取姿，用笔沉厚而线条粗细多变；近人向燊称其“以邓法写石鼓文，变横为纵，自成一派”。学篆者可由它对照原石鼓，看临摹如何变成创造。

### English

This hanging scroll copies the “Tianche” poem, the third of the Stone Drums. It is ink on paper, written in 1915 when Wu Changshuo was seventy-one, and is now in the Palace Museum. Wu turns the drums’ horizontal stance into a vertical one, offsets the characters for balance, and varies stroke weight within a heavy, blunt brush. Xiang Shen said he wrote the drums with Deng Shiru’s method, turning horizontal into vertical and founding a manner of his own. For a student of seal script it is a chance to set the copy beside the original drums and see imitation become invention.

## 待考

### 中文

- 吴昌硕一生临石鼓甚多，此轴所据拓本的版本未详。
- 释文中的个别字与今通行释读有出入，待对照原鼓。

### English

- Wu copied the Stone Drums many times; the edition of the rubbing behind this scroll is unidentified.
- A few characters in the transcription differ from current readings of the drums and need collation.
