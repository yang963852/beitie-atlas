# 大字阴符经

## 背景

### 中文

《大字阴符经》为传褚遂良书的楷书大字册，纸本，旧金山亚洲艺术博物馆藏，藏品号 2007.130.a-.u，叶氏家族捐赠。馆方藏品库记：纸本墨笔，册页（含装帧）高 25.4 厘米、宽 34.9 厘米、厚 3.2 厘米；楷书 461 字、96 行、24 开；款识「起居郎臣遂良奉旨书」；最早题跋纪年 916 年。馆方未断言真迹。注意：馆方页面屏蔽自动访问，字段经搜索引擎快照获取，引用时待人工复核。

### English

The Great Characters Yin Fu Jing is an album of large regular-script characters traditionally attributed to Chu Suiliang, ink on paper, held by the Asian Art Museum of San Francisco under number 2007.130.a-.u, a gift of the Yeh family. The museum's database gives, as captured from a snapshot, 25.4 by 34.9 by 3.2 cm for the bound album, 461 characters in 96 columns on twenty-four leaves, a closing line in Chu's name, and colophons the earliest of which is dated 916; the museum itself does not assert an autograph.
