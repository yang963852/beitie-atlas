# 隶书新洲诗轴

## 背景

### 中文

清邓石如书《新洲诗》七言律诗一首，纸本立轴，纵 134.7 厘米、横 62.6 厘米，故宫博物院藏，文物号「新00100405」。款署「皖口新洲诗，次江上草堂韵为楚桥八兄先生正之，完白山人邓石如初稿」；下钤「石如」「邓氏完白」印，无藏印，未见著录。馆方记其隶书「以汉人为宗，汲取篆书笔意，再将魏碑方整厚重的特点融入隶书当中」。与汉碑、汉简对读，可见清代碑学如何重取隶书；相关书家见本站[邓石如](../people/deng-shiru.md)人物页。

### English

A Qing hanging scroll by Deng Shiru carrying his seven-character regulated poem on Xinzhou, ink on paper, 134.7 by 62.6 cm, in the Palace Museum under the number Xin 00100405. The signature reads "Xinzhou poem at Wankou, following the rhyme of Jiangshang Caotang, for Mr Chuqiao; first draft by Deng Shiru, Wanbai shanren". It bears the seals "Shiru" and "Deng shi Wanbai", has no collectors' seals and is not recorded in earlier catalogues. The museum describes his clerical script as taking the Han as its ancestor while absorbing seal-script brushwork and the square weight of the Wei steles. Read beside Han steles and Han slips it shows how Qing stele studies returned to clerical script; see this library's page on [Deng Shiru](../people/deng-shiru.md).

## 待考

### 中文

- 具体书写年份未见于馆方页面，「晚年」是馆方据生平所作的推定，不写成年份。
- 作品未见著录，入藏经过与递藏留空。
- 英文标题为本项目为检索所拟，非来源所用题名。

### English

- No year is given on the museum page; "late in life" is the museum's inference from his biography and is not turned into a date.
- The scroll is unrecorded in earlier catalogues; its accession and ownership history are left open.
- The English title is a project rendering for navigation, not a title used by the source.
