# 同州圣教序

## 背景

### 中文

与《雁塔圣教序》同文而异石。碑阳刻唐太宗为玄奘新译经典所作的《圣教序》，碑阴刻当时身为太子的李治《述三藏圣教记》，龙朔三年（663）立于同州，原在龙朔寺，1972 年移入西安碑林。螭首方趺，通高三百五十厘米，二十九行，满行五十八字。[西安碑林博物馆，同州三藏圣教序碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2599) · [澎湃新闻，同州三藏圣教序碑](https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_12677129)

书者旧题褚遂良。因摹拓较少，刀口比《雁塔》清晰，清人赵崡甚至说它「书似胜《慈恩本》」。但立碑之年距褚遂良之死已五年，顾炎武《金石文字记》因此判断「恐是后人追刻」；也有学者认为它是据《雁塔》翻刻。究竟出自谁手，至今没有定论。[澎湃新闻，同州三藏圣教序碑](https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_12677129)

### English

The same text as the Wild Goose Pagoda preface, on a different stone. The face carries Taizong’s preface for Xuanzang’s new translations, the back Gaozong’s record, written while he was still crown prince; the stele was erected at Tongzhou in 663 and moved to the Beilin in 1972. It has a chi-dragon head and square base, stands 350 cm high, and runs to twenty-nine lines of fifty-eight characters. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Tongzhou recension](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2599) · [The Paper, Tongzhou recension](https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_12677129)

The writing is traditionally given to Chu Suiliang. Because it was rubbed less often, its cut edges are clearer than the Wild Goose Pagoda version, and the Qing scholar Zhao Han even thought it the better of the two. But the date falls five years after Chu’s death, and Gu Yanwu concluded in his Jinshi wenzi ji that it must have been cut later by another hand; others take it as a recutting after the Wild Goose Pagoda stone. Whose hand it is remains undecided. [The Paper, Tongzhou recension](https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_12677129)

## 待考

### 中文

- 书者归属未定：馆方题褚遂良，顾炎武疑为后人追刻，另有据《雁塔》翻刻之说。
- 碑末三十字建碑记录是否出自另一手，来源只提及，未展开。
- 与《雁塔》两本的文字异同未逐字校对。

### English

- The writer is undecided: the museum names Chu, Gu Yanwu suspected a later hand, and a third view takes the stone as a recutting of the Wild Goose Pagoda version.
- The sources mention, without discussing, whether the thirty-character record at the end was written by another hand.
- The wording has not been collated character by character against the Wild Goose Pagoda version.
