# 赠张抱一草书诗卷

## 背景

### 中文

《赠张抱一草书诗卷》是王铎为张培所书的自作诗卷，张培字抱一。崇祯十五年（1642），张培在怀州（今河南沁阳）设宴雅集，王铎挥毫写下自作诗五首。卷为绫本，卷末绫尽，接以纸本；纵32.0厘米，横658.0厘米，一卷，今藏东京国立博物馆，馆方管理编号 TB-1257，高岛菊次郎氏寄赠。[东京国立博物馆 ColBase，草書詩書巻](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/TB-1257?locale=ja)

馆方系于崇祯十五年，时王铎五十一岁，尚未降清；并称卷中笔致自由阔达。怀州、五十一岁与绫本接纸本之说，另见日本文化厅文化遗产数据库。[文化庁 文化遺産オンライン，草書詩書巻](https://online.bunka.go.jp/db/heritages/detail/519383)

### English

The Scroll of Poems for Zhang Baoyi carries Wang Duo’s own poems written for Zhang Pei, whose courtesy name was Baoyi. In 1642, the fifteenth year of Chongzhen, Zhang held a gathering at Huaizhou, present-day Qinyang in Henan, and Wang Duo wrote out five of his own poems. The scroll is silk, with paper joined where the silk ran out at the end; it measures 32.0 by 658.0 cm, is one scroll, and is now in the Tokyo National Museum under accession TB-1257, the gift of Takahashi Kikujiro. [Tokyo National Museum, ColBase, cursive-script poetry scroll](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/TB-1257?locale=ja)

The museum dates it to 1642, when Wang Duo was fifty-one and had not yet surrendered to the Qing, and describes the brushwork as free and expansive. The details of Huaizhou, the age of fifty-one and the silk-and-paper joining are also given by the Agency for Cultural Affairs heritage database. [Agency for Cultural Affairs, Cultural Heritage Online, cursive-script poetry scroll](https://online.bunka.go.jp/db/heritages/detail/519383)

## 待考

### 中文

- 诗体两说：ColBase 作品解説作七言律诗五篇，文化厅数据库作五言律诗五首。
- 雅集场所两说：ColBase 称在怀州厅舍，文化厅数据库称张培于湖畔设宴泛舟。
- 本库未见馆方中文著录，尺寸从东博日文记录。

### English

- The verse form is given two ways: the ColBase commentary says five seven-character regulated verses, while the Agency for Cultural Affairs database says five five-character regulated verses.
- The venue is given two ways: ColBase places the writing in the Huaizhou government office, while the cultural heritage database describes a lakeside banquet with boats.
- No Chinese-language museum record was found; the dimensions here follow the Japanese museum record.
