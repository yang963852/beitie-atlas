# 中山王厝鼎

## 背景

### 中文

战国中山王厝墓出土，外壁刻铭七十七行四百六十九字，记相邦司马赒伐燕之功与对后嗣的告诫，是战国铭文最长的铜器。字形修长、线条尖入尖出，装饰性强，被称为“中山篆”，在战国金文中独树一帜。

### English

From the tomb of King Cuo of Zhongshan comes this cauldron, its outer wall cut with seventy-seven lines and 469 characters. The text praises the minister Sima Zhou for the campaign against Yan and warns the heir; it is the longest Warring States bronze inscription. Tall, slender characters with sharp entries and exits give the script a strong decorative force, and it stands apart from other Warring States bronze writing as “Zhongshan seal script”.

- The three Zhongshan vessels are counted at either 1,123 or 1,124 characters in total; this record gives only the 469 on this cauldron.
