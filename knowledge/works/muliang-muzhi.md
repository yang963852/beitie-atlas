# 穆亮墓志

## 背景

### 中文

刻于北魏景明三年（502），志石高六十六厘米，宽五十九厘米，志文二十行，满行二十二字，楷书。1925 年河南洛阳城东北西山岭头西南出土，1938 年于右任捐藏西安碑林。[西安碑林博物馆，穆亮墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=151)

志主穆亮字幼辅，北魏献文帝拓跋弘时封长乐王；宣武帝元恪即位，拜尚书令、司空，封顿丘公。[西安碑林博物馆，穆亮墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=151)

馆方评此志：「用极其严正的楷书书写，其势如雄师列队，行伍成壮，字字皆有强悍的气概」，风格与《张猛龙碑》颇为接近，是临摹学书的范本。[西安碑林博物馆，穆亮墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=151)

### English

Carved in 502, the third year of Jingming, the stone measures 66 by 59 cm; the text runs to twenty lines of twenty-two characters in regular script. It was unearthed in 1925 southwest of Xishanlingtou, northeast of Luoyang, and given to the Beilin by Yu Youren in 1938. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Epitaph of Mu Liang](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=151)

Its subject, Mu Liang, courtesy name Youfu, was enfeoffed as Prince of Changle under Emperor Xianwen; when Emperor Xuanwu came to the throne he was appointed director of the Secretariat and minister of works, and enfeoffed as Duke of Dunqiu. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Epitaph of Mu Liang](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=151)

The museum writes that the epitaph “is written in an extremely severe regular script; its momentum is like a column of fierce lions, ranks drawn up in strength, every character carrying a fierce spirit”. It is close in style to the Zhang Menglong stele and serves as a model for copying. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Epitaph of Mu Liang](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=151)

## 待考

### 中文

- 志文未署撰文者与书丹者，馆方页亦未记。
- 字数馆方页未记，本记录不填。
- 尺寸与行款依馆方页，未据原石复核。

### English

- The epitaph names neither writer nor calligrapher, and the museum page supplies neither.
- The museum page gives no character count, so none is recorded.
- Measurements and line arrangement follow the museum page and have not been checked against the stone.
