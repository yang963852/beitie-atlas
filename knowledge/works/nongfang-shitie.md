# 秾芳诗帖

## 背景

### 中文

《秾芳诗帖》是宋徽宗赵佶的大字瘦金书诗帖，绢本，朱丝栏，字大近五寸，是传世徽宗书迹中字最大的一件，今藏台北故宫博物院。瘦金书把起笔、收笔与转折的锋芒留在纸上：横画起笔尖细，收笔重顿，转折处提按分明；此帖笔画细劲，可见瘦金体在大字上的控制力。

### English

Poem on Fragrant Blossoms is Emperor Huizong’s large-character poem in slender-gold script: ink on silk, ruled with red thread lines, characters nearly five inches tall, the largest surviving example of his hand, now in the National Palace Museum, Taipei. Slender-gold script keeps the sharp entries, exits and turns visible on the paper, with a heavy stop at the end of horizontal strokes and knife-like diagonals. Here the strokes stay fine and firm, showing how the manner holds up at monumental scale.
