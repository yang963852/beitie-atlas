# 毛公鼎

## 背景

### 中文

西周宣王时，毛公作鼎记宣王册命与赏赐，腹内壁铭三十二行、近五百字，是现存铭文最长的青铜器。铭文庄重饱满、结体谨严，篇幅足以呈现西周晚期金文的完整面貌，是金文学习中常被指定为范本的一件。

### English

Under King Xuan of Zhou, Duke Mao cast this cauldron to record his charge and the royal gifts. Thirty-two lines and nearly five hundred characters cover the inner wall, the longest bronze inscription known. Dense, grave and strictly built, it is long enough to show the full range of late Western Zhou bronze writing and is often assigned as a model for study.
