## 导读

### 中文

十件鼓形石上刻有篆书诗篇，内容涉及出行与田猎。故宫博物院把这一组著录为秦国遗物，但确切刻成年代仍有争议；作品条目的年代因此写作“秦国遗物，确切年代未定”，不指定某位秦王或某一年。[故宫博物院“秦石鼓”条目](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234438.html)

读这组作品，先分清石与纸。本站把[原石十件](../catalogue/stone-drum-inscriptions.md#stone-drums-original-set)与[故宫明拓册](../catalogue/stone-drum-inscriptions.md#dpm-rubbing-shigu-xin00045770)分列为两件实物：前者是现存石鼓，后者是明初拓本，各有自己的制作年代与收藏。[故宫博物院“明拓战国石鼓文册”条目](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228598.html)

看拓本时，不要把纸上的字口当成石面今日的样子。拓本反映的是拓制时的石面，不能直接当作原石现在的状态；石与纸分开记，年代与损泐的问题才不会混在一起。[故宫博物院“明拓战国石鼓文册”条目](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228598.html)

年代问题留在待考：目录列出不同断代，本站把作品列入先秦组，不等于采用某个确切年份。[故宫博物院“秦石鼓”条目](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234438.html)

### English

Ten drum-shaped stones carry seal-script poems about travel and hunting. The Palace Museum catalogues the group as a Qin relic, but the exact date of carving remains disputed; the work record therefore reads “a Qin relic, exact date undetermined” and names neither a ruler nor a year. [Palace Museum, “Qin Stone Drums”](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234438.html)

Begin by separating stone from paper. This site records [the ten original drums](../catalogue/stone-drum-inscriptions.md#stone-drums-original-set) and [the Palace Museum’s Ming rubbing album](../catalogue/stone-drum-inscriptions.md#dpm-rubbing-shigu-xin00045770) as two objects: the surviving drums and an early Ming impression, each with its own date and holding. [Palace Museum, Ming rubbing album](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228598.html)

When reading the album, do not treat ink on paper as the present surface of the stone. A rubbing records the stone as it was when taken; it cannot be read as the drums’ current condition. Keeping stone and paper apart prevents dates and damage from being merged. [Palace Museum, Ming rubbing album](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228598.html)

The dating question stays open: the catalogue gives competing dates, and placing the work in the pre-Qin group does not adopt one precise year. [Palace Museum, “Qin Stone Drums”](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234438.html)
