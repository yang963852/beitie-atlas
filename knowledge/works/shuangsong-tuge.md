# 双松图歌

## 背景

### 中文

绢本手卷，纵三十三点八厘米，横一千一百九十六厘米；行书，七十二行，一百四十四字。所书为杜甫《戏为双松图歌韦偃画》，从「天下几人画古松」起，到「请公放笔为直干」止。款署「张即之七十二岁写。时积雨连霉，槐龙舞翠，与客小饮醉中戏书」，钤「张」「张氏」「即之」印。[故宫博物院，张即之行书双松图歌卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228481.html)

自识「七十二岁」，馆方据此推为南宋理宗宝祐五年（1257）。卷前有苏轼古柏图，馆方注明非真迹；尾纸有明洪武时陈新、夏彦良二跋。清缪日藻《寓意录》、清内府《石渠宝笈·初编》著录。[故宫博物院，张即之行书双松图歌卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228481.html)

馆方说此卷是张即之晚年之笔。后人称他「以能书闻天下」，「大字古雅遒劲，细书尤俊健不凡」；此卷擘窠大字，神完气足，劲健雄肆，又时见险怪之态。他书宗唐人，结体严谨，笔法险劲，当时影响很大，北方金人亦多效其体。[故宫博物院，张即之行书双松图歌卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228481.html)

### English

Ink on silk, a handscroll 33.8 by 1,196 cm, in running script, seventy-two lines and 144 characters. The text is Du Fu’s “Playful Song on the Twin Pines Painted by Wei Yan”, from “how many in the world paint ancient pines” to “please, sir, let your brush go and make the straight trunk”. It is signed “written by Zhang Jizhi at seventy-two; the rain had been unbroken and mouldy, the locust trees danced green, I drank a little with a guest and wrote this in play while drunk”, with the seals “Zhang”, “Zhang shi” and “Jizhi”. [Palace Museum, Zhang Jizhi’s Song of the Twin Pines](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228481.html)

From the self-note “at seventy-two” the museum dates the scroll to 1257, the fifth year of Baoyou under Emperor Lizong of the Southern Song. The frontispiece carries a painting of ancient cypresses attributed to Su Shi, which the museum states is not genuine; the end paper has two colophons by Chen Xin and Xia Yanliang of the Hongwu reign. It is recorded in Miao Rizao’s Yuyi lu and the imperial Shiqu baoji, first series. [Palace Museum, Zhang Jizhi’s Song of the Twin Pines](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228481.html)

The museum calls the scroll a work of Zhang’s late years. Later readers said he “was known to the world for his ability to write”, that “his large characters are antique and robust, his small characters especially vigorous and uncommon”; this scroll’s huge characters are full of spirit, strong and unrestrained, with a vein of the strange. He took his model from the Tang, with strict structure and steep brushwork, and his influence was such that the Jin in the north copied his manner. [Palace Museum, Zhang Jizhi’s Song of the Twin Pines](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228481.html)

## 待考

### 中文

- 卷前古柏图，馆方只注非真迹，未记作者与年代。
- 尺寸、行款与字数依馆方藏品页，未据原件复核。
- 「七十二岁」为本人自识，馆方据此推年，未据其生年复核。

### English

- The museum says only that the frontispiece of ancient cypresses is not genuine; it gives neither painter nor date.
- Measurements, line count and character count follow the museum page and have not been checked against the object.
- “Seventy-two” is the writer’s own note and the museum’s basis for the date; the record does not recalculate the year from his birth year.
