# 苕溪诗卷

## 背景

### 中文

北宋米芾书《苕溪诗卷》。此卷纸本，清内府《石渠宝笈》初编著录并贮养心殿，民国时流出宫外，1964年由故宫博物院从荣宝斋收购。

### English

Mi Fu’s Tiaoxi Poems. The scroll is paper; the first Shiqu baoji records it in the Qing imperial collection, kept in the Hall of Mental Cultivation. It left the palace in the Republican period and was bought by the Palace Museum from Rongbaozhai in 1964.
