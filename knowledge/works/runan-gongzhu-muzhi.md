# 汝南公主墓志铭

## 背景

### 中文

虞世南为唐太宗第三女汝南公主所撰墓志的草稿，书于贞观十年（636），时年七十九。纸本，纵二十五点九厘米，横三十八点四厘米。因是草稿，撰书人不署款，后半亦残缺，文字止于公主去世之日。[上海博物馆，汝南公主墓志铭卷](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00004641)

此卷是虞世南传世唯一接近真迹的墨迹。笔意圆活，与《孔子庙堂碑》的沉着内敛看似两路，细看仍是同一种收得住的写法。也正因圆活近米，旧有「米临虞书」之说；馆方把两说并列，不作判定。[上海博物馆，汝南公主墓志铭卷](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00004641)

### English

A draft of the epitaph Yu Shinan composed for Princess Runan, Taizong’s third daughter, written in 636 when he was seventy-nine. Ink on paper, 25.9 by 38.4 cm. Because it is a draft, neither author nor writer signs it, and the second half is lost: the text breaks off at the date of the princess’s death. [Shanghai Museum, Epitaph for Princess Runan](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00004641)

It is the only manuscript of Yu’s that comes close to an autograph. The brushwork is rounded and lively, apparently unlike the reserved density of the Confucius Temple Stele, yet on closer reading the same habit of holding the stroke shows through. Precisely because it is so rounded, a minority view has called it a copy by Mi Fu; the museum sets the two attributions side by side without deciding between them. [Shanghai Museum, Epitaph for Princess Runan](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00004641)
