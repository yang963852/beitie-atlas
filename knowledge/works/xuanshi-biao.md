# 宣示表

## 背景

### 中文

《宣示表》是钟繇上魏文帝曹丕的奏文，请朝廷接受孙权归附。真迹早佚，传世只见刻本，一般认为是据王羲之临本摹刻，始见于北宋《淳化阁帖》，存十八行。结体宽博，点画遒劲朴茂，楷法尚未完全定型，是观察楷书从隶书脱出、走向成熟的关键样本，也是后世小楷取法的源头。

### English

The Memorial of Declaration is Zhong Yao’s memorial to Emperor Wen of Wei on accepting Sun Quan’s submission. The original is lost and only engravings survive, generally thought to reproduce a transcription by Wang Xizhi; the text first appears in the Northern Song Chunhua modelbooks in eighteen lines. Its broad frames and vigorous, unadorned strokes show regular script before its forms had settled, which makes it a key witness to the emergence of standard script and a source for later small-script practice.

## 待考

### 中文

- 传世单刻与丛帖本的行款互有出入，南宋贾似道摹刻石今存首都博物馆，它与本表的关系待核。
- 钟繇是否亲书此表、刻本是否即据王羲之临本，均属传称。

### English

- Single-block editions and modelbook editions differ in line arrangement. The Southern Song stone engraved for Jia Sidao is now in the Capital Museum; its relation to this text is unchecked.
- Both the claim that Zhong Yao wrote the memorial out himself and the claim that the engraving follows Wang Xizhi are traditional.
