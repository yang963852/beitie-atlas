# 陆游自书诗卷

## 背景

### 中文

《自书诗卷》书于南宋嘉泰四年（1204），陆游时年八十，录自作诗八首，今藏辽宁省博物馆。全卷行草相杂，笔势连绵而墨色浓淡自然，内容多写归乡闲居与感怀往事，是陆游晚年书法的代表，也让读者看到南宋文人以诗卷自遣的日常书写。

### English

The Scroll of Poems was written in 1204, when Lu You was eighty, and records eight of his own poems. It is now in the Liaoning Provincial Museum. Running and cursive script alternate, the brush moving continuously while ink varies naturally; the poems mostly concern retirement at home and remembered events. It represents Lu You’s late calligraphy and shows the everyday writing through which a Southern Song literatus entertained himself.
