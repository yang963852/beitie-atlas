# 洛神赋

## 背景

### 中文

元赵孟頫书《洛神赋》。此卷款署“子昂”而不署年，馆方据书风判断约作于赵孟頫五十岁前后；赵孟頫所书《洛神赋》存世多件。

### English

Zhao Mengfu’s Rhapsody on the Goddess of the Luo. The scroll is signed “Zi’ang” without a year; the museum dates it by style to about Zhao’s fiftieth year. Several versions of the rhapsody by Zhao survive.
