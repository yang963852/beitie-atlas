# 杨淮表纪

## 背景

### 中文

东汉熹平二年（173）的摩崖刻石，国家图书馆碑帖菁华著录为隶书，责任者作「（漢）卞玉撰」，刻立地记「陝西省褒城縣」。汉台区政府把《杨淮表纪》列为「石门十三品」第六品，并记上世纪七十年代初这一批石刻因水库工程凿迁入汉中市博物馆。该馆所藏「顧專70」是一件清初拓本，属此摩崖的传世拓本，不是摩崖本身。

### English

A cliff inscription of 173, catalogued as clerical script by the National Library of China, with Bian Yu named as the author of the text and "Baocheng County, Shaanxi" as the place of carving. The Han tai district government lists it as the sixth of the Thirteen Shimen Pieces and records that the group was cut free and moved to the Hanzhong Museum during the reservoir works of the early 1970s. The library's "Gu zhuan 70" is an early Qing rubbing of this cliff, a separate surviving witness rather than the cliff itself.

## 待考

### 中文

- 书丹者姓名、刻石行数与字数，所核两处页面均未给出。
- 国家图书馆所记「熹平2年2月22日」是碑帖著录的纪年，未说明是立石日还是文中所记事件之日；本条只取年份。
- 清初拓本与摩崖原石的现存状态未互相核对，不把拓本的纸面特征写成石面特征。

### English

- No calligrapher, line count or character count is given in the audited pages.
- The library's date of the second month, twenty-second day, is a catalogue date; the page does not say whether it is the carving day or a date inside the text. Only the year is recorded here.
- The condition of the early Qing rubbing and that of the cliff have not been compared; features of ink on paper are not treated as features of the stone.
