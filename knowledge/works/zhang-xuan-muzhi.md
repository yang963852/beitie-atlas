# 张玄墓志

## 背景

### 中文

《张玄墓志》刻于北魏普泰元年（531）十月，志主张玄字黑女，官至南阳太守；清人避康熙讳，多以“张黑女”称之。原石久佚，传世仅有何绍基旧藏的剪裱拓本，今藏上海博物馆。书法方圆兼备，结体扁方而含隶意，是魏碑墓志中兼有楷法与古意的一路。

### English

The epitaph was carved in the tenth month of 531 for Zhang Xuan, styled Heinu, who served as governor of Nanyang. Qing writers, avoiding the Kangxi emperor’s personal name, referred to the work by Zhang’s courtesy name. The stone is lost; only the cut-and-mounted album once owned by He Shaoji survives, now in the Shanghai Museum. Square and round brushwork meet in a broad frame with clerical remnants, a Northern Wei epitaph that joins regular-script method to an archaic flavour.

## 待考

### 中文

- 志文行款有二十行与十二行两说，现存字数亦有出入，未据拓本复核。
- 出土地不详，志文只记葬于蒲坂城东原。

### English

- Sources give the text as either twenty lines or twelve; the character count also differs. Neither has been checked against the album.
- The findspot is unknown. The text records only a burial on the eastern plain at Pupo.
