# 礼器碑

## 背景

### 中文

东汉永寿二年（156），碑记鲁相韩敕修孔庙、铸礼器之事。石今藏曲阜汉魏碑刻陈列馆。

### English

An Eastern Han stele of 156 recording the chancellor Han Chi’s repair of the Confucius Temple and the casting of ritual vessels. Held in the Qufu Han–Wei Stele Gallery.
