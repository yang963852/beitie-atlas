# 化度寺邕禅师舍利塔铭

## 背景

### 中文

这篇塔铭是欧阳询所书。吴湖帆所藏四种欧书宋拓中，便有这一册。

### English

Ouyang Xun wrote the inscription. This album was among the four Song rubbings of his calligraphy collected by Wu Hufan.
