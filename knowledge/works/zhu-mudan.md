# 牡丹赋

## 背景

### 中文

上海博物馆将此卷定为明祝允明草书，纸本，纵30.8厘米，横925.5厘米；来源项记“戚叔玉1980年捐赠”。1980年仅指捐赠，不能据此推定书写之年。[上海博物馆藏品目录](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/m/article/id/CI00000459)

馆方“简介”称，此卷是祝允明为记园内牡丹盛开而书写的即兴之赋，并以“笔法精熟，恣肆纵逸”评其笔势。页面没有说明所书赋文的作者，也没有给出此卷的具体书写年份或完整释文；祝允明在记录中只承担书者角色。

### English

The Shanghai Museum dates the scroll to the Ming and attributes its cursive calligraphy to Zhu Yunming. It is on paper, 30.8 cm high and 925.5 cm long; the provenance field reads “donated by Qi Shuyu in 1980.” That year records the donation, not the writing. [Shanghai Museum object catalogue](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/m/article/id/CI00000459)

The introduction describes the scroll as an impromptu rhapsody written when the peonies in a garden were in bloom, praising its practised, unrestrained brushwork. The page does not identify the rhapsody’s author or give a precise writing year or full transcription. Zhu Yunming is therefore recorded here as the calligrapher, not as the text’s author.
