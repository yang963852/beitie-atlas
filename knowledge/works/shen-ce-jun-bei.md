# 神策军碑

## 背景

### 中文

碑为记天子巡幸左神策军而立。原石在禁中，传拓不易，后来石佚。今日所见，是宋装拓本幸存的一卷。

### English

A stele erected inside the palace compound to commemorate an imperial visit to the left Shence Army. Because the site was closed, few rubbings were ever taken; the stone is lost and one volume of a Song mounting survives.
