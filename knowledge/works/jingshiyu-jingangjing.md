# 泰山经石峪金刚经

## 背景

### 中文

《金刚经》刻在泰山斗母宫东北的溪床石坪上，字径约五十厘米，现存一千余字。刻石无年月与书刻者姓名，一般定为北齐。它以隶书为骨，又杂篆意，在书法史上通常归入隶楷之间的过渡一路；康有为《广艺舟双楫》推为“榜书之宗”，是观察隶楷之变在大字摩崖上如何展开的样本。

### English

The Diamond Sutra is cut into a stream-bed rock platform northeast of Doumu Gong on Mount Tai. Its characters are about fifty centimetres high and roughly a thousand survive. The stone carries no date and no name of writer or engraver, and is generally assigned to the Northern Qi. Clerical script forms the frame, with seal elements mixed in, and the inscription is usually classed among the clerical-regular transitional works; Kang Youwei’s Guangyi zhou shuang ji called it the model for monumental writing. It shows how the clerical-to-regular transition works at the scale of a cliff.

## 待考

### 中文

- 现存字数各文记作一千零六十七、一千零六十九或一千零七十一字，未据原石复核。
- 书刻者旧有安道壹、王子椿等说，均无确证。

### English

- Published counts give 1,067, 1,069 or 1,071 surviving characters; none has been checked against the stone.
- An Daoyi and Wang Zichun have both been proposed as the writer, without proof.
