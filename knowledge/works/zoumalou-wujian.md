# 走马楼吴简·嘉禾吏民田家莂

## 背景

### 中文

1996年长沙走马楼出土十万余枚孙吴简牍，其中“嘉禾吏民田家莂”是长约五十厘米的大木简，逐户记录佃田、赋税与缴纳。隶意未退而楷法渐生，结体方整、笔画峻利，是观察隶楷之变在民间文书中的样本。

### English

More than 100,000 Sun Wu slips came from a well at Zoumalou in Changsha in 1996. Among them the Jiahe field-tax records are large wooden tablets about fifty centimetres long, listing each household’s rented land, tax and payments. Clerical forms have not yet gone and regular-script habits are appearing: square, firm characters that show the clerical-to-regular change inside everyday administration.
