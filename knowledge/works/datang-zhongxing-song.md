# 大唐中兴颂

## 背景

### 中文

元结撰颂，记平定安史之乱；颜真卿六十三岁书之，文字镌在浯溪崖壁。湖南方面的著录推此刻为现存“三绝”碑刻之首。

### English

Yuan Jie’s poem on the suppression of the An Lushan rebellion, cut into a cliff at Wuxi and written out by Yan Zhenqing at 63. The provincial page calls it the foremost of the surviving “three excellences” inscriptions.
