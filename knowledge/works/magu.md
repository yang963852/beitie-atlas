# 麻姑仙坛记

## 背景

### 中文

唐颜真卿书《麻姑仙坛记》。本条收录翻刻旧拓。颜真卿于大历六年（771）任抚州刺史时游麻姑山书此记，原碑旧藏仙都观，绍兴二十七年（1157）移入鲁公祠，南宋后期丢失；麻姑山今陈列者为1992年许亦农重刻，宋代拓本分藏北京图书馆、上海图书馆与上海博物馆。

### English

Yan Zhenqing’s Record of the Immortal Altar. This entry covers an old rubbing from a recutting. Yan wrote the record in 771 while prefect of Fuzhou; the original stele was kept at the Xiandu Abbey, moved to the Yan shrine in 1157, and lost in the late Southern Song. The stone now displayed on Mount Magu is a 1992 recutting by Xu Yinong; Song rubbings are held by the National Library of China, the Shanghai Library and the Shanghai Museum.
