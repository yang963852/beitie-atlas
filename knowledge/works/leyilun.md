# 乐毅论

## 背景

### 中文

小楷刻本，帖文以「乐毅论。夏侯泰初」起首。王羲之书此论，原墨迹不存，今日所读皆刻本；馆方所藏为宋拓《晋唐小楷》册中的一本，楷书，一件。

### English

A small-regular-script engraving, opening with the title and the name Xiahou Taichu. Wang Xizhi's manuscript is lost and the text is read today only in engravings; the palace museum holds a Song rubbing in its album of Jin and Tang small regular script, in regular script, one item.
