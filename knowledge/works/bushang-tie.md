# 卜商帖

## 背景

### 中文

纸本行书，六行。文记卜商读书见孔子，答以「书之论事，昭昭如日月之代明，离离如参辰之错行」，末言所受于夫子者志之于心、弗敢忘。墨色浓重，下笔果断，与其碑版的森严是一路，只是把刀口换成了笔锋。[故宫博物院，卜商读书帖页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/explode/others/256953.html)

此页清内府时与欧阳询《张翰帖》、王献之《东山松帖》、赵孟頫《道场何山诗》等十二帖合装为《法书大观》册，清末被太监藏于漱芳斋炕板之下，抗战期间清点文物时才发现。本库已收其中两开——《东山松帖》为第一开、《张翰帖》为第三开——此页是第二开，故三者文物号同出一源。[故宫博物院，卜商读书帖页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/explode/others/256953.html)

启功、徐邦达、王以坤、刘九庵的鉴定意见是「唐人摹本，定名为《唐摹欧阳询卜商帖》」。这句话把两件事分开了：字是欧阳询的，这一页是唐人的。[故宫博物院，卜商读书帖页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/explode/others/256953.html)

### English

Ink on paper, six lines. The text records Bu Shang coming to Confucius after reading and answering that books “speak of affairs as clearly as the sun and moon take turns to shine”; he ends by saying that what he received from the Master he keeps in mind and dares not forget. The ink is heavy and the brush decisive — the same severity as his steles, with the blade exchanged for a tip. [Palace Museum, leaf of the Bu Shang letter](https://www.dpm.org.cn/explode/others/256953.html)

In the Qing palace this leaf was mounted with eleven others — Ouyang’s Zhang Han letter, Wang Xianzhi’s Dongshan Pine letter and Zhao Mengfu’s poem on Daochang Mountain among them — as the album Fashu daguan. At the end of the dynasty a eunuch hid the album under the brick bed of the Shufang Studio, and it was found only when the collection was inventoried during the war. The library already holds two leaves of the same album, the Dongshan Pine letter first and the Zhang Han letter third; this is the second, and the three share one inventory stem. [Palace Museum, leaf of the Bu Shang letter](https://www.dpm.org.cn/explode/others/256953.html)

Qi Gong, Xu Bangda, Wang Yikun and Liu Jiuan identified it as “a Tang tracing copy, to be titled the Tang tracing of Ouyang Xun’s Bu Shang letter”. The sentence separates two things: the writing is Ouyang’s, the sheet is a Tang copyist’s. [Palace Museum, leaf of the Bu Shang letter](https://www.dpm.org.cn/explode/others/256953.html)
