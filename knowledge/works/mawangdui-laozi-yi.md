# 帛书《老子》乙本

## 背景

### 中文

1973 年湖南长沙马王堆三号汉墓出土的帛书，与卷前四篇古佚书合抄在一幅宽帛上，出土时沿折痕断成三十二片。馆方记为朱丝栏墨书、「规范的八分隶」，《德经》在前、《道经》在后，与通行本相反。藏品页据避讳字推断抄写年代「可能在文帝时期，即公元前179年至169年间」；该馆《帛书集粹》前言另记整批帛书抄写于约公元前 221 年至前 168 年之间，墓下葬于汉文帝前元十二年（前 168）。同墓所出《老子》甲本「字在篆隶间」，与此乙本字体不同，另作一案。

### English

A silk manuscript excavated in 1973 from Mawangdui Tomb 3 in Changsha, Hunan, copied on the same wide silk as four lost texts in front of it and broken into thirty-two pieces along its folds. The museum describes vermilion-ruled columns written in ink in "standard bafen clerical script", with the De section before the Dao section, the reverse of the received order. Using taboo characters, the collection page suggests a copying date "possibly in the Wen emperor's reign, 179-169 BCE"; the museum's thematic essay gives a broader range of about 221-168 BCE for the whole silk corpus and dates the burial to 168 BCE. The Laozi A manuscript from the same tomb, described as "between seal and clerical", is a different hand and a separate case.

## 待考

### 中文

- 藏品页的「约前 179—169」与《帛书集粹》的「约前 221—前 168」是两种口径（此卷与整批帛书），并列不改，也不合并成单一区间。
- 抄写者、帛书各篇的分章与残缺情况，所核两页未逐篇说明。
- 馆方记长 79.5 厘米、宽 55 厘米，又记整幅原高约 48 厘米；两种表述所指不同，本条不换算。

### English

- The collection page's 179-169 BCE and the thematic essay's 221-168 BCE describe different scopes (this copy and the whole corpus); both are kept rather than merged.
- The copyists, chapter divisions and losses of individual texts are not set out page by page in the audited sources.
- The museum gives 79.5 by 55 cm and also an original height of about 48 cm; these are different statements and are not reconciled here.
