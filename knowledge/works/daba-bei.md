# 胆巴碑

## 背景

### 中文

此为真定龙兴寺碑之纸本稿，奉敕记述吐蕃僧人胆巴（1230—1303）生平，身后追号帝师。赵孟頫自撰自书，并篆碑额。馆方称其用笔苍劲浑厚，取法唐李邕、柳公权，为赵书碑碣中的大楷名作。

### English

The paper draft of a stele text for the Longxing Temple in Zhending, written by imperial order to record the life of the Tibetan monk Dampa, who died in 1303 and was posthumously styled Imperial Preceptor. Zhao Mengfu composed the text, wrote it in large regular script and added the seal-script heading himself. The museum describes it as a late work in his large-character manner, taking 李邕 and 柳公权 as its models.

## 待考

### 中文

- 此件为纸本稿，非刻石。本记录不主张碑石存世，也不主张其所在。
- 款识中月份已损，只录年份。
- 胆巴卒年（1303）与稿本年份（1316）分属两事，不混为一谈。

### English

- The object is the paper draft, not the carved stele. The record does not assert that the stone survives or where it stands.
- The month in the colophon is damaged; only the year is recorded.
- The date of Dampa's death (1303) and the 1316 date of the draft belong to different events and are not conflated.
