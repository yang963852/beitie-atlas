# 颜勤礼碑（草稿）

## 中文

颜勤礼是书写者的曾祖父。唐大历十四年（779），[颜真卿](../people/yan-zhenqing.md)为他撰文并书写神道碑。今天从拓本上读到的文字，把两个人的名字留在了一起。[西安碑林博物馆藏品说明](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2582&c=View&m=home)

故宫图录中的新00045406是一册民国拓本。看图时可以先记下这个编号，再分别翻阅[纸本拓册](../catalogue/yan-qinli-bei.md#dpm-rubbing-xin00045406)和[西安原石](../catalogue/yan-qinli-bei.md#yan-qinli-original-stele)的经历。纸上的某个字究竟留下了哪一时的石面？可以从[碑背的“故”字](../notes/yan-qinli-story.md)读起。[故宫藏品号与简介](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234175.html)

这方碑仍有人拿来临写。2024年11月6日，淮北师范大学舒柳书画协会用它上了一堂临摹课。学院的记录点出了碑名，课堂所用拓本的版本还有待辨认。[美术学院活动简报，第七次授课](https://msxy.chnu.edu.cn/xyxw/content_125348)

## English

[Yan Zhenqing](../people/yan-zhenqing.md) composed and wrote this inscription for his great-grandfather Yan Qinli in 779. Both names belong to the stone, each in a different role. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, object description](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2582&c=View&m=home)

The Palace Museum entry numbered 新00045406 describes a Republican-era paper album. Follow [that impression](../catalogue/yan-qinli-bei.md#dpm-rubbing-xin00045406) and [the stone in Xi’an](../catalogue/yan-qinli-bei.md#yan-qinli-original-stele) separately. For a way to ask which state of a stone an impression preserves, start with [the character 故 on its back](../notes/yan-qinli-story.md). [Palace Museum, number and description](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234175.html)

On 6 November 2024 a calligraphy society at Huaibei Normal University taught a copying session using the inscription. The faculty report identifies the work. The edition used by the class remains open. [Faculty activity report, seventh session](https://msxy.chnu.edu.cn/xyxw/content_125348)
