# 五凤刻石

## 背景

### 中文

石质为灰岩，刻辞中留有五凤二年的纪年，馆方据此著录。陈列馆条目另记刻字在石的右半部、书体为隶书，并说此刻「隶中带篆」，对研究书体演变有价值。

### English

An inscribed limestone dated to the second year of Wufeng in the gallery catalogue. The gallery's full entry adds that the inscription occupies the right half of the stone, is written in clerical script, and carries "seal within clerical" features relevant to the history of script change.

## 待考

### 中文

- 书体曾有异说：该条目所引王澍《竹云题跋》记朱彝尊以为是「篆文」，王澍则断言「断然是隶不是篆」；馆方正文作隶书，异说并存。
- 英文标题为本项目为检索所拟，非来源所用题名。

### English

- The script has a recorded alternative: the gallery quotes Wang Shu's Zhu yun ti ba, where Zhu Yizun called the writing "seal script" and Wang Shu insisted it is "certainly clerical, not seal". The gallery's own text says clerical; both readings are kept.
- The English title is a project rendering for navigation, not a title used by the source.
