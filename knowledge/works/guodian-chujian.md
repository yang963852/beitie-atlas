# 郭店楚简

## 背景

### 中文

1993年荆门郭店一号楚墓出土，八百余枚竹简，抄录《老子》《太一生水》《五行》《缁衣》等十八篇先秦典籍。字体为典型楚系文字，秀丽欹侧，用笔提按分明，是研究战国楚简书法与简册制度的第一手材料。

### English

The tomb at Guodian near Jingmen yielded more than eight hundred bamboo slips in 1993, carrying eighteen pre-Qin texts including the Laozi, Tai Yi Sheng Shui, Wuxing and Ziyi. The script is typical Chu writing: graceful, slightly tilted, with clear pressure changes. It is primary evidence for Warring States Chu calligraphy and for how slip manuscripts were assembled.
