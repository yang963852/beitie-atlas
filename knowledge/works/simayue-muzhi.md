# 司马悦墓志

## 背景

### 中文

1979 年元月，河南孟县城关斗鸡台村司马悦墓出土。志盖长一百一十厘米、宽七十八厘米、厚八厘米，上刻「墓志盖」三字，存孟州市文化馆；志石长一百零八厘米、宽七十八厘米、厚十二厘米，1997 年入藏河南博物院。志刻于北魏永平四年（511）二月十五日，正书二十二行，行三十三字，除去空格实有六百六十一字，字迹清晰。首行题「魏故持节督豫州诸军事、征虏将军、渔阳县开国子、豫州刺史司马悦墓志」。[河南博物院，司马悦墓志](https://www.chnmus.net/ch/collection/appraise/details.html?id=512151229156943641)

志主司马悦（462—508），司州河内温县都乡孝敬里（今属河南孟州）人，历任征虏将军、豫州刺史，封渔阳县开国子，永平元年为白早生所杀。馆方说志文所记享年、被杀月日与埋葬年月日，可补《魏书》《北史》之缺，志与传「基本相符，并可相互补充」。[河南博物院，司马悦墓志](https://www.chnmus.net/ch/collection/appraise/details.html?id=512151229156943641)

馆方评其书：「刚健隽逸，笔画洗练爽明，气均力匀，运笔着实而又轻盈飘洒」；结构「以方严为尚」，「主体结构严密稳当」，有「里紧外舒，骨气通达」之感，称其「代表了魏碑书法方严紧舒一派的书法艺术精神」。[河南博物院，司马悦墓志](https://www.chnmus.net/ch/collection/appraise/details.html?id=512151229156943641)

### English

The epitaph was unearthed in January 1979 from Sima Yue’s tomb at Doujitai village, Meng county, Henan. The cover, 110 by 78 by 8 cm, is inscribed “tomb epitaph cover” and is kept in the Mengzhou cultural centre; the stone, 108 by 78 by 12 cm, entered the Henan Museum in 1997. It was carved on the fifteenth day of the second month of 511, the fourth year of Yongping, in regular script, twenty-two lines of thirty-three characters, 661 characters after blanks, clearly cut. The first line titles it “Epitaph of Sima Yue, late holder of the tally, commander of the military affairs of Yu province, general of the expeditionary force, founding viscount of Yuyang, governor of Yu province”. [Henan Museum, Epitaph of Sima Yue](https://www.chnmus.net/ch/collection/appraise/details.html?id=512151229156943641)

Sima Yue (462–508), a native of Xiaojing hamlet, Duxiang, Wen county, Henei commandery, Sizhou (modern Mengzhou, Henan), served as general of the expeditionary force and governor of Yu province and was enfeoffed as founding viscount of Yuyang. He was killed by Bai Zaosheng in the first year of Yongping. The museum says the epitaph supplies the age at death, the month and day of the killing and the date of burial, all missing from the Wei shu and Bei shi, and that text and biography “agree and supplement each other”. [Henan Museum, Epitaph of Sima Yue](https://www.chnmus.net/ch/collection/appraise/details.html?id=512151229156943641)

The museum describes the calligraphy as “vigorous and free, the strokes clean and bright, the energy even, the brush both solid and light”, with a structure “that takes square severity as its standard” and a main frame “tight and steady”, giving an impression of “tight within and relaxed without, the bone-spirit reaching through”. It calls the stone representative of the square, severe and close-knit school of Wei stele calligraphy. [Henan Museum, Epitaph of Sima Yue](https://www.chnmus.net/ch/collection/appraise/details.html?id=512151229156943641)

## 待考

### 中文

- 国家图书馆的拓片记录作正书二十一行、行二十三字，年代作二月十八日，刻立地作河南温县出土；与河南博物院所记的二十二行、行三十三字、二月十五日、孟县斗鸡台村不同，两说并存。
- 志盖存孟州市文化馆，不在河南博物院；本记录只把志石列作该院藏品。
- 志文未署撰文者与书丹者，馆方页亦未记。

### English

- The National Library’s rubbing record gives twenty-one lines of twenty-three characters, dates the stone to the eighteenth of the second month, and places the find at Wen county, Henan; the Henan Museum gives twenty-two lines of thirty-three characters, the fifteenth of the second month, and Doujitai village in Meng county. Both readings are kept.
- The cover is in the Mengzhou cultural centre, not the Henan Museum; only the stone is recorded as the museum’s holding.
- The epitaph names neither writer nor calligrapher, and the museum page supplies neither.
