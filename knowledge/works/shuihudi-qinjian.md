# 睡虎地秦简

## 背景

### 中文

1975年云梦睡虎地十一号秦墓出土，一千一百余枚竹简，多为秦律与官文书，墨书秦隶。笔画短直、结体扁方，还带篆意，正处在篆书向隶书转变的关口；与汉代石刻隶书对读，能看到毛笔书写的自然笔势。

### English

Tomb 11 at Shuihudi in Yunmeng yielded more than 1,100 bamboo slips in 1975, mostly Qin statutes and official documents written in ink in Qin clerical script. Strokes are short and straight, forms broad and slightly seal-like: the script stands at the point where seal turns into clerical. Read beside Han stone inscriptions, it shows the natural movement of the brush that carving flattens.
