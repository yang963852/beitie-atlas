# 城南唱和诗卷

## 背景

### 中文

至正二十二年（1362）书，杨维桢时年六十七。缘起是虞子贤藏有朱熹《城南唱和》手迹，而张栻原诗已佚，遂请杨维桢追和并补书张栻《城南杂咏》二十首。纸本，纵三十一点六厘米，横二百一十六点六厘米，卷后有其所书「赘评」二则。《石渠宝笈三编》著录，贮延春阁。[故宫博物院，张栻城南杂咏卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_31.html)

吴宽评其书「大将班师，三军奏凯，破斧缺斨，倒载而归」，徐有贞说「铁崖狂怪不经，而步履自高」。这两句放在此卷上最合适：笔锋硬峭，筋骨强健，结体奇崛，末字「也」甚至独占五六字的位置，通篇却站得住。[故宫博物院，张栻城南杂咏卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_31.html)

此卷原与朱熹《城南唱和诗》装在同一卷上，清初孙承泽重装时分为二卷。此后入内府，1922 年溥仪以「赏溥杰」名义携出宫，散于民间，1959 年由故宫博物院收藏。[故宫博物院，张栻城南杂咏卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_31.html)

### English

Written in 1362, when Yang was sixty-seven. Yu Zixian owned Zhu Xi’s manuscript of the South of the City matching poems, but Zhang Shi’s originals were lost, so he asked Yang to match them and supply Zhang’s twenty poems in his own hand. Ink on paper, 31.6 by 216.6 cm, followed by two short notes in Yang’s own writing. It is recorded in the third Shiqu baoji catalogue, stored at Yanchun Pavilion. [Palace Museum, Zhang Shi’s South of the City poems](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_31.html)

Wu Kuan described his script as a great general returning with the army, drums beating, axes chipped, halberds carried backwards; Xu Youzhen said it was “wild and unorthodox, yet every step high”. Both fit this scroll: the edges are hard, the bone strong, the frames strange, and the final “ye” takes the space of five or six characters — yet the whole stands. [Palace Museum, Zhang Shi’s South of the City poems](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_31.html)

The scroll was originally mounted together with Zhu Xi’s matching poems, and Sun Chengze separated them into two scrolls when he remounted the pair in the early Qing. It then entered the palace, was taken out by Puyi in 1922 under the pretence of rewarding Pujie, scattered among private owners, and was acquired by the Palace Museum in 1959. [Palace Museum, Zhang Shi’s South of the City poems](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_31.html)

## 待考

### 中文

- 孙承泽重装分卷的具体年份，馆方未记。
- 与朱熹卷分离后各自的题跋次序，未见完整清单。
- 二十首诗的释文未录，诗题与今本《南轩集》的异同未校。

### English

- The museum does not date Sun Chengze’s remounting.
- No complete list of the colophons on the two scrolls after their separation was found.
- The twenty poems are not transcribed here, and their titles have not been collated against the present Nanzuan ji.
