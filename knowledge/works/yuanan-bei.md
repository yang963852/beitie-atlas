# 袁安碑

## 背景

### 中文

东汉司徒袁安的墓碑，1929年在河南偃师辛家村被发现——此前石碑长期倒置作牛王庙供案，刻字面向下，故久不为人知。碑文十行，满行存十五字，记袁安历任官职与卒葬年月，与《后汉书·袁安传》基本相合而较简约；碑穿位于碑身正中第五、六行之间，为汉碑所仅见。因发现晚近，字口锋颖如新，是现存最完整的东汉篆书碑之一。字形较秦篆“微微加方”，线条婉转流畅、笔画瘦劲，河南博物院称其为汉代篆书的典型代表，费声骞评为“可供学篆取法善本”。

### English

The tomb stele of Yuan An, Minister over the Masses of the Eastern Han, was found in 1929 at Xinjia village, Yanshi, Henan — before that the stone had long lain face-down as an offering table in a village temple, so the inscription stayed unknown. Ten lines survive, fifteen characters per line at most, recording Yuan An's offices and death; the text agrees broadly with his biography in the Hou Hanshu, more briefly. The round stele-bore sits exactly at the centre, between lines five and six, a placement unique among Han steles. Because the find is recent, the characters remain sharp, and it is one of the most complete Eastern Han seal-script steles extant. Its forms are slightly squared compared with Qin seal writing, its lines supple and thin; the Henan Museum calls it the classic representative of Han seal script, and the modern calligrapher Fei Shengqian judged it a good model for students of seal script.

## 待考

### 中文

- 刻立年代有两说：一说定于永元四年（92年），一说因碑文已称“孝和皇帝”而推至和帝崩逝（106年）之后。本记录不取一说。
- 碑侧有明万历二十六年（1598年）题记，说明明代已被发现；其最初出土与早年经历无考。

### English

- Two datings circulate: Yongyuan 4 (92), from the record of Yuan An's death and burial on the stone, or after 106, since the text already names the posthumous temple title of Emperor He. This record adopts neither.
- A side inscription dated 1598 shows the stone was found by the Ming dynasty; its first excavation and early history are unknown.
