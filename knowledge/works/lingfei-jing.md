# 灵飞经

## 背景

### 中文

《灵飞经》是道教经文的抄本，唐开元二十六年（738）前后由玉真公主请人写就，今存四十三行，藏纽约大都会艺术博物馆。旧题钟绍京书，馆方亦只作“传”。小楷结体端稳而笔势流动，明代董其昌推为唐代小楷的精品，刻入《渤海藏真》后广为流传，明清以来一直是小楷入门的范本。

### English

The Classic of Spiritual Flight is a copy of a Daoist scripture, commissioned around 738 by Princess Yuzhen and now surviving in forty-three columns in the Metropolitan Museum of Art. It is traditionally ascribed to Zhong Shaojing, though a sutra copyist has also been proposed. The small script is steady in structure and fluid in movement. Dong Qichang judged it one of the finest Tang examples of small writing, and its inclusion in the Bohai cangzhen made it a standard model for beginners from the Ming onward.
