# 草书孟浩然诗卷

## 背景

### 中文

故宫博物院定此卷为清傅山书，纸本，纵28.2厘米，横394.8厘米。全卷三接纸，共114行，书孟浩然《与诸子登岘山》等十八首诗。末识：「张山人钺持此纸要书，雪中惜研上余墨，孟诗十八首与之。」款署「山」，下钤「傅山私印」；本幅无藏印，具体书写时间不详。[故宫博物院，藏品著录与简介第一、二段](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228215.html)

故宫引述傅山「宁拙毋巧，宁丑毋媚，宁支离毋轻滑，宁真率毋安排」的主张，并评此卷字间不相连属而笔意相连，结字欹正相间，古拙雄健，视为傅山草书中的上乘佳作，称其代表傅山中、晚期行草书的最高艺术水平。这是馆方的审美评价，不是此卷的系年。[故宫博物院，简介第三段](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228215.html)

### English

The Palace Museum identifies this as a Qing scroll written by Fu Shan, in ink on paper, 28.2 cm high and 394.8 cm long. Three sheets are joined, with 114 lines copying eighteen poems by Meng Haoran, including “Climbing Mount Xian with My Companions.” The final inscription reads, “Zhang Shanren Yue brought this paper and asked for writing; in the snow I spared the remaining ink on the inkstone and gave him eighteen of Meng’s poems.” It is signed 山 and bears the seal 傅山私印. There are no collectors’ seals on the scroll, and its exact writing date is unknown. [Palace Museum, catalogue description and first two paragraphs of the introduction](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228215.html)

The museum quotes Fu Shan’s maxims “rather awkward than artful, rather ugly than pleasing, rather fragmented than slick, rather spontaneous than arranged.” It praises the disconnected characters and continuous brush momentum, the alternating character structures, and the antique, forceful writing, calling the scroll an outstanding example of Fu’s cursive and the highest achievement of his middle and late running-cursive. This is the museum’s aesthetic judgment, not a date for the scroll. [Palace Museum, third paragraph of the introduction](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228215.html)

- The exact writing year remains unknown; Zhang Shanren Yue’s request for the scroll cannot date the event.
