# 洛神赋十三行

## 背景

### 中文

王献之所书曹植《洛神赋》入宋已残。南宋权相贾似道先得九行、又续得四行，刻于似碧玉的佳石，世称「玉版十三行」。石后佚，明万历间于西湖葛岭复得，清康熙间入内府，1900 年后流散民间；1981 年由北京市文物商店购得，转交首都博物馆。

### English

Wang Xianzhi's copy of Cao Zhi's fu survived only in part: the hemp-paper original was already damaged by the Song. The Southern Song minister Jia Sidao obtained nine lines and then four more, and had the thirteen carved on a stone that resembled green jade, hence 玉版十三行. The stone was lost, rediscovered at 葛岭 in the Wanli period, entered the Qing court under Kangxi, left it after 1900, and was bought for the Beijing cultural relics shop in 1981 and transferred to the Capital Museum.

## 待考

### 中文

- 原墨迹已佚，存世者为十三行刻石，不是唐宋纸本摹本。
- 来源未记石之尺寸与藏品号，也未清点字数，故诸字段从缺。
- 递藏按事件链记录。仅近代购藏有明确年份；宋时刻石与万历复得均只记朝代与年号。

### English

- The original manuscript is lost; what survives is a stone carving of thirteen lines, not a Tang or Song copy on paper.
- The sources give neither the stone's dimensions nor an accession number, and they do not count its characters, so those fields stay empty.
- The transmission is recorded as a chain of episodes. Only the modern purchase is dated by year; the Song carving and the Wanli rediscovery are given by dynasty and reign only.
