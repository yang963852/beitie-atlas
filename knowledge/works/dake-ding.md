# 大克鼎

## 背景

### 中文

西周孝王时期，膳夫克作鼎祭祀祖父师华父。腹内壁铸铭二十八行二百九十字，前段颂扬师华父辅弼王室的功德，后段记周王册命克并赏赐礼服、土地、奴隶。铭文上半部有整齐的长方格，每格一字，布局规整，字迹端庄质朴、笔画均匀遒劲，是西周晚期长篇金文的代表。清光绪年间出土于陕西扶风，后为潘祖荫收藏；1951年潘达于捐赠上海博物馆，成为上博镇馆之宝。习金文者常以它与大盂鼎对读：两鼎一南一北，同出关中，同为潘氏旧藏，铭文的规整与雄浑各具面目。

### English

Under King Xiao of Zhou, the household steward Ke cast this cauldron in honour of his grandfather Shi Huafu. Its inner wall carries twenty-eight lines and 290 characters: the first half praises Shi Huafu's service to the royal house, the second records the king's investiture of Ke and the gifts of robes, land and serfs. The upper part of the inscription is ruled into neat squares, one character to a cell; the layout is orderly and the writing dignified and vigorous, a representative long late-Western-Zhou bronze text. Found at Fufeng in Shaanxi in the Guangxu era, it entered the collection of Pan Zuyin, and in 1951 Pan Dayu donated it to the Shanghai Museum, where it is the museum's most famous bronze. Students often read it beside the Da Yu Ding: both came from Guanzhou lands, both were Pan family heirlooms, and the two inscriptions show the ordered and the vigorous faces of Western Zhou bronze writing.

## 待考

### 中文

- 出土时间诸家作“清光绪年间”或“光绪中期”，上博页面作光绪年间出土于扶风法门寺窖藏，具体年份无考；窖藏与“任村”等出土地表述诸家亦有出入。
- 二百九十字的计数依上博（合文2、重文7计入），各书著录或有微异。

### English

- The find is dated variously “Guangxu era” or “mid-Guangxu”; the Shanghai Museum page says it was excavated in the Guangxu era from a hoard at Faymen temple, Fufeng, with no exact year. Accounts of the hoard and the “Rencun” findspot also differ.
- The 290-character count follows the Shanghai Museum (two joined characters and seven repeated-character marks included); other catalogues may differ slightly.
