# 大盂鼎

## 背景

### 中文

西周康王二十三年，贵族盂作鼎祭祀祖父南公，腹内壁铸铭十九行二百九十一字，记康王册命与殷商酗酒亡国的训诫。铭文雄壮凝练、方圆兼备，是西周早期金文的代表作；学金文者常由它入手，先看字形的庄重，再看行气的整饬。

### English

In the twenty-third year of King Kang of Zhou, the noble Yu cast this cauldron for his grandfather Nan Gong. Its inner wall carries a nineteen-line, 291-character inscription recording the royal charge and the warning that Shang fell through drunkenness. Vigorous, compact and square in stance, it is a representative early Western Zhou bronze inscription and a common starting point for learning bronze script.

## 待考

### 中文

- 出土地有陕西郿县礼村与岐山两说，馆方不同页面即不一致。
- 铭文所说“二十三年”依小盂鼎铭文推定为康王，非鼎铭自书王名。

### English

- The findspot is given as either Li village in Mei county or Qishan; the museum’s own pages differ.
- The “twenty-third year” is assigned to King Kang through the Xiao Yu Ding inscription, not named in this vessel’s text.
