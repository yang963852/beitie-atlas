# 韭花帖

## 背景

### 中文

《韭花帖》是杨凝式答谢友人馈赠韭花的短札，行楷书，麻纸本，七行六十余字，今藏无锡博物院。帖末只署“七月十一日”，没有年份。字距行距疏朗，用笔时收时放，在五代乱世里留下一种萧散安静的气息。

### English

The Letter on Leek Flowers is Yang Ningshi’s thank-you note for a gift of leek flowers: running-regular script, ink on hemp paper, seven lines and just over sixty characters, now in the Wuxi Museum. It closes with the date “the eleventh of the seventh month” and no year. Widely spaced columns and strokes that tighten and release give it a quiet, unhurried air amid the disorder of the Five Dynasties.

## 待考

### 中文

- 字数有六十三字与六十五字两说，馆方两篇文章即不一致，未据原件复核。
- 馆方记传世三本，除清内府本外，另举罗振玉藏本；真伪之争尚未定论。

### English

- The museum’s own pages give either sixty-three or sixty-five characters; the original has not been checked.
- The museum records three versions and names the Luo Zhenyu album alongside the Qing palace album; their authenticity remains debated.
