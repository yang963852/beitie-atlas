# 黄庭经

## 背景

### 中文

小楷，写《黄庭经》文本。馆方所藏为宋拓《晋唐小楷》册的一种，一册七开，本幅七开十四幅，楷书。所读为拓本，不是王羲之墨迹。

### English

Small regular script carrying the text of the Yellow Court Classic. The palace museum holds a Song rubbing in its album of Jin and Tang small regular script: one volume of seven leaves, fourteen panels, in regular script. What is read is a rubbing, not Wang Xizhi's manuscript.

## 待考

### 中文

- 馆方只给释文与册页数，未记原石、拓制年份与尺寸。
- 「换鹅」一类传说不见于所引馆方页面，本记录不取。

### English

- The museum gives the transcription and leaf count only; it records no stone, rubbing date or measurement.
- The "trading for geese" story does not appear on the cited museum page and is not used here.
