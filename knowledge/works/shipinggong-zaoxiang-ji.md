# 始平公造像记

## 背景

### 中文

此刻位于龙门古阳洞北壁，是龙门二十品中唯一阳刻之作：字与界格均凸出石面。额二行六字，正文十行、行二十字。清乾隆间金石学者黄易将其置于龙门造像题记之首。

### English

Carved on the north wall of Guyang Cave at Longmen, this is the only relief-carved (阳刻) inscription among the twenty celebrated Longmen dedications: the characters and the ruled grid both stand proud of the rock. The title runs two lines of six characters; the text, ten lines of twenty. The Qing scholar Huang Yi placed it first among the Longmen inscriptions.

## 待考

### 中文

- 未引石面测量。展览说明记龙门石窟研究院藏拓片长八十九、宽三十九厘米，那是纸幅，不是石面尺寸。
- 题记纪年一行有残；太和二十二年（498）从展览说明，记为著录年代，非据石面测得。
- 此记属「龙门二十品」，然二十品是后人所选，非一体实物；本记录不把二十品当作一件实物。

### English

- No measured survey of the stone face is cited. The exhibition notice gives 89 × 39 cm for the rubbing held by the Longmen research institute, which is a paper size and not the rock's.
- The inscription's date line is damaged; the 498 year is the one given by the exhibition notice and is recorded as documented rather than measured.
- The dedication is one of the 龙门二十品, but the group is a later selection, not a physical set; the record does not treat the twenty as a single object.
