# 瘗鹤铭

## 背景

### 中文

这是一篇刻在崖上的悼鹤铭。作者署别号，年代用干支；石曾坠入长江，后以残块捞起。残字犹存，遂为后世习大字者所重。

### English

A cliff elegy for a crane, signed with a sobriquet rather than a name, and dated by a cyclical year. It fell into the Yangtze, was recovered in fragments, and became the model for large-character calligraphy.
