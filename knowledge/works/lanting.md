# 兰亭序

## 背景

### 中文

读王羲之《兰亭序》，可以对看神龙本的摹写与定武本的刻拓。

### English

The Shenlong manuscript copy and the Dingwu rubbing preserve two ways of transmitting Wang Xizhi’s preface.

## 导读

### 中文

永和九年（353），王羲之写下这篇序。原迹不存，今天能读到的是两条传本：唐摹神龙本与定武刻拓本；作品条目不把任何一件当作原迹。[故宫博物院《冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷》](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)

神龙本是一件唐摹本，传统上归为[冯承素](../people/feng-chengsu.md)摹写。故宫博物院对这一归名与卷首“神龙”印的年代都提出疑问，所以条目写作“传冯承素摹”，不把“传”字去掉；[王羲之](../people/wang-xizhi.md)是序文的作者，不是这卷的书写者。[神龙本条目](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)

定武本则是刻拓本，不是摹本。本站把[神龙本](../catalogue/lanting.md#witness-lanting)与[定武本](../catalogue/lanting.md#dingwu-zhan)分列为两件实物：一件是唐摹墨迹，一件是宋代拓本，各有自己的制作与收藏。读的时候先分清“摹”与“拓”，再把它们与王羲之的原稿分开。[故宫博物院《宋拓定武兰亭序》](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/233468.html)

### English

In 353, the ninth year of Yonghe, Wang Xizhi wrote this preface. The original is not extant; what can be read today are two lines of transmission, the Tang tracing known as the Shenlong copy and the Dingwu engraved rubbing, and the record treats neither as the original. [Palace Museum, Feng Chengsu manuscript copy of the Lanting Preface](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)

The Shenlong copy is a Tang tracing, traditionally attributed to [Feng Chengsu](../people/feng-chengsu.md). The Palace Museum questions both that attribution and the date of the “Shenlong” seal at the front, so the record says “traditionally attributed to Feng Chengsu” and keeps the qualification; [Wang Xizhi](../people/wang-xizhi.md) is the author of the text, not the writer of this scroll. [Shenlong copy entry](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)

The Dingwu version, by contrast, is an engraved rubbing, not a tracing. This site records [the Shenlong copy](../catalogue/lanting.md#witness-lanting) and [the Dingwu version](../catalogue/lanting.md#dingwu-zhan) as two objects: one a Tang ink tracing, the other a Song rubbing, each with its own making and holding. First separate tracing from rubbing, then separate both from Wang Xizhi’s original. [Palace Museum, Song rubbing of the Dingwu Lanting](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/233468.html)
