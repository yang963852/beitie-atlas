# 甲种本大楷习字帖（草稿）

## 中文

要找[沈尹默](../people/shen-yinmo.md)写给青少年的这本字帖，书名里的“甲种本”和“大楷”都值得记下。西泠印社所载年表将上海教育出版社出版这本字帖一事列于1962年。同条还列有他写的另一本小楷字帖。姜涛谈沈尹默晚年在上海教人习字，也分别列出了这两种书。[西泠印社年表，1962年条](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf) · [姜涛，第二节论青少年习字](https://shufa.pku.edu.cn/whsf/8dc5e55fd0e445a781a861c438b81cf9.htm)

若你手边有一本，可先抄下封面题名，再翻版权页核对出版年月、版次和印次。年表在1962年条内还引了一则展览报道，其中把供青少年的大小楷字帖泛称为前一年出版；报道没有逐种列名。初版的具体年月、印次和可辨认的馆藏册仍待用书本核实。[西泠印社年表，1962年条内的展览报道](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf)

目前所引材料解释了当初给谁编写。仍想找到一份当代课程书单或课堂记录，能指出具体使用了这部甲种本，以及老师选了哪一页供学生临写。

## English

Two details identify [Shen Yinmo](../people/shen-yinmo.md)’s copybook for young learners: the designation 甲种本 (edition A) and the large regular script. A chronology hosted by the Xiling Seal Art Society lists its publication by Shanghai Education Publishing House under 1962. The same entry names a separate small-script book. Jiang Tao identifies both titles in his study of Shen’s later work in Shanghai. [Xiling chronology, 1962 entry](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf) · [Jiang Tao, second section](https://shufa.pku.edu.cn/whsf/8dc5e55fd0e445a781a861c438b81cf9.htm)

A reader with a copy can compare its cover title, imprint, edition and printing. Under 1962 the chronology also quotes a contemporary exhibition report that dates books for young learners to the preceding year without naming each title. The first publication month, printing and an identifiable library copy still need evidence from an actual volume. [Exhibition report quoted in the 1962 entry](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf)

These sources establish the intended audience. A recent syllabus or class record naming this edition and the pages assigned would document its use by learners today.
