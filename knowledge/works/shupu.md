# 书谱

## 背景

### 中文

唐孙过庭撰并书《书谱》，草书，垂拱三年（687）成书。上卷墨迹原藏清内府，后归故宫博物院，今在台湾。

### English

Sun Guoting’s Book Manual, written in cursive and completed in 687. The surviving upper scroll was held in the Qing imperial collection and later in the Palace Museum; it is now in Taiwan.
