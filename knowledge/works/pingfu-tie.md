# 平复帖

## 背景

### 中文

故宫称此帖为现存年代最早、真实可信的西晋名家法帖：秃笔书于麻纸，凡九行。此帖宋代入宣和内府，明代经韩世能、张丑，清代经安岐、乾隆内府，后归恭王府；1956 年张伯驹、潘素夫妇捐献国家。

### English

The Palace Museum describes this as the earliest surviving authenticated letter by a named Western Jin master: nine lines written with a worn brush on hemp paper. It passed through the Song imperial collection, the Ming collectors Han Shineng and Zhang Chou, the Qing collections of An Qi and the Qianlong court, and the household of Prince Gong; Zhang Boju and Pan Su gave it to the state in 1956.

## 待考

### 中文

- 馆方记九行八十六字，书体作「草隶书」；本记录照录，不改称章草或草书。
- 自宋内府至 1956 年的递藏依馆方段落概述；该段未给中间各家系年。
- 本记录不主张书写年份：馆方只系于西晋，未给具体年。

### English

- The museum gives 9 lines and 86 characters and calls the script 草隶书; the wording is kept rather than converted to 章草 or 草书.
- The transmission chain from the Song imperial collection to 1956 is summarised from the museum's own paragraph; no intermediate owner is dated there.
- The record does not assert a writing year: the museum places the letter in the Western Jin without one.
