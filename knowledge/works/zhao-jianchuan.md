# 汲黯传

## 背景

### 中文

《汉汲黯传》为《史记·汲黯传》的小楷抄本，册有「延祐七年九月三十日赵孟頫手抄此传于松雪斋」款识，宋淡黄藏经纸本，乌丝栏，共十页，每页 17.6×17.4 厘米，藏日本东京细川家永青文库。2022 年东京「赵孟頫与他的时代」特展借展。此册书风与赵孟頫传世小楷迥异，真伪历来有争议：张光宾、傅申、王连起等推断为俞和仿赵之作，亦有持赵作说者；文徵明跋已记「或疑其轨方峻劲，不类公书」。两说并存，本记录不断言。

### English

The Biography of Ji An is a small-regular-script copy of the Shiji chapter, bearing a colophon dated 1320 in Zhao Mengfu's name. The album, on pale-yellow sutra paper with ruled columns, has ten leaves of 17.6 by 17.4 cm and is held by the Eisei Bunko of the Hosokawa family in Tokyo; it was lent to the 2022 Tokyo exhibition on Zhao Mengfu and his era. Because the style differs sharply from Zhao's surviving small script, its authenticity has long been debated: Zhang Guangbin, Fu Shen and Wang Lianqi infer an imitation by Yu He, while others keep the Zhao attribution, and even Wen Zhengming's colophon records doubt. Both views are kept; no verdict is asserted.
