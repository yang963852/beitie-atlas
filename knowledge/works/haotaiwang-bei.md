# 好太王碑

## 背景

### 中文

碑以角砾凝灰岩略加修琢成方柱形，四面环刻汉字隶书：通计四十四行、一千七百七十五字，可辨识者约一千五百九十字。碑文记高句丽建国传说、好太王功业及三百三十户守墓烟户的摊派。1961 年随洞沟古墓群公布为第一批全国重点文物保护单位。

### English

A single block of breccia tuff cut into a square column and inscribed on all four faces with Chinese characters in clerical script: 44 lines, 1,775 characters in all, of which about 1,590 remain legible. The text gives the founding legend of Koguryo, the deeds of King Gwanggaeto, and the allocation of 330 households of tomb guards. It was designated a national key cultural relic in 1961 together with the Donggou tomb complex.

## 待考

### 中文

- 方志旧条目记四十四行、满行四十一字，新条目记四十四行、一千七百七十五字；二者相合，但均未逐面说明满行字数。
- 今人常写作「好大王碑」；本项目以「好太王碑」为条名，别写存为别名。
- 碑文所涉历史地理及五世纪初的对外关系，此处不作概述；只录方志明言者。

### English

- The gazetteer's older entry counts 44 lines of 41 characters per full line; the newer entry gives 44 lines and 1,775 characters. The two are consistent, but neither states how many characters a full line holds on each face.
- The stele is often called 好大王碑 in modern writing; the project keeps 好太王碑 as the title and records the other form as an alias.
- The inscription's historical geography and its bearing on early fifth-century relations are not summarised here; only what the gazetteer states is recorded.
