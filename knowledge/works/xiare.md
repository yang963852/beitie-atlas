# 夏热帖

## 背景

### 中文

《夏热帖》是故宫博物院著录的五代杨凝式书札，纸本手卷，草书八行三十二字，首署款“凝式”。馆方概述其内容为天气炎热时送给僧人消夏饮料“酥密水”以示问候，但没有具名收信僧人。[故宫博物院藏品页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228202.html)

馆方称此帖是杨凝式唯一的传世草书作品，并评价其“兼取唐颜真卿、柳公权笔法”。这是馆方的作品评价，不作为本库自行提出的临习规则。[故宫博物院藏品页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228202.html)

### English

The Palace Museum catalogues the Letter on Summer Heat as a Five Dynasties letter by Yang Ningshi, a paper handscroll in cursive script with eight lines and thirty-two characters, signed “凝式” at the beginning. The museum summarizes the contents as a summer greeting accompanied by the drink “酥密水” for a monk, but does not name the recipient monk. [Palace Museum collection entry](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228202.html)

The museum calls it Yang Ningshi’s only surviving cursive work and says that it “兼取唐颜真卿、柳公权笔法”, drawing on the brush methods of Yan Zhenqing and Liu Gongquan. This is recorded as the museum’s evaluation, not adopted as an independent practice prescription. [Palace Museum collection entry](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228202.html)

## 待考

### 中文

- 馆方定“五代”，未记书写年月，不能据杨凝式生卒推得此帖的创作年份。
- 释文有残损，馆方也注明“以下数字残损难识”及“下二行残损”；不补写缺字。
- 收信僧人未被具名。
- 赵孟頫题跋有延祐三年（1316）十一月十三日的明确纪年，其他题跋的日期不能照搬。

### English

- The museum gives “Five Dynasties” but no writing year or month; Yang Ningshi’s lifespan cannot supply an exact date for the letter.
- Parts of the transcription are damaged. The museum itself marks characters and the last two lines as illegible or damaged; no missing text is reconstructed.
- The recipient monk is not named.
- Zhao Mengfu’s colophon is explicitly dated to the thirteenth day of the eleventh month of Yanyou 3 (1316); that date is not transferred to the other colophons.
