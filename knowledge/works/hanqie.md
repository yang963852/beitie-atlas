# 寒切帖

## 背景

### 中文

唐摹本，纸本，草书五行五十一字，是王羲之收到两封来信后的答书，末及「谢司马」。帖后有明董其昌、娄坚等跋。馆方引徐邦达语，称此帖「双钩淡墨帖，极为明显，觚棱转折，备见锋芒，精好亦不在《远宦》之下，是唐摹善本无疑」。

### English

A Tang tracing copy in paper, five columns and fifty-one characters of cursive: Wang Xizhi's reply after receiving two letters, ending with a message for the Marshal Xie. Ming colophons by Dong Qichang and Lou Jian follow the sheet. The museum quotes Xu Bangda, who called it an unmistakable double-outline copy in pale ink, its edges and turns still sharp, in quality not below the Yuanhuan letter.

## 待考

### 中文

- 馆方英文说明将王羲之生卒写作 321—379，与中文页及通行系年不同，本记录不取该组生卒。
- 释文与「五十一字」从馆方中文页，未另据他本对校。
- 藏品号、引首与拖尾尺寸未见于此页。

### English

- The English caption dates Wang Xizhi to 321-379, which differs from the Chinese page and the usual reckoning; those dates are not adopted here.
- The transcription and the count of fifty-one characters follow the museum's Chinese page and have not been collated against another edition.
- The accession number and the mounting measurements are not given on the page.
