# 行书送友诗轴

## 背景

### 中文

《行书送友诗轴》书王铎自作五律一首，绢本，纵179.5厘米，横58厘米，今藏故宫博物院。诗云：「已觉鸿声至，那堪秋叶稀。一年频送客，千里尔言归。驿路烽常报，江帆雨又飞。谁知惆怅处，还掩旧荆扉。」款署「丙子，送友俚作，浴老张先生正，王铎」，钤「王铎之印」「字觉斯」。[故宫博物院，新理异态——明末清初五家书画特展·王铎行书送友诗轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/show/236325.html)

馆方说明：诗收入《拟山园选集》五律卷三，作于崇祯九年（1636）秋，其时王铎任南京翰林院掌院学士；此轴点画沉厚，笔势开张，可见颜真卿、米芾二家的影响。尺寸与绢本据同馆研究文章。[故宫博物院，王铎及其书画艺术谈略](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2019/05/13/u5cd90d3d47fb0.pdf)

### English

The Scroll of a Poem on Sending a Friend carries one of Wang Duo’s own five-character regulated verses. Ink on silk, 179.5 by 58 cm, it is now in the Palace Museum. The poem reads: “Already I hear the wild geese arrive, how can I bear the thinning of autumn leaves? A whole year of seeing guests off, and you, a thousand li away, say you are going home. On the post road the beacon fires are always reported; on the river sails the rain flies again. Who knows the place of disappointment? I close the old thorn gate once more.” The inscription reads “bingzi, a rustic poem on sending a friend, for the elder Mr Zhang to correct, Wang Duo”, with the seals “Wang Duo’s seal” and “Zi Juesi”. [Palace Museum, exhibition of five late-Ming and early-Qing masters, Wang Duo’s poem on sending a friend](https://www.dpm.org.cn/show/236325.html)

The museum notes that the poem is included in the fifth-character regulated verse section, juan 3, of the Nishan yuan xuanji, was written in the autumn of 1636, the ninth year of Chongzhen, when Wang Duo was chancellor of the Nanjing Hanlin Academy, and that the scroll’s strokes are deep and heavy, its momentum open, showing the influence of Yan Zhenqing and Mi Fu. The dimensions and silk support are from the same museum’s research article. [Palace Museum, Wang Duo and his painting and calligraphy](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2019/05/13/u5cd90d3d47fb0.pdf)
