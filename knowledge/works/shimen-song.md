# 石门颂

## 背景

### 中文

东汉建和二年（148），汉中太守王升撰颂，表彰杨孟文开通石门之功。刻辞原在崖壁，后连石凿取，移藏汉中市博物馆。

### English

A Eastern Han cliff inscription of 148 by the Hanzhong governor Wang Sheng, praising Yang Mengwen for opening the Shimen passage. Cut from the cliff and moved into the Hanzhong Museum.

## 导读

### 中文

东汉建和二年（148），汉中太守王升撰此颂，表彰杨孟文开通石门之功。刻辞原在崖壁，属摩崖刻石；条目只记撰者王升，没有另记书丹者。[中国社会科学网《陕西碑刻文献存藏丰富》](https://cssn.cn/skgz/bwyc/202403/t20240301_5736431.shtml)

20 世纪 70 年代初，因修建石门水库，入选的一批石门石刻被凿离原址，迁入汉中市博物馆保存。这批刻石常称“石门十三品”，《石门颂》为其中之一。[汉台区人民政府《石门十三品摩崖石刻》](https://www.htq.gov.cn/hzshtqzf/zjht/lswh/201707/312e445fc8eb26fd26e26ed8d7f0edf8.shtml)

因此，今天读到的已不是崖壁上的原位刻辞，而是凿迁后入藏博物馆的石刻。本站的[石门颂（摩崖）](../catalogue/shimen-song.md#cliff-shimen-song)记录收藏机构与迁移，不把“摩崖”理解为仍在原址。[中国社会科学网](https://cssn.cn/skgz/bwyc/202403/t20240301_5736431.shtml)

### English

In 148, the second year of Jianhe, the Hanzhong governor Wang Sheng composed this eulogy praising Yang Mengwen for opening the Shimen passage. The text was first cut into a cliff; the record names Wang Sheng as its author and records no calligrapher. [Chinese Social Sciences Net, Shaanxi stone inscriptions](https://cssn.cn/skgz/bwyc/202403/t20240301_5736431.shtml)

In the early 1970s, during the construction of the Shimen reservoir, a selected group of Shimen inscriptions was cut from the cliff and moved to the Hanzhong Museum. The group is commonly called the Thirteen Works of Shimen, and the Eulogy of Shimen is one of them. [Hantai District Government, Shimen cliff inscriptions](https://www.htq.gov.cn/hzshtqzf/zjht/lswh/201707/312e445fc8eb26fd26e26ed8d7f0edf8.shtml)

What can be read today is therefore no longer the inscription in its original cliff position, but the stone after it was cut away and taken into a museum. This site’s [Eulogy of Shimen](../catalogue/shimen-song.md#cliff-shimen-song) records the holding and the move, and does not treat “cliff inscription” as meaning that it still stands on the cliff. [Chinese Social Sciences Net](https://cssn.cn/skgz/bwyc/202403/t20240301_5736431.shtml)
