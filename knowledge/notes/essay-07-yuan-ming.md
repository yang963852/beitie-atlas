# 07｜专题：元明——复古与性灵

## 中文

[楷书《玄妙观重修三门记》卷](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=TB1461)所记为纸本碑文原稿、非碑石：玄妙观重修三门记文由牟巘撰、[赵孟頫](../people/zhao-mengfu.md)誊写并篆额，馆方称以唐李邕为法。[楷书《玄妙观重修三门记》卷（ColBase）](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/TB-1461?locale=zh)另记「四十九至五十六岁」系据碑文与官职所推，是推断非著录年月；馆方未记书写年份、尺寸。本页的编排置此卷于起首读「复古」，不定年。

[书画交辉——赵孟頫的艺术成就（楷书帝师胆巴碑卷）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/subject_zhaomengfu/achievement/245821.html)所录《胆巴碑》为龙兴寺碑纸本稿，记吐蕃僧胆巴（1230—1303）生平、追号帝师，赵孟頫自撰自书并篆额。款作延祐三年（1316）□月立石，月份已损、只录年份；馆方称取法李邕、柳公权。待考：卒年1303与稿本1316分属两事，此件纸本非刻石，本记录不主张碑石存世。本页的编排同读「复古」。

[洛神赋](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228244.html)条目只题「元赵孟頫书《洛神赋》」，行书，年代、尺寸、递藏来源未记，不从别处补。本页的编排把三门记、胆巴碑与此件并作一组：前两件楷书碑文稿、此件行书，合看「复古」在不同书体里的样子——这是编排上的并置，不排高下，不主张因果，馆方并未作此归并。

[元 杨维祯 行书真镜庵募缘疏卷](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00000621)记此卷为[杨维桢](../people/yang-weizhen.md)晚年为真镜庵所写募缘启事：纸本，纵三十三点三、横二百七十八点四厘米，卷装行书，庵址原在浦东、今不存。馆方称其「用笔多章草意」；卷中行草带入章草收笔与波磔，墨色干湿对比强，字距开、行距紧。卷无年款，馆方只称晚年，行数与字数未据馆方页面核对。本页的编排由此转入「奇崛」。

[上博历代书法馆常设展换展，新增九件书画精品](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/I00004231)记：正德十五年（1520）[王宠](../people/wang-chong.md)游包山作诗结为《包山集》，嘉靖五年（1526）冬补庵居士索书，次年（1527）以小楷抄寄，纸本纵二十一点六、横三百二十三点五厘米，文嘉称「天才妙绝」。首数两说并列：来源作二十二首，[上博历代书法馆常设展换展，新增九件书画精品](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/I00004231)亦存二十五首之说，未据原卷核对，不替读者择一；刘九庵对归属有疑问，馆方两说并存。本页的编排系于「吴门」。

[明拓墨池堂选帖](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234595.html)记为明拓丛帖：章藻以家藏及借得书迹摹勒上石，自万历三十年（1602）至三十八年（1610）八年刻成五卷，晋人尺牍到宋元诸家一帖对读；馆方并提醒选入书迹间有伪托。作品页未记书体，馆方于此页未记更多。本页的编排读「刻帖」一层：汇书迹为一编是另一种成书方式，不推因果。

[王铎行草书自书诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228265.html)著录此卷为[王铎](../people/wang-duo.md)自作诗六首，纸本，明崇祯十二年（1639）正月书，时年四十七，行草连绵、靠行气贯通；末款「己卯正月弟王铎具草」，卷纵二十六点九、横一百六十六厘米，本幅无藏印、未见著录。待考并列：馆方朝代作「清」而款识己卯，本库按书写年份系于明；上款「君宣」与同页释文「群宣」一字之异未详。本页的编排归在「晚明」。

1. **[《三门记》](../works/sanmen-ji.md)**：看纸本稿如何以李邕为法，留意篆额与正文同出一手。
2. **[《胆巴碑》](../works/daba-bei.md)**：看延祐三年稿本中月份已损的款识，且为纸本非刻石。
3. **[《洛神赋》](../works/zhao-luoshen.md)**：看行书一件，作品页未记年代与尺寸，馆方只题元人书。
4. **[《真镜庵募缘疏卷》](../works/zhenjing-an-muyuan-shu.md)**：看章草收笔与墨色对比，卷无年款、只称晚年。
5. **[《游包山集》](../works/you-baoshan-ji.md)**：看小楷抄旧作，记住二十二与二十五两说并列、并不取舍。
6. **[《墨池堂选帖》](../works/mochitang.md)**：看八年刻成五卷，自晋人尺牍到宋元诸家，间有伪托。
7. **[《王铎行草书自书诗卷》](../works/wangduo-zishu-shi.md)**：看己卯年自书诗六首，本幅无藏印、未见著录。

结尾只记边界：三门记与胆巴碑都是纸本稿，本记录不主张碑石存世；真镜庵卷无年款，游包山首数与王铎上款均存两说，馆方未定案，[楷书《玄妙观重修三门记》卷](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=TB1461)亦未记尺寸。本页的编排按「复古—奇崛—吴门、刻帖、晚明」把七件排成阅读顺序，又各系元代、明代分期；这是编辑取舍，不是时代因果，不补确定答案。 续读：[路径 11](../notes/path-03-running-cursive.md)、[路径 13](../notes/path-05-modelbook.md)。

## English

[楷书《玄妙观重修三门记》卷](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=TB1461) records a paper draft, not the stone: the Xuanmiao gate text was composed by Mou Yan（牟巘）, transcribed with a seal-script heading by [Zhao Mengfu（赵孟頫）](../people/zhao-mengfu.md); the museum says it follows Tang Li Yong（李邕）. [楷书《玄妙观重修三门记》卷（ColBase）](https://colbase.nich.go.jp/collection_items/tnm/TB-1461?locale=zh) adds "forty-nine to fifty-six," inferred from text and office, not a recorded date; no year or dimensions. This page arranges it first to read "revival," not to date it.

[书画交辉——赵孟頫的艺术成就（楷书帝师胆巴碑卷）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/subject_zhaomengfu/achievement/245821.html) records the Dampa stele as a Longxing Temple paper draft on the monk Dampa（胆巴） (1230–1303), later Imperial Preceptor; Zhao Mengfu composed, wrote and sealed it. Dated "Yanyou 3 (1316), □ month," the month damaged, only the year kept; the museum says it follows Li Yong and Liu Gongquan（柳公权）. Keep apart: the 1303 death and the 1316 draft are two matters; paper, not stone, no surviving stone claimed. This page arranges it under "revival."

[洛神赋](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228244.html) says only "Yuan Zhao Mengfu wrote the Rhapsody on the Goddess of the Luo," in running script; date, dimensions, provenance unrecorded, not filled in. This page arranges the Three Gates scroll, the Dampa stele and this running script as one group: two regular-script stele drafts and one running script, reading "revival" across scripts — a juxtaposition in the arrangement, no ranking, no causation, no grouping the museum itself makes.

[元 杨维祯 行书真镜庵募缘疏卷](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00000621) records a fund-raising notice [Yang Weizhen（杨维桢）](../people/yang-weizhen.md) wrote late in life for the Zhenjing Nunnery: paper, 33.3 by 278.4 cm, a running-script handscroll, the nunnery once in Pudong, now gone. The museum notes "much zhangcao manner in the brush"; zhangcao endings, flared strokes, wet-dry ink contrast, wide spacing, tight lines. No date, only "late years"; line and character counts unchecked. This page arranges the turn to "strange-rugged."

[上博历代书法馆常设展换展，新增九件书画精品](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/I00004231) records 1520: [Wang Chong（王宠）](../people/wang-chong.md) toured Baoshan and made the Baoshan poems; in winter 1526 Bu'an（补庵居士） asked for writing; in 1527 he copied them in small regular script, paper 21.6 by 323.5 cm; Wen Jia（文嘉） called it "genius beyond compare." Two counts stand side by side: the source gives twenty-two, [上博历代书法馆常设展换展，新增九件书画精品](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/I00004231) also twenty-five; neither checked against the original, this page does not choose. Liu Jiuan（刘九庵） doubted the attribution; the museum keeps both. This page arranges it under "Wumen."

[明拓墨池堂选帖](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234595.html) describes a Ming rubbing of a collected modelbook: Zhang Zao（章藻） traced family-held and borrowed writings onto stone, and from 1602 to 1610 cut five volumes in eight years, Jin letters through Song-Yuan masters in one book; the museum warns some are spurious. The page records no script type. This page arranges it to read the "cut modelbook" layer: gathering writings is another way a book is made, no causation drawn.

[王铎行草书自书诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228265.html) records six poems [Wang Duo（王铎）](../people/wang-duo.md) wrote himself, paper, first month of 1639, at forty-seven; the running-cursive runs on, held by the line's breath. The signature reads "jimao, first month"; the scroll is 26.9 by 166 cm, no seal, no catalogue record. Kept side by side: the entry labels the dynasty "Qing" while the signature is jimao, so this library files it under Ming by writing year; "Junxuan" and "Qunxuan" in the page's transcription differ by one character, unsettled. This page arranges it under "late Ming."

1. **[Record of the Three Gates of the Xuanmiao Abbey](../works/sanmen-ji.md)**: Look at the paper draft following Li Yong, heading and text from one hand.
2. **[Stele of the Imperial Preceptor Dampa](../works/daba-bei.md)**: Look at the Yanyou-3 draft's damaged month, on paper not cut stone.
3. **[Rhapsody on the Goddess of the Luo](../works/zhao-luoshen.md)**: Look at one running-script piece; no date, the museum only a Yuan writer.
4. **[Fund-raising Notice for the Zhenjing Nunnery](../works/zhenjing-an-muyuan-shu.md)**: Look at zhangcao endings, ink contrast, wide spacing; no date, only "late years."
5. **[Poems from the Baoshan Excursion](../works/you-baoshan-ji.md)**: Look at old poems in small regular script; keep the twenty-two and twenty-five counts side by side.
6. **[Mochitang Modelbook](../works/mochitang.md)**: Look at five volumes cut in eight years, Jin letters through Song-Yuan, some spurious.
7. **[Scroll of Wang Duo's Own Poems in Running-Cursive Script](../works/wangduo-zishu-shi.md)**: Look at six self-written poems of the jimao year, no seals, no catalogue record.

The close records boundaries: the Three Gates scroll and Dampa stele are paper drafts, no surviving stones claimed; the Zhenjing scroll has no date, the Baoshan count and Wang Duo's addressee each keep two views, the museum settles neither, and [楷书《玄妙观重修三门记》卷](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=TB1461) records no dimensions. This page arranges the seven into one reading order — "revival, strange-rugged, Wumen / cut modelbook / late Ming" — with Yuan and Ming period labels; editorial selection, not a causation of periods, no fixed answers.
