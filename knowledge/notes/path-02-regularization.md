# 10｜阅读路径：楷化——从钟繇到唐楷

## 中文

《宣示表》真迹未存，今只见刻本，存十八行。故宫博物院[谈《石渠宝笈·续编》中的“宋拓”《锺王楷帖》](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf)记传世本一般据王羲之临本摹刻，分单刻与丛帖，行款互有出入，本页不取其一。贾似道摹刻石今存首都博物馆，与本表关系待考；钟繇是否亲书、刻本是否即据临本，馆方记为传称。

[元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)是另一种载体。西安碑林博物馆条目记元桢削爵、复爵与助迁洛阳，刻于太和二十年（496）；志石高、宽各七十厘米，依馆方页面，未据原石复核。行款字数馆方未记，出土地只记「洛阳」，未记村名。馆方以「雄奇、恣肆、朴拙、刚劲」评其书；1926 年出土、1938 年于右任捐藏，亦出该条目。

[凹凹凹凸凹凹凹，这块题记很特别](https://m.gmw.cn/2023-05/27/content_1303386306.htm)记龙门古阳洞北壁此刻字与界格凸出石面，额二行六字、正文十行、行二十字；[跨越时空，对话龙门石窟云文博](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/cn/news/%E3%80%90art-forward%E3%80%91across-time-and-space-dialogue-with-longmen-grottoesyunwenbo-717187/)拓片条目记太和二十二年（498）与八十九乘三十九厘米纸幅。两处非一物：石面尺寸馆方未记；纪年一行有残，498 为著录年代，非据石面测得。「二十品」为后选，非一物。

曲阜汉魏碑刻陈列馆的[北魏•张猛龙碑（魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑）](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/wei/2016-04-03/2864.html)为北魏正光三年（522）正书，碑文颂张猛龙治郡，尤记兴学。本页的编排把元桢墓志、始平公造像记与此碑并列，读作墓志、造像题记、碑三种载体上的楷书，不写成演进因果；行款、石面与著录各依本来页面，馆方未记者留白。

[正定大佛寺存宋代转轮藏 仍能正常转动](https://culture.people.com.cn/BIG5/n/2014/0813/c22219-25458469.html)记龙藏寺碑隋开皇六年（586）、碑阳三十行、行五十字；[正定：往事与美，可寻可看](https://tzcjj.sjz.gov.cn/columns/66c7ea0c-6942-4c45-ac98-a23f17b523bf/202311/18/ad85a0d9-f42f-4733-a321-e556f7619bff.html)第 4 段记通高 3.24 米。两页所量不同：一作碑阳 1.56×0.89 米，一为通高，无法互校，本页不取平均、不作推测。本页的编排把此碑接向九成宫至玄秘塔诸碑，读作唐楷之「法」，不作因果。

1. **[《宣示表》](../works/xuanshi-biao.md)**：看十八行刻本与单刻、丛帖两本行款之异。
2. **[《元桢墓志》](../works/yuanzhen-muzhi.md)**：看太和二十年志石与馆方未记的行款。
3. **[《始平公造像记》](../works/shipinggong-zaoxiang-ji.md)**：看凸出石面的阳刻与十行、行二十字。
4. **[《张猛龙碑》](../works/zhang-menglong-bei.md)**：看北魏正光三年正书与碑文所记兴学之事。
5. **[《龙藏寺碑》](../works/longzang-si-bei.md)**：看碑阳三十行、行五十字与两处高度之异。
6. **[《九成宫醴泉铭》](../works/jiuchenggong-liquan-ming.md)**：看唐贞观六年魏徵撰铭、欧阳询书丹。
7. **[《雁塔圣教序》](../works/yanta.md)**：看褚遂良所书与收录的慈恩寺拓本。
8. **[《多宝塔感应碑》](../works/duobao-ta-bei.md)**：看天宝十一载颜真卿书碑与今藏西安碑林。
9. **[《颜勤礼碑》](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)**：看大历十四年著录、书体从缺与民国拓本。
10. **[《玄秘塔碑》](../works/xuanmi-ta-bei.md)**：看会昌元年裴休撰、柳公权书与明初拓册。

边界仍在：宣示表刻本与贾似道摹刻石的关系、元桢墓志行款字数、始平公石面尺寸、龙藏寺碑两处高度，馆方未记或待考者，本页不补答案（见[谈《石渠宝笈·续编》中的“宋拓”《锺王楷帖》](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf)、[元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)）。本页的编排只把这些对象按序排开，供逐件核对，不是演进定论；年代、书体、著录仍以各自来源页为准。 续读：[专题 04](../notes/essay-04-northern-southern.md)、[专题 05](../notes/essay-05-sui-tang.md)。

## English

No autograph of the Xuanshi biao survives; only cut copies remain, in eighteen lines. The Palace Museum's [谈《石渠宝笈·续编》中的“宋拓”《锺王楷帖》](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf) records a text generally engraved from Wang Xizhi's (王羲之) copy, in single-cut and collectanea forms with differing line arrangements — this page takes neither. Jia Sidao's (贾似道) stone is in the Capital Museum, its relation to this memorial left open (待考); whether Zhong Yao (钟繇) wrote it, or the cut copy follows Wang's copy, the museum calls hearsay (传称).

[元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149) is another carrier. The Beilin Museum entry records Yuan Zhen stripped of his title, restored for aiding the move to Luoyang, and dates the stone to Taihe 20 (496); height and width, each seventy centimetres, follow the museum page, not the stone. Line arrangement and character count are not recorded (馆方未记); the find spot is only Luoyang, no village. The museum praises the writing as 雄奇、恣肆、朴拙、刚劲; the 1926 unearthing and 1938 Yu Youren donation come from it.

[凹凹凹凸凹凹凹，这块题记很特别](https://m.gmw.cn/2023-05/27/content_1303386306.htm) places it on Guyang Cave's north wall at Longmen, characters and grid raised, a two-line six-character title, ten lines of twenty characters; the rubbing entry in [跨越时空，对话龙门石窟云文博](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/cn/news/%E3%80%90art-forward%E3%80%91across-time-and-space-dialogue-with-longmen-grottoesyunwenbo-717187/) gives Taihe 22 (498) and an 89-by-39-centimetre paper sheet. The two are not one object: the stone-face size is not recorded (馆方未记); the dating line is damaged, so 498 is a catalogued date, not one measured from the stone. The Twenty Items are a later selection, not one object.

The [北魏•张猛龙碑（魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑）](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/wei/2016-04-03/2864.html) of the Han and Wei Stele Gallery at Qufu is regular script of Zhengguang 3 (522); the text praises Zhang Menglong's rule, especially his founding of schools. This page arranges the Yuan Zhen epitaph, the Shiping dedication and this stele side by side, read as regular script on three carriers — epitaph, dedication, stele — not as a cause of evolution; line arrangements, stone faces and cataloguing follow their own pages; what is unrecorded stays blank.

[正定大佛寺存宋代转轮藏 仍能正常转动](https://culture.people.com.cn/BIG5/n/2014/0813/c22219-25458469.html) records the Longzang Temple stele, Sui Kaihuang 6 (586), thirty lines of fifty characters on the face; [正定：往事与美，可寻可看](https://tzcjj.sjz.gov.cn/columns/66c7ea0c-6942-4c45-ac98-a23f17b523bf/202311/18/ad85a0d9-f42f-4733-a321-e556f7619bff.html), in its fourth paragraph, gives an overall height of 3.24 metres. The two pages measure different parts: a face of 1.56 by 0.89 metres against an overall height; they cannot be cross-checked, so this page takes no average or guess. This page arranges this stele next to those from Jiucheng Palace to the Xuanmi Pagoda, read as Tang kai's 'fa', not as cause and effect.

1. **[Memorial of Declaration](../works/xuanshi-biao.md)**: Look at the eighteen-line cut copy and the differing line arrangements.
2. **[Epitaph of Yuan Zhen](../works/yuanzhen-muzhi.md)**: Look at the Taihe-20 stone and the unrecorded line arrangement.
3. **[Dedication for the Late Duke of Shiping](../works/shipinggong-zaoxiang-ji.md)**: Look at the raised yang-carving and ten lines of twenty characters.
4. **[Stele of Zhang Menglong](../works/zhang-menglong-bei.md)**: Look at the 522 regular script and the record of founding schools.
5. **[Stele of the Longzang Temple](../works/longzang-si-bei.md)**: Look at thirty lines of fifty characters and two differing heights.
6. **[Sweet Spring at Jiucheng Palace](../works/jiuchenggong-liquan-ming.md)**: Look at the 632 text by Wei Zheng, written by Ouyang Xun.
7. **[Wild Goose Pagoda Preface](../works/yanta.md)**: Look at Chu Suiliang's hand and the Ci'en Temple rubbing collected here.
8. **[Duobao Pagoda Stele](../works/duobao-ta-bei.md)**: Look at Yan Zhenqing's 752 stele and its home in the Xi'an Beilin.
9. **[Stele of Yan Qinli](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)**: Look at the 779 record, the blank script type and a Republican rubbing.
10. **[Xuanmi Pagoda Stele](../works/xuanmi-ta-bei.md)**: Look at the 841 text by Pei Xiu, written by Liu Gongquan, and an early-Ming rubbing.

Boundaries stay on the pages: the relation of the Xuanshi biao cut copies to Jia Sidao's stone, the Yuan Zhen epitaph's line count, the Shiping dedication's stone-face size, and the Longzang Temple stele's two heights — what is unrecorded (馆方未记) or left open (待考), this page supplies no answer (see [谈《石渠宝笈·续编》中的“宋拓”《锺王楷帖》](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf), [元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)). This page arranges these objects in reading order for item-by-item checking; it is no verdict on script evolution, and each date, script type and catalogue record follows its own source page.
