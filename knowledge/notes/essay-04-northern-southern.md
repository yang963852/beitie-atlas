# 04｜专题：南北朝——碑、志与摩崖

## 中文

西安碑林所藏《元桢墓志》为高、宽各七十厘米的志石，刻于太和二十年（496）。志主元桢（447—496）为北魏宗室，皇兴二年（468）封南安王，因敛财枉法削爵，后襄助孝文帝迁都洛阳而复爵。馆方页记年代、尺寸、生卒封爵、1926 年洛阳出土与 1938 年于右任捐藏；馆方称其书法雄奇刚劲。行款、字数馆方页未记，出土地只记「洛阳」。[元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)

《始平公造像记》在龙门古阳洞北壁，字与界格凸出石面。光明网该文称其为龙门唯一阳刻题记，记额二行六字、正文十行行二十字；上海市文旅局说明著录 498 年、孟达撰文、朱义章书，拓片 89 × 39 厘米、龙门石窟研究院藏。纸幅非石面尺寸；纪年一行有残，498 为著录年代。场合为洞壁造像题记。[凹凹凹凸凹凹凹，这块题记很特别](https://m.gmw.cn/2023-05/27/content_1303386306.htm) [跨越时空，对话龙门石窟云文博](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/cn/news/%E3%80%90art-forward%E3%80%91across-time-and-space-dialogue-with-longmen-grottoesyunwenbo-717187/)

《张猛龙碑》正光三年（522）立，书体作正书；碑文颂张猛龙治郡之绩，尤记兴学之事，曲阜汉魏碑刻陈列馆以全名《魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑》著录并附说明。作品页未记行款、字数、碑石尺寸，本记录不补。墓志、造像题记、碑，是三种场合；本页据此并置，为编排选择，不说三者有先后。[北魏•张猛龙碑（魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑）](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/wei/2016-04-03/2864.html)

《张玄墓志》刻于普泰元年（531）十月，志主张玄字黑女，官至南阳太守，清人避康熙讳多称「张黑女」。原石久佚，传世仅有何绍基旧藏剪裱拓本，今藏上海博物馆；来源记正书二十行、行二十字，何绍基道光五年得旧拓。待考另记行款二十行与十二行两说、字数有出入，未据拓本复核；出土地不详，志文只记葬于蒲坂城东原。[蒲坂城遇见南阳太守张黑女](https://www.yongji.gov.cn/doc/2024/09/02/472291.shtml)

元桢墓志刻于太和二十年（496），元桢生卒 447—496；张玄墓志刻于普泰元年（531）十月。本页把两志的时间线并置，只照录作品页年份，不补中间年代，也不把先后排成因果；两志各记各的志主与场合，来源未记它们之间有何关联。[元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149) [蒲坂城遇见南阳太守张黑女](https://www.yongji.gov.cn/doc/2024/09/02/472291.shtml)

《瘗鹤铭》刻在焦山西麓崖壁，为悼鹤铭，署别号、纪年用干支；石坠入长江，后捞起残块。中国青年报记 514 年刻、原刻 178 字、存 93 字（11 字不全）、1713 年捞起，列王羲之、陶弘景、顾况、王瓒等候选作者，未作定论；江苏省文旅厅页记五块残石今在焦山碑林，未给尺寸。514 与陶弘景之署属旧说，作品页年代标「传」，不坐实。[一座书法宝库，“浮”在大江之上](https://m.cyol.com/gb/articles/2024-11/19/content_dqZV9BU07E.html) [江苏大学戏曲传承基地推出《瘗鹤铭》京剧之歌](https://wlt.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2023/8/31/art_695_11000960.html)

《爨宝子碑》署「大亨四年乙巳」，书体在隶楷过渡之间。云南宣传网读作 405 年、记宽 0.86 米；作品页另记乾隆四十三年（1778）曲靖城南杨旗田出土、咸丰二年（1852）重出移置武侯祠、1937 年移入曲靖中学碑亭、1961 年列为首批全国重点文物保护单位。待考 405 与 404、宽度 0.68 米与 0.86 米两说并存，均不取舍；撰书者姓名所引页面未载，不主张作者。[读懂「二爨碑」 领略爨文化](https://www.ynxc.gov.cn/html/2024/whxdfocus_0709/3013517.html)

1. **[《元桢墓志》](../works/yuanzhen-muzhi.md)**：看七十厘米见方的志石与馆方所记削爵、复爵、迁都一段。
2. **[《始平公造像记》](../works/shipinggong-zaoxiang-ji.md)**：看字与界格凸出石面的阳刻，拓片纸幅非石面尺寸。
3. **[《张猛龙碑》](../works/zhang-menglong-bei.md)**：看正书碑文所颂张猛龙治郡之绩，其中尤记兴学之事。
4. **[《张玄墓志》](../works/zhang-xuan-muzhi.md)**：看原石久佚后仅存的何绍基旧藏拓本，行款二十行与十二行两说并存。
5. **[《瘗鹤铭》](../works/yi-he-ming.md)**：看坠江后捞起的五块残石，原刻 178 字存 93 字，作者候选未定。
6. **[《爨宝子碑》](../works/cuan-baozi-bei.md)**：看隶楷过渡的书体，碑自署大亨四年，405 与 404、宽度两说并存。

待考：元桢墓志行款、字数馆方未记；始平公造像记未引石面测量，二十品是后人所选、非一体实物；瘗鹤铭作者候选未定、年代标「传」；爨宝子碑 405 与 404、宽 0.68 与 0.86 米两说并存。本页的编排按墓志、造像、碑，再接南朝与边疆排开，为编辑选择；「南朝与边疆，不是均质的南北朝」是本页的编排视角，不是来源的断语，不替待考补确定答案。[读懂「二爨碑」 领略爨文化](https://www.ynxc.gov.cn/html/2024/whxdfocus_0709/3013517.html) 续读：[路径 10](../notes/path-02-regularization.md)、[专题 05](../notes/essay-05-sui-tang.md)。

## English

The Yuan Zhen epitaph in the Xi'an Beilin is a stone seventy centimetres square, cut in Taihe 20 (496). Yuan Zhen (447–496), a Northern Wei clansman made Prince of Nan'an in 468, lost rank for corruption and regained it after backing the capital's move to Luoyang. The museum page gives the date, size, ranks, the 1926 find and Yu Youren's 1938 donation; the museum calls the writing bold and stiff. Line arrangement and character count are unrecorded; the find spot is only 'Luoyang'. [元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149)

At Longmen's Guyang cave, the Dedication for the Late Duke of Shiping has raised characters and grid. The Guangming feature calls it Longmen's only yang-carved inscription: a two-line six-character title, ten lines of twenty; the Shanghai notice gives 498, Meng Da as author, Zhu Yizhang as writer, a rubbing 89 × 39 cm in the Longmen Academy. Those centimetres are paper, not stone face; the date line is damaged, 498 a catalogue date. The occasion is a cave-wall dedication. [凹凹凹凸凹凹凹，这块题记很特别](https://m.gmw.cn/2023-05/27/content_1303386306.htm) [跨越时空，对话龙门石窟云文博](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/cn/news/%E3%80%90art-forward%E3%80%91across-time-and-space-dialogue-with-longmen-grottoesyunwenbo-717187/)

The Stele of Zhang Menglong was erected in 522 in regular script; its text praises Zhang's governance, especially his schools, and the Qufu gallery catalogues it as 'Stele of the Pure Praise of Governor Zhang of Lu Commandery'. The work page gives no line arrangement, character count or stone measurements, so this record adds none. Epitaph, dedication and stele are three occasions; this page sets them side by side as an editorial choice, not stages of development. [北魏•张猛龙碑（魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑）](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/wei/2016-04-03/2864.html)

The Epitaph of Zhang Xuan was cut in the tenth month of 531; its subject, styled Heinv and Governor of Nanyang, was called 'Zhang Heinv' by Qing writers avoiding the Kangxi taboo. The stone is long lost; only He Shaoji's cut-and-mounted rubbing survives, in the Shanghai Museum. The source gives regular script, twenty lines of twenty, and acquisition in Daoguang's fifth year; the work notes add two line counts, twenty and twelve, differing character totals unchecked, a find spot unknown beyond 'east plain of Puban city'. [蒲坂城遇见南阳太守张黑女](https://www.yongji.gov.cn/doc/2024/09/02/472291.shtml)

The Yuan Zhen epitaph was cut in 496, its subject living 447–496; the Zhang Xuan epitaph was cut in the tenth month of 531. This page sets the two timelines side by side, transcribing only these given years, adding no intervening dates and no causal order between the two; each epitaph records its own subject and its own occasion, and the sources record no link at all between them. [元桢墓志](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=149) [蒲坂城遇见南阳太守张黑女](https://www.yongji.gov.cn/doc/2024/09/02/472291.shtml)

The Elegy for a Crane is a crane elegy on the cliff west of Jiao Mountain, signed by sobriquet, dated by gan-zhi; the stone fell into the Yangtze, raised in fragments. China Youth Daily gives 514, 178 original characters, 93 surviving (11 incomplete), 1713 recovery, and lists Wang Xizhi, Tao Hongjing, Gu Kuang and Wang Zan as candidates, undecided. The Jiangsu culture department page puts five fragments in the Jiaoshan stele forest; no dimensions. The 514 date and Tao Hongjing attribution are traditional; the work page marks it 'transmitted'; this record does not fix it. [一座书法宝库，“浮”在大江之上](https://m.cyol.com/gb/articles/2024-11/19/content_dqZV9BU07E.html) [江苏大学戏曲传承基地推出《瘗鹤铭》京剧之歌](https://wlt.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2023/8/31/art_695_11000960.html)

The Stele of Cuan Baozi is signed 'the fourth year of Daheng, yisi', its script between clerical and regular. The Yunnan publicity page reads it as 405, width 0.86 m; the work page adds a 1778 unearthing at Yangqitian south of Qujing, an 1852 reappearance moved to the Wuhou Shrine, a 1937 move to the Qujing middle school pavilion, and 1961 national protection. Its notes keep 405 and 404, 0.68 m and 0.86 m, without choosing; the cited pages give no author; none is claimed. [读懂「二爨碑」 领略爨文化](https://www.ynxc.gov.cn/html/2024/whxdfocus_0709/3013517.html)

Open questions stay: the Yuan Zhen epitaph's unrecorded line count; the Shiping dedication without stone-face measurement, the Twenty Items a later selection, not one object; the Elegy's undecided authors and 'transmitted' date; Cuan Baozi's 405 versus 404 and 0.68 versus 0.86 m. This page arranges epitaph, dedication, stele, then south and frontier in sequence: an editorial choice. 'South and frontier are not a homogeneous Northern and Southern dynasties' is this page's editorial view, not a verdict from the sources; no firm answer supplied.
