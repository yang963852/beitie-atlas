# 03｜专题：魏晋——名家、信札与复制

## 中文

1996 年长沙走马楼出土十万余枚吴简，「嘉禾吏民田家莂」为长约五十厘米的大木简，逐户记佃田、赋税与缴纳。嘉禾为孙权年号，当 232—238 年；简上隶意未退而楷法渐生，是民间文书里的隶楷之变。馆方页面只记形制与内容，田家莂总数未给，完整释文与编连还须查整理本。[吴简｜三国农民的纳税凭证——嘉禾吏民田家莂](https://www.chinajiandu.cn/News/Details/zhshkp?nid=1660)

故宫系《平复帖》于西晋，馆方称此帖为真实可信的西晋名家法帖：秃笔书于麻纸，凡九行。馆方记九行八十六字，另有八十四字之说，两说并存，不取平均；书体照录作「草隶书」，不改称章草或草书。递藏自宋宣和内府，历明、清各家至恭王府，1956 年由张伯驹、潘素夫妇捐献国家；馆方未给书写年份。[陆机草隶书平复帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html)

本页把《平复帖》与《宣示表》并置，谈「原作缺席、只剩刻本或摹本」：前者虽经馆方称为真实可信的西晋法帖，所见仍只麻纸九行、未给年；后者真迹早佚，传世只见刻本。《宣示表》是[钟繇](../people/zhong-yao.md)上魏文帝曹丕、请受孙权归附的奏文，一般认为据[王羲之](../people/wang-xizhi.md)临本摹刻，存十八行；是否亲书、是否据临本，均属传称。[谈《石渠宝笈·续编》中的“宋拓”《锺王楷帖》](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf)

万岁通天二年（697），王方庆进献先祖[王羲之](../people/wang-xizhi.md)、[王献之](../people/wang-xianzhi.md)等一门二十八人法书十卷，武则天命弘文馆勾填摹写留内府，今仅存一卷、七人书翰十通。辽博条记为硬黄纸唐人双钩填墨本，《石渠宝笈》著录；光明网记 26.3 × 253.8 厘米与两次火劫。十通本为私人书信而非文章，卷上无摹手署款，火劫年份来源未记。[藏品：摹王氏一门书翰卷](https://www.lnmuseum.com.cn/) [那些有故事的镇馆之宝（唐摹王羲之一门书翰卷）](https://museum.gmw.cn/2017-12/15/content_27109986_6.htm)

读[王羲之](../people/wang-xizhi.md)《兰亭序》，可对看神龙本的摹写与定武本的刻拓。故宫藏品题名作「冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷」，属摹写一路，「其流传经过」段记其递传；定武本属刻拓一路。一摹一拓，非同一实物，本页不互串。作品页未记年代，两本关系来源未记，也不替读者判定。[冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)

[王献之](../people/wang-xianzhi.md)所书曹植《洛神赋》入宋已残。南宋贾似道先得九行、又续得四行，刻于似碧玉的佳石，世称「玉版十三行」；石后佚，明万历间西湖葛岭复得，清康熙间入内府，1900 年后流散，1981 年北京市文物商店购得转交首都博物馆。原墨迹已佚，存世为十三行刻石，非纸本摹本；石之尺寸、藏品号与字数来源未记。[聚焦汉字文明（玉版十三行）](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrbhwb/page/2016-04/19/10/rmrbhwb2016041910.pdf)

《伯远帖》为东晋[王珣](../people/wang-xun.md)一通信札，本页把它作为另一封信与前面书翰并置。故宫藏品条记其乾隆十一年入内府、与三希堂合藏一段；题名与该段之外，作品页年代原文未记，本记录也不补书写年份。它排在复制链之后，为留「信」这一层，不算进摹本或刻本。[伯远帖](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228199.html)

1. **[《走马楼吴简·嘉禾吏民田家莂》](../works/zoumalou-wujian.md)**：看大木简上逐户记录佃田、赋税的隶楷笔迹。
2. **[《平复帖》](../works/pingfu-tie.md)**：看麻纸秃笔九行，馆方记八十六字，另有八十四字之说。
3. **[《宣示表》](../works/xuanshi-biao.md)**：看真迹早佚之后只剩刻本，十八行小楷与传称的临本。
4. **[《万岁通天帖》](../works/wansui-tongtian-tie.md)**：看硬黄纸双钩填墨的七人十通，本是私信而非文章。
5. **[《兰亭序》](../works/lanting.md)**：看神龙本摹写与定武本刻拓之别，一摹一拓两者不互串。
6. **[《洛神赋十三行》](../works/luoshen-shisanhang.md)**：看原墨迹已佚，存世为十三行刻石，非纸本摹本。
7. **[《伯远帖》](../works/boyuan.md)**：看王珣这另一封信，馆方记乾隆十一年入内府、三希堂合藏。

待考仍在：平复帖八十六字与八十四字两说并存、宣示表是否亲书未定，贾似道摹刻石今存首都博物馆而与本表关系待核，都不取其一；万岁通天帖摹手未署款、火劫未记年份。本页的编排把日常文书、原作与缺席、复制链、另一封信依次排开，是编辑选择，不是书体演变的因果，也不替待考补确定答案。[谈《石渠宝笈·续编》中的“宋拓”《锺王楷帖》](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf) 续读：[路径 11](../notes/path-03-running-cursive.md)、[路径 13](../notes/path-05-modelbook.md)。

## English

Among the hundred-thousand-plus Wu slips unearthed at Zoumalou, Changsha, in 1996, the Jiahe field-tax records are large wooden slips about fifty centimetres long, noting tenancy, taxes and payments household by household. Jiahe was Sun Quan's reign title, 232–238; clerical habits have not faded while regular-script ones emerge, a change in everyday documents. The museum page gives form and content, not the total; full transcription and joining need the edited volumes. [吴简｜三国农民的纳税凭证——嘉禾吏民田家莂](https://www.chinajiandu.cn/News/Details/zhshkp?nid=1660)

The Palace Museum dates the Letter on Recovery to the Western Jin, calling it a credible Western Jin model-letter: worn brush on hemp paper, nine lines. It gives eighty-six characters, another reading eighty-four; both stand, no average taken; the script stays 'clerical cursive', not renamed. Transmission runs from the Song Xuanhe court through Ming and Qing owners to Prince Gong's mansion; Zhang Boju and Pan Su donated it in 1956. No writing year is given. [陆机草隶书平复帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html)

This page sets the Letter on Recovery beside the Memorial of Declaration to raise absent originals, surviving engravings or copies: the former is called a credible Western Jin model-letter, yet still nine lines on hemp paper, no year given; the latter's original is lost, only engravings remain. The memorial is [Zhong Yao](../people/zhong-yao.md)'s address to Cao Pi, Emperor Wen of Wei, on Sun Quan's submission, generally held to be engraved from [Wang Xizhi](../people/wang-xizhi.md)'s copy in eighteen lines; authorship and that copying are attributions. [谈《石渠宝笈·续编》中的“宋拓”《锺王楷帖》](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf)

In 697 Wang Fangqing presented ten scrolls by twenty-eight ancestors, including [Wang Xizhi](../people/wang-xizhi.md) and [Wang Xianzhi](../people/wang-xianzhi.md); Wu Zetian had them traced at the Hongwen Institute. One scroll survives, ten letters by seven hands. The Liaoning entry calls it a Tang double-outline ink-filled copy on hardened yellow paper, in the Shiqu Baoji; Guangming Online gives 26.3 × 253.8 cm and two fires. These were private letters, not essays; no copyist signed, fire dates unrecorded. [藏品：摹王氏一门书翰卷](https://www.lnmuseum.com.cn/) [那些有故事的镇馆之宝（唐摹王羲之一门书翰卷）](https://museum.gmw.cn/2017-12/15/content_27109986_6.htm)

Reading [Wang Xizhi](../people/wang-xizhi.md)'s Preface to the Orchid Pavilion, one compares the Shenlong tracing with the Dingwu cut-and-rubbed edition. The Palace Museum titles its object 'Feng Chengsu's running-script tracing of the Orchid Pavilion Preface', its 'transmission' section recording the handings-down; the Dingwu edition is the rubbed line. These are not one object; this page does not conflate them. The work page gives no date; the two versions' relation is unrecorded, with no verdict. [冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)

[Wang Xianzhi](../people/wang-xianzhi.md)'s Goddess of the Luo, after Cao Zhi, was incomplete by the Song. Jia Sidao got nine lines, then four, cut on jade-like stone: the 'Jade Tablet Thirteen Lines'. Lost, recovered at Geling by West Lake in Wanli, into the inner court under Kangxi, dispersed after 1900, it was bought in 1981 by the Beijing Antique Store and passed to the Capital Museum. The ink original is lost; a thirteen-line carving survives, no paper copy. Measurements, accession number and character count are unrecorded. [聚焦汉字文明（玉版十三行）](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrbhwb/page/2016-04/19/10/rmrbhwb2016041910.pdf)

The Letter to Boyuan is a letter by [Wang Xun](../people/wang-xun.md) of the Eastern Jin, set here as another letter. The Palace Museum entry records its entry into the inner court in the Qianlong eleventh year and its keeping with the Three Treasures Hall group; beyond that record the work page gives no date; this record adds none. It stands after the chain of copies so the 'letter' stays visible, not counted among tracings or engravings. [伯远帖](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228199.html)

1. **[Zoumalou Wu slips, Jiahe field-tax records](../works/zoumalou-wujian.md)**: Look at the clerical-to-regular hand noting tenancy and tax, household by household, on a wooden slip.
2. **[Letter on Recovery](../works/pingfu-tie.md)**: Look at nine worn-brush lines on hemp paper; eighty-six characters, or eighty-four in another reading.
3. **[Memorial of Declaration](../works/xuanshi-biao.md)**: Look at eighteen lines of small regular script surviving only in engravings, the original lost.
4. **[Model Letters of the Wan Sui Tong Tian Year](../works/wansui-tongtian-tie.md)**: Look at ten private letters by seven hands, traced and ink-filled on yellow paper.
5. **[Preface to the Orchid Pavilion](../works/lanting.md)**: Look at the Shenlong tracing and the Dingwu rubbing; the two are not conflated.
6. **[Thirteen Lines from the Goddess of the Luo](../works/luoshen-shisanhang.md)**: Look at how the ink original is lost and only a thirteen-line carving survives.
7. **[Letter to Boyuan](../works/boyuan.md)**: Look at this other letter by Wang Xun; the museum records its Qianlong-eleventh-year entry into the inner court and the Three Treasures Hall.

Open questions stay: eighty-six versus eighty-four characters in the Letter on Recovery, whether Zhong Yao wrote the memorial, and the Jia Sidao stone in the Capital Museum whose relation to it awaits checking — no side is chosen. The Wan Sui Tong Tian scroll has no copyist's signature and unrecorded fire dates. This page arranges everyday documents, originals and absences, the copy chain and another letter in sequence: an editorial choice, not a cause of script change, no firm answer supplied. [谈《石渠宝笈·续编》中的“宋拓”《锺王楷帖》](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf) Continue with [Path 11](../notes/path-03-running-cursive.md) and [Path 13](../notes/path-05-modelbook.md).
