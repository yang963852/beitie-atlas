# 从一个「故」字读《颜勤礼碑》（草稿）

## 中文

临《颜勤礼碑》，可以先留意碑背的一个「故」字。颜真卿为曾祖父颜勤礼撰文并书写的这通碑，立于唐大历十四年（779）。一千多年后，恰好穿过这个字的一道断口，让人得以追看碑石重见天日以后的经历。 [碑林藏品说明](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2582&c=View&m=home) · [碑林移碑记述](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2440&c=View&m=home)

1922 年 10 月，工人在西安修整省长公署的地基时掘出了断成两截的碑石。西安碑林博物馆的文章记载，有人就在坑边取拓；当时两截石头还能对上，「故」字的笔画也能接起来。数日后，碑立到公署前院，字的断处已合不拢了。 [碑林移碑记述](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2440&c=View&m=home)

1928 年春，碑迁到新城，成为当地「小碑林」的一部分。馆方记述，此时「故」只剩几笔。1948 年，这批碑石又迁入西安碑林。临写时若遇到残笔，可以先问一句：我眼前这一笔，留下的是笔锋，还是石头后来受过的伤？ [碑林移碑记述](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2440&c=View&m=home)

再翻故宫的藏品目录，同一个碑名下是一册纸本，编号新00045406。馆方列出册页图，并引录旧目录里的装裱和购价信息，只将拓制年代定为民国。我们还不知道这册具体拓于哪一年、何时入藏，也没有依据把它认作当年坑边拓下的那一份。 [故宫藏品简介](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234175.html)

## English

Look for the character 故 on the back of the Yan Qinli Stele. Yan Zhenqing composed and wrote the inscription for his great-grandfather Yan Qinli. The stone was erected in 779. More than a thousand years later, a break running through that one character would help tell the story of the stone after its rediscovery. [Beilin object entry](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2582&c=View&m=home) · [Beilin account of the transfers](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2440&c=View&m=home)

In October 1922, workers repairing the foundations of the governor’s compound in Xi’an uncovered the stone in two pieces. According to the Beilin Museum, people took impressions at the edge of the pit. The halves still fitted together, joining the strokes of 故. A few days later, when the stone stood in the front courtyard, the broken edges of the character no longer met. [Beilin account of the transfers](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2440&c=View&m=home)

In spring 1928 the stone moved to Xincheng, where it became part of a small collection of steles. The museum recounts that only a few strokes of 故 remained. In 1948 the collection moved to the Xi’an Beilin. When a stroke breaks off in your copybook, consider what its edge preserves: the movement of the brush, or later damage to the stone? [Beilin account of the transfers](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2440&c=View&m=home)

Under the same title, the Palace Museum catalogues a paper album, number 新00045406. Its entry shows the leaves and quotes an earlier catalogue on mounting and purchase prices. The museum dates the impression to the Republican era. Its precise year and accession date remain open, as does any connection to the impressions taken beside the pit. [Palace Museum catalogue](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234175.html)
