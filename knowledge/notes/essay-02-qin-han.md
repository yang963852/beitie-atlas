# 02｜专题：秦汉——统一文字与隶书登场

## 中文

[《泰山刻石》](../works/taishan-keshi.md)今存秦二世诏书残字十字，在岱庙东御座内；[岱庙（景点介绍）](https://tsgw.taian.gov.cn/art/2021/6/13/art_363479_10320189.html)记岱庙为泰安市博物馆址，刻石清代移存山下。作品页记始皇二十八年（前219）立石、二世元年（前209）加刻，两刻传为[李斯](../people/li-si.md)小篆。222字为两刻合计，始皇诏今靠拓本流传；残字中『臣去疾臣请矣臣』七字完整、『斯昧死』三字残泐，另有『九个半字』一说，尺寸馆方未记。

[《睡虎地秦简》](../works/shuihudi-qinjian.md)1975年出土于云梦睡虎地十一号秦墓。[湖北简牍地理，一窥湖北简牍之风采！](https://m-www.hbww.org.cn/p/8892.html)记竹简1155枚、残片80片，长23.1—27.8厘米、宽0.3—0.7厘米，秦隶，湖北省博物馆藏。作品页记多秦律与官文书，笔画短直、结体扁方带篆意。本页把泰山刻石公告文字与墓中日常书写并列，为编排对读，不写先后或因果；字数与墓主该页未记，各篇抄写年代不一，墓主『喜』职务与简册编连馆方未展开，属待考。

[《五凤刻石》](../works/wufeng-inscription.md)为灰岩，刻辞留有五凤二年纪年，[西汉刻石：五凤刻石](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/xihan/)据此著录。作品页记西汉宣帝五凤二年（公元前56年）、分类属碑；书体作品页未记，来源未记的不补。本页由西汉刻石接到东汉碑版，只是把两类石刻排在一起对看，是编排次序，非书体递变的因果说明。

[《礼器碑》](../works/liqi-bei.md)记东汉永寿二年（156）鲁相韩敕修孔庙、铸礼器，石今藏曲阜汉魏碑刻陈列馆。[孔庙三碑，惊艳了时光](https://www.sdxc.gov.cn/whql/whcc/202508/t20250822_16374659.htm)记1978年由同文门移东庑、1998年入曲阜汉魏碑刻陈列馆；[暑期攻略｜阅览碑刻 品读千年石墨精华](https://www.confuciusmuseum.cn/news/wb/2023-07-23/2630.html)逐碑记藏所、年代与行数。作品页记书体隶书；英文题名 Stele of the Ritual Vessels 为本项目为检索所拟、非来源题名，属待考。

[《曹全碑》](../works/cao-quan-bei.md)为东汉曹全而立，今藏西安碑林，碑留受颂者姓名、执笔者未署名。[国宝名碑 · 曹全碑](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=145)记20行、行45字、隶书，年代一作中平三年（186），常见目录作中平二年（185），作品页从后者，差异记录不判定。[曹全碑（馆方另一条目）](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2996&c=View&lang=en&m=home)记186年、宽95厘米，故宫页记123厘米，两说并列。英文题名本项目所拟，属待考。

[《张迁碑》](../works/zhangqian-bei.md)为东汉中平三年（186）县令张迁而立，作品页记书体隶书。[竹云阁帖（石门颂、神君碑、张迁碑、礼器碑）](https://g2.ltfc.net/work/SUFA/67d64a1899a6320270c19860)一则为二手帖目，记明初出土、立于岱庙碑廊，并称无馆方页可确认；[岱庙（景点介绍）](https://tsgw.taian.gov.cn/art/2021/6/13/art_363479_10320189.html)记岱庙为泰安市博物馆址，不给对象坐标、馆藏号。原石今在岱庙，作品页记泰安市博物馆活动列为馆藏；英文题名本项目所拟，属待考。

六件作品并看，可核项各有所重：泰山刻石记残字、睡虎地简记枚数与尺寸、五凤刻石记纪年、礼器碑记搬迁、曹全碑记行数、张迁碑记立碑之年。[国宝名碑 · 曹全碑](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=145)与[孔庙三碑，惊艳了时光](https://www.sdxc.gov.cn/whql/whcc/202508/t20250822_16374659.htm)都在行款、藏所落笔。本页把三碑并列，为对看碑刻不同面向；[李斯](../people/li-si.md)传为书手亦只作转述，不连成笔法传授的因果。

1. **[《泰山刻石》](../works/taishan-keshi.md)**：看二世诏书残字十字与岱庙移存，两刻传为李斯小篆而尺寸未记。
2. **[《睡虎地秦简》](../works/shuihudi-qinjian.md)**：看1155枚竹简的秦隶墨书，与公告文字并列，抄写年代不一。
3. **[《五凤刻石》](../works/wufeng-inscription.md)**：看灰岩刻辞所留五凤二年纪年，书体作品页未记、不补。
4. **[《礼器碑》](../works/liqi-bei.md)**：看永寿二年修庙铸器之事与同文门、东庑、陈列馆的移存。
5. **[《曹全碑》](../works/cao-quan-bei.md)**：看20行45字的隶书与受颂者留名，中平二年、三年两说并列。
6. **[《张迁碑》](../works/zhangqian-bei.md)**：看中平三年立碑与二手帖目所记岱庙碑廊，馆方页未确认。

待考：泰山刻石『九个半字』一说、李斯『传为』、尺寸馆方未记；睡虎地字数、墓主职务与编连未展开；五凤刻石书体未记；礼器、曹全、张迁英文题名本项目所拟；曹全碑中平二年与三年、[曹全碑（馆方另一条目）](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2996&c=View&lang=en&m=home)与常见目录差异不判定。本页的编排按刻石、简牍到东汉碑版列出六件作品，是编辑选择，不写成书体演变的因果；来源未记处不补确定答案。 续读：[专题 01](../notes/essay-01-pre-qin.md)、[路径 09](../notes/path-01-clerical-turn.md)。

## English

[Mount Tai Inscription](../works/taishan-keshi.md): ten residual Second Emperor edict characters survive in the Dai Temple's East Imperial Seat; [岱庙（景点介绍）](https://tsgw.taian.gov.cn/art/2021/6/13/art_363479_10320189.html) says Dai Temple is the Tai'an Museum site, the stone moved here in the Qing. The work page: set up 219 BCE, edict added 209 BCE, both traditionally attributed to [Li Si (李斯)](../people/li-si.md)'s small seal. The 222 characters total both inscriptions, the First Emperor's text in rubbings; seven ('chen qu ji chen qing yi chen') intact, three ('si mei si') worn, plus a 'nine and a half' account. No stone dimensions recorded.

[Shuihudi Qin Slips](../works/shuihudi-qinjian.md): unearthed 1975 from Qin Tomb 11, Shuihudi, Yunmeng. [湖北简牍地理，一窥湖北简牍之风采！](https://m-www.hbww.org.cn/p/8892.html) records 1155 slips plus 80 fragments, 23.1–27.8 cm by 0.3–0.7 cm, Qin clerical script, Hubei Provincial Museum. The work page: mostly Qin laws and official documents, short straight strokes, squat structure, seal traces. This page sets Mount Tai's public notice writing beside this tomb's everyday writing as comparison, not cause; character count and occupant unrecorded, copying dates differing, Xi's office and binding order unexplored — all open.

[Wufeng Inscription](../works/wufeng-inscription.md): limestone, its text keeping a date 'second year of Wufeng', on which [西汉刻石：五凤刻石](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/xihan/) bases its entry. The work page: Wufeng 2 of Emperor Xuan, Western Han (56 BCE), a stele; the script unrecorded there, and what sources omit is not filled in. This page runs from a Western Han stone to Eastern Han steles as editorial arrangement placing two stone kinds side by side, not a cause of script change.

[Stele of the Ritual Vessels](../works/liqi-bei.md): in Yongshou 2 (156) the Lu Chancellor Han Chi repaired the Confucian temple and cast ritual vessels; the stone is at the Han and Wei Stele Gallery, Qufu. [孔庙三碑，惊艳了时光](https://www.sdxc.gov.cn/whql/whcc/202508/t20250822_16374659.htm) gives its 1978 move from Tongwen Gate to the East Corridor and 1998 move into the Gallery; [暑期攻略｜阅览碑刻 品读千年石墨精华](https://www.confuciusmuseum.cn/news/wb/2023-07-23/2630.html) gives holding, date and line count. The work page: clerical script; the English title devised by this project, no source title — open.

[Stele of Cao Quan](../works/cao-quan-bei.md): set up for Cao Quan, now at Xi'an Beilin Museum; the stone keeps the praised man's name, the writer unsigned. [国宝名碑 · 曹全碑](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=145) gives 20 lines of 45 clerical characters, dating it to Zhongping 3 (186), while common catalogues give Zhongping 2 (185); the work page follows the latter, discrepancy recorded but unresolved. [曹全碑（馆方另一条目）](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2996&c=View&lang=en&m=home) gives 186 and width 95 cm, a Palace Museum page 123 cm, both side by side. The English title devised by this project — open.

[Stele of Zhang Qian](../works/zhangqian-bei.md): set up in Zhongping 3 (186) for the magistrate Zhang Qian; the work page gives clerical script. [竹云阁帖（石门颂、神君碑、张迁碑、礼器碑）](https://g2.ltfc.net/work/SUFA/67d64a1899a6320270c19860) is a secondary album note recording early Ming unearthing and the Dai Temple stele corridor, no museum page confirming it; [岱庙（景点介绍）](https://tsgw.taian.gov.cn/art/2021/6/13/art_363479_10320189.html) says Dai Temple is the Tai'an Museum site but gives no coordinates or accession number. The original is at Dai Temple; the work page notes a Tai'an Museum event lists it held; the English title devised by this project — open.

The six works emphasize different checkable items: the Mount Tai stone its residual characters, the Shuihudi slips count and dimensions, the Wufeng stone its date, the Liqi stele its moves, the Cao Quan stele its line count, the Zhang Qian stele its erection year. [国宝名碑 · 曹全碑](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=145) and [孔庙三碑，惊艳了时光](https://www.sdxc.gov.cn/whql/whcc/202508/t20250822_16374659.htm) both give layout and holding. This page sets the three steles side by side to compare stele faces; [Li Si (李斯)](../people/li-si.md) as traditional hand is a paraphrase, not transmission.

1. **[Mount Tai Inscription](../works/taishan-keshi.md)**: Look at ten residual edict characters and Dai Temple move; both cuts traditionally Li Si's small seal.
2. **[Shuihudi Qin Slips](../works/shuihudi-qinjian.md)**: Look at Qin clerical ink on 1155 slips, set beside public notice writing; copying dates differ.
3. **[Wufeng Inscription](../works/wufeng-inscription.md)**: Look at the Wufeng 2 date in the limestone text; the script unrecorded and not filled in.
4. **[Stele of the Ritual Vessels](../works/liqi-bei.md)**: Look at Yongshou 2 temple repair and vessel casting, and moves from Tongwen Gate to the East Corridor.
5. **[Stele of Cao Quan](../works/cao-quan-bei.md)**: Look at 20 lines of 45 clerical characters and praised man's name; Zhongping 2 and 3 side by side.
6. **[Stele of Zhang Qian](../works/zhangqian-bei.md)**: Look at Zhongping 3 erection and the secondary album's Dai Temple corridor, unconfirmed by a museum page.

Open points: Mount Tai's 'nine and a half' account, Li Si's 'traditionally attributed' hand and unrecorded stone dimensions; Shuihudi count, occupant Xi's office and binding order unexplored; the unrecorded Wufeng script; the Liqi, Cao Quan and Zhang Qian English titles devised by this project; the Zhongping 2 versus 3 discrepancy, [曹全碑（馆方另一条目）](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2996&c=View&lang=en&m=home) and common catalogues each holding one account, unresolved. This page arranges the six works from stone and slips to Eastern Han steles — an editorial choice, not a cause of script change; no certain answer where sources omit.
