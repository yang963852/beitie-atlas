# 09｜阅读路径：隶变——从青铜到汉碑

## 中文

[“盂”青铜鼎](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/csp/202008/t20200826_247375.shtml) 腹内壁铸铭 19 行、291 字，馆方条目记周康王时期、通高 101.9 厘米，1959 年入藏。出土地两说并列：[“盂”青铜鼎](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/csp/202008/t20200826_247375.shtml) 一页记郿县礼村，[“盂”青铜鼎](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/csp/202008/t20200826_247375.shtml) 另一页作岐山，馆方不同页面即不一致，本页不取舍。铭文「二十三年」据小盂鼎推定为康王，鼎铭未书王名，待考。

[宝博微课堂｜周原的世家大族——微氏家族（上）](https://www.bjqtm.com/?p=5143) 记 1976 年扶风庄白一号窖藏、墙盘内底铸铭 284 字、墙为微氏第六代史官；[史墙盘](https://cssn.cn/skgz/bwyc/202212/t20221228_5574599.shtml) 另记通高 16.2 厘米、口径 47.3 厘米，现藏宝鸡青铜器博物院。恭王为学界据世系推定，铭文未书王名，微氏高祖封地有异说。[秦石鼓](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234438.html) 著录十件鼓形石刻篆书诗篇，年代有争议；[明拓战国石鼓文册](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228598.html) 则标注原鼓所在。

[湖北简牍地理，一窥湖北简牍之风采！](https://m-www.hbww.org.cn/p/8892.html) 记睡虎地秦简 1975 年云梦睡虎地出土、竹简 1155 枚（另残片 80 片）、长 23.1—27.8 厘米、字体秦隶、湖北省博物馆藏。墨书笔画短直、结体扁方带篆意，处篆隶转变关口。各篇抄写年代不一，字数与墓主「喜」的职务，馆方该页未展开，待考。

[国际博物馆日｜解锁兰州这些博物馆的“镇馆之宝”](https://m.thepaper.cn/wap/v3/jsp/newsDetail_forward_12738181) 记居延新简·隧长侯永死驹劾状，16 简、可释读 409 字、全篇章草。简主名有「焦永」「侯永」两录文，题名不取其一；十六简编连依整理本，原编绳已朽，待考。收笔带燕尾，字字独立而气脉贯通。

东汉碑版一节：五凤刻石留五凤二年（前 56 年）纪年，石为灰岩，书体馆方未记；石门颂（148）、礼器碑（156）、曹全碑（185）、张迁碑（186）皆隶书。以 [湖北简牍地理，一窥湖北简牍之风采！](https://m-www.hbww.org.cn/p/8892.html) 所记墨书秦隶与诸碑并置，见笔势由简牍入石。石门颂原在崖壁后移藏汉中市博物馆，曹全碑藏西安碑林而执笔者未署名；四碑英文题名皆本项目为检索所拟。

1. **[《大盂鼎》](../works/dayu-ding.md)**：看 19 行 291 字铸铭的字形与整饬行气。
2. **[《史墙盘》](../works/shiqiang-pan.md)**：看内底 18 行 284 字铭文排列的整饬。
3. **[《石鼓文》](../works/stone-drum-inscriptions.md)**：看十件鼓形石上的篆书诗篇与确切年代之争议。
4. **[《睡虎地秦简》](../works/shuihudi-qinjian.md)**：看墨书秦隶短直笔画与扁方结体。
5. **[《居延新简·隧长焦永死驹劾状》](../works/juyan-hanjian.md)**：看 16 简章草的燕尾收笔与字字独立。
6. **[《五凤刻石》](../works/wufeng-inscription.md)**：看灰岩上留存的五凤二年纪年刻辞。
7. **[《石门颂》](../works/shimen-song.md)**：看 148 年刻辞原在崖壁、后移藏的经过。
8. **[《礼器碑》](../works/liqi-bei.md)**：看 156 年记修孔庙、铸礼器一事的隶书。
9. **[《曹全碑》](../works/cao-quan-bei.md)**：看 185 年碑留受颂者姓名而未署执笔者。
10. **[《张迁碑》](../works/zhangqian-bei.md)**：看 186 年为县令张迁所立碑的隶书字形。

本页的编排依青铜铸款、石鼓、简牍日常书写、东汉碑版隶书分化依次展开，是阅读顺序的编辑选择，不写成历史因果。[秦石鼓](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234438.html) 的确切年代与目录不同断代、五凤刻石书体、各碑英文题名，并列作待考，不补确定答案；两说处两存而不取舍。 续读：[专题 01](../notes/essay-01-pre-qin.md)、[专题 02](../notes/essay-02-qin-han.md)。

## English

The [“盂”青铜鼎](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/csp/202008/t20200826_247375.shtml) bears a 19-line, 291-character inscription cast inside its belly; the museum entry dates it to King Kang's reign, gives a height of 101.9 cm, and records 1959 accession. Two findspots stand side by side: one page of the [“盂”青铜鼎](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/csp/202008/t20200826_247375.shtml) gives Meixian Licun, another [“盂”青铜鼎](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/csp/202008/t20200826_247375.shtml) page gives Qishan, so this page chooses neither. The "twenty-third year" is inferred as King Kang from the Small Yu Ding, the inscription naming no king; left open.

[宝博微课堂｜周原的世家大族——微氏家族（上）](https://www.bjqtm.com/?p=5143) records the 1976 Zhuangbai No. 1 hoard at Fufeng, a 284-character inscription on the inner bottom, and Qiang as sixth-generation scribe of the Wei; [史墙盘](https://cssn.cn/skgz/bwyc/202212/t20221228_5574599.shtml) adds a height of 16.2 cm, a diameter of 47.3 cm, and the Baoji holding. King Gong is inferred from the genealogy, no king is named, and the Wei ancestor's fief is disputed. [秦石鼓](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234438.html) catalogues ten drum-shaped stones with seal-script poems, its date contested; [明拓战国石鼓文册](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228598.html) marks where the original drums are.

[湖北简牍地理，一窥湖北简牍之风采！](https://m-www.hbww.org.cn/p/8892.html) records the Shuihudi Qin Slips, unearthed at Yunmeng Shuihudi in 1975, 1155 slips (plus 80 fragments), 23.1–27.8 cm long, in Qin clerical script, held by Hubei Provincial Museum. The ink hand has short straight strokes and a squat structure tinged with seal script, at the turn from seal to clerical. Copying dates differ, and the character count and the occupant Xi's office are not set out, left open.

[国际博物馆日｜解锁兰州这些博物馆的“镇馆之宝”](https://m.thepaper.cn/wap/v3/jsp/newsDetail_forward_12738181) records the Juyan document on the death of a post-station horse, 16 slips, 409 legible characters, entirely in draft cursive. The name is read as Jiao Yong or Hou Yong, and the title adopts neither; the 16 slips follow an edited order, the binding cords rotted, left open. The finishes carry a swallow-tail, each character separate yet the breath running through.

In the Eastern Han stelae section, the Wufeng Inscription keeps a Wufeng second-year (56 BCE) date on grey limestone, its script not recorded; the Eulogy of Shimen (148), Ritual Vessels (156), Cao Quan (185), and Zhang Qian (186) stelae are all clerical. Setting the Qin clerical hand noted in [湖北简牍地理，一窥湖北简牍之风采！](https://m-www.hbww.org.cn/p/8892.html) beside them shows the brush passing from slip to stone. The Shimen text was cut from a cliff and moved to Hanzhong Museum, the Cao Quan stele held at Xi'an Beilin with no writer signed; the four English titles are coined by this project for retrieval.

1. **[Da Yu Ding, the Great Yu Cauldron](../works/dayu-ding.md)**: Look at the character forms and even spacing of the 19-line, 291-character cast inscription.
2. **[Shi Qiang Pan, the Qiang Basin](../works/shiqiang-pan.md)**: Look at the ordered layout of the 18-line, 284-character inscription on the inner bottom.
3. **[Stone Drum Inscriptions](../works/stone-drum-inscriptions.md)**: Look at the ten drums' seal-script poems and the contested date.
4. **[Shuihudi Qin Slips](../works/shuihudi-qinjian.md)**: Look at the short straight strokes of the ink-written Qin clerical.
5. **[Juyan document on the death of a post-station horse](../works/juyan-hanjian.md)**: Look at the swallow-tail finishes of the 16 draft-cursive slips on the horse document.
6. **[Wufeng Inscription](../works/wufeng-inscription.md)**: Look at the Wufeng second-year date cut on the grey limestone.
7. **[Eulogy of Shimen](../works/shimen-song.md)**: Look at the 148 inscription cut from a cliff and later moved.
8. **[Stele of the Ritual Vessels](../works/liqi-bei.md)**: Look at the 156 clerical account of temple repair and vessels cast.
9. **[Stele of Cao Quan](../works/cao-quan-bei.md)**: Look at the 185 stele naming the praised man but not the writer.
10. **[Stele of Zhang Qian](../works/zhangqian-bei.md)**: Look at the 186 clerical stele raised for magistrate Zhang Qian.

This page arranges bronze cast inscriptions, the stone drums, everyday writing on slips, and Eastern Han stele clerical script in sequence, an editorial choice of reading order, not a historical cause. The exact date of [秦石鼓](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234438.html) and the catalogue's competing dates, the Wufeng script, and the stelae's English titles remain open questions; no firm answer is added, and two readings are kept without choosing.
