# 06｜专题：宋代——刻帖、墨迹与士人书

## 中文

[《淳化阁帖》](../works/chunhua-getie.md)读的是一册宋拓：故宫[宋拓懋勤殿本淳化阁帖册](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/261938.html)条目为「懋勤殿本」，淳化三年王著奉敕编次内府墨迹、摹刻十卷，「简介」第一、二段记此，临王羲之从卷六读起。作品页未记书体，本页不补。把刻帖列在首位是本页的编排，不是说它先于其余各件。

[《自书诗卷》](../works/cai-zishu.md)所收为[蔡襄](../people/cai-xiang.md)行书：皇祐二年自福州北行，沿途所见皆成诗，卷中十一首，故宫[蔡襄行书自书诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228526.html)推测写于诗成后不久，列尺寸、行数字数、十三家题跋、欧阳修批语、朱文钧跋语。后人刻入《秋碧堂》等法帖，诗稿遂成案头临本。作品页系北宋，书体行书。

[《治平帖》](../works/zhiping-tie.md)是一封信，不是诗稿。[苏轼](../people/su-shi.md)写给治平史院主与徐大师，问法眷安好、佛阁是否建成、念经度得几人徒弟，末托石头桥、堋头两处坟茔照管，署「八月十八日」。故宫[苏轼行书治平帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231008.html)记纸本行书、纵 29.2 厘米、横 45.2 厘米，据赵孟頫、文徵明、王穉登三跋定为熙宁年间京师作。全篇不谈书法；帖无年款，不填年份。

[《黄州寒食诗帖》](../works/hanshi.md)同出苏轼，却是诗稿而非信：北宋苏轼自书《黄州寒食诗》，行书，年代未记。國立故宮博物院[黄州寒食诗帖](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/14714?dep=P)条目作品说明记颜韵伯售予菊池惺堂一段，只记此条，不补尺寸与递藏。信与诗稿并置，见同一人笔下两种用途；本页的编排不把「一封信」读成「一件诗稿」，也不替两件排先后。

[《松风阁诗》](../works/songfeng.md)为[黄庭坚](../people/huang-tingjian.md)行书诗帖，北宋、行书、帖，作品页年代未记；國立故宮博物院[松风阁诗](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/43?dep=P)条目有「收传印记及鉴藏宝玺」栏。字数 150 与 153 两说，待考两存；尺寸、行数与跋语姓名未据著录核对，不补。[《蜀素帖》](../works/shusu.md)系元祐三年（1088），[米芾](../people/mi-fu.md)书、行书，见[蜀素帖](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/912?dep=P)条目藏品题名及作品说明。

[《诸上座帖》](../works/zhushangzuo-tie.md)是[黄庭坚](../people/huang-tingjian.md)草书纸本手卷，九十二行、纵 33 厘米、横 729.5 厘米，为李任道录五代僧人文益《语录》，全篇禅语，署「山谷老人书」。故宫[黄庭坚草书诸上座帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228218.html)记吴宽、梁清标二跋，说此卷师法怀素，卷后另有大字行楷自识，草楷并见，馆方称「晚年杰作」；递藏自南宋内府、贾似道，历明清至乾隆内府，民国归张伯驹后捐献国家。年份不详，馆方不系年。

[《双松图歌》](../works/shuangsong-tuge.md)是[张即之](../people/zhang-jizhi.md)行书绢本手卷，纵 33.8 厘米、横 1196 厘米，七十二行、一百四十四字，书杜甫《戏为双松图歌韦偃画》。故宫[张即之行书双松图歌卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228481.html)录款署「张即之七十二岁写」，馆方据此推为南宋宝祐五年（1257），注卷前苏轼古柏图非真迹，尾纸有明洪武陈新、夏彦良二跋，《寓意录》《石渠宝笈·初编》著录。1257 为推年、未据生年复核；古柏图作者、年代未记。

1. **[《淳化阁帖》](../works/chunhua-getie.md)**：看懋勤殿本宋拓，王著摹刻内府墨迹十卷，卷六可临王羲之。
2. **[《自书诗卷》](../works/cai-zishu.md)**：看蔡襄皇祐二年北行十一首，后被刻入《秋碧堂》等法帖。
3. **[《治平帖》](../works/zhiping-tie.md)**：看一封信：问佛阁、托坟茔，署八月十八日，全篇不谈书法。
4. **[《黄州寒食诗帖》](../works/hanshi.md)**：看苏轼自书的诗稿，与前一封信分作两种用途，勿混。
5. **[《松风阁诗》](../works/songfeng.md)**：看黄庭坚行书诗帖，字数 150 与 153 两说并存。
6. **[《蜀素帖》](../works/shusu.md)**：看元祐三年米芾行书，条目记藏品题名及作品说明。
7. **[《诸上座帖》](../works/zhushangzuo-tie.md)**：看九十二行草书《语录》，草书与行楷并见，馆方称晚年杰作。
8. **[《双松图歌》](../works/shuangsong-tuge.md)**：看张即之七十二岁款，馆方据此推 1257，古柏图非真迹。

待考留着：故宫[苏轼行书治平帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231008.html)记年份由三跋推得、帖无年款，引首画像、像赞只说明人所画、释东皋妙声所书，姓名年份未记；松风阁诗字数 150 与 153 两说并存、不取舍；诸上座帖年份不详、点去十六字未计、装裱是否原装未说明；双松图歌 1257 为据自识推年、未据生年复核。本页的编排按刻帖、信与诗稿、再到蔡襄至张即之诸件排列，为编辑所设次序，非历史因果，不替待考补确定答案。 续读：[路径 11](../notes/path-03-running-cursive.md)、[路径 13](../notes/path-05-modelbook.md)。

## English

[Chunhua Modelbooks](../works/chunhua-getie.md) are read in a Song rubbing: the Palace Museum entry [宋拓懋勤殿本淳化阁帖册](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/261938.html) is the Maoqin Hall edition, ordered in Chunhua 3, when Wang Zhu had the imperial ink works cut in ten juan — its first two '简介' paragraphs say so, and Wang Xizhi begins at juan six. No script is given; this page fills nothing. Placing it first is this page's arrangement, not a claim of priority.

[Scroll of Poems in the Author's Hand](../works/cai-zishu.md) is [Cai Xiang (蔡襄)](../people/cai-xiang.md)'s running script: in Huangyou 2 he went north from Fuzhou and made eleven poems; the Palace Museum [蔡襄行书自书诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228526.html) surmises they were written soon after, listing dimensions, lines, characters, thirteen colophons, Ouyang Xiu's note and Zhu Wenjun's colophon. Later hands cut them into modelbooks like Qiubitang; a travel draft became a desk copy. The work page gives Northern Song, running script.

[Letter to the Zhipping Monastery](../works/zhiping-tie.md) is a letter, not a poem draft. [Su Shi (苏轼)](../people/su-shi.md) wrote to the monastery's director and Master Xu, asking after health, the Buddha hall and ordained disciples, then entrusting two graves at Shitouqiao and Pengtou, signed 'the eighteenth of the eighth month'. The Palace Museum [苏轼行书治平帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231008.html) records a paper running-script letter, 29.2 × 45.2 cm, dated by colophons of Zhao Mengfu, Wen Zhengming and Wang Zhideng to the Xining years in the capital. Not a line discusses calligraphy; no year is filled.

[Cold Food Poems](../works/hanshi.md) is by the same Su Shi but a poem draft, not a letter: his running-script Huangzhou Cold Food poems, Northern Song, undated. The National Palace Museum entry [黄州寒食诗帖](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/14714?dep=P) notes a sale by Yan Yunbo to Juchi Xingtang; this page records only that, adding no size or provenance. The pair shows two uses of one hand; this page's arrangement reads neither as the other, nor ranks them.

[Poem on the Hall of Wind and Pines](../works/songfeng.md) is [Huang Tingjian (黄庭坚)](../people/huang-tingjian.md)'s running-script poem, filed Northern Song, running script, a tie, undated on the work page; the National Palace Museum [松风阁诗](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/43?dep=P) entry has a '收传印记及鉴藏宝玺' column. The count has two accounts, 150 and 153, both kept; dimensions, lines and colophon names unchecked, not added. [Poems on Shu Silk](../works/shusu.md), dated Yuanyou 3 (1088), is [Mi Fu (米芾)](../people/mi-fu.md)'s running script, per the [蜀素帖](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/912?dep=P) entry.

[Discourse to the Venerable Ones](../works/zhushangzuo-tie.md) is [Huang Tingjian (黄庭坚)](../people/huang-tingjian.md)'s cursive handscroll on paper, ninety-two lines, 33 × 729.5 cm, copying for Li Rendao the monk Wenyi's Recorded Sayings, all Chan talk, signed '山谷老人书'. The Palace Museum [黄庭坚草书诸上座帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228218.html) notes Wu Kuan and Liang Qingbiao colophons, says it follows Huaisu, and records a large running-regular self-inscription beside the cursive, calling it a '晚年杰作'. Custody runs from the Southern Song court and Jia Sidao through Ming and Qing to Qianlong's court, then to Zhang Boju, who donated it. No writing year is given.

[Song of the Twin Pines](../works/shuangsong-tuge.md) is [Zhang Jizhi (张即之)](../people/zhang-jizhi.md)'s running-script handscroll on silk, 33.8 × 1196 cm, seventy-two lines, 144 characters, writing Du Fu's '戏为双松图歌韦偃画'. The Palace Museum [张即之行书双松图歌卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228481.html) records the signature '张即之七十二岁写', infers Southern Song Baoyou 5 (1257) from it, notes the cypress painting before the scroll is not genuine, and lists Hongwu colophons by Chen Xin and Xia Yanliang plus cataloguing in Yuyi Lu and Shiqu Baoji. The museum says he follows Tang models, tightly structured and forceful; 1257 is inferred, unchecked against his birth year, and the painting's maker and date are unrecorded.

1. **[Chunhua Modelbooks](../works/chunhua-getie.md)**: Look at the Maoqin Hall Song rubbing, ten juan from court ink works; juan six for Wang Xizhi.
2. **[Scroll of Poems in the Author's Hand](../works/cai-zishu.md)**: Look at Cai Xiang's eleven travel poems, later cut into Qiubitang modelbooks.
3. **[Letter to the Zhipping Monastery](../works/zhiping-tie.md)**: Look at a letter: a Buddha hall, two graves, signed the eighteenth; no word on calligraphy.
4. **[Cold Food Poems](../works/hanshi.md)**: Look at Su Shi's poem draft, distinct from the letter's use.
5. **[Poem on the Hall of Wind and Pines](../works/songfeng.md)**: Look at Huang Tingjian's running-script poem; the 150 and 153 counts stand.
6. **[Poems on Shu Silk](../works/shusu.md)**: Look at Mi Fu's 1088 running script, per the entry.
7. **[Discourse to the Venerable Ones](../works/zhushangzuo-tie.md)**: Look at ninety-two lines of cursive Chan talk, with a running-regular self-inscription.
8. **[Song of the Twin Pines](../works/shuangsong-tuge.md)**: Look at the 'seventy-two' signature, the inferred 1257, and the non-genuine cypress painting.

Open points: the Palace Museum [苏轼行书治平帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231008.html) dates the letter from three colophons, the text undated, the frontispiece portrait and eulogy only as by a Ming hand and the monk Miaosheng, no names or years. Songfeng's 150 and 153 stand side by side, no choice; Zhushangzuo's year is unknown, its deleted characters uncounted, the mounting unstated as to original; Shuangsong's 1257 is inferred from a self-note, unchecked against his birth year. This page arranges modelbook, letter, poem draft, the Cai Xiang to Zhang Jizhi pieces as an editorial order, not a historical cause, no answer.
