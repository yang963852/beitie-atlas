# 05｜专题：隋唐——楷则与碑版

## 中文

[《龙藏寺碑》](../works/longzang-si-bei.md)碑阳楷书三十行、行五十字，碑额浮雕六龙相交，原石仍在正定隆兴寺内。[正定大佛寺存宋代转轮藏 仍能正常转动](https://culture.people.com.cn/BIG5/n/2014/0813/c22219-25458469.html)「隋碑第一 开启唐楷先河」一节系于隋开皇六年（586），记碑面 1.56 米 × 0.89 米、碑额 0.42 米，引欧阳修、康有为；「隋碑第一」为该文之称，本页照录、不作等级判断。八十年代垫高地基、加装防护罩，页面未记年份。

[《董美人墓志》](../works/dong-meiren.md)收在故宫[董美人墓志（清拓本）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234149.html)条目，所收为清拓本、楷书；作品页未记年代，本页亦不补。本页的编排把它与龙藏寺碑并置为隋代一组，为并读两件隋楷而设，并非断定二者之间有师承或先后。原石与拓本各是一层，此处读的是拓本纸上的字。

[《九成宫醴泉铭》](../works/jiuchenggong-liquan-ming.md)作品页系于唐贞观六年（632）：魏徵撰铭、颂辞寄规谏，[欧阳询](../people/ouyang-xun.md)书丹。[对市十六届人民代表大会六次会议第216号建议的答复函](https://wwj.baoji.gov.cn/col1892/col9955/202608/t20260821_1295837.html)「基本情况」一节只记隋仁寿宫·唐九成宫遗址内存此碑与万年宫铭碑，未记碑石年代、尺寸、现状，撰书年份亦未载。作品页的 632 与该页「未记」两存，本页不据一方补另一方。

[《雁塔圣教序》](../works/yanta.md)为唐[褚遂良](../people/zhu-suiliang.md)所书，故宫[雁塔圣教序（慈恩寺圣教序拓本）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234134.html)条目收慈恩寺圣教序拓本，楷书；作品页未记年代，本页不补。把九成宫与雁塔排在一组是本页的编排，为并读唐初两件楷书，不是书风演变的因果；一件据遗址著录、一件据拓本，本页不替二者统一来历。

[颜真卿](../people/yan-zhenqing.md)一人两见。[《多宝塔感应碑》](../works/duobao-ta-bei.md)系天宝十一载（752），为千福寺多宝塔书碑，今藏西安碑林；馆方[国宝名碑 · 多宝塔感应碑](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=147)条目记尺寸与北宋崇宁二年移存，未给藏品号、入藏经过。[《颜勤礼碑》](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)系大历十四年（779），撰文并书；[颜勤礼碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2582&c=View&m=home)页记 1922 年旧巡抚署修缮时出土、1948 年移存碑林，来源未记书体，该字段从缺，不以常识填补。

[《玄秘塔碑》](../works/xuanmi-ta-bei.md)系会昌元年（841），裴休撰文，[柳公权](../people/liu-gongquan.md)书丹并篆额。西安碑林[国宝名碑 · 玄秘塔碑](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=148)条目记尺寸、行数与「柳公权书并篆额」，未记出土或 1949 年后存藏；故宫另藏明初拓册，石与纸各有来历。多宝塔、颜勤礼、玄秘塔三件的英文题名为本项目所拟，非来源题名，列为待考。

[《道因法师碑》](../works/daoyin-fashi-bei.md)的书者须与欧阳询分开：唐龙朔三年（663）十月镌立，李俨撰文，书者是[欧阳通](../people/ouyang-tong.md)，欧阳询之子，父子号「大小欧阳」。[道因法师碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2598)馆方页记螭首龟趺、通高 312 厘米、北宋移存西安碑林，录王世贞「眉宇间有风霜之气」与康有为「遒密峻整」评语，龟趺两侧线刻胡人，馆方称为精绝。碑宽、厚与行款未记，迁置经过未记。

1. **[《龙藏寺碑》](../works/longzang-si-bei.md)**：看碑阳三十行、行五十字与碑额六龙，高度两说并存。
2. **[《董美人墓志》](../works/dong-meiren.md)**：看故宫所收清拓本，楷书，年代作品页未记、不补。
3. **[《九成宫醴泉铭》](../works/jiuchenggong-liquan-ming.md)**：看魏徵撰、欧阳询书，答复函未记年代尺寸现状。
4. **[《雁塔圣教序》](../works/yanta.md)**：看慈恩寺圣教序拓本，褚遂良书，年代从缺。
5. **[《多宝塔感应碑》](../works/duobao-ta-bei.md)**：看天宝十一载颜真卿书碑，馆方记崇宁二年移存。
6. **[《颜勤礼碑》](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)**：看 1922 年出土、1948 年移存碑林，书体一栏从缺。
7. **[《玄秘塔碑》](../works/xuanmi-ta-bei.md)**：看柳公权书并篆额，条目记尺寸行数、未记出土。
8. **[《道因法师碑》](../works/daoyin-fashi-bei.md)**：看书者为欧阳通而非欧阳询，螭首龟趺、北宋移存。

待考留着：[正定大佛寺存宋代转轮藏 仍能正常转动](https://culture.people.com.cn/BIG5/n/2014/0813/c22219-25458469.html)记碑面 1.56 米 × 0.89 米、碑额 0.42 米，作品页待考另记通高 3.24 米，两者所量部位不同、无法校核，本页两说并存，不取平均、不作推测；八十年代维修未记年份，颜勤礼碑书体从缺；道因碑宽厚行款、迁置经过与欧阳通生年复核未记。本页的编排把隋两件、唐初两件、中晚唐四件依次排列，是编辑所设的并读次序，非历史因果，不替待考补确定答案。 续读：[专题 04](../notes/essay-04-northern-southern.md)、[路径 10](../notes/path-02-regularization.md)。

## English

[Stele of the Longzang Temple](../works/longzang-si-bei.md) has a regular-script face of thirty lines of fifty characters and a six-dragon heading; the stone remains in Longxing Temple at Zhengding. [正定大佛寺存宋代转轮藏 仍能正常转动](https://culture.people.com.cn/BIG5/n/2014/0813/c22219-25458469.html), in its section '隋碑第一 开启唐楷先河', dates it to Sui Kaihuang 6 (586), gives a 1.56 m × 0.89 m face and 0.42 m heading, and quotes Ouyang Xiu and Kang Youwei; '隋碑第一' is the article's phrase, copied without a ranking judgment. The 1980s works lack an exact year.

[Epitaph of Dong Meiren](../works/dong-meiren.md) is in the Palace Museum entry [董美人墓志（清拓本）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234149.html), a Qing rubbing in regular script; the work page records no date and this page fills none. This page arranges it beside the Longzang Temple stele as a Sui pair, set for reading two Sui regular-script pieces together, not a claim of lineage or priority. Original stone and rubbing are two layers; read here is the rubbing.

[Sweet Spring at Jiucheng Palace](../works/jiuchenggong-liquan-ming.md) is dated on the work page to Tang Zhenguan 6 (632): Wei Zheng composed the inscription with admonition, [Ouyang Xun (欧阳询)](../people/ouyang-xun.md) writing it out. [对市十六届人民代表大会六次会议第216号建议的答复函](https://wwj.baoji.gov.cn/col1892/col9955/202608/t20260821_1295837.html), in section '基本情况', records only that this stele and the Wannian Palace stele survive within the Sui Renshou Palace / Tang Jiucheng Palace site, with no date, size, condition or year of composition. The page's 632 and the reply's silence stand side by side; neither fills the other.

[Wild Goose Pagoda Preface](../works/yanta.md) was written by [Chu Suiliang (褚遂良)](../people/zhu-suiliang.md) of the Tang; the Palace Museum entry [雁塔圣教序（慈恩寺圣教序拓本）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234134.html) holds the Ci'en Temple preface rubbing, in regular script, undated on the work page. Setting Jiucheng Palace and Wild Goose Pagoda together is this page's arrangement, for reading two early-Tang regular-script works side by side, not a cause of stylistic change; one rests on a site record, the other on a rubbing, their histories not merged here.

[Yan Zhenqing (颜真卿)](../people/yan-zhenqing.md) appears twice. [Duobao Pagoda Stele](../works/duobao-ta-bei.md), dated Tianbao 11 (752), was written for Qianfu Temple's pagoda and is now in Xi'an Beilin; [国宝名碑 · 多宝塔感应碑](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=147) gives dimensions and a Northern Song Chongning 2 transfer, no shelfmark or accession history. [Stele of Yan Qinli](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md), dated Dali 14 (779), he composed and wrote; [颜勤礼碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2582&c=View&m=home) records its 1922 excavation and 1948 transfer to Beilin. The source gives no script, so the field is blank, not filled by common knowledge.

[Xuanmi Pagoda Stele](../works/xuanmi-ta-bei.md) is dated Huichang 1 (841): Pei Xiu composed the text, [Liu Gongquan (柳公权)](../people/liu-gongquan.md) wrote it out and titled the heading in seal script. The Xi'an Beilin entry [国宝名碑 · 玄秘塔碑](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=148) gives dimensions, line count and '柳公权书并篆额', but no excavation or post-1949 storage; the Palace Museum holds an early-Ming rubbing album, stone and paper each with their own history. The English titles of the Duobao, Yan Qinli and Xuanmi steles are this project's labels, not the sources' titles, left as open.

[Stele of the Dharma Master Daoyin](../works/daoyin-fashi-bei.md) must be kept apart from Ouyang Xun: erected in the tenth month of Tang Longshuo 3 (663), Li Yan composing the text, its writer is [Ouyang Tong (欧阳通)](../people/ouyang-tong.md), Ouyang Xun's son, the two called '大小欧阳'. The museum page [道因法师碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2598) records a chi-head and turtle base, a total height of 312 cm and a Northern Song transfer to Xi'an Beilin, quotes Wang Shizhen's '眉宇间有风霜之气' and Kang Youwei's '遒密峻整', and calls the incised foreign figures on the turtle's sides exquisite. Width, thickness, line arrangement and transfer steps are unrecorded.

1. **[Stele of the Longzang Temple](../works/longzang-si-bei.md)**: Look at thirty lines of fifty characters, the six-dragon heading, and two height accounts.
2. **[Epitaph of Dong Meiren](../works/dong-meiren.md)**: Look at the Palace Museum's Qing rubbing; the date is unrecorded and not filled.
3. **[Sweet Spring at Jiucheng Palace](../works/jiuchenggong-liquan-ming.md)**: Look at Wei Zheng's text and Ouyang Xun's hand; the reply gives no date.
4. **[Wild Goose Pagoda Preface](../works/yanta.md)**: Look at the Ci'en Temple rubbing by Chu Suiliang; the date is left open.
5. **[Duobao Pagoda Stele](../works/duobao-ta-bei.md)**: Look at Yan Zhenqing's 752 stele and the Northern Song transfer noted by the museum.
6. **[Stele of Yan Qinli](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)**: Look at the 1922 excavation and 1948 transfer; the script field is blank.
7. **[Xuanmi Pagoda Stele](../works/xuanmi-ta-bei.md)**: Look at Liu Gongquan's hand and seal heading; no excavation is recorded.
8. **[Stele of the Dharma Master Daoyin](../works/daoyin-fashi-bei.md)**: Look at Ouyang Tong, not Ouyang Xun, as the writer, and the Song transfer.

The open points stay open: [正定大佛寺存宋代转轮藏 仍能正常转动](https://culture.people.com.cn/BIG5/n/2014/0813/c22219-25458469.html) gives a 1.56 m × 0.89 m face and 0.42 m heading, while the work page's notes give another total height of 3.24 m; the two measure different parts and cannot be checked against each other, so both stand, without averaging or inference. The 1980s repairs lack a year, Yan Qinli's script is blank, and Daoyin's width, transfer steps and Ouyang Tong's birth-year check are unrecorded. This page arranges two Sui, two early-Tang and four later-Tang pieces as an editorial order, not a historical cause, with no certain answer.
