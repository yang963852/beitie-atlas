# 14｜阅读路径：一作两命——原石与拓本

## 中文

[宋拓定武兰亭序](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/233468.html)记拓本新00135367、拓纸与翻刻特征、题跋及木匣；[冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)记唐摹神龙本、传冯承素摹，尤重「其流传经过」段——馆藏号与拓纸是可核之项。本页把[神龙本摹本](../objects/witness-lanting.md)与[定武本拓本](../objects/dingwu-zhan.md)并看：前者摹本、后者拓本，两者不同，递藏各归本条。作品层为[《兰亭序》](../works/lanting.md)，实物层为这两件，分两层写，不混用。

[暑期攻略｜阅览碑刻 品读千年石墨精华](https://www.confuciusmuseum.cn/news/wb/2023-07-23/2630.html)逐碑列礼器碑藏所、年代与行数，东汉永寿二年（156年）隶书；[孔庙三碑，惊艳了时光](https://www.sdxc.gov.cn/whql/whcc/202508/t20250822_16374659.htm)记其 1978 年由同文门移东庑、1998 年入曲阜汉魏碑刻陈列馆。迁移与藏所属[礼器碑原石](../objects/stele-liqi.md)一件，不复制给[张伯英题签明拓本](../objects/liqi-ming-44512.md)：后者为明拓、馆藏号新00044512，其递藏与来历来源未记，写「馆方未记」。作品层[《礼器碑》](../works/liqi-bei.md)与两件实物分列。

[北魏•张猛龙碑（魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑）](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/wei/2016-04-03/2864.html)记正光三年（522年）正书、颂治郡兴学，是[张猛龙碑原石](../objects/stele-zhang-menglong-bei.md)著录；[张猛龙碑明拓本](../objects/rubbing-zhang-menglong-bei.md)馆藏号新00045763，递藏来源未记，写「馆方未记」。本页的编排把原石与拓本成对排，不把前者年代、行数挪给后者。[《颜勤礼碑》](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)为唐大历十四年（779年）作品，另见下段。

[颜勤礼碑（民国拓本，文物号 新00045406）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234175.html)是民国拓本（31.8 × 37.7 厘米）而非石，「简介」段另记原石出土处与藏所；[颜勤礼碑再造了一个“碑林”](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2440&c=View&m=home)为 2007 年馆方网文，记 1922、1927—1928、1948 年迁移，不核今日存藏。故[原石](../objects/yan-qinli-original-stele.md)的 1922 年西安出土、后藏西安碑林，与[民国拓本](../objects/dpm-rubbing-xin00045406.md)的纸幅各归本件：拓本馆藏号新00045406，原石馆藏号馆方未记，递传须另案考察，不互串。

本页的编排把[《晋祠之铭并序》](../works/jinci-zhi-ming.md)两件实物并立，不写成相因：作品页系于唐贞观二十年（646），唐太宗以行书入石、碑阴留七臣署名，来源讨论碑文内容、未细述书法流传。[唐碑](../objects/stele-jinci-ming.md)为原石、馆藏号馆方未记；[1772 摹刻](../objects/stele-jinci-ming-1772.md)为清乾隆摹刻、馆藏号亦馆方未记。两件各记本条所给；[北魏•张猛龙碑（魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑）](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/wei/2016-04-03/2864.html)一类的曲阜著录，不补这里的未记。

1. **[《兰亭序》](../works/lanting.md)**：看[神龙本摹本](../objects/witness-lanting.md)与[定武本拓本](../objects/dingwu-zhan.md)，一摹一拓，两途不同。
2. **[《礼器碑》](../works/liqi-bei.md)**：看[原石](../objects/stele-liqi.md)与[明拓本](../objects/liqi-ming-44512.md)，两件实物各自分记。
3. **[《张猛龙碑》](../works/zhang-menglong-bei.md)**：看[原石](../objects/stele-zhang-menglong-bei.md)与[明拓本](../objects/rubbing-zhang-menglong-bei.md)，年代彼此不互挪。
4. **[《颜勤礼碑》](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)**：看[原石](../objects/yan-qinli-original-stele.md)与[民国拓本](../objects/dpm-rubbing-xin00045406.md)，各归本件所记。
5. **[《晋祠之铭并序》](../works/jinci-zhi-ming.md)**：看[唐碑](../objects/stele-jinci-ming.md)与[1772 摹刻](../objects/stele-jinci-ming-1772.md)并立，馆藏号均馆方未记。

本页的编排以「一作两命」把五组实物并置，顺序是编辑选择，不补定论。待考：兰亭两件递藏各归本条、摹本与拓本不互串；两件明拓本递藏来源未记，写「馆方未记」；颜勤礼碑原石馆藏号未记、民国拓本递传须另案考察；晋祠铭两件馆藏号均馆方未记；[冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)「其流传经过」段只及摹本一层。作品层与实物层分列，不并成一件。 续读：[专题 08](../notes/essay-08-qing-modern.md)、[路径 13](../notes/path-05-modelbook.md)。

## English

[宋拓定武兰亭序](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/233468.html) records the rubbing 新00135367, paper, recarving traits, colophons and wooden box; [冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html) records a Tang tracing, the Shenlong version, attributed to Feng Chengsu, especially its "transmission history"—accession and paper checkable. This page arranges the [Shenlong tracing](../objects/witness-lanting.md) and the [Dingwu rubbing](../objects/dingwu-zhan.md) together: one a tracing, one a rubbing, different, transmission kept to each entry. The work layer is the [Preface to the Orchid Pavilion](../works/lanting.md), the object layer these two, not mixed.

[暑期攻略｜阅览碑刻 品读千年石墨精华](https://www.confuciusmuseum.cn/news/wb/2023-07-23/2630.html) lists the Ritual Vessels Stele's holding, date, line count, a clerical stele of 156; [孔庙三碑，惊艳了时光](https://www.sdxc.gov.cn/whql/whcc/202508/t20250822_16374659.htm) records a 1978 move from Tongwen Gate to the east side hall, 1998 into the Qufu Han and Wei Stele Gallery. Both belong to the [original stone](../objects/stele-liqi.md), not copied to the [Ming rubbing with Zhang Boying's label](../objects/liqi-ming-44512.md): a Ming rubbing, accession 新00044512, transmission and provenance unrecorded, written "not recorded by the museum". The [Stele of the Ritual Vessels](../works/liqi-bei.md) layer and two objects listed apart.

[北魏•张猛龙碑（魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑）](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/wei/2016-04-03/2864.html) records a 522 regular-script stele praising governance and schools, the catalogue of the [original stone](../objects/stele-zhang-menglong-bei.md); the [Ming rubbing](../objects/rubbing-zhang-menglong-bei.md) has accession 新00045763, transmission unrecorded, written "not recorded by the museum". This page arranges stone and rubbing in pairs without shifting the former's date or line count to the latter. The [Stele of Yan Qinli](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md), a work of 779, is taken up below.

[颜勤礼碑（民国拓本，文物号 新00045406）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234175.html) is a Republican-era rubbing (31.8 × 37.7 cm), not the stone; its "introduction" records where the stone was unearthed and held. [颜勤礼碑再造了一个“碑林”](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2440&c=View&m=home) is a 2007 museum web article recording moves in 1922, 1927—1928 and 1948, not verifying present storage. So the 1922 Xi'an unearthing and later holding at Xi'an Beilin of the [original stone](../objects/yan-qinli-original-stele.md), and the paper size of the [Republican-era rubbing](../objects/dpm-rubbing-xin00045406.md), each stay with its object: the rubbing has accession 新00045406, the stone's accession is unrecorded, transmission needing separate study, not conflated.

This page arranges the two objects of the [Inscription for the Jin Shrine](../works/jinci-zhi-ming.md) side by side, not causal: the work page dates it 646, Tang Taizong in running script, seven ministers' names on the reverse, the source discussing the text, not calligraphy transmission. The [Tang stele](../objects/stele-jinci-ming.md) is the original stone, accession unrecorded; the [1772 recarving](../objects/stele-jinci-ming-1772.md) a Qianlong recarving, accession likewise unrecorded. Each keeps what this entry gives; a Qufu catalogue like [北魏•张猛龙碑（魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑）](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/wei/2016-04-03/2864.html) is not used to fill gaps.

1. **[Preface to the Orchid Pavilion](../works/lanting.md)**: Look at the [Shenlong tracing](../objects/witness-lanting.md) and the [Dingwu rubbing](../objects/dingwu-zhan.md), one a tracing, one a rubbing.
2. **[Stele of the Ritual Vessels](../works/liqi-bei.md)**: Look at the [original stone](../objects/stele-liqi.md) and the [Ming rubbing](../objects/liqi-ming-44512.md), each recorded apart.
3. **[Stele of Zhang Menglong](../works/zhang-menglong-bei.md)**: Look at the [original stone](../objects/stele-zhang-menglong-bei.md) and the [Ming rubbing](../objects/rubbing-zhang-menglong-bei.md), dates not shifted between them.
4. **[Stele of Yan Qinli](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)**: Look at the [original stone](../objects/yan-qinli-original-stele.md) and the [Republican-era rubbing](../objects/dpm-rubbing-xin00045406.md), each kept to its own entry.
5. **[Inscription for the Jin Shrine](../works/jinci-zhi-ming.md)**: Look at the [Tang stele](../objects/stele-jinci-ming.md) and the [1772 recarving](../objects/stele-jinci-ming-1772.md) side by side, both accessions unrecorded.

This page arranges five sets of objects under "one work, two lives"; the order is editorial, no final answer. Open: the two Lanting objects keep their own transmission, tracing and rubbing not conflated; the two Ming rubbings' provenance unrecorded, "not recorded by the museum"; the Yan Qinli stone's accession unrecorded, the rubbing's acquisition needing separate study; both Jin Shrine accessions unrecorded; the "transmission history" in [冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html) covers tracing. Work and object layers listed apart, not merged. Continue with [Essay 08](../notes/essay-08-qing-modern.md) and [Path 13](../notes/path-05-modelbook.md).
