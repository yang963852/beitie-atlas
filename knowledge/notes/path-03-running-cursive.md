# 11｜阅读路径：行草——从信札到狂草

## 中文

[陆机草隶书平复帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html)秃笔书于麻纸，凡九行；馆方记九行八十六字，另有八十四字之说，两说并记，书体照录作「草隶书」。递藏自宋宣和内府至恭王府，1956 年由张伯驹、潘素夫妇捐献国家；馆方只系于西晋，未给具体书写年份，中间各家系年亦未记。

[冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)神龙本摹写、[宋拓定武兰亭序](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/233468.html)定武本刻拓，两本分列不互串；兰亭序行书，作品页未记年代。[聚焦汉字文明（玉版十三行）](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrbhwb/page/2016-04/19/10/rmrbhwb2016041910.pdf)记王献之书《洛神赋》入宋已残，贾似道刻石称玉版十三行；原墨迹已佚，存世为刻石非纸本摹本。[双说顾恺之与王献之的《洛神赋图》](https://www.cnr.cn/jx/2011jxfw/gjms/20151113/t20151113_520493084.shtml)记 1981 年购藏转首都博物馆，另一说 1982 年，两说并列。

[祭侄文稿](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/3?dep=P)行书，唐颜真卿书，作品页未记年代，拖尾有鲜于枢两则题记与王顼龄雍正二年跋。[自叙帖](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/14901?dep=P)草书，怀素书，年代有 776 与 777 两说，本库依自署系于 777、两说并记；前六行补书作「据说」，补者与补写时间未见馆方著录，真伪有议待考。本页把两件接在复制之后并看。

宋人三件：寒食帖苏轼行书、松风阁诗黄庭坚行书，作品页均未记年代；松风阁字数有 150 与 153 两说，本条不记。诸上座帖草书九十二行，署「山谷老人书」，草书与行楷并见；馆方说它师法怀素狂草，可与[自叙帖](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/14901?dep=P)并看；馆方称其为「晚年杰作」而不系年，装裱是否原装馆方未记。

王铎由二王上溯，[冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)所摹王羲之兰亭可与此卷并看。王铎行草书自书诗卷作于明崇祯十二年（1639）正月，笔势连绵而字间连带不多，靠行气贯通。馆方条目朝代作「清」而款识己卯，本库系于明，两说并记；上款「君宣」「群宣」未详孰是，本幅无藏印，早期流传不明。

待考与边界：字数、购藏年份、纪年、上款诸处两说，本页均并记不取舍；书写年份多处馆方未记或来源未记，如[陆机草隶书平复帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html)条只系西晋、不系年。本页的编排从平复帖信札，经兰亭与十三行的复制，到稿书、狂草、宋人、晚明依次排列；这是为对看而设的次序，不写成先后因果，也不补确定答案。 续读：[专题 03](../notes/essay-03-wei-jin.md)、[专题 06](../notes/essay-06-song.md)。

1. **[《平复帖》](../works/pingfu-tie.md)**：看秃笔麻纸九行，八十六与八十四字两说并记。
2. **[《兰亭序》](../works/lanting.md)**：看神龙本摹写与定武本刻拓，两本分列不互串。
3. **[《洛神赋十三行》](../works/luoshen-shisanhang.md)**：看原墨迹已佚，存世为玉版刻石非纸本摹本。
4. **[《祭侄文稿》](../works/jizhi.md)**：看行书稿本与拖尾题跋，作品页未记年代。
5. **[《自叙帖》](../works/zixu.md)**：看 776 与 777 两说并记，前六行补书作「据说」，真伪待考。
6. **[《黄州寒食诗帖》](../works/hanshi.md)**：看苏轼行书诗稿，作品页未记年代，待考。
7. **[《松风阁诗》](../works/songfeng.md)**：看 150 与 153 字两说并列，本条不记字数，尺寸行数未及核对。
8. **[《诸上座帖》](../works/zhushangzuo-tie.md)**：看九十二行草书与行楷并见，不系年，待考。
9. **[《王铎行草书自书诗卷》](../works/wangduo-zishu-shi.md)**：看清、明两说并记，「君宣」「群宣」未详孰是。

## English

[陆机草隶书平复帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html): a worn brush on hemp paper, nine lines. The museum records nine lines, eighty-six characters; another account eighty-four — both kept. Script transcribed as "grass-clerical", unrenamed. Transmission from the Song Xuanhe collection to the Prince Gong mansion; Zhang Boju (张伯驹) and Pan Su (潘素) donated it to the state in 1956. Dated only to the Western Jin; year of writing (馆方未记) and intermediate dates unrecorded.

The [冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html) is the Shenlong tracing, the [宋拓定武兰亭序](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/233468.html) the Dingwu rubbing — two kept apart, not conflated. Lanting Xu (兰亭序) is running script; the work page gives no date. [聚焦汉字文明（玉版十三行）](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrbhwb/page/2016-04/19/10/rmrbhwb2016041910.pdf) records Wang Xianzhi's (王献之) Luo Shen Fu (洛神赋) fragmentary by the Song, Jia Sidao (贾似道) cutting it on jade-like stone as the "Jade Version Thirteen Lines"; the ink is lost, the survivor a stone cut, not a paper copy. [双说顾恺之与王献之的《洛神赋图》](https://www.cnr.cn/jx/2011jxfw/gjms/20151113/t20151113_520493084.shtml) records a 1981 purchase and transfer to the Capital Museum, another account 1982 — both kept.

[祭侄文稿](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/3?dep=P): running script by Yan Zhenqing (颜真卿) of the Tang; the work page dates it not, the tail carrying two notes by Xianyu Shu (鲜于枢) and a Yongzheng-2 colophon by Wang Xuling (王顼龄). [自叙帖](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/14901?dep=P): cursive by Huaisu (怀素), its date two readings, 776 and 777; this record follows the self-signature at 777, keeping both. The first six lines are "said to be" a supplement, the hand and date unrecorded (馆方未记), authenticity debated (待考). This page sets both after the copies.

Three Song works: Hanshi Tie (寒食帖) by Su Shi (苏轼) and Songfeng Ge Shi (松风阁诗) by Huang Tingjian (黄庭坚), both running script, neither work page dated; the latter's count two readings, 150 and 153, no count given. Zhushangzuo Tie (诸上座帖): ninety-two cursive lines, signed "Shangu Laoren Shu", cursive beside running-regular; the museum says it follows Huaisu's wild cursive, read alongside [自叙帖](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/14901?dep=P), calls it a "late masterpiece" undated, the mounting unrecorded (馆方未记).

Wang Duo (王铎) traced back to the Two Wangs; the [冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html) tracing of Wang Xizhi's (王羲之) Preface read with this scroll. His own-poem scroll in running-cursive dates to the first month of 1639 (Chongzhen 12): momentum continuous, few characters linked, held by the line's flow. The museum entry labels the dynasty "Qing", the signature reading Jimao; this record places it in the Ming, keeping both. The dedication "Junxuan" or "Qunxuan" undetermined; no seal, early transmission unknown.

Open questions (待考) and boundaries: where two readings exist — counts, purchase years, dates, dedications — this page keeps both, choosing neither; years of writing often unrecorded (馆方未记) or absent from the source, as in the [陆机草隶书平复帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html) entry, dated only to the Western Jin, to no year. This page arranges the sequence from the Letter on Recovery, through the Preface and Thirteen Lines copies, to the draft, wild cursive, Song and late Ming — a reading order for comparison, not historical cause, no settled answers. Continue with [Essay 03](../notes/essay-03-wei-jin.md) and [Essay 06](../notes/essay-06-song.md).
