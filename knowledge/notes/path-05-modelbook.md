# 13｜阅读路径：帖学——刻帖的谱系

## 中文

[唐摹王氏一门书翰卷](../objects/wansui-tongtian-scroll.md)是硬黄纸唐人双钩填墨本。光明网[那些有故事的镇馆之宝（唐摹王羲之一门书翰卷）](https://museum.gmw.cn/2017-12/15/content_27109986_6.htm)记其 26.3 × 253.8 厘米、七人十通书翰、两次火劫；尺寸与件数可核，卷上无摹手署款。本页由此唐摹读起，把摹、拓、刻、印排成一线；「刻帖的谱系」为本页的编排，非相因历史。

[辽宁省博物馆展出展馆之宝王羲之一门《万岁通天帖》](https://www.chinanews.com/cul/2018/02-13/8448541.shtml)记万岁通天二年（697年）王方庆进十卷、存七人书翰、1922 年携往长春；两次火劫页面未记年份与损伤评估，待考。辽宁省博物馆[藏品：摹王氏一门书翰卷](https://www.lnmuseum.com.cn/)列硬黄纸唐人双钩填墨本、万岁通天二年王方庆进、《石渠宝笈》著录；藏品页为单页应用、条目无稳定地址，七人姓名从光明网，摹手未署。

本页的编排把[《兰亭序》](../works/lanting.md)两件实物并看，不互串：故宫[冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)记唐摹神龙本、传冯承素摹，尤重「其流传经过」段；[宋拓定武兰亭序](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/233468.html)记新00135367、拓纸与翻刻特征、题跋及木匣。神龙本是摹本、定武本（詹东图藏本）是拓本，两者不同：一为[唐摹神龙本](../objects/witness-lanting.md)墨迹层，一为[定武本拓本](../objects/dingwu-zhan.md)椎拓层，年代与递藏各记本条所给。

[故宫藏王羲之拓本首次与绍兴兰亭碑刻相对](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_3271354)引张彦远《法书要录》记《十七帖》一百七行、九百四十三字、书于 347—361；系唐内府卷著录数，非在世拓本册清点，具足一百七行与否未据图版核对，不主张完缺。[On the Seventeenth Day (十七帖拓本册, 1991.380)](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/39899)记馆藏号 1991.380 的[拓本册](../objects/shiqi-tie-album.md)，列题名、年代、材质、尺寸与公有领域状态；馆方以起首语「十七日」题名，本项目以《十七帖》为作品名，原墨迹已佚、以刻本流传。

本页的编排把后四件按「官刻 / 私刻 / 皇家刻 / 印刷」排为线索，为编辑取舍、非相因谱系。三希堂总数两说并列：故宫拓本条目称「三千四百余件」，报道记一百三十五位书家、三百四十件作品、刻石四百九十五方，差一个数量级，均不入结构化字段；帖石在北海原址，故宫清拓另是一件实物。[冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)所记摹写与刻帖分属两层。

1. **[《万岁通天帖》](../works/wansui-tongtian-tie.md)**：看[唐摹本](../objects/wansui-tongtian-scroll.md)硬黄纸双钩填墨，收七人书翰十通。
2. **[《兰亭序》](../works/lanting.md)**：看[神龙本摹本](../objects/witness-lanting.md)与[定武本拓本](../objects/dingwu-zhan.md)，摹、拓两途不同。
3. **[《十七帖》](../works/shiqi-tie.md)**：看[拓本册](../objects/shiqi-tie-album.md)草书，起首语「十七日」为馆方题名。
4. **[《淳化阁帖》](../works/chunhua-getie.md)**：看[懋勤殿本卷六](../objects/chunhua-maoqin-6.md)，淳化三年奉敕摹刻十卷之一。
5. **[《墨池堂选帖》](../works/mochitang.md)**：看[明拓本](../objects/dpm-mochitang-026.md)，章藻八年刻成五卷，间有伪托。
6. **[《三希堂法帖》](../works/sanxitang-fatie.md)**：看[刻石](../objects/sanxitang-stones.md)嵌北海阅古楼，乾隆十二年编刻。
7. **[《甲种本大楷习字帖》](../works/shen-yinmo-dakai-jia.md)**：沈尹默 1962 年为青少年编，初版月份与印次待核。

本页的编排以「官刻 / 私刻 / 皇家刻 / 印刷」收束四件，为编辑选择，不补定论。待考：万岁通天帖两次火劫年份、损伤评估未记、七人姓名从[那些有故事的镇馆之宝（唐摹王羲之一门书翰卷）](https://museum.gmw.cn/2017-12/15/content_27109986_6.htm)所列、无摹手署款；三希堂丛刻年代为编刻之年非书迹作年；沈尹默习字帖初版月份、印次待核；墨池堂选入书迹有伪托。摹、拓、刻、印各一层，不并成一件。 续读：[专题 03](../notes/essay-03-wei-jin.md)、[专题 06](../notes/essay-06-song.md)。

## English

The [Tang tracing of letters by the Wang family](../objects/wansui-tongtian-scroll.md) is a Tang double-outline-and-fill copy on hard yellow paper. A Guangming Online entry, [那些有故事的镇馆之宝（唐摹王羲之一门书翰卷）](https://museum.gmw.cn/2017-12/15/content_27109986_6.htm), gives 26.3 × 253.8 cm, seven hands, ten letters and the two fires; size and count checkable, no copyist signed. This page arranges from this Tang copy tracing, rubbing, carving and printing in one line; "modelbook genealogy" is this page's arrangement, not causal history.

[辽宁省博物馆展出展馆之宝王羲之一门《万岁通天帖》](https://www.chinanews.com/cul/2018/02-13/8448541.shtml) records the 697 submission of ten scrolls by Wang Fangqing, seven surviving hands, and the 1922 removal to Changchun; the cited pages give no fire years or damage assessment, left open. The Liaoning Provincial Museum's [藏品：摹王氏一门书翰卷](https://www.lnmuseum.com.cn/) lists a Tang double-outline-and-fill copy on hard yellow paper, the 697 submission and a Shiqu Baoji entry; the collection page is a single-page app with no stable per-object address, the seven names following the Guangming list, no copyist signing.

This page arranges the two objects of the [Preface to the Orchid Pavilion](../works/lanting.md), not conflated: [冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html) records a Tang Shenlong tracing attributed to Feng Chengsu, especially its transmission history; [宋拓定武兰亭序](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/233468.html) records 新00135367, a rubbing with paper, recarving traits, colophons, a wooden box. The Shenlong version a tracing, the Dingwu version (Zhan Dongtu copy) a rubbing, differing: one the ink layer of the [Tang Shenlong copy](../objects/witness-lanting.md), the other the inked layer of the [Dingwu rubbing](../objects/dingwu-zhan.md), each its own transmission.

[故宫藏王羲之拓本首次与绍兴兰亭碑刻相对](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_3271354), quoting Zhang Yanyuan's Fashu Yaolu, gives 107 lines and 943 characters over 347—361 for the Seventeen-Day Letters, figures for a Tang court scroll, not a surviving album's count; whether all 107 lines present is unchecked, no completeness claimed. [On the Seventeenth Day (十七帖拓本册, 1991.380)](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/39899) records the [rubbing album](../objects/shiqi-tie-album.md), accession 1991.380, listing title, date, medium, dimensions and public-domain status; the museum titles it from the opening words "on the seventeenth day", this project uses Seventeen-Day Letters, the ink lost, text in carved editions.

This page arranges the last four along "official / private / imperial / printing", an editorial choice, not a causal genealogy. Two totals for the Three Rarities stand side by side: the Palace Museum rubbing entry says "over 3,400 pieces", a report gives 135 calligraphers, 340 works and 495 stones, an order of magnitude apart, neither entering structured fields; the stones stay at Beihai, the Palace Museum's Qing rubbing another object. The tracing in [冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html) and these carvings are different layers.

1. **[Model Letters of the Wan Sui Tong Tian Year](../works/wansui-tongtian-tie.md)**: Look at the [Tang tracing copy](../objects/wansui-tongtian-scroll.md) on hard yellow paper, seven hands, ten letters.
2. **[Preface to the Orchid Pavilion](../works/lanting.md)**: Look at the [Shenlong tracing](../objects/witness-lanting.md) and the [Dingwu rubbing](../objects/dingwu-zhan.md), two different paths.
3. **[Seventeen-Day Letters](../works/shiqi-tie.md)**: Look at the [rubbing album](../objects/shiqi-tie-album.md), titled by the museum from its opening words.
4. **[Chunhua Modelbooks](../works/chunhua-getie.md)**: Look at [Maoqin Hall edition, vol. 6](../objects/chunhua-maoqin-6.md), one of ten carved volumes, the sixth.
5. **[Mochitang Modelbook](../works/mochitang.md)**: Look at the [Ming rubbing](../objects/dpm-mochitang-026.md), five volumes in eight years, with some forgeries.
6. **[Modelbook of the Three Rarities Hall](../works/sanxitang-fatie.md)**: Look at the [carved blocks](../objects/sanxitang-stones.md) set in the Beihai Yuegu Tower walls.
7. **[Large Regular-Script Copybook, Edition A](../works/shen-yinmo-dakai-jia.md)**: Shen Yinmo's 1962 copybook for the young; first-edition month and print run open.

This page arranges these four along "official / private / imperial / printing" to close; the order is editorial, no final answer. Much remains open: the fires' years and damage assessment unrecorded, the seven names follow [那些有故事的镇馆之宝（唐摹王羲之一门书翰卷）](https://museum.gmw.cn/2017-12/15/content_27109986_6.htm) while no copyist signed; a collectanea's date is the carving year, not the works' dates; the copybook's first-edition month and print run await checking; Mochitang includes some forgeries. Tracing, rubbing, carving and printing are each one object layer, not merged. Continue with [Essay 03](../notes/essay-03-wei-jin.md) and [Essay 06](../notes/essay-06-song.md).
