# 08｜专题：清近代——碑学与日常书写

## 中文

[《三希堂法帖》](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242597.html)记一部丛帖：乾隆十二年（1747）编刻，三十二卷、艾叶青石，帖名取自三希堂三件王氏墨迹，帖石嵌北海阅古楼。待考两说并列：拓本条目称精品「三千四百余件」（见[《三希堂法帖》](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242597.html)），另处报道记一百三十五位书家、三百四十件、刻石四百九十五方（见[《三希堂法帖》](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242597.html)），相差一个数量级，均不入结构化字段，不替读者择一。此帖为丛刻，系编刻之年。本页的编排视之为皇家刻帖的总结。

故宫[楷书诗轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231788.html)条目只题「清邓石如书《楷书诗轴》」，书体楷书，年代、尺寸与说明来源未记，不从别处补。本页的编排把它与下两件并作「碑学三种取法」的一组，先取楷书一体；三件并置只为对读书体，不排高下，也不写成时代先后的次序，馆方并未作此归并处理。

故宫[隶书五字横幅](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228281.html)条目只题「清伊秉绶书《隶书五字横幅》」，书体隶书，年代、尺寸与说明来源未记。本页的编排以隶书为第二种取法，与楷书一件并列对读，不排高下，也不主张先后因果；这类取舍只是编辑并读，馆方本身未作归并，作品页未记的在此不从别处补。

故宫[篆书急就章轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228366.html)条目只题「清赵之谦书《篆书急就章轴》」，书体篆书，作品页未记年代与更多说明。本页的编排以篆书为第三种取法；楷、隶、篆三件成组是编辑的并读，馆方并未如此归并，也不由此推出碑学取代帖学一类的因果，更不把三件排成取法高下。

[吴俊卿篆书临石鼓文轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228268.html)记此轴临《石鼓文》第三鼓「田车」篇，纸本，1915年书，吴昌硕时年七十一，把石鼓横势改为纵势、结字参差、线条粗细多变；近人向燊称其「以邓法写石鼓文，变横为纵，自成一派」。待考：所据拓本版本未详；个别字与今释读有出入，待对照原鼓。

西安碑林[林则徐游华山诗并序](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2644&c=View&m=home)记清道光二十二年（1842）林则徐赴伊犁途中游华山、作诗并亲书，行书，分类系于刻石；诗题所记也是行旅中一段可考的停留，馆方未记尺寸。本页的编排把它接在临石鼓之后，读「石」这一层，不作因果，也不把刻石与墨迹排成高下。

[姜涛：沈尹默寓居沪渝时期的书风探索](https://shufa.pku.edu.cn/whsf/8dc5e55fd0e445a781a861c438b81cf9.htm)「二、沈尹默晚年居沪时期的书风」记1962年在人民教育的书写与为青少年编字帖；作品页记[沈尹默](../people/shen-yinmo.md)编大楷习字帖，西泠印社年表列1962年上海教育出版社出版，初版待核实，英文标题系本项目译。[现代 赵朴初 行书跋沈尹默论书札卷](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)记1976年[赵朴初](../people/zhao-puchu.md)题沈氏论书札后，赵跋行书、高20.8宽10.0厘米；沈札年月待查，上博所记沈卒年与西泠年表不同，1976属赵跋之年。

1. **[《三希堂法帖》](../works/sanxitang-fatie.md)**：看三十二卷艾叶青石，帖石嵌北海阅古楼，丛刻非单一作品。
2. **[《楷书诗轴》](../works/deng-kaishu.md)**：看邓石如楷书一件，作品页未记年代，馆方未记更多。
3. **[《隶书五字横幅》](../works/yi-wuzi.md)**：看伊秉绶隶书一件，年代、尺寸与说明来源未记。
4. **[《篆书急就章轴》](../works/zhao-jijiu.md)**：看赵之谦篆书一件，作品页年代与说明均待考。
5. **[《吴昌硕临石鼓文轴》](../works/wuchangshuo-lin-shiguwen.md)**：看1915年临第三鼓「田车」，变横为纵，所据拓本未详。
6. **[《林则徐游华山诗并序》](../works/lin-zexu-huashan.md)**：看1842年行旅中作诗亲书，分类系于刻石一路。
7. **[《甲种本大楷习字帖》](../works/shen-yinmo-dakai-jia.md)**：看1962年为青少年编的大楷帖，英文标题系本项目所译。
8. **[《行书跋沈尹默论书札卷》](../works/zhao-puchu-shen-colophon.md)**：看1976年赵朴初行书题跋，沈氏原札书写年月待查。

本页的编排把三希堂、邓石如至赵之谦、吴昌硕临石鼓、林则徐刻石、沈尹默字帖与赵跋排成一条「石—印刷—现代」的阅读线索；这是阅读顺序的编排，不主张石刻引出印刷、印刷引出今日书写这类技术决定论或历史因果。待考：两处总数、吴昌硕所据拓本、沈尹默字帖初版与沈氏原札年月，[姜涛：沈尹默寓居沪渝时期的书风探索](https://shufa.pku.edu.cn/whsf/8dc5e55fd0e445a781a861c438b81cf9.htm)亦记当代课堂采用尚需另证。本页的编排不补确定答案。 续读：[路径 12](../notes/path-04-stele-studies.md)、[路径 14](../notes/path-06-one-work-two-lives.md)。

## English

[《三希堂法帖》](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242597.html) records a collected modelbook: 1747, thirty-two volumes on Aiye-qing stone, named for three Wang manuscripts in the Three Rarities Hall, stones in Beihai's Yuegu Tower. Two totals side by side: a rubbing entry says "over 3,400 fine pieces" (see [《三希堂法帖》](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242597.html)), another report gives 135 calligraphers, 340 works, 495 stones (see [《三希堂法帖》](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242597.html)); an order apart, neither enters structured fields, this page does not choose. A collected cutting, dated by compilation. This page arranges it as a summing-up of imperial cutting.

The Palace Museum's [楷书诗轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231788.html) says only "Qing Deng Shiru（邓石如） wrote the Poem in Regular Script," in regular script; date, dimensions, description unrecorded, not filled in elsewhere. This page arranges it with the next two as "three stele-studies approaches," regular script first; the three are set together to compare scripts, no ranking, no claim of before-and-after order, and the museum makes no such grouping.

The Palace Museum's [隶书五字横幅](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228281.html) says only "Qing Yi Bingshou（伊秉绶） wrote the Five Characters in Clerical Script," in clerical script; date, dimensions and description unrecorded. This page arranges clerical script as the second approach, beside the regular-script piece, no ranking, no causation; this selection is only editorial reading together, no grouping the museum itself makes, and what the page omits is not filled in here.

The Palace Museum's [篆书急就章轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228366.html) says only "Qing Zhao Zhiqian（赵之谦） wrote the Jijiu Zhang in Seal Script," in seal script; the page records no date or fuller description. This page arranges seal script as the third approach; grouping the three scripts is an editorial reading, not a grouping the museum makes, no causation of stele studies replacing modelbook studies is drawn; nor are the three ranked by approach.

[吴俊卿篆书临石鼓文轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228268.html) records this scroll copying the "Tian che" of the third Stone Drum, paper, 1915, when Wu Changshuo（吴昌硕） was seventy-one; he turned the horizontal posture to vertical, with uneven structure and varied line weight. Xiang Shen（向燊） called it "writing the Stone Drums with Deng's method, turning horizontal to vertical, a school of its own." Open: the rubbing used is unidentified; a few transcribed characters differ from current readings, pending check against the drums.

Xi'an Beilin Museum's [林则徐游华山诗并序](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2644&c=View&m=home) records that in 1842 Lin Zexu（林则徐）, on his way to Yili, toured Mount Hua and wrote out the poem and preface himself, in running script, filed under stone carving; the title records a verifiable stop, and the museum records no dimensions. This page arranges it after the Stone Drums copy to read the "stone" layer, no causation, no ranking of stone against ink.

[姜涛：沈尹默寓居沪渝时期的书风探索](https://shufa.pku.edu.cn/whsf/8dc5e55fd0e445a781a861c438b81cf9.htm), in its section on Shen's late Shanghai style, records 1962 writing in People's Education and copybooks for the young; the page records a large regular-script copybook [Shen Yinmo（沈尹默）](../people/shen-yinmo.md), the Xiling chronology listing a 1962 Shanghai Education Press edition, first edition unverified, English title a project translation. [现代 赵朴初 行书跋沈尹默论书札卷](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807) records [Zhao Puchu（赵朴初）](../people/zhao-puchu.md)'s 1976 running-script colophon after Shen's writing, 20.8 by 10.0 cm; Shen's note undated, death year in the museum differs from the Xiling chronology, 1976 belongs to Zhao's colophon.

Open: the totals, Wu's rubbing, the copybook's first edition, Shen's note date; [姜涛：沈尹默寓居沪渝时期的书风探索](https://shufa.pku.edu.cn/whsf/8dc5e55fd0e445a781a861c438b81cf9.htm) also notes classroom adoption needs separate evidence.
