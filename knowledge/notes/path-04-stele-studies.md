# 12｜阅读路径：碑学——清人如何重读石刻

## 中文

[礼器碑（原石）](../objects/stele-liqi.md)是东汉永寿二年（156年）隶书碑，记韩敕修孔庙、铸礼器。孔子博物馆[暑期攻略｜阅览碑刻 品读千年石墨精华](https://www.confuciusmuseum.cn/news/wb/2023-07-23/2630.html)逐碑列藏所、年代、行数，行数即可核之项。本页由此石读到汉碑、魏碑、唐碑与清人墨迹；此顺序是本页的编排，非相因的历史。

[孔庙三碑，惊艳了时光](https://www.sdxc.gov.cn/whql/whcc/202508/t20250822_16374659.htm)记礼器碑 1978 年由同文门移东庑、1998 年入曲阜汉魏碑刻陈列馆。[泰安市博物馆开展国际博物馆日系列活动](https://tsgw.taian.gov.cn/art/2025/5/19/art_48253_10316874.html)列张迁碑原石为馆藏，展览为图版陈列。[竹云阁帖（石门颂、神君碑、张迁碑、礼器碑）](https://g2.ltfc.net/work/SUFA/67d64a1899a6320270c19860)记张迁碑中平三年（186年）刻、明初出土、在岱庙碑廊；此出丛帖题记、未得馆方印证，年代与出土待考。

[凹凹凹凸凹凹凹，这块题记很特别](https://m.gmw.cn/2023-05/27/content_1303386306.htm)记始平公造像记在古阳洞北壁，额二行六字、正文十行行二十字，字与界格凸出石面，该文称其为龙门唯一阳刻。[跨越时空，对话龙门石窟云文博](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/cn/news/%E3%80%90art-forward%E3%80%91across-time-and-space-dialogue-with-longmen-grottoesyunwenbo-717187/)列 498 年、孟达撰文、朱义章书，拓片 89 × 39 厘米为纸幅非石面，纪年一行残，498 为著录年代；二十品系后人所择、非一体实物。

[北魏•张猛龙碑（魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑）](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/wei/2016-04-03/2864.html)记正光三年（522年）正书、颂治郡兴学。本页的编排接[《颜勤礼碑》](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)作对照面：原石系唐大历十四年（779年），颜真卿撰并书，作品页记 1922 年西安出土、后藏西安碑林，故宫另藏民国拓本一册，递传须另案考察；来源未记书体，字段从缺，不以常识填补。

作品页记清乾隆间黄易把始平公题记置于龙门造像题记之首，为清人重读石刻一例。本页的编排由此转到邓、伊、赵、吴、林的墨迹，作「碑学如何进入创作」的观察，不写成因果；[凹凹凹凸凹凹凹，这块题记很特别](https://m.gmw.cn/2023-05/27/content_1303386306.htm)所记凸字属实物层，墨迹另是一层。

1. **[《礼器碑》](../works/liqi-bei.md)**：看[原石](../objects/stele-liqi.md)隶书，永寿二年（156年）记韩敕修孔庙事。
2. **[《张迁碑》](../works/zhangqian-bei.md)**：看[原石](../objects/stele-zhangqian.md)中平三年（186年）为张迁立，藏所两见待考。
3. **[《始平公造像记》](../works/shipinggong-zaoxiang-ji.md)**：看[北壁题记](../objects/shipinggong-inscription.md)阳刻十行，纪年一行有残。
4. **[《张猛龙碑》](../works/zhang-menglong-bei.md)**：看[原石](../objects/stele-zhang-menglong-bei.md)正书，正光三年（522年）颂兴学。
5. **[《颜勤礼碑》](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)**：看[原石](../objects/yan-qinli-original-stele.md)唐大历十四年（779年），书体来源未记。
6. **[《楷书诗轴》](../works/deng-kaishu.md)**：看[邓石如墨迹](../objects/witness-deng-kaishu.md)楷书，馆藏号新00125110。
7. **[《隶书五字横幅》](../works/yi-wuzi.md)**：看[伊秉绶墨迹](../objects/witness-yi-wuzi.md)隶书五字，馆藏号未记。
8. **[《篆书急就章轴》](../works/zhao-jijiu.md)**：看[赵之谦墨迹](../objects/witness-zhao-jijiu.md)篆书，馆藏号新00094049。
9. **[《吴昌硕临石鼓文轴》](../works/wuchangshuo-lin-shiguwen.md)**：看[临石鼓墨迹](../objects/scroll-wuchangshuo-shigu.md)篆书，1915 年夏五月，号新00111675。
10. **[《林则徐游华山诗并序》](../works/lin-zexu-huashan.md)**：看[刻石](../objects/stele-lin-zexu-huashan.md)行书，1842 年游华山作诗亲书。

本页的编排把汉碑、魏碑、唐碑与清人墨迹排成一线，顺序是编辑选择，不补定论。待考：礼器、张迁、颜勤礼的英文题名为本项目所拟或所译；张迁碑年代与出土未得馆方印证；始平公拓片尺寸是纸幅；颜勤礼碑书体从缺；吴昌硕所据拓本待考。[暑期攻略｜阅览碑刻 品读千年石墨精华](https://www.confuciusmuseum.cn/news/wb/2023-07-23/2630.html)所列行数是可核之项。 续读：[专题 08](../notes/essay-08-qing-modern.md)、[专题 05](../notes/essay-05-sui-tang.md)。

## English

The [original stone of the Stele of the Ritual Vessels](../objects/stele-liqi.md) is a clerical-script stele of 156, on Han Chi repairing the Confucian temple and casting vessels. The Confucius Museum's [暑期攻略｜阅览碑刻 品读千年石墨精华](https://www.confuciusmuseum.cn/news/wb/2023-07-23/2630.html) lists each stele's holding, date and line count, the line count checkable. This page arranges this stone with Han, Wei and Tang steles and Qing ink works, not as causal history.

[孔庙三碑，惊艳了时光](https://www.sdxc.gov.cn/whql/whcc/202508/t20250822_16374659.htm) records the Ritual Vessels Stele moved from Tongwen Gate to the east side hall in 1978 and into the Qufu Han and Wei Stele Gallery in 1998. [泰安市博物馆开展国际博物馆日系列活动](https://tsgw.taian.gov.cn/art/2025/5/19/art_48253_10316874.html) lists the Zhang Qian original stone as a Tai'an Museum holding, the exhibition a panel display. [竹云阁帖（石门颂、神君碑、张迁碑、礼器碑）](https://g2.ltfc.net/work/SUFA/67d64a1899a6320270c19860) adds it was carved in 186, unearthed in the early Ming, in the Dai Temple corridor; from an album note, unconfirmed by a museum page, so date and unearthing remain open.

[凹凹凹凸凹凹凹，这块题记很特别](https://m.gmw.cn/2023-05/27/content_1303386306.htm) places the Shiping Gong dedication on Guyang Cave's north wall, with a two-line six-character title and ten lines of twenty characters, strokes and grid raised above the stone; the article calls it Longmen's only yang-carved inscription. [跨越时空，对话龙门石窟云文博](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/cn/news/%E3%80%90art-forward%E3%80%91across-time-and-space-dialogue-with-longmen-grottoesyunwenbo-717187/) lists 498, text by Meng Da, written by Zhu Yizhang, a rubbing 89 × 39 cm of paper, not the stone face, a damaged dating line and 498 as catalogued date; the Twenty Samples a later selection, not one object.

[北魏•张猛龙碑（魏鲁郡太守张府君清颂之碑）](https://hwbk.qfwbw.cn/wei/2016-04-03/2864.html) records a 522 regular-script stele praising Zhang Menglong's governance and schools. This page arranges the [Stele of Yan Qinli](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md) next as a contrast: its stone dated 779, written by Yan Zhenqing; the work page notes its 1922 unearthing in Xi'an, later holding at Xi'an Beilin, and a separate Republican-era rubbing album at the Palace Museum, acquisition untraced. The cited sources give no script type, so the field is left blank, not filled from common knowledge.

The work page notes that the Qianlong-era scholar Huang Yi ranked the Shiping Gong dedication first among the Longmen dedications, one instance of Qing readers reopening the stone. This page arranges from here the ink works of Deng, Yi, Zhao, Wu and Lin, as an observation of "how stele studies entered composition", not as cause and effect; the raised strokes in [凹凹凹凸凹凹凹，这块题记很特别](https://m.gmw.cn/2023-05/27/content_1303386306.htm) belong to the object layer, the ink works to another.

1. **[Stele of the Ritual Vessels](../works/liqi-bei.md)**: Look at the clerical script of the [original stone](../objects/stele-liqi.md), dated 156.
2. **[Stele of Zhang Qian](../works/zhangqian-bei.md)**: Look at the [original stone](../objects/stele-zhangqian.md), dated 186, its holding doubly attested and open.
3. **[Dedication for the Late Duke of Shiping](../works/shipinggong-zaoxiang-ji.md)**: Look at the yang-carved [north-wall inscription](../objects/shipinggong-inscription.md), ten lines, a damaged dating line.
4. **[Stele of Zhang Menglong](../works/zhang-menglong-bei.md)**: Look at the [original stone](../objects/stele-zhang-menglong-bei.md) in regular script, dated 522, praising schools.
5. **[Stele of Yan Qinli](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)**: Look at the [original stone](../objects/yan-qinli-original-stele.md), dated 779, with the script type unrecorded.
6. **[Poem in Regular Script](../works/deng-kaishu.md)**: Look at Deng Shiru's [ink manuscript](../objects/witness-deng-kaishu.md) in regular script, with accession number 新00125110.
7. **[Five Characters in Clerical Script](../works/yi-wuzi.md)**: Look at Yi Bingshou's [ink manuscript](../objects/witness-yi-wuzi.md), five clerical characters, accession unrecorded.
8. **[Jijiu Zhang in Seal Script](../works/zhao-jijiu.md)**: Look at Zhao Zhiqian's [ink manuscript](../objects/witness-zhao-jijiu.md) in seal script, accession 新00094049.
9. **[Hanging Scroll after the Stone Drums](../works/wuchangshuo-lin-shiguwen.md)**: Look at Wu Changshuo's [ink scroll](../objects/scroll-wuchangshuo-shigu.md), fifth month of 1915, accession 新00111675.
10. **[Lin Zexu's Mount Hua Poem and Preface](../works/lin-zexu-huashan.md)**: Look at the [carved stone](../objects/stele-lin-zexu-huashan.md) in running script, written on a Mount Hua journey in 1842.
