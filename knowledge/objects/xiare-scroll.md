# 杨凝式草书夏热帖卷

## 待考

### 中文

- 此件为纸本墨迹手卷，不是拓本；制作年份只定“五代”，无确切年月。
- 王钦若、鲜于枢、赵孟頫、张照的题跋归属及乾隆释文均据藏品页；除赵孟頫跋外，不替其他题跋系年。
- 1316年11月13日是赵孟頫题跋日期，不是杨凝式书写日期。
- 馆方未记入藏年份与具体入藏经过。
- 藏品页称此帖曾刻入《三希堂法帖》，但未给本件刻入时间，故不另设刻石实物或年代事件。

### English

- This is a paper ink manuscript, not a rubbing. Its production is assigned only to the Five Dynasties, without an exact year or month.
- The attributions of the colophons by Wang Qinruo, Xian Yushu, Zhao Mengfu, and Zhang Zhao, and of the Qianlong Emperor’s transcription, follow the collection entry. No dates are assigned to them except Zhao Mengfu’s.
- 13 November 1316 dates Zhao Mengfu’s colophon, not Yang Ningshi’s writing.
- The museum gives neither an accession year nor a detailed acquisition history.
- The collection entry says the letter was included in the Sanxitang fatie but gives no date for this particular carving; no separate stone object or dated carving event is asserted.
