# 宋拓锺王楷帖册（宣示表）

## 待考

### 中文

- “宋拓”为旧签与著录用语，册中诸帖是否同为宋拓，未见逐开鉴定。
- 册内《宣示表》与南宋贾似道摹刻石的拓本关系待核。

### English

- “Song rubbing” is the old label and catalogue wording. Whether every model in the album is a Song impression has not been established sheet by sheet.
- The relation between this copy of the Memorial and impressions from Jia Sidao’s Southern Song stone is unchecked.
