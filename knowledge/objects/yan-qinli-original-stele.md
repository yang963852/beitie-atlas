# 颜勤礼碑（原石）

## 待考

### 中文

- 西安碑林目录记原碑立于凤栖原，本项目未为该处绘制点位。
- 1922 至 1948 年的序列出自 2007 年发表的一篇博物馆文章。各次移动之间的保管情况没有记载，时间线只列出有据可查的移动。
- 出土地点在故宫条目中写作「旧藩廨库基」，在博物馆文章中写作「省长公署」。两种表述都原样保留，不合并成一个规范化地址。
- 所有事件都没有坐标记录：来源给出的是区域与建筑名称，不是实测点位。
- 今日是否展出、确切存放位置，均不清楚。

### English

- Xi’an Beilin’s catalogue gives Fengqiyuan as the original setting. The site has no point in this dataset.
- The 1922–1948 sequence comes from a single museum article published in 2007. Custody between the named moves is not recorded, and the timeline shows only the movements that are documented.
- The find-place is described as 旧藩廨库基 in the Palace Museum entry and as 省长公署 in the museum article. Both wordings are preserved instead of being merged into one normalized address.
- No coordinates are recorded for any event: the sources name areas and buildings, not surveyed points.
- Present-day display status and the exact storage location are unknown.
