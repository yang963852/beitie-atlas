## 待考

### 中文

### English

- Mounted width: the museum record gives 24.7 by 46.8 cm, the 2012 designation 24.7 by 47.3 cm; neither is chosen.
- The copying period remains open between two statements.
- The authenticators' half-signatures and the 598 colophon are themselves reproduced in the copy, as the museum record states.
