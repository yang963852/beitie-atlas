# 智永真草千字文碑（关中本原石）

## 待考

### 中文

- 石高石宽三说并存：官网藏品页 268×97，《陕西省志·文物志》356×102，《文化艺术报》实测 353×97×32。本记录尺寸取省志口径并注明异说，不作裁定。
- 行款两说并存：官网页「共6层，每层26行，每行10字」，省志「两面刻，共8栏，每栏27行，行10字」。
- 现展位置两说：《文化艺术报》（2021）记第三展室东侧，馆方微信记北区「汉字·书法·碑林」展，或系搬迁前后差异。
- 石数「凡八石」与「两面刻共八栏」的口径差异未作裁定。

### English

- Three height readings coexist: 268 by 97 cm (museum page), 356 by 102 cm (provincial gazette) and 353 by 97 by 32 cm (paper's own measurement). The structured field takes the gazette figure and the variants are noted, with no verdict.
- Line layout also has two readings: six layers of 26 ten-character lines (museum page) against two faces, eight bands of 27 ten-character lines (gazette).
- Two display locations are recorded for 2021 and the museum's account, probably before and after a move.
- "Eight stones" and "two faces, eight bands" may be the same arrangement described differently; no verdict is asserted.
