# 孙过庭《书谱》

## 待考

### 中文

- 卷首写“书谱卷上”，隔水上的题签却写“序下”。临卷时，可以分别看孙过庭的草书与后人题签的楷书。

### English

- The manuscript opens with “upper scroll”, while the label on the mounting reads “lower preface”. Compare Sun’s cursive hand with the later regular-script label.
