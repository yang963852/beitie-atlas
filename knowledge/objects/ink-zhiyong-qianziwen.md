# 真草千字文（日本藏墨迹本）

## 待考

### 中文

- 现持有人与保存状况无官方记录：文化厅登记只写「個人」「京都府」，不作机构藏品断言。
- 国宝重新指定的日期两说并存：文化厅数据库作 1953年11月14日，张明杰文作 1953年2月14日；本记录取官方库口径并保留异说。
- 1912 年圣华房珂罗版影印本与「1931年首次指定」的说法见张明杰文，未核实物与原始档案。
- 「不避隋唐帝讳」等文本观察见二手转述，未核原件。

### English

- No official record of the current holder or condition: the register lists only "a private individual" in Kyoto Prefecture, and the piece is not asserted as an institutional holding.
- The 1953 re-designation date has two readings — 14 November (national register) against 14 February (Zhang's article); the official figure is recorded with the variant kept.
- The 1912 Seika-bō facsimile and the claimed 1931 first designation rest on the article, unchecked against the volumes or archives.
- Textual observations such as the avoidance taboos come from secondhand accounts and have not been checked against the original.
