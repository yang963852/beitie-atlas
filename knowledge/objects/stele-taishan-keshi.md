# 泰山刻石（残石）

## 待考

### 中文

- 页面记残存十字，但没有碑石尺寸或保存状况调查。
- 移存岱庙只记到清代，因此事件只记朝代，不填年份。
- 页面提到的被盗与追回没有年份，只在本条说明中记下，不单列为事件。
- 此石是原碑残石，不是拓本，也不是后代的摹刻。

### English

- The page gives ten surviving characters; it does not give the stone's size or a condition survey.
- The move to Dai Temple is dated only to the Qing dynasty, so the event carries an era rather than a year.
- The theft and recovery mentioned on the page are not dated and are recorded only in this note, not as separate events.
- The stone is a fragment of the original stele, not a rubbing or a later copy.
