## 待考

### 中文

- 纪年换算在两处省级来源间有分歧；结构化年份取 405，异说照录，不予隐去。
- 1778 年出土后此碑曾湮没；所引页面未记湮没期间存放何处。
- 碑上未署撰书者，故不主张作者。

### English

- The date conversion is disputed between two provincial sources; the structured year is 405 and the alternative is stated rather than hidden.
- The 1778 find was followed by a loss of the stone; the cited page does not say where it lay while lost.
- The stele is unsigned, so no attribution is asserted.
