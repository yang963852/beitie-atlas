# 唐摹王氏一门书翰卷

## 待考

### 中文

- 此卷为双钩填墨摹本，非真迹；实物类型即按此区分。
- 两次火劫，所引页面未记年份，故不设损伤事件。
- 697 年据卷末款识；所摹书翰本身跨数代。

### English

- The scroll is a tracing copy (双钩填墨), not an autograph; the record keeps that distinction in the object type.
- The two fires are not dated in the cited pages, so no damage event is recorded.
- The inscription's subscription is the basis for the 697 date; the letters themselves span several generations.
