# 虞恭公碑（南宋拓本）

## 待考

### 中文

- 此册为剪裱装，故每页尺寸非石面尺寸。
- 馆方所引购价出自《欧斋藏碑帖目录》，未必即本册的入藏记录。

### English

- The album is a cut-and-mounted edition: the museum calls it a 剪裱装册, so the leaf size is not the stone's.
- The museum's purchased-for figure belongs to the 欧斋 catalogue it quotes, not necessarily to this album's own acquisition.
