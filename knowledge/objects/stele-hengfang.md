# 衡方碑（原石）

## 待考

### 中文

- 该条目没有碑石尺寸；同一处的 223 × 107 厘米是清拓本的尺寸。
- 两处页面都记为岱庙，但一作炳灵门、一作东碑廊；结构化地点只记到岱庙。
- 雍正八年是水灾后重立，不是有记载的保管转移；中间迁移无记录。

### English

- The entry gives no stone measurements. The number 223 × 107 cm in the same entry belongs to its Qing rubbing.
- Two pages agree on 岱庙 but differ on 炳灵门 versus the east stele corridor; the structured place is the temple only.
- The 1730 event is a re-erection after a flood, not a documented transfer of custody; no intermediate moves are recorded.
