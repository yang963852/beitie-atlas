### English

- The museum page records only Yu Youren’s gift in 1938; earlier hands and the accession year are not given.
- The epitaph names neither writer nor calligrapher, and the museum page supplies neither.
- Measurements and line arrangement follow the museum page and have not been checked against the stone.
