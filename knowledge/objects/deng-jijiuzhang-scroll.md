# 邓文原《章草书急就章》卷

## 待考

### 中文

- 张雨及元明诸跋未记署年，不据此编排题跋先后。
- 入藏日期、递藏经过、现裱与展室未记。
- 尺寸、印鉴与馆藏号依目录，未据原件复核；事件地点只保留历史措辞。

### English

- Zhang Yu’s and the other Yuan and Ming colophons have no recorded dates, so no sequence is imposed on them.
- The accession date, custody history, present mounting and gallery are not recorded.
- Measurements, seals and the inventory number follow the catalogue and have not been checked against the object; the event retains only the historical place wording.
