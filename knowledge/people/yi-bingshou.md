# 伊秉绶

## 中文

### 生平与身份

伊秉绶（1753—1815，清华大学艺术博物馆页作 1754—1815），字组似（该页作「祖似」），号墨卿，福建宁化人。清乾隆五十四年（1789）进士，授刑部主事，迁员外郎，曾官惠州、扬州知府，在任颇有政声。工诗，善画墨梅，尤以书名，书工楷、行，以隶书最负盛名，位列乾嘉八隶之首；与邓石如并为有清一代碑学之鼻祖。著《留春草堂诗钞》《墨庵集锦》，《清史稿》有传。[故宫博物院，伊秉绶隶书五字横幅](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228281.html) · [深圳博物馆，伊秉绶隶书四字横幅](https://www.shenzhenmuseum.com/worksDetail?id=1789588770438533120)。生年与字的两说并存，本库不取其一。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于清。

### 书体与师承

故宫记其「书工楷、行，以隶书最负盛名，位列乾嘉八隶之首」，并称其与邓石如「共为有清一代碑学之鼻祖」；深圳博物馆称其「以先秦篆籀、汉魏碑刻砖瓦之学开创隶书新气象」；清华艺博称其隶书「受《衡方碑》影响，以篆笔作隶」。[故宫博物院，隶书五字横幅](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228281.html) · [深圳博物馆，隶书四字横幅](https://www.shenzhenmuseum.com/worksDetail?id=1789588770438533120)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《隶书五字横幅》](../works/yi-wuzi.md)纸本，纵三十二厘米、横一百三十点五厘米，隶书「华屿读书堂」，末识「简田十六先生雅属并正，秉绶。嘉庆壬申岁」；嘉庆壬申为嘉庆十七年（1812），时年六十，为晚年所书。[故宫博物院，伊秉绶隶书五字横幅](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228281.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Yi Bingshou (1753–1815; a Tsinghua University Art Museum page gives 1754–1815), courtesy name Zusi (that page gives "Zusi" 祖似), style name Moqing, was a native of Ninghua in Fujian. He passed the examination in 1789, served as a justice ministry secretary and then vice director, and governed Huizhou and Yangzhou with a good reputation. He wrote poetry, painted ink plum, and above all was famed for calligraphy: regular and running, but most for clerical, first among the eight clerical masters of the Qianlong-Jiaqing era, and with Deng Shiru a founder of Qing stele studies. His works include the Liuchun caotang poems; the Qing shi gao carries his biography. [Palace Museum, Yi Bingshou's five-character clerical banner](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228281.html) · [Shenzhen Museum, four-character clerical banner](https://www.shenzhenmuseum.com/worksDetail?id=1789588770438533120). Both birth years and both courtesy-name readings are kept. By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Qing.

### Scripts and models

The Palace Museum records that he "wrote regular and running script, and was most famed for clerical, first among the eight clerical masters of the Qianlong-Jiaqing era," and with Deng Shiru "a founder of Qing stele studies." The Shenzhen Museum says he "opened a new climate for clerical script through the study of pre-Qin seal and Han-Wei stone inscriptions and tiles"; Tsinghua's museum says his clerical script, "influenced by the Hengfang stele, used seal brushwork for clerical forms." [Palace Museum, five-character banner](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228281.html) · [Shenzhen Museum, four-character banner](https://www.shenzhenmuseum.com/worksDetail?id=1789588770438533120)

### Works in this library

The [five-character clerical banner](../works/yi-wuzi.md) held here is ink on paper, 32 by 130.5 cm, reading "Huayu reading hall," inscribed to Mr. Jiantian and dated the renshen year of Jiaqing (1812), when he was sixty, a late work. [Palace Museum, five-character banner](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228281.html)
