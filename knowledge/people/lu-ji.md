# 陆机

## 中文

### 生平与身份

陆机（261—303），字士衡，吴郡（今江苏苏州）人。三国东吴丞相陆逊之孙、大司马陆抗之子。吴亡后与弟陆云入洛阳，参与司马氏政权；受成都王司马颖重用，为平原内史、后将军、河北大都督，讨司马乂兵败，为司马颖所杀。少有异才，文章冠世，以《文赋》最知名；善章草，为文名所掩。[故宫博物院，陆机草隶书平复帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于西晋。

### 书体与师承

故宫小传记其「善章草」，而《平复帖》的藏品著录称其字体为「草隶书」，并释「草隶，又称隶草，是隶书的草率写法，流行于后汉至西晋间」。两词并存，本库不改写成一种。[故宫博物院，陆机草隶书平复帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《平复帖》](../works/pingfu-tie.md)为纸本手卷，纵二十三点七厘米、横二十点六厘米，草隶书九行八十六字。故宫称它是「现存年代最早并真实可信的西晋名家法帖」，用秃笔写于麻纸之上，笔意婉转，风格平淡质朴；此帖宋代入宣和內府，1956 年由张氏夫妇捐献国家。[故宫博物院，陆机草隶书平复帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Lu Ji (261–303), courtesy name Shiheng, was a native of Wu commandery, present-day Suzhou in Jiangsu. He was a grandson of Lu Xun, chancellor of Wu in the Three Kingdoms, and son of Lu Kang, grand marshal. After Wu fell he went to Luoyang with his brother Lu Yun and entered the service of the Sima regime; favoured by Sima Ying, Prince of Chengdu, he held the posts of interior minister of Pingyuan, general of the rear and commander of Hebei, was defeated in the campaign against Sima Yi and killed by Sima Ying. A prodigy, he was famed for prose, above all the Wenfu, and was good at draft cursive, though his literary fame overshadowed it. [Palace Museum, Lu Ji's Pingfu tie in cao-li script](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Western Jin.

### Scripts and models

The museum biography says he was "good at draft cursive," while the object record calls the script of the Pingfu tie "cao-li" and explains that cao-li, also called li-cao, was a cursive manner of clerical script current from the Later Han to the Western Jin. Both terms are kept. [Palace Museum, Pingfu tie](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html)

### Works in this library

The [Pingfu tie](../works/pingfu-tie.md) held here is ink on paper, a handscroll 23.7 by 20.6 cm, in cao-li script, nine lines and eighty-six characters. The museum calls it "the earliest surviving and credible masterwork of the Western Jin," written with a worn brush on hemp paper, its brushwork supple and its style plain and unadorned; it entered the Xuanhe palace collection in the Song and was given to the state by the Zhang family in 1956. [Palace Museum, Pingfu tie](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234553.html)
