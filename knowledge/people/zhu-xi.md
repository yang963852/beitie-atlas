# 朱熹

## 中文

### 生平与身份

朱熹（1130—1200），字元晦，号晦庵，人称考亭先生，徽州婺源人，寓居建州。绍兴十八年进士，光宗朝官至焕章阁待制、提举南京鸿庆宫，庆元六年卒，年七十二。[故宫博物院，朱熹行书城南唱和诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228388.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于南宋。

### 书体与师承

故宫小传记其工书、早年学钟繇，并指出后人误作曹操；《书史会要》说他「善行草，尤善小字，下笔即沉着典雅」。[故宫博物院，朱熹行书城南唱和诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228388.html) · [故宫博物院，朱熹行书城南唱和诗卷（数字展厅）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_21.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

故宫著录《城南唱和诗》卷，纸本手卷，纵三十一点五厘米、横二百七十五点五厘米，行书六十五行、四百六十四字，为朱熹自书和友人张栻诗二十首。据《朱子年谱》，乾道三年（1167）八月朱熹访张栻于潭州，馆方据此定此卷为朱熹三十八岁时所书；卷后有明司马垔跋，引首李东阳篆书「晦翁手泽」，1953 年归故宫博物院。本库未另建该作品档案，此处只记馆方所述。[故宫博物院，朱熹行书城南唱和诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228388.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Zhu Xi (1130–1200), courtesy name Yuanhui, style name Hui'an, known as Mister Kaoting, was a native of Wuyuan in Huizhou, with residence in Jianzhou. He passed the examination in 1148, rose under Guangzong to attendant of the Zhanghua Pavilion and superintendent of the Nanjing Hongqing Palace, and died in 1200 at seventy-two. [Palace Museum, Zhu Xi's poem scroll on the South of the City](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228388.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Southern Song.

### Scripts and models

The museum biography records that he was skilled at calligraphy and in early years studied Zhong Yao, with a note that later readers misidentified one model as Cao Cao. The Shushi huiyao is quoted: "good at running and cursive, especially small characters, his brush settles into calm elegance at once." [Palace Museum, Zhu Xi's South of the City scroll](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228388.html) · [Palace Museum, digital exhibition of the scroll](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_21.html)

### Works in this library

The museum records the South of the City poems, ink on paper, 31.5 by 275.5 cm, running script in sixty-five lines and 464 characters, twenty poems answering his friend Zhang Shi. From the Zhuzi nianpu the museum dates the meeting at Tanzhou to 1167 and the scroll to Zhu Xi at thirty-eight. It carries a Ming colophon by Sima Wen, a frontispiece by Li Dongyang and entered the Palace Museum in 1953. This library holds no separate work record; only the museum's account is given. [Palace Museum, Zhu Xi's South of the City scroll](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228388.html)
