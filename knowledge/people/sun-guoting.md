# 孙过庭

## 中文

### 生平与身份

孙过庭生卒年不详，主要活动在唐初。字虔礼，富阳（今浙江）人，一说江苏人；上海图书馆人物规范档作「吴郡富阳（今浙江富阳县）人」。官率府录事参军。唐代书法家、书法理论家。[故宫博物院，宋拓孙过庭书谱](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html) · [上海图书馆开放数据平台，sunguoting](https://data.library.sh.cn/entity/person/ut4rfolpzuibyprs.html)。籍贯两说并存，本库不取其一。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于唐。

### 书体与师承

故宫记其「工正、行、草书」，为唐代著名书法家、书法理论家。[故宫博物院，宋拓孙过庭书谱](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《书谱》](../works/shupu.md)为孙过庭垂拱三年（687）之作，上卷谈「运笔」，下卷已佚，为其代表作品；墨迹现藏台北故宫博物院，本库所据为故宫所藏宋拓，即北宋大观年间内府所刻《太清楼书谱》，原石早佚，此拓称「海内孤本」。[故宫博物院，宋拓孙过庭书谱](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html)


### 可临之作

- [《书谱》](../works/shupu.md)：孙过庭687年撰并书的草书《书谱》墨迹，上卷原藏清内府，今在台湾。

## English

### Life and identity

Sun Guoting's dates are unknown; he was active mainly in the early Tang. His courtesy name was Qianli; he was a native of Fuyang in Zhejiang, some say of Jiangsu, while the Shanghai Library authority record gives "Fuyang in Wu commandery, present-day Fuyang county, Zhejiang." He served as recorder in the household of the crown prince and was a Tang calligrapher and theorist. [Palace Museum, Song rubbing of Sun Guoting's Shupu](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html) · [Shanghai Library Open Data, Sun Guoting](https://data.library.sh.cn/entity/person/ut4rfolpzuibyprs.html). Both native-place readings are kept. By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Tang.

### Scripts and models

The Palace Museum records that he was "skilled in regular, running and cursive script" and was a noted Tang calligrapher and theorist. [Palace Museum, Shupu](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html)

### Works in this library

The [Shupu](../works/shupu.md) held here is his work of 687; the upper volume discusses brush movement, the lower is lost, and it is his representative work. The ink manuscript is in the National Palace Museum, Taipei; what this library holds is a Song rubbing in the Palace Museum, the Northern Song imperial Taqinglou edition whose stones are long lost, called "the only copy under heaven." [Palace Museum, Shupu](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html)

### Works to copy

- [Treatise on Calligraphy](../works/shupu.md): Sun Guoting’s 687 cursive-script ink manuscript Treatise on Calligraphy, whose first scroll passed through the Qing imperial collection and is now in Taiwan.
