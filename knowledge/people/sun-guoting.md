# 孙过庭

## 中文

### 生平与身份

孙过庭生卒年不详，主要活动在唐初。字虔礼，富阳（今浙江）人，一说江苏人；上海图书馆人物规范档作「吴郡富阳（今浙江富阳县）人」。官率府录事参军。唐代书法家、书法理论家。[故宫博物院，宋拓孙过庭书谱](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html) · [上海图书馆开放数据平台，sunguoting](https://data.library.sh.cn/entity/person/ut4rfolpzuibyprs.html)。籍贯两说并存，本库不取其一。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于唐。

### 书体与师承

故宫记其「工正、行、草书」，为唐代著名书法家、书法理论家。[故宫博物院，宋拓孙过庭书谱](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html)台北故宫《书谱》释文开篇作「吴郡孙过庭撰」，卷末署「垂拱三年（西元六八七年）写记」，据此将他记为撰文并书写者。[国立故宫博物院，唐孙过庭书谱卷](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail?dep=P&id=14900)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《书谱》](../works/shupu.md)为孙过庭垂拱三年（687）撰并书的草书纸本卷，本幅纵26.5厘米、横900.8厘米，现藏台北故宫博物院；作品层以这卷墨迹为实物依据。其释文开篇作「书谱卷上」，正文自称六篇两卷；故宫宋拓页称下卷已佚，台北故宫页未说明是否另有传世下卷墨迹，两种记述不合并。[国立故宫博物院，唐孙过庭书谱卷](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail?dep=P&id=14900) · [故宫博物院，宋拓孙过庭书谱](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html)

故宫另藏北宋大观年间内府所刻《太清楼书谱》宋拓，馆方称其为海内孤本、原石早佚。这是另一件实物，不与本库所据的台北墨迹卷混称，也不把宋拓的递藏与馆号移作墨迹信息。[故宫博物院，宋拓孙过庭书谱](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html)


### 可临之作

- [《书谱》](../works/shupu.md)：孙过庭687年撰并书的草书《书谱》墨迹，上卷原藏清内府，今在台湾。

## English

### Life and identity

Sun Guoting's dates are unknown; he was active mainly in the early Tang. His courtesy name was Qianli; he was a native of Fuyang in Zhejiang, some say of Jiangsu, while the Shanghai Library authority record gives "Fuyang in Wu commandery, present-day Fuyang county, Zhejiang." He served as recorder in the household of the crown prince and was a Tang calligrapher and theorist. [Palace Museum, Song rubbing of Sun Guoting's Shupu](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html) · [Shanghai Library Open Data, Sun Guoting](https://data.library.sh.cn/entity/person/ut4rfolpzuibyprs.html). Both native-place readings are kept. By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Tang.

### Scripts and models

The Palace Museum records that he was "skilled in regular, running and cursive script" and was a noted Tang calligrapher and theorist. [Palace Museum, Shupu](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html) The National Palace Museum's [Shupu](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail?dep=P&id=14900) opens with "Sun Guoting of Wu commandery writes [this treatise]" and closes with a colophon dating the writing to 687; the work record therefore credits him as both author and calligrapher.

### Works in this library

The library’s [*Essay on Calligraphy*](../works/shupu.md) is Sun Guoting’s cursive-script paper manuscript of 687, 26.5 cm high and 900.8 cm long, now at the National Palace Museum in Taipei; this is the physical witness used for the work record. Its transcription opens “Shupu, upper scroll”, and the text describes six chapters in two juan. The Palace Museum’s rubbing page calls the lower juan lost, while the National Palace Museum page does not say whether another lower-scroll manuscript survives; the two accounts are not merged. [National Palace Museum, Essay on Calligraphy by Sun Guoting](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail?dep=P&id=14900) · [Palace Museum, Song rubbing of Sun Guoting’s Shupu](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html)

The Palace Museum also holds a Song rubbing of the Taqinglou edition carved for the Northern Song imperial household during the Daguan era; the original stones are lost, and the museum calls this rubbing “the only copy under heaven”. It is a separate physical witness, not the Taipei manuscript, and its catalogue number and custody history are not transferred to that manuscript.[Palace Museum, Song rubbing of Sun Guoting’s Shupu](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234584.html)

### Works to copy
