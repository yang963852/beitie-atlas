# 智永

## 中文

### 生平与身份

智永生卒年不详，名法极，会稽（浙江绍兴）人，南朝陈、隋年间僧人，因居永欣寺人称「永禅师」；俗姓王，是王羲之第七世孙。[故宫博物院，宋拓隋智永真草千字文册](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228599.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于隋；馆方原文称「南朝陈、隋年间」，两存。

### 书体与师承

故宫说明记其承祖法、草楷皆工，相传于永欣寺潜研书法三十年，书《千字文》八百余本；又引张怀瓘《书断》列其章草、草书入妙品、隶书入能品，谓「微尚有道之风，半得右军之肉，兼能诸体，于草最优」。[故宫博物院，宋拓隋智永真草千字文册](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228599.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

故宫著录《真草千字文》有三种可靠本子：唐代流传日本的墨迹本、北宋大观三年（1109）薛嗣昌刻于西安的「关中本」、南宋《群玉堂帖》四十行残本；关中本凡八石、每石二十七行，原石今存西安碑林。馆方称三本风格一致。本库未为这些本子另建作品档案，此处只记馆方所述。[故宫博物院，宋拓隋智永真草千字文册](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228599.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Zhiyong's dates are unknown. His name was Faji; he came from Kuaiji, present-day Shaoxing in Zhejiang, and was a monk of the Chen and Sui period, known as Monk Yong for his residence at the Yongxin Temple. He took the secular surname Wang and was the seventh-generation descendant of Wang Xizhi. [Palace Museum, Song rubbing of the Thousand Character Classic in regular and cursive](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228599.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Sui; the museum's own wording "Chen and Sui" is kept alongside.

### Scripts and models

The museum records that he inherited the family method and excelled at both cursive and regular script, that he is said to have spent thirty years at the Yongxin Temple and written more than eight hundred copies of the Thousand Character Classic, and quotes Zhang Huaiguan's Shuduan listing his draft cursive and cursive in the fine class and his clerical script in the competent class. [Palace Museum, Song rubbing of the Thousand Character Classic](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228599.html)

### Works in this library

The museum names three reliable recensions of the Thousand Character Classic: a Tang ink manuscript transmitted to Japan, the "Guanzhong" version cut by Xue Sichang at Xi'an in 1109, and a forty-line fragment in the Song Qunyutang tie. The Guanzhong stones, eight in number with twenty-seven lines each, are kept at the Xi'an Beilin Museum. The museum says the three agree in style. This library holds no separate work record for them; only the museum's account is given here. [Palace Museum, Song rubbing of the Thousand Character Classic](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228599.html)
