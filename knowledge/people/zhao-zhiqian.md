# 赵之谦

## 中文

### 生平与身份

赵之谦（1829—1884），字益甫，号撝叔，别号悲盦等，浙江绍兴（会稽）人。清咸丰年间举人（上博作咸丰九年，1859），曾历任江西鄱阳、奉新知县（上博另记南城知县）。其书初宗颜真卿，后专意于北碑，篆隶师邓石如，又融化入己法，自成一家；精书画、篆刻，并工金石之学，著有《六朝别字》《补寰宇访碑录》等，为「海派」先驱之一。[故宫博物院，赵之谦篆书急就章轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228366.html) · [上海博物馆，清赵之谦四体书屏](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005002)。字号与官职两馆著录宽窄不一，本库并列。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于清。

### 书体与师承

故宫记其「书初宗颜真卿，后专意于北碑，篆隶师邓石如，又融化入己法，自成一家」，并引时评「撝叔书初师颜平原……实乃邓派之三变也」；又记所收《篆书急就章轴》「书宗邓石如篆法，又融入魏碑笔意，折笔由圆变方」。[故宫博物院，赵之谦篆书急就章轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228366.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《篆书急就章轴》](../works/zhao-jijiu.md)纸本，纵一百一十二点四厘米、横四十六点四厘米，释文为《急就篇》句，末识「史游急就篇，益斋仁兄正。赵之谦」；馆方记「本幅无藏印。具体创作年代不详，但从书风推测当属赵氏晚年书作」。[故宫博物院，赵之谦篆书急就章轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228366.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Zhao Zhiqian (1829–1884), courtesy name Yifu, style name Huishu and later Beiyan among others, was a native of Kuaiji, present-day Shaoxing in Zhejiang. A provincial graduate of the Xianfeng years (the Shanghai Museum gives 1859), he served as magistrate of Poyang and Fengxin in Jiangxi (the Shanghai Museum adds Nancheng). His calligraphy began with Yan Zhenqing, then turned to the Northern steles, taking seal and clerical from Deng Shiru and fusing them into a manner of his own; he was also a painter, seal carver and epigrapher, author of works on Six Dynasties variant characters and a supplement to the Huanyu fangbei lu, and a forerunner of the Shanghai school. [Palace Museum, Zhao Zhiqian's seal-script Jijiu zhang](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228366.html) · [Shanghai Museum, four-script screen](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005002). The two museums give different ranges of names and offices; both are kept. By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Qing.

### Scripts and models

The Palace Museum records that his writing "began with Yan Zhenqing, then turned to the Northern steles, took seal and clerical from Deng Shiru, and fused them into a manner of his own," quoting a contemporary judgement that "Huishu began with Yan and became the third transformation of the Deng school"; it also notes that the Jijiu zhang scroll "follows Deng Shiru's seal method while bringing in Wei stele manner, turning round folds into square ones." [Palace Museum, Jijiu zhang](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228366.html)

### Works in this library

The [seal-script Jijiu zhang scroll](../works/zhao-jijiu.md) held here is ink on paper, 112.4 by 46.4 cm, with text from the Jijiu pian and the inscription "Jijiu pian by Shi You, for the correction of brother Yizhai, Zhao Zhiqian"; the museum notes "no collection seals on this piece; the date is unknown, but the style suggests a late work." [Palace Museum, Jijiu zhang](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228366.html)
