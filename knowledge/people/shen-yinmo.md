# 沈尹默

## 中文

给年轻人谈学书时，沈尹默一面写讲解，一面为他们写范字。姜涛记述，1962年《人民教育》刊有他讨论毛笔练习的文章。他编写的[《甲种本大楷习字帖》](../works/shen-yinmo-dakai-jia.md)由上海教育出版社出版。沿着这一本书，可以把他的教育工作落实到一个可辨认的版本。[姜涛，晚年居沪一节](https://shufa.pku.edu.cn/whsf/8dc5e55fd0e445a781a861c438b81cf9.htm)

他和[赵朴初](zhao-puchu.md)还用诗词相答。沈氏年表在1965年条中把一次唱和放在《兰亭序》真伪讨论的背景下。日后赵朴初在沈氏论书札后题跋，那篇[写于1976年的跋](../works/zhao-puchu-shen-colophon.md)留住了朋友追念他的一个瞬间。[西泠印社年表，1965年条](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf) · [上海博物馆，题跋简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)

两处记载对沈氏卒年有出入。年表列在1971年，上海博物馆的赵跋简介写作1972年。这项分歧保留在此，读者可以分别核对。[年表，1971年条](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf) · [上博藏品简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)

字号、籍贯与官职待考——本库现有来源未记。西泠印社年表逐字记其卒于 1971 年，上海博物馆的赵跋简介则写作 1972 年，两说并存，上文已照录，不取其一。[西泠印社年表](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf) · [上博藏品简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于中华人民共和国时期。

## English

Shen Yinmo gave young learners both accounts of brush practice and characters to copy. Jiang Tao records a 1962 article by Shen in the education journal 人民教育. Shanghai Education Publishing House published his [large regular-script copybook, edition A](../works/shen-yinmo-dakai-jia.md). Together they give a concrete starting point for studying his teaching. [Jiang Tao, section on Shen’s later years in Shanghai](https://shufa.pku.edu.cn/whsf/8dc5e55fd0e445a781a861c438b81cf9.htm)

Shen and [Zhao Puchu](zhao-puchu.md) also answered one another in verse. A chronology places an exchange in 1965 amid debate about the authenticity of the Lanting Preface. Zhao later remembered Shen in a [1976 colophon](../works/zhao-puchu-shen-colophon.md) attached to Shen’s writing about calligraphy. [Xiling chronology, 1965 entry](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf) · [Shanghai Museum, colophon description](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)

The chronology records Shen’s death in 1971. The Shanghai Museum description gives 1972. Both readings are retained here for comparison. [Chronology, 1971 entry](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf) · [Museum description](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)

### Life and identity

Courtesy name, native place and offices remain open: the sources listed for this page do not record them. The Xiling chronology gives 1971 for his death verbatim and the Shanghai Museum description of Zhao's colophon gives 1972; both readings are kept as above and neither is preferred. [Xiling chronology](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf) · [Shanghai Museum, colophon description](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the People's Republic period.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited catalogues do not address them. The [copybook, edition A](../works/shen-yinmo-dakai-jia.md) held here contains regular-script models for teaching and is not a statement about his teachers. [Jiang Tao, section on Shen's later years in Shanghai](https://shufa.pku.edu.cn/whsf/8dc5e55fd0e445a781a861c438b81cf9.htm)

### Works in this library

The library holds the [large regular-script copybook, edition A](../works/shen-yinmo-dakai-jia.md), published by Shanghai Education Publishing House; Jiang Tao records Shen's 1962 article in the education journal Renmin Jiaoyu. A [1976 running-script colophon on Shen's letter on calligraphy](../works/zhao-puchu-shen-colophon.md) was written by Zhao Puchu and is kept on that page.
