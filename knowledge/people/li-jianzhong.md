# 李建中

## 中文

### 生平与身份

李建中（945—1013），字得中，自称岩夫民伯，京兆（河南开封）人，北宋初年书家。太平兴国进士，曾官曹、解、颖、葵四州刺史，进金部员外郎；因掌西京留司御台史，世称「李西台」。[故宫博物院，林逋自书诗卷名词解释中李建中条](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228219.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于北宋。

### 书体与师承

书体与师承待考——本库现有著录未逐字写明其师承。故宫所引《宋史》本传说他「善书札，行笔尤工，多构新体，草、隶、篆籀、八分亦妙。人多摹习，争取以为楷法」，同页又称时人林逋书风「与李建中风骨俊整的书风极为接近」。[故宫博物院，林逋自书诗卷名词解释中李建中条](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228219.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Li Jianzhong (945–1013), courtesy name Dezhong, who called himself Yanfu Minbo, was a native of Jingzhao, present-day Kaifeng in Henan, and a calligrapher of the early Northern Song. He passed the Taiping Xingguo examination, served as prefect of four prefectures and rose to vice director of the Bureau of Finance; from his post at the western capital he was known as Li Xitai. [Palace Museum, Li Jianzhong entry in the notes to Lin Bu's poem scroll](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228219.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Northern Song.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited catalogue does not transcribe them. The Song History passage quoted by the museum says he "was skilled at letters, especially fine in running brushwork, often making new forms, and accomplished in cursive, clerical, seal and eight-part script; many copied him and sought his work as a model." The same page says Lin Bu's style came close to Li's "upright and stern" manner. [Palace Museum, Li Jianzhong entry](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228219.html)
