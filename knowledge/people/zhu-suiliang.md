# 褚遂良

## 中文

褚遂良（596—658，一作 659），字登善，杭州钱塘人，祖籍阳翟。父褚亮是太宗的智囊。贞观十二年虞世南卒，太宗叹「世南殁后，无人可以论书矣」，魏徵把他荐了上去；此后他替内府鉴定王羲之帖，真伪一判一个准。贞观二十三年与长孙无忌同受太宗遗诏辅政，高宗朝进尚书右仆射，封河南郡公，世称褚河南。因坚决反对立武则天为后，贬潭州、桂州，再贬爱州，显庆三年卒于任上，年六十三。[西安碑林博物馆，同州三藏圣教序碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2599)

他的楷书可以分两段看。早年《伊阙佛龛碑》《孟法师碑》刻意欧、虞，方笔多，隶意重，结体端严；晚年《房玄龄碑》《雁塔圣教序》转为空明飞动，笔画起伏有了节奏。清人李宗瀚说「遒丽处似虞，端劲处似欧」，把两头都点到了。[澎湃新闻，同州三藏圣教序碑](https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_12677129) · [西安碑林博物馆，同州三藏圣教序碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2599)

库内原只有[《雁塔圣教序》](../works/yanta.md)一件明拓。今补[《同州圣教序》](../works/tongzhou-shengjiao-xu.md)：与《雁塔》同文而异石，龙朔三年（663）立于同州，1972 年移入碑林。顾炎武疑此碑非褚遂良亲书——立碑时他已去世五年——这疑问至今没有定论，读的时候不妨把它当作「同一文本的另一种刻法」，而不是第二件真迹。[澎湃新闻，同州三藏圣教序碑](https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_12677129)

## English

Chu Suiliang (596–658, some sources 659), courtesy name Dengshan, was a native of Qiantang in Hangzhou whose ancestral home was Yangzhai. His father Chu Liang was one of Taizong’s closest advisers. When Yu Shinan died in 638 the emperor lamented that no one remained to discuss calligraphy, and Wei Zheng recommended Chu; thereafter he authenticated Wang Xizhi’s letters for the imperial collection, and judged them without error. In 649 he and Zhangsun Wuji received Taizong’s deathbed charge; under Gaozong he rose to right vice director of the secretariat and was enfeoffed as duke of Henan, hence the name Chu Henan. For opposing the elevation of Wu Zetian he was banished to Tanzhou, then Guizhou, then Aizhou, and died in office in 658, aged sixty-three. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Tongzhou recension of the Sacred Teaching Preface](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2599)

His regular script falls into two phases. The early Yique and Meng Fashi steles follow Ouyang and Yu deliberately: square strokes, heavy clerical traces, strict frames. The late Fang Xuanling stele and the Wild Goose Pagoda preface open into a limpid, moving hand with a rhythm in every rise and fall. Li Zonghan said that “where it is graceful it resembles Yu, where it is firm it resembles Ouyang”, naming both ends at once. [The Paper, Tongzhou recension](https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_12677129) · [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Tongzhou recension](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2599)

The library previously held only a Ming rubbing of the [Wild Goose Pagoda preface](../works/yanta.md). It now adds the [Tongzhou recension](../works/tongzhou-shengjiao-xu.md), the same text on a different stone, erected in 663 at Tongzhou and moved to the Beilin in 1972. Gu Yanwu doubted that Chu wrote it at all, since he had been dead five years by the date on the stone; the question is still open, and the stele is best read as another engraving of one text rather than a second autograph. [The Paper, Tongzhou recension](https://m.thepaper.cn/baijiahao_12677129)
