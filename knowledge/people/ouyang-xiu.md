# 欧阳修

## 中文

### 生平与身份

欧阳修（1007—1072），字永叔，号醉翁，晚年又号六一居士，吉州庐陵（今江西吉安）人，北宋政治家，以文章著称，为唐宋古文八家之一，主修《新唐书》《新五代史》。[故宫博物院，欧阳修行楷书灼艾帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234575.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于北宋。

### 书体与师承

书体与师承待考——本库现有来源未逐字写明其师承。故宫记其「书法字体新丽，神采秀发，自成一家」，并引苏轼评「公用尖笔干墨作方阔字，清眸丰颊，进退晔如」、李东阳跋赞欧书语，是评语而非师承记载。[故宫博物院，欧阳修行楷书灼艾帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234575.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

故宫著录《灼艾帖》卷，纸本册页，纵二十五厘米、横十八厘米，行楷书六行六十九字，馆方考释为欧阳修致学生焦千之的书信；卷后有苏轼、李东阳评语，《墨缘汇观》著录。另国家图书馆著录《集古录》十卷，责任者「(宋)欧阳修撰」，版本为清抄本，藏国家图书馆，善本书号 13257——此著录对象是清抄本，不是《集古录跋尾》墨迹卷。[故宫博物院，欧阳修行楷书灼艾帖卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234575.html) · [国家图书馆，集古录十卷](https://read.nlc.cn/allSearch/searchDetail?fid=411999022425&indexName=data_892&searchType=10024&showType=1)

## English

### Life and identity

Ouyang Xiu (1007–1072), courtesy name Yongshu, style names Zuiweng and, in later life, Liuyi Jushi, was a native of Luling in Jizhou, present-day Ji'an in Jiangxi. A Northern Song statesman, he was famed for his prose as one of the Eight Masters of Tang and Song and compiled the New History of the Tang and the New History of the Five Dynasties. [Palace Museum, Ouyang Xiu's Zhuai ai tie](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234575.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Northern Song.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited sources do not transcribe them. The museum records that "his calligraphy is new and elegant, with a fine spirit, a manner of his own," and quotes Su Shi's description of his pointed brush and dry ink and Li Dongyang's verse in praise of his script — judgements, not a record of teachers. [Palace Museum, Zhuai ai tie](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234575.html)

### Works in this library

The museum records the Zhuai ai tie, ink on paper in album form, 25 by 18 cm, running-regular script in six lines and sixty-nine characters, which the museum reads as a letter to his student Jiao Qianzhi, with judgements by Su Shi and Li Dongyang and a Motuan huiguan record. The National Library catalogues the Jigulu in ten juan, attributed to "Ouyang Xiu of Song," as a Qing manuscript, shelf number 13257 — a bibliographic record, not the autograph colophon scroll. [Palace Museum, Zhuai ai tie](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/234575.html) · [National Library, Jigulu](https://read.nlc.cn/allSearch/searchDetail?fid=411999022425&indexName=data_892&searchType=10024&showType=1)
