# 李邕

## 中文

### 生平与身份

李邕（687—747，故宫展览页作 678—747），字泰和，广陵江都（今江苏扬州）人，李善之子。少知名，荐为左拾遗；天宝初为汲郡北海太守，故人称「李北海」。天宝元年被诬奸赃入狱，宰相李林甫令杖杀，年七十。北海文章、书翰、公直、词辨、义烈、英迈皆过人，时称「六绝」；两《唐书》有传。[故宫博物院，宋拓唐云麾将军李思训碑](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228408.html) · [故宫博物院展览，宋拓唐李邕书云麾将军李秀碑册](https://gugongzhanlan.dpm.org.cn/artShare/12656)。生卒两说并存，本库不取其一；故宫藏品页又把「天宝元年」系于 747 年，与通行纪年（742）不合，此处照录而不擅改。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于唐。

### 书体与师承

故宫小传记其「善正行草书」，并称「其书善学王右军，又得力于王献之，行草尤为卓著」；《宣和书谱》记「邕精于翰墨，行草之名尤著」，又有「碑版照四裔」之誉，传其一生书碑达八百通。[故宫博物院，宋拓唐云麾将军李思训碑](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228408.html) · [故宫博物院展览，李秀碑册](https://gugongzhanlan.dpm.org.cn/artShare/12656)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《麓山寺碑》](../works/lushan-si-bei.md)为唐开元十八年（730）李邕撰书、黄仙鹤刻，碑通高四百一十厘米，正文纵二百七十一厘米、横一百四十三厘米，阳文篆额「麓山寺碑」四字；岳麓书院称其为「最能体现李邕成熟的行书风格的代表作」，因文辞、书法、刻工俱佳而有「北海三绝碑」之称。[岳麓书院，书院碑刻](https://ylsy.hnu.edu.cn/info/1011/9008.htm)。另据故宫著录，《云麾将军李思训碑》为李邕撰并书，行书三十三行、行七十字，篆额四行十二字，为其四十四岁所书。[故宫博物院，宋拓唐云麾将军李思训碑](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228408.html)


### 可临之作

- [《麓山寺碑》](../works/lushan-si-bei.md)：李邕730年叙麓山寺沿革的行书碑，文、书、刻素有「三绝」之称。

## English

### Life and identity

Li Yong (687–747; a Palace Museum exhibition page gives 678–747), courtesy name Taihe, was a native of Jiangdu in Guangling, present-day Yangzhou in Jiangsu, and son of Li Shan. Known from youth, he was recommended as left reminder; in the early Tianbao years he became governor of Beihai in Jijun, hence the name Li Beihai. In 742 he was falsely accused of corruption, imprisoned, and had killed by order of Chancellor Li Linfu at seventy. His prose, calligraphy, uprightness, eloquence, loyalty and vigour were all judged outstanding, "six excellences" in his day; both Tang histories carry his biography. [Palace Museum, Song rubbing of the Yunhui general Li Sixun stele](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228408.html) · [Palace Museum exhibition, Li Xiu stele album](https://gugongzhanlan.dpm.org.cn/artShare/12656). Both death dates are kept; the collection page also places "the first year of Tianbao" in 747, which conflicts with the usual 742, and is recorded as the museum states it. By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Tang.

### Scripts and models

The museum biography says he was "good at regular, running and cursive script," that "he studied Wang Youjun well and drew on Wang Xianzhi, especially in running-cursive," and the Xuanhe shupu says "Yong was refined in brush and ink, his running-cursive especially famed," with a name for steles "shining to the four frontiers" and a tradition of eight hundred steles in his hand. [Palace Museum, Li Sixun stele](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228408.html) · [Palace Museum exhibition, Li Xiu stele](https://gugongzhanlan.dpm.org.cn/artShare/12656)

### Works in this library

The [Lushan Temple stele](../works/lushan-si-bei.md) held here was composed and written by Li Yong in 730, cut by Huang Xianhe; it stands 410 cm high, its text 271 by 143 cm, with a four-character seal heading. Yuelu Academy calls it "the work that best shows Li Yong's mature running script," known as the "three excellences" stele for text, calligraphy and cutting. [Yuelu Academy, stele inscriptions](https://ylsy.hnu.edu.cn/info/1011/9008.htm). The Palace Museum also records the Yunhui general Li Sixun stele as composed and written by Li Yong, running script in thirty-three lines of seventy characters with a four-line, twelve-character seal heading, written at forty-four. [Palace Museum, Li Sixun stele](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/228408.html)

### Works to copy

- [Stele of Lushan Temple](../works/lushan-si-bei.md): Li Yong’s 730 running-script stele recounting the history of Lushan Monastery, traditionally praised as three excellences of text, calligraphy and carving.
