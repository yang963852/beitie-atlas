# 索靖

## 中文

### 生平与身份

索靖（239—303），字幼安，敦煌（今甘肃敦煌）人，张芝姐之孙，官尚书郎，封安乐亭侯，卒谥「庄」。[故宫博物院，章草书出师颂卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229006.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于西晋。

### 书体与师承

故宫藏品说明记他精章草、承张芝衣钵，书风「险峻坚劲」，并引前人评其书「遒竦」「银钩虿尾」，又记与卫瓘并称、时人谓「瓘得伯英之筋，靖得伯英之肉」。[故宫博物院，章草书出师颂卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229006.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

故宫著录《出师颂》卷，其归属诸说并存：绍兴本无款，后人认为西晋索靖或南朝梁萧子云作，或谓隋贤、唐人书，并无定论，馆方据米友仁跋定为「隋贤书」；宣和本有宋徽宗标题「征西司马索靖书」，一般定为索靖书，也有认为是萧子云书。金运昌文更指出《出师颂》的时代风格已带王羲之烙印，书写年代上限不早于东晋。两说并存，本库不把这一卷写成索靖亲笔。[故宫博物院，章草书出师颂卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229006.html) · [故宫博物院，《出师颂》的艺术定位](https://www.dpm.org.cn/study_detail/98494.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Suo Jing (239–303), courtesy name You'an, was a native of Dunhuang, present-day Dunhuang in Gansu, a grandson of Zhang Zhi's elder sister. He served as a palace gentleman, was enfeoffed as Marquis of Anle, and received the posthumous name Zhuang. [Palace Museum, Chushi song in draft cursive](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229006.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Western Jin.

### Scripts and models

The museum entry records that he excelled at draft cursive, inherited the manner of Zhang Zhi, and that his script was "steep and firm"; it quotes earlier judgements and the pairing with Wei Guan, "Guan took Bo Ying's sinew, Jing took his flesh." [Palace Museum, Chushi song in draft cursive](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229006.html)

### Works in this library

The Chushi song carries several attributions. The Shaoxing version has no signature: it has been assigned to Suo Jing, to Xiao Ziyun of Liang, or to a Sui or Tang hand, with no consensus; the museum follows Mi Youren's colophon in calling it a Sui work. The Xuanhe version bears Emperor Huizong's title "written by Suo Jing, Marshal of the West," generally taken as his, though some read it as Xiao Ziyun. A museum research article adds that the style already bears Wang Xizhi's imprint and that the date cannot be earlier than the Eastern Jin. Both readings are kept; the scroll is not written up as Suo Jing's autograph. [Palace Museum, Chushi song](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229006.html) · [Palace Museum, On the artistic position of the Chushi song](https://www.dpm.org.cn/study_detail/98494.html)
