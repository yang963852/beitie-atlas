# 于右任

## 中文

### 生平与身份

于右任（1879—1964），字伯循，号太平老人，陕西三原人。上海博物馆称其为「中国近现代高等教育奠基人之一」。本页只据该藏品页记录这些生平事实，不补写未见出处的事迹。[上海博物馆，藏品简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160765)

### 书体与师承

馆方称于右任工楷、行、草，倡导「标准草书」。所引页面未载其具体师承；对本库所收《草书五言联》，馆方评为「笔力雄强深厚，以魏碑之法作草，气势磅礴」。[上海博物馆，藏品简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160765)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《草书五言联》](../works/yu-couplet.md)为于右任书写的纸本楹联，纵138.4厘米，横33.7厘米，今藏上海博物馆。款识「民国三十九年十一月于右任」，即1950年11月，时年七十二岁；具体日未记。题跋「万里先生法正」是献词，页面未转录联语正文。藏品由庄长江、庄良有捐赠，捐赠日期未载。[上海博物馆，藏品字段与简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160765)

## English

### Life and identity

Yu Youren (1879–1964), courtesy name Boxun, style name Taiping laoren, was a native of Sanyuan in Shaanxi. The Shanghai Museum calls him one of the founders of modern higher education in China. This page records only these biographical facts from that collection entry and adds no events unsupported by the cited source. [Shanghai Museum, collection introduction](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160765)

### Scripts and models

The museum says that Yu was skilled in regular, running and cursive scripts and advocated “standard cursive script.” The cited page does not name his teachers. Of the library’s [Five-Character Couplet in Cursive Script](../works/yu-couplet.md), it says that the brushwork is forceful and profound, using the method of Wei steles in cursive writing, with majestic momentum. [Shanghai Museum, collection introduction](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160765)

### Works in this library

The library’s [Five-Character Couplet in Cursive Script](../works/yu-couplet.md) is a paper couplet written by Yu Youren, 138.4 cm high and 33.7 cm long, now held by the Shanghai Museum. The inscription “民国三十九年十一月于右任” corresponds to November 1950, when he was seventy-two; no exact day is given. The colophon “万里先生法正” is a dedication, and the page does not transcribe the couplet. Zhuang Changjiang and Zhuang Liangyou donated the object, but the donation date is not stated. [Shanghai Museum, catalogue fields and introduction](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160765)
