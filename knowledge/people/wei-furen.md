# 卫夫人

## 中文

### 生平与身份

生卒、字号与籍贯待考——本库现有馆方来源只记她为王羲之早年之师，未著录其独立生平。[故宫博物院，王羲之词条](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242566.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把她系于东晋。

### 书体与师承

故宫王羲之词条记「他早年以卫夫人（铄）为师，草书学张芝，正书则取法钟繇」，据此可记卫铄为王羲之早年之师；本库现有来源未逐字写明卫夫人自己的师承与书体，故不另补。[故宫博物院，王羲之词条](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242566.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库未为卫夫人关联作品档案。其师承一笔见上文所引故宫词条，除此之外无馆方著录可记，不据旧说补《笔阵图》等归属。


### 可临之作

本页尚未关联可供临写的作品页。
### 本页依据

本页依据：[故宫博物院「王羲之」词条](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242566.html)中「早年以卫夫人（铄）为师」一段；这是王羲之词条而非卫夫人专题传记，也未记她的生卒与籍贯。

## English

### Life and identity

Dates, courtesy name and native place remain open: the museum source available here records her only as Wang Xizhi's early teacher and gives no independent biography. [Palace Museum, Wang Xizhi lemma](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242566.html). By `dynastySource` this page places her in the Eastern Jin.

### Scripts and models

The Palace Museum's Wang Xizhi lemma states that "in his early years he took Lady Wei (Wei Shuo) as his teacher, studied cursive under Zhang Zhi and took regular script from Zhong Yao"; this supports noting Wei Shuo as Wang Xizhi's early teacher. The source does not transcribe her own teacher or script, so nothing further is added. [Palace Museum, Wang Xizhi lemma](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/242566.html)

### Works in this library

The library holds no work record for Lady Wei. The single note of her teaching is quoted above; no attribution such as the Bizhen tu is added without a museum source.
