# 鲜于枢

## 中文

《祭侄文稿》卷后鲜于枢两跋都记从曹彦礼处得卷，但丙戌署款跋记壬午（1282），戊子署款跋记癸未（1283）。两说并列，不合并为一个确定年份，也不据此补出交接地点。

### 生平与身份

生卒、字号与籍贯待考——本库现有来源未记。三条来源都指向《祭侄文稿》卷上的题跋，不含鲜于枢的小传。[故宫，祭侄文稿](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/3?dep=P)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于元。

### 书体与师承

书体与师承待考——本库现有著录未及。丙戌、戊子两跋只记从曹彦礼处得卷的年份，不曾论及他的师承。[丙戌署款跋](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/3?dep=P) · [戊子署款跋](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/3?dep=P)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收的[《祭侄文稿》](../works/jizhi.md)卷后存鲜于枢两跋。两跋都记从曹彦礼处得卷，年份作 1282 与 1283 两说，前文已并列存录；本页不据此补出交接地点或合并年份。

### 书迹可靠性

以下标注仅就本库所收实物与馆方著录而言，是对存世载体的层级说明，不是真伪鉴定结论；◎ 本库有馆方著录为墨迹或原石的一路，○ 本库所见为摹本、拓本或刻帖，✗ 传说人物或后世伪托。本库现有 29 份书家档案均不标 ✗。

◎ 本库所收[《祭侄文稿》](../works/jizhi.md)实物记录为墨迹本。鲜于枢两跋写在卷后，两跋所记得卷年份不同；把两跋归为同一次得卷是编辑推定，交接地点未详，这一点保留待考。本页所标 ◎ 指所收墨迹本的载体，不是对两跋归组的鉴定结论。

## English

Two colophons by Xianyu Shu on the Draft Requiem both say that he received the scroll from Cao Yanli, but the bingxu colophon gives 1282 and the wuzi colophon gives 1283. The project keeps both readings and does not supply a place of transfer.

### Life and identity

Dates, courtesy name and native place remain open: the sources listed for this page do not record them. All three sources point to colophons on the Draft Requiem and carry no biography of Xianyu Shu. [Palace Museum, Draft Requiem](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/3?dep=P). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Yuan.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited catalogues do not address them. The two colophons record only the years in which the scroll was received from Cao Yanli and say nothing about his teachers. [Bingxu colophon](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/3?dep=P) · [Wuzi colophon](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/3?dep=P)

### Works in this library

The library holds the [Draft Requiem](../works/jizhi.md) with two colophons by Xianyu Shu at its end. Both say he received the scroll from Cao Yanli, with the years given as 1282 and 1283; both readings are kept above, and this page supplies neither a merged year nor a place of transfer.
