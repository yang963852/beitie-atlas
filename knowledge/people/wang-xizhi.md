# 王羲之

## 中文

《兰亭序》传统上归于王羲之。故宫博物院将神龙本列为唐摹本，并在同一页面讨论摹者与神龙印的年代。读这一页时，可把「王羲之其文」与「某位唐人摹写」分开记。

本库另收其尺牍数种，皆以唐或唐代以前的摹本传世：《远宦帖》为双钩廓填，《平安何如奉橘三帖》的摹写年代尚有两说，《行穰帖》为唐代双钩摹本，《上虞帖》《寒切帖》馆方均定为唐摹本。小楷《乐毅论》《黄庭经》则只有宋拓刻本，所读不是墨迹。《宣示表》刻本多称出自他的临本，此说属传称。天津博物馆说其真迹早已毁佚，今日可见唯钩摹墨本与法帖拓本二种。

### 生平与身份

生卒、字号与籍贯待考——本库现有来源未记。本页所收著录都是各帖的馆藏说明，没有一条是王羲之的小传。馆方英文说明把他的生卒写作 321—379，与中文页及通行系年不同，两说并存，本库不取其一。[天津博物馆，寒切帖](https://www.tjbwg.cn/cn/collectionInfo.aspx?id=2384)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于东晋。

### 书体与师承

书体与师承待考——本库现有著录未及。《平安何如奉橘三帖》的著录列有书体字段，并记录卷中摹出的开皇十八年（598）题记，那是摹本上的信息，不是他的传记。[故宫，平安何如奉橘三帖](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/1?dep=P)。《宣示表》刻本多称出自他的临本，这一条仍是传称。[故宫，谈《石渠宝笈·续编》中的《锺王楷帖》](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收有[《兰亭序》](../works/lanting.md)、[《快雪时晴帖》](../works/kuaixue.md)、[《十七帖》](../works/shiqi-tie.md)、[《万岁通天帖》](../works/wansui-tongtian-tie.md)、[《远宦帖》](../works/yuanhuan.md)、[《平安何如奉橘三帖》](../works/pingan-hero-fengju.md)、[《行穰帖》](../works/xingrang.md)、[《上虞帖》](../works/shangyu.md)、[《寒切帖》](../works/hanqie.md)、[《乐毅论》](../works/leyilun.md)、[《黄庭经》](../works/huangting.md)与[《宣示表》](../works/xuanshi-biao.md)。馆方把这些分别著录为摹本、拓本或传称的临本；这里只说本库收了什么，不把某一件当作他亲笔的定本。

### 书迹可靠性

以下标注仅就本库所收实物与馆方著录而言，是对存世载体的层级说明，不是真伪鉴定结论；◎ 本库有馆方著录为墨迹或原石的一路，○ 本库所见为摹本、拓本或刻帖，✗ 传说人物或后世伪托。本库现有 29 份书家档案均不标 ✗。

○ 本库所收王羲之诸件皆为摹本或拓本：神龙本《兰亭序》为唐摹，[《远宦帖》](../works/yuanhuan.md)为双钩廓填，[《平安何如奉橘三帖》](../works/pingan-hero-fengju.md)的摹写年代两说并存，[《行穰帖》](../works/xingrang.md)为唐双钩摹本，[《上虞帖》](../works/shangyu.md)与[《寒切帖》](../works/hanqie.md)馆方定为唐摹本，[《乐毅论》](../works/leyilun.md)、[《黄庭经》](../works/huangting.md)只有宋拓刻本。[《宣示表》](../works/xuanshi-biao.md)刻本多称据其临本摹刻，此说属传称。天津博物馆说其真迹早已毁佚。[天津博物馆，寒切帖](https://www.tjbwg.cn/cn/collectionInfo.aspx?id=2384)

## English

The Preface to the Orchid Pavilion is traditionally attributed to Wang Xizhi. The Palace Museum catalogues the Shenlong copy as a Tang manuscript copy and discusses both the copyist and the date of the Shenlong seal. Keep the author and the later copyist as separate claims.

The library also holds several of his letters, all through Tang or pre-Tang tracing copies: the Yuanhuan Letter is a double-outline tracing, the period of the Three Letters Ping'an, Heru and Fengju is still given two ways, the Xingrang Letter is a Tang double-outline copy, and the Shangyu and Hanqie letters are both called Tang tracing copies by their museums. The small-script On Yue Yi and Yellow Court Classic survive only as Song rubbings, not ink. The engravings of the Memorial of Declaration are often said to reproduce his transcription, a traditional attribution. The Tianjin Museum states that his autographs were destroyed long ago, leaving traced ink copies and engraved modelbooks as what can be read today.

### Life and identity

Dates, courtesy name and native place remain open: the sources listed for this page do not record them. The nine catalogued sources here are entries for individual works, none of them a biography of Wang Xizhi. The museum's English caption gives 321–379 while the Chinese page and the usual chronology differ; both readings are kept and neither is preferred here. [Tianjin Museum, Hanqie letter](https://www.tjbwg.cn/cn/collectionInfo.aspx?id=2384). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Eastern Jin.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited catalogues do not address them. The record for the Three Letters Ping'an, Heru and Fengju lists a script field and a copied Kaihuang 18 (598) colophon, but that information belongs to the tracing copy, not to a biography of him. [Palace Museum, Three Letters](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/1?dep=P). The engravings of the Memorial of Declaration are often said to reproduce his transcription; this remains a traditional attribution. [Palace Museum, on the Zhong-Wang album](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf)
