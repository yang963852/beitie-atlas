# 颜真卿

## 中文

读颜真卿，可以先并看两方碑。在写寺院建塔的[《多宝塔感应碑》](../works/duobao-ta-bei.md)里，文字出自岑勋，颜真卿应楚金之请书丹。看颜书之前，先在图上找徐浩写的碑额，便能分清同一块石上两个人的笔迹。[西安碑林博物馆，多宝塔感应碑](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=147)

到了家族的[《颜勤礼碑》](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)，撰文、书写都由颜真卿承担。碑题里的颜勤礼是他的曾祖父。从一通碑读到另一通，连着问“谁撰、谁书、写给谁”，两篇文字的来由就清楚了。[西安碑林博物馆，颜勤礼碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2582&c=View&m=home)

还可以沿三种不同的载体读颜书。浯溪崖壁上的[《大唐中兴颂》](../catalogue/datang-zhongxing-song.md)由元结撰文、颜真卿书写。台北故宫著录的[《祭侄文稿》](../catalogue/jizhi.md)是一卷行书，馆方释文标出了涂改处。北京故宫的[《麻姑仙坛记》](../catalogue/magu.md)则可循翻刻的拓本去读。另有一块[《颜氏家庙碑》](../catalogue/yan-jiamiao-bei.md)，建中元年（780）为他父亲颜惟贞而立，篆额出李阳冰之手，是现存最晚之作。[湖南省政府，浯溪碑林「历史寻迹」](https://hunan.gov.cn/hnszf/c101484/202108/t20210830_20409590.html) · [台北故宫，藏品基本资料与释文](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/3?dep=P) · [北京故宫，麻姑仙坛记](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234172.html) · [西安碑林博物馆，颜氏家庙碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2615)

### 生平与身份

生卒、字号与籍贯待考——本库现有来源未记。六条著录记的是各碑与文稿的分工，不含颜真卿的小传；上文「颜勤礼是他的曾祖父」沿用碑林条目原文。[西安碑林博物馆，颜勤礼碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2582&c=View&m=home)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于唐。

### 书体与师承

书体与师承待考——本库现有著录未及。上文所记「谁撰、谁书」是作品的撰书分工，不是关于他师承的记载。

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《多宝塔感应碑》](../works/duobao-ta-bei.md)、[《颜勤礼碑》](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md)、[《大唐中兴颂》](../catalogue/datang-zhongxing-song.md)、[《祭侄文稿》](../catalogue/jizhi.md)、[《麻姑仙坛记》](../catalogue/magu.md)与[《颜氏家庙碑》](../catalogue/yan-jiamiao-bei.md)。湖南省政府记《大唐中兴颂》于 771 年刻于峿台崖壁、元结文、颜真卿书；碑林记《颜氏家庙碑》780 年立、李阳冰篆额、982 年重立。[湖南省政府，浯溪碑林](https://hunan.gov.cn/hnszf/c101484/202108/t20210830_20409590.html) · [西安碑林博物馆，颜氏家庙碑](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2615)

## English

Two steles offer a way into Yan Zhenqing’s work. For the [Duobao Pagoda Stele](../works/duobao-ta-bei.md), Cen Xun composed the text and the monk Chujin invited Yan to write it. Xu Hao wrote the heading. Look for it first to distinguish the two hands on the stone. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Duobao entry](https://beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=147)

For the [Stele of Yan Qinli](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md), Yan Zhenqing both composed and wrote the text, commemorating his great-grandfather. Asking who composed each inscription, who wrote it and whom it concerns brings its occasion into view. [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Yan Qinli entry](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2582&c=View&m=home)

Three other works let us follow his writing across different surfaces. At Wuxi, Yuan Jie’s [Eulogy of the Tang Restoration](../catalogue/datang-zhongxing-song.md) was carved on a cliff in Yan’s hand. The National Palace Museum in Taipei transcribes cancelled words in Yan’s running-script [Draft Requiem for a Nephew](../catalogue/jizhi.md). The Palace Museum in Beijing catalogues the [Record of the Immortal Altar](../catalogue/magu.md) in an impression taken from a recarving. One more stone, the [Stele of the Yan Family Shrine](../catalogue/yan-jiamiao-bei.md) of 780, was raised for his father Yan Weizhen with a seal-script heading by Li Yangbing; it is his latest surviving work. [Hunan government, Wuxi account](https://hunan.gov.cn/hnszf/c101484/202108/t20210830_20409590.html) · [National Palace Museum, scroll and transcription](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/3?dep=P) · [Palace Museum, Magu entry](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234172.html) · [Xi’an Beilin Museum, Yan family shrine stele](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?m=home&c=View&a=index&aid=2615)

### Life and identity

Dates, courtesy name and native place remain open: the sources listed for this page do not record them. The six catalogues concern the composition and writing of individual works and carry no biography; the note that Yan Qinli was his great-grandfather is kept from the Beilin entry. [Xi'an Beilin Museum, Yan Qinli entry](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2582&c=View&m=home). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Tang.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited catalogues do not address them. The "who composed, who wrote" account above describes the division of labour on individual works, not a statement about his teachers.

### Works in this library

The library holds the [Duobao Pagoda Stele](../works/duobao-ta-bei.md), [Stele of Yan Qinli](../works/yan-qinli-bei.md), [Eulogy of the Tang Restoration](../catalogue/datang-zhongxing-song.md), [Draft Requiem for a Nephew](../catalogue/jizhi.md), [Record of the Immortal Altar](../catalogue/magu.md) and [Stele of the Yan Family Shrine](../catalogue/yan-jiamiao-bei.md). The Hunan government records the Eulogy carved on the Wuxi cliff in 771, composed by Yuan Jie and written by Yan; the Beilin records the family shrine stele of 780 with a heading by Li Yangbing and re-erection in 982.
