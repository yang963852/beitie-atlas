# 王僧虔

## 中文

### 生平与身份

王僧虔（426—485），字简穆，琅邪临沂人，王羲之四世族孙，南朝齐书法家，官至尚书令。[临沂市纪委监委网站，王祥及其后人名人介绍](https://www.ymlz.gov.cn/info/1077/19705.htm)。需说明：本条来源是政府网站的地方名人资料，不是馆方文物著录；「简穆」亦为其谥号，该页作字，本库照录并注此异。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于南朝。

### 书体与师承

该页记其「工真、行书。书承祖法，丰厚淳朴而有骨力」，并引《齐书》本传「僧虔善隶楷书，宋文帝见其书素扇，叹曰：非惟迹逾子敬，方当器雅过之」，又引张怀瓘《书断》「祖述小王，尤尚古直」、窦臮《述书赋》「致丰富，得能失刚，满目辉光」。[临沂市纪委监委网站，王祥及其后人名人介绍](https://www.ymlz.gov.cn/info/1077/19705.htm)

### 代表作与本库关联

该页记其「墨迹有《王琰帖》。著有《论书》等」。本库未为《王琰帖》单列作品档案；台北故宫数位典藏的王僧虔法帖著录页本次多次无法打开，故不据检索摘要写成定论。[临沂市纪委监委网站，王祥及其后人名人介绍](https://www.ymlz.gov.cn/info/1077/19705.htm)

## English

### Life and identity

Wang Sengqian (426–485), courtesy name Jianmu, was a native of Linyi in Langya, a fourth-generation descendant of Wang Xizhi, a calligrapher of the Southern Qi, and rose to director of the Secretariat. [Linyi Discipline Inspection Commission site, Wang Xiang and his descendants](https://www.ymlz.gov.cn/info/1077/19705.htm). Note that this source is a local worthies page on a government site, not a museum object record; "Jianmu" is also his posthumous name, which the page gives as a courtesy name, and both are recorded. By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Southern Dynasties.

### Scripts and models

The page records that he "was skilled in regular and running script, inheriting the family method, rich and plain with bone strength," and quotes the Qi shu biography: "Sengqian was good at clerical and regular script; Emperor Wen of Song, seeing his writing on a white fan, sighed that not only did his trace surpass Zijing, his bearing would too." It also quotes Zhang Huaiguan's Shuduan and Dou Ji's Shushu fu. [Linyi site, Wang Sengqian](https://www.ymlz.gov.cn/info/1077/19705.htm)

### Works in this library

The page records that "his ink traces include the Wang Yan tie; he also wrote the Lunshu." This library holds no separate work record for the Wang Yan tie; the National Palace Museum's digital records for his modelbook leaves could not be opened during this check, so nothing is written up from search summaries. [Linyi site, Wang Sengqian](https://www.ymlz.gov.cn/info/1077/19705.htm)
