# 邓文原

## 中文

### 生平与身份

邓文原（1258—1328），字善之，绵州（今四川绵阳）人。曾官翰林修撰，预修《成宗实录》，历任江浙儒学提举、国子祭酒、经筵官等。故宫博物院藏品页末的小传列有这些生卒、字号、籍贯与职官。[故宫博物院，邓文原章草书急就章卷·邓文原](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

### 书体与师承

馆方称邓文原“擅楷、行、草书，尤以章草著称于世，与赵孟頫、鲜于枢齐名”。本库另有[赵孟頫](zhao-mengfu.md)与[鲜于枢](xianyu-shu.md)页；这里只复述馆方小传，不另增师承。该页没有说明邓文原承皇象一系，本记录不作此断言。[故宫博物院，邓文原章草书急就章卷·邓文原](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《章草书急就章卷》](../works/deng-jijiuzhang.md)为纸本，纵23.3厘米，横368.7厘米，故宫藏文物号故00002791。卷后自识“为理仲雍书于大都庆寿寺僧房”，署大德三年三月十日（1299），时年42岁。所书文本为史游《急就篇》，书写者为邓文原。馆方称此卷为其早年书，并引张雨跋“早年大合作”之评；张雨跋未记年，本页不补。[故宫博物院，邓文原章草书急就章卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Deng Wenyuan (1258–1328), courtesy name Shanzhi, was a native of Mianzhou, present-day Mianyang in Sichuan. He served as a Hanlin compiler, participated in compiling the *Chengzong shilu*, and held offices including Jiangzhe Confucianism supervisor, National University libationer and classics lecturer. The Palace Museum’s biography at the end of its object page records these dates, courtesy name, native place and offices.[Palace Museum, Deng Wenyuan’s clerical-cursive Jijiu Zhang, biography of Deng Wenyuan](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

### Scripts and models

The museum says that Deng was “skilled in regular, running and cursive scripts, and especially celebrated for clerical cursive, ranked alongside Zhao Mengfu and Xianyu Shu”. This library also has pages for [Zhao Mengfu](zhao-mengfu.md) and [Xianyu Shu](xianyu-shu.md). The biography does not describe Deng’s teacher lineage, and this record does not claim a line of transmission from Huangxiang.[Palace Museum, Deng Wenyuan’s clerical-cursive Jijiu Zhang, biography of Deng Wenyuan](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)

### Works in this library

The library’s [*Jijiu Zhang in Clerical Cursive Script*](../works/deng-jijiuzhang.md) is on paper, 23.3 cm high and 368.7 cm long, held by the Palace Museum under 故00002791. The self-note reads “Written for Li Zhongyong in a monk’s room at Qingshou Temple in Dadu” and is dated 大德三年三月十日 (1299), when Deng was forty-two. Shi You authored the *Jijiu pian* text; Deng wrote the calligraphy. The museum calls the scroll an early work and quotes Zhang Yu’s judgment “a great work of his early years”. Zhang Yu’s colophon is undated on the page, so no year is supplied.[Palace Museum, Deng Wenyuan’s clerical-cursive Jijiu Zhang](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231875.html)
