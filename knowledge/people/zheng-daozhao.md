# 郑道昭

## 中文

### 生平与身份

郑道昭（?—516），字僖伯，自号中岳先生，北魏荥阳开封人。魏孝文帝时始为官，历任秘书郎、秘书丞兼中书侍郎、中书郎、通直散骑常侍、国子监祭酒、秘书监、荥阳邑中正等职；永平年间任光州刺史兼平东将军，后任青州刺史兼平东将军，复为秘书监加平南将军；熙平元年（516）卒，谥文恭。[烟台市委党史研究院，魏碑体鼻祖郑道昭](https://dsyjy.yantai.gov.cn/art/2010/11/15/art_1324_377720.html)。生年馆方作「?」，本库照录，不补 455。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于北朝。

### 书体与师承

烟台政府页称其为「魏碑体鼻祖」，记其「尤工书法」，笔力圆劲苍健、结构严谨、运笔娴熟自然，郑文公上下二碑被誉为「隶楷之极」。[烟台市委党史研究院，魏碑体鼻祖郑道昭](https://dsyjy.yantai.gov.cn/art/2010/11/15/art_1324_377720.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《郑文公碑》](../works/zheng-wengong.md)即郑文公上碑与下碑，刻于天柱、云峰二山，碑文颂扬其父才智功德；其题刻分布于云峰山摩崖二十处、城东大基山十五处、天柱山八处。云峰山北魏刻石二十三处，1988 年由国务院公布为全国重点文物保护单位，隶属莱州市博物馆管理。[烟台市委党史研究院，魏碑体鼻祖郑道昭](https://dsyjy.yantai.gov.cn/art/2010/11/15/art_1324_377720.html) · [莱州市人民政府，云峰山摩崖石刻保护工作情况](https://www.laizhou.gov.cn/art/2021/11/12/art_23203_2932809.html)


### 可临之作

- [《郑文公碑》](../works/zheng-wengong.md)：郑道昭为父记功的北魏摩崖刻石，上碑在平度天柱山、下碑在莱州云峰山。

## English

### Life and identity

Zheng Daozhao (birth unknown; died 516), courtesy name Xibo, styled himself Zhongyue xiansheng, was a native of Xingyang Kaifeng in the Northern Wei. He entered office under Emperor Xiaowen and served as secretary, assistant director of the secretariat and concurrent vice director of the Chancellery, vice director of the Secretariat, attendant, libationer of the Imperial Academy, director of the Secretariat and rectifier of Xingyang; under the Yongping reign he was governor of Guangzhou and general of the east, later governor of Qingzhou with the same title, then director of the Secretariat with the title general of the south; he died in 516 and received the posthumous name Wengong. [Yantai Party History Institute, Zheng Daozhao, ancestor of the stele style](https://dsyjy.yantai.gov.cn/art/2010/11/15/art_1324_377720.html). The birth year is given as unknown and is kept so. By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Northern Dynasties.

### Scripts and models

The Yantai government page calls him "the ancestor of the stele style," records that he "was especially skilled at calligraphy," with round, vigorous strokes, strict structure and easy movement, and says his two Zheng Wen Gong steles are praised as "the ultimate of clerical and regular." [Yantai Party History Institute, Zheng Daozhao](https://dsyjy.yantai.gov.cn/art/2010/11/15/art_1324_377720.html)

### Works in this library

The [Zheng Wen Gong stele](../works/zheng-wengong.md) held here is the upper and lower steles carved on Mount Tianzhu and Mount Yunfeng, praising his father's wisdom and merit; his inscriptions are distributed across twenty cliff sites on Yunfeng, fifteen on Daji and eight on Tianzhu. Twenty-three Northern Wei carvings on Yunfeng were declared a national key cultural relic in 1988 and are managed by the Laizhou Museum. [Yantai Party History Institute, Zheng Daozhao](https://dsyjy.yantai.gov.cn/art/2010/11/15/art_1324_377720.html) · [Laizhou government, conservation of the Yunfeng cliff carvings](https://www.laizhou.gov.cn/art/2021/11/12/art_23203_2932809.html)

### Works to copy
