# 赵佶

## 中文

### 生平与身份

赵佶（1082—1135），即宋徽宗，号宣和主人。北宋第八位皇帝，元符三年即位，在位二十五年（1101—1125）。[故宫博物院，听琴图轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_16.html) · [上海博物馆，北宋赵佶楷书千字文卷](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00000521)。需说明：故宫另一份 PDF 研究文章作「北宋第七位皇帝」，与本页「第八位」不同，两说并存待核。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于北宋。

### 书体与师承

故宫记其「书法自成一体，称『瘦金体』，笔画瘦劲尖峭，极具特色」；上博记其「能画擅书，工楷、草。楷书融合褚遂良、薛曜笔意，独创一体，自名『瘦金体』」。[故宫博物院，听琴图轴](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_16.html) · [上海博物馆，楷书千字文卷](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00000521)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《秾芳诗帖》](../works/nongfang-shitie.md)为绢本大字瘦金书，界朱丝栏，纵二十七点二厘米、横二百六十五点九厘米，今藏台北故宫博物院，为其传世惟一大字作品（据故宫研究文章，该 PDF 本次未解析，仅作已有著录之引述）。另上博藏《楷书千字文卷》书于崇宁三年（1104），时年二十三，赐宦官童贯。[上海博物馆，楷书千字文卷](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00000521)

## English

### Life and identity

Zhao Ji (1082–1135), Emperor Huizong of Song, styled himself Xuanhe zhuren. He was the eighth emperor of the Northern Song, ascending in 1100 and reigning twenty-five years (1101–1125). [Palace Museum, Listening to the Qin](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_16.html) · [Shanghai Museum, Zhao Ji's regular-script Thousand Character Classic](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00000521). A Palace Museum PDF elsewhere calls him the seventh emperor, differing from the eighth given here; both readings are kept. By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Northern Song.

### Scripts and models

The Palace Museum records that "his calligraphy formed a manner of its own, called the Thin Gold script, lean, sharp and distinctive"; the Shanghai Museum says he "could paint and was skilled at writing, in regular and cursive; his regular script fused the manners of Chu Suiliang and Xue Yao into a style of his own, which he named the Thin Gold script." [Palace Museum, Listening to the Qin](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_16.html) · [Shanghai Museum, Thousand Character Classic](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00000521)

### Works in this library

The [Nongfang poem](../works/nongfang-shitie.md) held here is a large-character Thin Gold manuscript on silk with ruled lines, 27.2 by 265.9 cm, now in the National Palace Museum, Taipei, and his only surviving large-character work (per a Palace Museum research article whose PDF was not parsed in this check, so quoted only as an existing record). The Shanghai Museum's regular-script Thousand Character Classic was written in 1104 at twenty-three and given to the eunuch Tong Guan. [Shanghai Museum, Thousand Character Classic](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00000521)
