# 吴昌硕

## 中文

### 生平与身份

吴昌硕（1844—1927），初名俊卿（故宫作原名俊），字昌硕，号缶庐、苦铁，晚号大聋，浙江安吉人，寓居上海。1913 年被公推为西泠印社首任社长。书法初师颜真卿，后法钟繇、王羲之，得力于石鼓文；55 岁被荐任安东知县，为官仅一月便弃官而去；1927 年 11 月 29 日卒于上海寓所，享年八十四。诗书画印博采众长，被誉为「四绝」。[西泠印社，吴昌硕（第一任社长）](https://www.xlys.org.cn/waplrsc/4256.jhtml) · [故宫博物院，吴昌硕篆书八言联](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231408.html) · [上海博物馆，清吴昌硕石鼓文八言联](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005001)。三处对名与字的先后表述不一，本库并列不合并。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于清；其卒年已入民国，此处只标主要活动朝代。

### 书体与师承

西泠印社记其「书法初师颜真卿，后法钟繇、王羲之，得力于石鼓文，笔力遒劲，气势磅礴」；故宫记其「书法一变前人成法，风格独特，长于摹写石鼓文，并以此擅名于近代书坛」；上博称其「工书法，擅写『石鼓文』，用笔结体，朴茂雄健，古气盘旋，能破陈规，自成一家」。[西泠印社，吴昌硕](https://www.xlys.org.cn/waplrsc/4256.jhtml) · [故宫博物院，篆书八言联](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231408.html) · [上海博物馆，石鼓文八言联](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005001)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《吴昌硕临石鼓文轴》](../works/wuchangshuo-lin-shiguwen.md)为纸本篆书，款署乙卯（1915），时年七十一，临石鼓第三鼓「田车」篇；故宫另藏《篆书八言联》，集石鼓文，书于民国六年（1917），时年七十四；上博藏《石鼓文八言联》，款「七十六岁吴昌硕」。[故宫博物院，吴昌硕篆书八言联](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231408.html) · [上海博物馆，石鼓文八言联](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005001)

## English

### Life and identity

Wu Changshuo (1844–1927), originally named Junqing (the Palace Museum gives Jun), courtesy name Changshuo, style names Fouliu and Kutie, in later years Dalong, was a native of Anji in Zhejiang who lived in Shanghai. In 1913 he was elected first president of the Xiling Seal Art Society. His calligraphy began with Yan Zhenqing, then took Zhong Yao and Wang Xizhi as models and drew its strength from the Stone Drums; at fifty-five he was recommended as magistrate of Anton and abandoned the post after a month; he died in his Shanghai home on 29 November 1927 at eighty-four. Poetry, calligraphy, painting and seal carving were all gathered in him, praised as "four excellences." [Xiling Seal Art Society, Wu Changshuo](https://www.xlys.org.cn/waplrsc/4256.jhtml) · [Palace Museum, Wu Changshuo's eight-character seal couplet](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231408.html) · [Shanghai Museum, Stone Drums eight-character couplet](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005001). The three sources order his names differently; all are kept. By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Qing, noting that his death fell in the Republic.

### Scripts and models

The seal society records that "his calligraphy began with Yan Zhenqing and later took Zhong Yao and Wang Xizhi as models, drawing on the Stone Drums, his strokes vigorous and his momentum majestic"; the Palace Museum says he "changed the methods of his predecessors, with a style of his own, excelling at the Stone Drums and famous in modern calligraphy for them"; the Shanghai Museum says he "was skilled at calligraphy, especially the Stone Drums, his brush and structure rich and strong, full of antique spirit, breaking convention and forming a manner of his own." [Xiling, Wu Changshuo](https://www.xlys.org.cn/waplrsc/4256.jhtml) · [Palace Museum, eight-character couplet](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231408.html) · [Shanghai Museum, Stone Drums couplet](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005001)

### Works in this library

The [Wu Changshuo copy of the Stone Drums](../works/wuchangshuo-lin-shiguwen.md) held here is a seal-script scroll on paper, dated yimao (1915) at seventy-one, copying the third drum, "Tianche." The Palace Museum also holds an eight-character seal couplet gathered from the Stone Drums, written in 1917 at seventy-four; the Shanghai Museum's eight-character couplet is signed "Wu Changshuo at seventy-six." [Palace Museum, eight-character couplet](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231408.html) · [Shanghai Museum, Stone Drums couplet](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005001)
