# 陆游

## 中文

### 生平与身份

陆游（1125—1210），字务观，号放翁，越州山阴（今浙江绍兴）人，南宋书法家，曾官枢密院编修、知夔严二州、宝章阁待制等。[故宫博物院，陆游行草书怀成都十韵诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231256.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于南宋。

### 书体与师承

书体与师承待考——本库现有著录未逐字写明其师承。故宫记其亦擅书法、「笔姿瘦硬清新，风格独具，然为诗名所掩，知之者稀」，是评价而非师承记载。[故宫博物院，陆游行草书怀成都十韵诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231256.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

故宫著录《怀成都十韵诗》卷，纸本行草书，纵三十四点六厘米、横八十二点四厘米，为陆游晚年为友人手录旧作七言古诗，写其五十岁左右在成都做官时的生活；格调豪放跌宕，书风瘦硬通神。卷后有明陆釴、谢铎、程敏政、王鏊、周经、杨循古、沈周题跋，钤乾隆、嘉庆、宣统内府诸玺，《石渠宝笈》著录。本库未另建该作品档案，此处只记馆方所述。[故宫博物院，陆游行草书怀成都十韵诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231256.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Lu You (1125–1210), courtesy name Wuguan, style name Fangweng, was a native of Shanyin in Yuezhou, present-day Shaoxing in Zhejiang, and a calligrapher of the Southern Song. He served as a compiler in the Privy Council, as prefect of Kui and Yan prefectures and as an attendant of the Baozhang Pavilion. [Palace Museum, Lu You's poem scroll on his years in Chengdu](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231256.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Southern Song.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited catalogue does not transcribe them. The museum records that he also excelled at calligraphy, with a "lean, fresh brush and a style of his own, known to few because his poetry overshadowed it" — a judgement, not a record of teachers. [Palace Museum, Lu You's poem scroll](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231256.html)

### Works in this library

The museum records the scroll of Ten Rhymes on Missing Chengdu, ink on paper in running-cursive script, 34.6 by 82.4 cm, a late-life copy of an old seven-character poem written for a friend about his years in Chengdu around fifty. It carries colophons by seven Ming writers, imperial seals of three Qing reigns and a Shiqu baoji record. This library holds no separate work record; only the museum's account is given. [Palace Museum, Lu You's poem scroll](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/231256.html)
