# 王献之

## 中文

王献之（344—386），字子敬，小名官奴，王羲之第七子，官至中书令，人称「王大令」，与父并称「二王」。故宫博物院所附小传记其传世书迹有《鸭头丸帖》《中秋帖》《玉版十三行》等。[故宫，东山松帖页所附小传](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229389.html)

本库所收，没有一件可定为他的手笔：《鸭头丸帖》为唐摹本，《东山松帖》为墨迹页而馆方未论摹本，《万岁通天帖》卷中存其书翰；《中秋帖》旧题其书，故宫指出纸料与用笔与晋人书迹有别；《洛神赋十三行》所存为玉版刻石。读这几条时，可把「原书」与「存世一本」分开记。

## English

Wang Xianzhi (344-386), courtesy name Zijing, seventh son of Wang Xizhi, rose to the Palace Secretariat and was known as the Great Minister Wang; father and son are called the Two Wangs. The Palace Museum's note lists the Duck-Head Pill Letter, the Mid-Autumn Letter and the Jade-version Thirteen Lines among his transmitted works. [Palace Museum, biography attached to the Dongshan Pine leaf](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/229389.html)

Nothing in this library can be called his own hand: the Duck-Head Pill Letter is a Tang tracing copy, the Dongshan Pine leaf is an ink leaf whose copy status the museum leaves open, and one of his letters survives inside the Wan Sui Tong Tian scroll. The Mid-Autumn Letter is traditionally his, but the palace museum notes that its paper and brushwork differ from Jin practice. The Thirteen Lines survive as a stone engraving. In each record the original writing and the surviving witness are kept apart.
