# 赵朴初

## 中文

在上海博物馆著录的一卷书迹中，赵朴初接着故友的字写下了自己的话。卷前是[沈尹默](shen-yinmo.md)的论书札，赵朴初用行书写的[卷后题跋](../works/zhao-puchu-shen-colophon.md)作于1976年。馆方分别量出沈书和赵跋的尺寸，翻看藏品说明时可以据此辨认两段。[上海博物馆，尺寸与简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)

这卷书迹之前，两人已有诗词往来。西泠印社所载年表给了一个可追索的题目：1965年，两人因《兰亭序》真伪之议而有唱和。[年表，1965年条](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf)

## English

On a scroll catalogued by the Shanghai Museum, Zhao Puchu added his own words after those of his friend [Shen Yinmo](shen-yinmo.md). Shen’s text discusses calligraphy. Zhao’s [running-script colophon](../works/zhao-puchu-shen-colophon.md) dates to 1976. The museum gives separate measurements for the two contributions. [Shanghai Museum, measurements and description](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)

The friends had exchanged poems before the colophon was written. A chronology identifies the debate over the authenticity of the Lanting Preface as the subject of an exchange in 1965. [Xiling chronology, 1965 entry](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf)
