# 赵朴初

## 中文

在上海博物馆著录的一卷书迹中，赵朴初接着故友的字写下了自己的话。卷前是[沈尹默](shen-yinmo.md)的论书札，赵朴初用行书写的[卷后题跋](../works/zhao-puchu-shen-colophon.md)作于1976年。馆方分别量出沈书和赵跋的尺寸，翻看藏品说明时可以据此辨认两段。[上海博物馆，尺寸与简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)

这卷书迹之前，两人已有诗词往来。西泠印社所载年表给了一个可追索的题目：1965年，两人因《兰亭序》真伪之议而有唱和。[年表，1965年条](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf)

### 生平与身份

生卒、字号与籍贯待考——本库现有来源未记。上博条目与西泠年表分别记 1976 年题跋与 1965 年唱和，均非赵朴初的小传；年表中的 1971 年卒条指沈尹默，不移作赵朴初。[上海博物馆，尺寸与简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807) · [年表](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于中华人民共和国时期。

### 书体与师承

书体与师承待考——本库现有著录未及。上博藏品题名写作「行书跋」，指的是这卷题跋的书体，不是关于他师承的记载。[上海博物馆，尺寸与简介](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收的[《行书跋沈尹默论书札卷》](../works/zhao-puchu-shen-colophon.md)是赵朴初接沈尹默论书札而写的题跋，作于 1976 年。馆方分别量出两段尺寸；1965 年两人因《兰亭序》真伪之议唱和，见上文所引年表。

## English

On a scroll catalogued by the Shanghai Museum, Zhao Puchu added his own words after those of his friend [Shen Yinmo](shen-yinmo.md). Shen’s text discusses calligraphy. Zhao’s [running-script colophon](../works/zhao-puchu-shen-colophon.md) dates to 1976. The museum gives separate measurements for the two contributions. [Shanghai Museum, measurements and description](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)

The friends had exchanged poems before the colophon was written. A chronology identifies the debate over the authenticity of the Lanting Preface as the subject of an exchange in 1965. [Xiling chronology, 1965 entry](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf)

### Life and identity

Dates, courtesy name and native place remain open: the sources listed for this page do not record them. The Shanghai Museum entry and the Xiling chronology give a 1976 colophon and a 1965 exchange of poems respectively, neither of them a biography of Zhao Puchu; the 1971 death in the chronology belongs to Shen Yinmo, not to Zhao Puchu. [Shanghai Museum, measurements and description](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807) · [Chronology](https://www.xlys.org.cn/wenku/www/202205/17174437ptfn.pdf). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the People's Republic period.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited catalogues do not address them. The museum title "running-script colophon" names the script of this colophon, not a statement about his teachers. [Shanghai Museum, measurements and description](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00160807)

### Works in this library

The museum gives separate measurements for the two contributions, and the 1965 exchange on the authenticity of the Lanting Preface is noted above from the chronology.
