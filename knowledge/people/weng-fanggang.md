# 翁方纲

## 中文

故宫博物院《明拓东汉礼器碑》条目引翁方纲说，认为碑文由七人书写。这是有出处的学术意见；本项目不据此生成七名确定书家。

### 生平与身份

生卒、字号与籍贯待考——本库现有来源未记。故宫《翁方纲》条目 locator 指向「人物小传首段」，但本库所存 source 文件未转录小传内容，故不据此增添传记事实。[故宫，翁方纲](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/239791.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于清。

### 书体与师承

书体与师承待考——本库现有著录未及。故宫《明拓东汉礼器碑》条目引的是他对碑文书写者的学术意见，不是关于他本人师承的记载。[故宫，明拓东汉礼器碑](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234635.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收的[《礼器碑》](../works/liqi-bei.md)条目引翁方纲说碑文由七人书写。这一说法以有出处的学术意见存录，本项目不据此生成七名确定书家。


### 可临之作

- [《礼器碑》](../works/liqi-bei.md)：东汉156年隶书碑，记韩敕修孔庙、铸礼器，原石藏曲阜汉魏碑刻陈列馆。

## English

The Palace Museum’s entry for the Ming rubbing of the Liqi Stele cites Weng Fanggang’s view that seven people wrote the inscription. It is a sourced scholarly opinion; the project does not turn it into seven established calligraphers.

### Life and identity

Dates, courtesy name and native place remain open: the sources listed for this page do not record them. The Palace Museum entry for Weng Fanggang points to "the opening paragraph of the biography," but the source file kept here does not transcribe that biography, so no further biographical facts are added. [Palace Museum, Weng Fanggang](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/239791.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Qing.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited catalogues do not address them. The Palace Museum's entry for the Ming rubbing of the Liqi Stele cites his scholarly opinion on the writers of the inscription, not a statement about his own teachers. [Palace Museum, Ming rubbing of the Liqi Stele](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234635.html)

### Works in this library

The library's record for the [Liqi Stele](../works/liqi-bei.md) cites Weng Fanggang's view that seven people wrote the inscription. The view is kept as a sourced scholarly opinion, and the project does not turn it into seven established calligraphers.
