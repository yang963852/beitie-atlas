# 冯承素

## 中文

神龙本卷后项元汴题记将摹写归于冯承素。故宫博物院在藏品说明中对这一归名及神龙印的年代提出疑问，因此本项目把冯承素列作有争议的摹写者，而不把归名当作定论。

## English

Xiang Yuanbian’s colophon on the Shenlong copy attributes the copying to Feng Chengsu. The Palace Museum questions both that attribution and the date of the Shenlong seal, so the project records Feng as a disputed copyist rather than a settled one.
