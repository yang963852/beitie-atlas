# 冯承素

## 中文

神龙本卷后项元汴题记将摹写归于冯承素。故宫博物院在藏品说明中对这一归名及神龙印的年代提出疑问，因此本项目把冯承素列作有争议的摹写者，而不把归名当作定论。

### 生平与身份

生卒、字号与籍贯待考——本库现有来源未记。本页唯一的来源是故宫《冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷》条目，其中不含他的小传。[故宫，冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于唐。

### 书体与师承

书体与师承待考——本库现有著录未及。故宫藏品题名中的「行书摹」指的是这一卷的摹写，不是关于他师承的记载。[故宫，冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收的[《兰亭序》](../works/lanting.md)神龙本，卷后项元汴题记把摹写归于他。归名与神龙印的年代均在疑问之中，馆方与本项目都不把它当作定论。


### 可临之作

- [《兰亭序》](../works/lanting.md)：王羲之353年序文，原迹不存，可对看唐摹神龙本与定武刻拓本。

### 本页依据

本页依据：[故宫博物院《冯承素行书摹兰亭序卷》藏品说明](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)；这是摹写归名有争议的作品条目，不含冯承素小传。

## English

Xiang Yuanbian’s colophon on the Shenlong copy attributes the copying to Feng Chengsu. The Palace Museum questions both that attribution and the date of the Shenlong seal, so the project records Feng as a disputed copyist rather than a settled one.

### Life and identity

Dates, courtesy name and native place remain open: the sources listed for this page do not record them. The only source here is the Palace Museum entry for the Shenlong Orchid Pavilion, which carries no biography. [Palace Museum, Feng Chengsu, running-script copy of the Orchid Pavilion Preface](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Tang.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited catalogue does not address them. The "running-script copy" in the museum's title describes this scroll's copying, not a statement about his teachers. [Palace Museum, Feng Chengsu, running-script copy of the Orchid Pavilion Preface](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html)

### Works in this library

The library holds the Shenlong copy of the [Orchid Pavilion Preface](../works/lanting.md), whose colophon by Xiang Yuanbian attributes the copying to him. Both the attribution and the date of the Shenlong seal are in question; neither the museum nor this project treats the attribution as settled.

### Works to copy

- [Preface to the Orchid Pavilion](../works/lanting.md): Wang Xizhi’s 353 preface, whose original is lost and which survives through the Tang Shenlong tracing and the Dingwu engraved rubbing.

### Basis for this page

This page relies on the [Palace Museum entry for the Shenlong copy of the Orchid Pavilion Preface](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228279.html), a work entry with a disputed attribution of the copying and no biography of Feng Chengsu.
