# 钟繇

## 中文

钟繇（151—230），字元常，颍川长社（今河南长葛）人。举孝廉，历尚书郎、阳陵令、廷尉正、黄门侍郎；魏国初建为大理，迁相国；文帝时改廷尉，进封崇高乡侯，迁太尉；明帝即位，进封定陵侯，迁太傅，太和四年（230）卒，谥成侯。后世称「钟太傅」。[中国大百科全书，钟繇](https://www.zgbk.com/ecph/words?Name=%E9%92%9F%E7%B9%87&SiteID=1&SourceID=52427&Type=bkzyb&subSourceType=000003000013000004)

他的书名在政事之外。《宣和书谱》说「备尽法度，为正书之祖」，后世遂与王羲之并称「钟王」。学曹喜、蔡邕、刘德昇，隶、楷、行都写；楷书还带着隶意，结体宽博，是隶楷递变之间的样子。[中国大百科全书，钟繇](https://www.zgbk.com/ecph/words?Name=%E9%92%9F%E7%B9%87&SiteID=1&SourceID=52427&Type=bkzyb&subSourceType=000003000013000004)

没有一件真迹留下来。《荐季直表》的墨迹本毁于民国年间，只剩影印；今天能读的是刻帖——《宣示表》《贺捷表》《力命表》《墓田丙舍帖》。本库的[《宣示表》](../works/xuanshi-biao.md)是一册宋拓，刻本多称据王羲之临本摹刻，这一条仍是传称。[中国大百科全书，钟繇](https://www.zgbk.com/ecph/words?Name=%E9%92%9F%E7%B9%87&SiteID=1&SourceID=52427&Type=bkzyb&subSourceType=000003000013000004) · [故宫博物院，谈《石渠宝笈·续编》中的「宋拓」《锺王楷帖》](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf)

## English

Zhong Yao (151–230), courtesy name Yuanchang, was a native of Zhangshe in Yingchuan, present-day Changge in Henan. Recommended as a filial and incorruptible candidate, he served as a ministry clerk, a county magistrate, commandant of justice and attendant at the Yellow Gate; when the Wei state was founded he became minister of justice and then chancellor; under Emperor Wen he was made commandant of justice, enfeoffed as marquis of Chonggao and promoted to grand commandant; on Emperor Ming’s accession he was enfeoffed as marquis of Dingling and raised to grand tutor. He died in 230, the fourth year of Taihe, and received the posthumous name Cheng. Later ages called him Grand Tutor Zhong. [Chinese Encyclopedia, Zhong Yao](https://www.zgbk.com/ecph/words?Name=%E9%92%9F%E7%B9%87&SiteID=1&SourceID=52427&Type=bkzyb&subSourceType=000003000013000004)

His fame as a writer of script stands apart from his office. The Xuanhe shupu says his regular script “exhausts method and measure” and calls him the ancestor of standard script, and later ages paired him with Wang Xizhi as Zhong and Wang. He studied under Cao Xi, Cai Yong and Liu Desheng and wrote clerical, regular and running script; his regular script still carries clerical manner and broad frames, a form caught between the two scripts. [Chinese Encyclopedia, Zhong Yao](https://www.zgbk.com/ecph/words?Name=%E9%92%9F%E7%B9%87&SiteID=1&SourceID=52427&Type=bkzyb&subSourceType=000003000013000004)

No autograph survives. The ink copy of the Memorial Recommending Ji Zhi was destroyed in the Republican period and only reproductions remain; what can be read today are engravings: the [Memorial of Declaration](../works/xuanshi-biao.md), the Memorial of Victory, the Memorial of Fate and the BingShe letter. The library’s Memorial of Declaration is a Song rubbing, and the copies are generally said to reproduce a transcription by Wang Xizhi — a traditional attribution, not a documented one. [Chinese Encyclopedia, Zhong Yao](https://www.zgbk.com/ecph/words?Name=%E9%92%9F%E7%B9%87&SiteID=1&SourceID=52427&Type=bkzyb&subSourceType=000003000013000004) · [Palace Museum, on the “Song rubbing” Zhong and Wang models](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/14/u6553200bb65a8.pdf)
