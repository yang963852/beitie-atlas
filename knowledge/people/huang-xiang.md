# 皇象

## 中文

### 生平与身份

皇象生卒年不详，字休明，广陵江都（今江苏扬州西南）人，三国吴书法家，在吴官至侍中，曾任青州刺史。[故宫博物院，宋拓三国天发神谶碑](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234552.html)。上海博物馆记其为今江苏扬州人，历官吴侍中、青州刺史，二说相合。[上海博物馆，三国皇象章草急就章册](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005325)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于三国。

### 书体与师承

故宫说明记其自幼工书、师法东汉章草大家杜操，擅篆、八分、章草，尤以章草见长。[故宫博物院，宋拓三国天发神谶碑](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234552.html)。上海博物馆称其工书、尤以草书为最。[上海博物馆，三国皇象章草急就章册](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005325)

### 代表作与本库关联

故宫著录《天发神谶碑》立于吴天玺元年（276），相传为皇象书而无确据，原石嘉庆十年（1805）毁于火，所藏为北宋拓本。上海博物馆著录「松江本」《急就章》相传为皇象书，明正统四年（1439）杨政据北宋叶梦得刻本重刻，碑石今在松江博物馆。两件的归名均为相传，本库照馆方所述留余地。[故宫博物院，宋拓三国天发神谶碑](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234552.html) · [上海博物馆，三国皇象章草急就章册](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005325)

## English

### Life and identity

Huang Xiang's dates are unknown. His courtesy name was Xiuming; he was a native of Jiangdu in Guangling, southwest of present-day Yangzhou in Jiangsu, and a calligrapher of Wu in the Three Kingdoms, rising to attendant in the palace and serving as governor of Qingzhou. [Palace Museum, Song rubbing of the Tianfa shenchen stele](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234552.html). The Shanghai Museum likewise gives Yangzhou in Jiangsu and the offices of Wu attendant and Qingzhou governor; the two agree. [Shanghai Museum, Huang Xiang's Jijiu zhang in draft cursive](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005325). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Three Kingdoms.

### Scripts and models

The Palace Museum records that he studied from an early age under the Eastern Han draft-cursive master Du Cao and excelled at seal, eight-part and draft cursive, especially the last. [Palace Museum, Tianfa shenchen stele](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234552.html). The Shanghai Museum calls him a master of writing, most accomplished in cursive. [Shanghai Museum, Jijiu zhang](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005325)

### Works in this library

The Tianfa shenchen stele was set up in 276; the museum says it is traditionally attributed to Huang Xiang but without firm evidence, that the stone was destroyed by fire in 1805, and that its holding is a Northern Song rubbing. The Shanghai Museum records the "Songjiang" Jijiu zhang as traditionally attributed to Huang Xiang, recut in 1439 by Yang Zheng from a Northern Song carving by Ye Mengde, with the stone now at the Songjiang Museum. Both attributions are traditional and are kept as such. [Palace Museum, Tianfa shenchen stele](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234552.html) · [Shanghai Museum, Jijiu zhang](https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/article/id/CI00005325)
