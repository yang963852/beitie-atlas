# 蔡邕

## 中文

### 生平与身份

生卒、字号、籍贯与官职待考——本库现有三处馆方来源均未著录。故宫只记「蔡邕、李巡等奉诏」写定经本，中国国家博物馆记「蔡邕等人以隶书写定」，河南博物院记「汉灵帝命书法家蔡邕等人用标准的八分隶书体写成」；三处一致之处是他的参与，而非其生平。[故宫博物院，熹平石经残石](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/231915.html) · [中国国家博物馆，「熹平石经」残石](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/kgfjp/202008/t20200824_247256.shtml) · [河南博物院，第二组 文化教育](https://www.chnmus.net/clzl/2015-12/18/content_233700.htm)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于东汉。

### 书体与师承

馆方一致记石经为隶书：故宫称「隶书字体方正，结构谨严，是当时通行的标准字体」，国博与河南博物院均称「标准的八分隶书体」，故又称「一体石经」。[故宫博物院，熹平石经残石](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/231915.html) · [中国国家博物馆，「熹平石经」残石](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/kgfjp/202008/t20200824_247256.shtml)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《熹平石经》](../works/xiping-stone-classics.md)刻于汉熹平四年（175），内容包括《诗》《书》《礼》《易》《春秋》五经，并《公羊》《论语》二传；国博记光和六年（183）刻成四十六碑。故宫引《后汉书·蔡邕传》「邕乃自书丹于碑，使工镌刻立于太学门外」，残石今分藏河南洛阳博物馆、偃师商城博物馆、陕西西安碑林博物馆等处。[故宫博物院，熹平石经残石](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/231915.html) · [中国国家博物馆，「熹平石经」残石](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/kgfjp/202008/t20200824_247256.shtml)


### 可临之作

- [《熹平石经》](../works/xiping-stone-classics.md)：东汉朝廷校定经文后立于洛阳太学的八分隶书残石，本库只收国博目录所见残石。

## English

### Life and identity

Dates, courtesy name, native place and offices remain open: none of the three museum sources used here records them. The Palace Museum says the classics were written out "by Cai Yong, Li Xun and others by imperial order"; the National Museum of China says "Cai Yong and others wrote them in clerical script"; the Henan Museum says Emperor Ling ordered "the calligrapher Cai Yong and others to write them in standard eight-part clerical script." What the three agree on is his participation, not his biography. [Palace Museum, fragment of the Xiping stone classics](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/231915.html) · [National Museum of China, Xiping stone classics fragment](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/kgfjp/202008/t20200824_247256.shtml) · [Henan Museum, display text](https://www.chnmus.net/clzl/2015-12/18/content_233700.htm). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Eastern Han.

### Scripts and models

The museums agree that the classics are in clerical script: the Palace Museum calls it "square and strict, the standard script of the day," while the National Museum and the Henan Museum call it "standard eight-part clerical," hence the name "single-script stone classics." [Palace Museum, Xiping fragment](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/231915.html) · [National Museum of China, Xiping fragment](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/kgfjp/202008/t20200824_247256.shtml)

### Works in this library

The [Xiping stone classics](../works/xiping-stone-classics.md) held here were cut in 175 and cover the Poetry, Documents, Rites, Changes and Spring and Autumn Annals together with the Gongyang and Analects commentaries; the National Museum records forty-six steles completed in 183. The Palace Museum quotes the Hou Hanshu biography: "Cai Yong then wrote the text in red on the stones and had craftsmen carve them, setting them up outside the Imperial Academy." Surviving fragments are held in Luoyang Museum, the Yanshi Shangcheng Museum and the Xi'an Beilin Museum. [Palace Museum, Xiping fragment](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/231915.html) · [National Museum of China, Xiping fragment](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/kgfjp/202008/t20200824_247256.shtml)
