# 林则徐

## 中文

### 生平与身份

林则徐（1785—1850），字少穆（碑林作「字符抚，一字少穆」；陕西省志作「字少穆，又字元抚、石麟」），晚号竢村老人、竢退叟、七十二峰退叟，福建侯官人。嘉庆九年（1804）中举，十六年（1811）进士；历任江苏巡抚、湖广总督、两广总督、云贵总督等职，谥文忠；1850 年 11 月 22 日卒于潮州普宁行馆。著《政书》《云左山房诗钞》等，《清史稿》有传。[西安碑林博物馆，林则徐游华山诗并序](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2644&c=View&m=home) · [陕西省地方志办公室，林则徐](https://dfz.shaanxi.gov.cn/zslm/sxsq/sqrw/jxd1840z1978n/200903/t20090317_2620174.html)。字号两说并存，本库不取其一。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于清。

### 书体与师承

碑林记其「颇工书法，尤以欧体楷书为人称道。此诗行草酣畅淋漓，颇有法度，略有赵孟頫行草的风格」。[西安碑林博物馆，林则徐游华山诗并序](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2644&c=View&m=home)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《林则徐游华山诗并序》](../works/lin-zexu-huashan.md)为清道光二十二年（1842）行书，林则徐撰并书；此诗为道光二十二年林则徐革官后前往伊犁途中路过陕西登华山而作并书写，诗文前后钤「书生结习」「此间不可无我吟」「少穆初稿」「名岂文章著」诸印。[西安碑林博物馆，林则徐游华山诗并序](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2644&c=View&m=home)

## English

### Life and identity

Lin Zexu (1785–1850), courtesy name Shaomu (the Beilin gives "Zifu, also Shaomu"; the Shaanxi chronicle gives "Shaomu, also Yuanfu and Shilin"), in later years styled Sicun laoren, Situi sou and Qishierfeng tuisou, was a native of Houguan in Fujian. He passed the provincial examination in 1804 and the metropolitan examination in 1811; he served as governor of Jiangsu, governor-general of Huguang, Liangguang and Yungui, and received the posthumous name Wenzhong; he died at Puning in Chaozhou on 22 November 1850. His works include the Zhengshu and Yunzuo shanfang poems; the Qing shi gao carries his biography. [Xi'an Beilin Museum, Lin Zexu's poem on Mount Hua](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2644&c=View&m=home) · [Shaanxi Local Chronicles Office, Lin Zexu](https://dfz.shaanxi.gov.cn/zslm/sxsq/sqrw/jxd1840z1978n/200903/t20090317_2620174.html). Both courtesy-name readings are kept. By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Qing.

### Scripts and models

The Beilin records that he "was quite skilled at calligraphy, especially praised for regular script in the Ouyang manner; this poem's running-cursive is fluent and full, disciplined, with something of Zhao Mengfu's running-cursive." [Xi'an Beilin Museum, poem on Mount Hua](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2644&c=View&m=home)

### Works in this library

The [poem and preface on visiting Mount Hua](../works/lin-zexu-huashan.md) held here is in running script, dated 1842, composed and written by Lin Zexu on his way to Ili after dismissal, when he climbed Mount Hua in Shaanxi; it bears the seals "Shusheng jiexi," "Cijian buke wu wo yin," "Shaomu chugao" and "Ming qi wenzhang zhu." [Xi'an Beilin Museum, poem on Mount Hua](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2644&c=View&m=home)
