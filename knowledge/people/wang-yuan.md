# 王远

## 中文

### 生平与身份

生卒、字号待考——本库现有馆方来源未记。故宫页末关键词记「王远，祖籍山西省太原」，永平年间任梁、秦二州典签，是处理文书的小吏。[故宫博物院，徐悲鸿行书评刺轴（关键词「石门铭」条）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230556.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于北朝。

### 书体与师承

书体待考——故宫页只说《石门铭》「字体类似《石门颂》《孔庙》等碑，淳古秀茂，结体跌宕恣肆，韵趣横生」，并未标出书体名，本库不补作「楷书」。[故宫博物院，徐悲鸿行书评刺轴（关键词「石门铭」条）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230556.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

本库所收[《石门铭》](../works/shimen-ming.md)为北魏摩崖石刻，永平二年（509）刻于褒斜道（今属陕西汉中），纵二百四十四厘米、横三百零四厘米，二十八行、每行二十二字；王远书，武阿仁刻。刻工奉命将王远所撰表彰梁、秦二州刺史羊祉等人修复褒斜道阁道之文刻于石门崖壁，故名《石门铭》。[故宫博物院，徐悲鸿行书评刺轴（关键词「石门铭」条）](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230556.html)


### 可临之作

- [《石门铭》](../works/shimen-ming.md)：王远书、武阿仁刻的509年北魏楷书摩崖，后凿移汉中市博物馆。

### 本页依据

本页依据：[故宫博物院《徐悲鸿行书评刺轴》页末关键词释义](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230556.html)与[央广网《感悟魏碑神品〈石门铭〉》](https://www.cnr.cn/hnfw/hnfwsh/20150723/t20150723_519292763.shtml)；前者不是王远专题传记，后者是文化报道而非馆方目录。

## English

### Life and identity

Dates, courtesy name and style name remain open: the museum source used here does not record them. A keyword note on a Palace Museum page says "Wang Yuan, ancestral home Taiyuan in Shanxi," who under the Yongping reign served as recorder for the Liang and Qin prefectures, a minor clerk handling documents. [Palace Museum, Xu Beihong's running-script colophon (note on the Shimen ming)](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230556.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Northern Dynasties.

### Scripts and models

Script remains open: the museum page says only that the Shimen ming's "letterforms resemble the Shimen song and the Kongmiao steles, archaic and rich, with a swaying structure full of flavour," and does not name the script, so "regular script" is not supplied here. [Palace Museum, note on the Shimen ming](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230556.html)

### Works in this library

The [Shimen ming](../works/shimen-ming.md) held here is a Northern Wei cliff inscription cut in 509 on the Baoxie road in present-day Hanzhong, Shaanxi: 244 by 304 cm, twenty-eight lines of twenty-two characters, written by Wang Yuan and cut by Wu Aren. The cutter was ordered to carve Wang Yuan's text praising the restoration of the Baoxie gallery road by Yang Zhi, prefect of Liang and Qin, and others, hence the name. [Palace Museum, note on the Shimen ming](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230556.html)
