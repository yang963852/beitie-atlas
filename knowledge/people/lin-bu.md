# 林逋

## 中文

### 生平与身份

林逋（967—1028），字君复，杭州钱塘人，北宋诗人。性恬淡好古，初游江、淮间，后归隐西湖孤山，一生未做官；善诗文书画，酷爱梅花仙鹤，有「梅妻鹤子」之说，世称「林和靖」，卒后赐谥「和靖先生」，著有《和靖集》。[故宫博物院，林逋行书自书诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228219.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于北宋。

### 书体与师承

书体与师承待考——本库现有著录未逐字写明其师承。故宫记其书「体瘦劲，秀逸，笔法厚重，风致绰约，与时人李建中风骨俊整的书风极为接近」，是书风比较，不是师承记载。[故宫博物院，林逋行书自书诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228219.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

故宫著录《自书诗》卷，纸本手卷，纵三十二厘米、横三百零二点六厘米，行书三十四行、六段接纸，为林逋归隐西湖孤山时所作五首诗。据卷后自识「时皇上登宝位岁夏五月」，馆方定在天圣元年（1023），时年五十七；另一故宫页面称此卷为存世唯一长篇巨制。本库未另建该作品档案，此处只记馆方所述。[故宫博物院，林逋行书自书诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228219.html) · [故宫博物院，宋林逋行书自书诗卷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_8.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Lin Bu (967–1028), courtesy name Junfu, was a native of Qiantang in Hangzhou and a poet of the Northern Song. Of a quiet and antique disposition, he travelled in the Jiang-Huai region, then retired to the solitary hill of West Lake and never took office. He wrote poetry and prose, painted and practised calligraphy, and loved plum blossoms and cranes, "taking plum as wife and crane as son"; he was known as Lin Hejing and received the posthumous name Hejing xiansheng. His Hejing ji survives. [Palace Museum, Lin Bu's poem scroll in his own hand](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228219.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Northern Song.

### Scripts and models

Scripts and teacher lineage remain open; the cited catalogues do not transcribe them. The museum describes his script as "lean and vigorous, elegant and free, thick in brushwork and graceful in bearing," close to the "upright and stern" manner of his contemporary Li Jianzhong — a comparison of style, not a record of teachers. [Palace Museum, Lin Bu's poem scroll](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228219.html)

### Works in this library

The museum records the Scroll of Poems in His Own Hand, ink on paper, 32 by 302.6 cm, running script in thirty-four lines over six joined sheets, five poems written at his retreat on West Lake. From his own inscription the museum dates it to 1023, when he was fifty-seven; a second museum page calls it his only surviving long work. This library holds no separate work record; only the museum's account is given. [Palace Museum, Lin Bu's poem scroll](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/228219.html) · [Palace Museum, Song Lin Bu, self-written poems](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_8.html)
