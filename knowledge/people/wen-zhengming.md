# 文徵明

## 中文

### 生平与身份

文徵明（1470—1559），原名壁，字徵明，后以字行，改字徵仲，长洲（今江苏苏州）人。文师吴宽，书法学李应祯，绘画宗沈周；与祝允明、唐寅、徐祯卿并称「吴中四才子」，五十四岁以贡生进京待诏翰林院，五十七岁归里，与沈周共创吴门画派、并称「明四家」，书法与祝允明、王宠并誉「吴中三家」。[故宫博物院，文徵明楷书归去来兮辞页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230983.html)。据 `dynastySource`，本页把他系于明。

### 书体与师承

上文所记书学李应祯、笔法出自《黄庭经》《乐毅论》而受欧书影响，均出故宫著录；馆方又引王世贞《弇州山人四部稿》记文氏早晚必书小楷一篇为日课。[故宫博物院，文徵明楷书归去来兮辞页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230983.html)

### 代表作与本库关联

故宫著录《归去来兮辞》页，纸本楷书，纵十三点七厘米、横十六点一厘米，款署「辛亥九月十一日，横塘舟中书。徵明。时年八十又二」，馆方定辛亥为嘉靖三十年（1551）、时年八十二。此页与赵孟頫、邓文原等作同入《元明人书册》，清代安岐曾藏并收入《墨缘汇观》。本库未另建该作品档案，此处只记馆方所述。[故宫博物院，文徵明楷书归去来兮辞页](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230983.html)

## English

### Life and identity

Wen Zhengming (1470–1559), originally named Bi, courtesy name Zhengming and later Zhengzhong, was a native of Changzhou, present-day Suzhou in Jiangsu. He studied prose under Wu Kuan, calligraphy under Li Yingzhen and painting under Shen Zhou; with Zhu Yunming, Tang Yin and Xu Zhenqing he was one of the Four Talents of Wu. At fifty-four he went to the capital as a tribute student awaiting appointment at the Hanlin Academy, returning home at fifty-seven; with Shen Zhou he founded the Wu school of painting and was one of the Four Masters of Ming, while in calligraphy he was grouped with Zhu Yunming and Wang Chong as the Three Masters of Wu. [Palace Museum, Wen Zhengming's regular-script leaf of the Return Home](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230983.html). By `dynastySource` this page places him in the Ming.

### Scripts and models

The study under Li Yingzhen and the brush deriving from the Yellow Court Classic and the Yue Yi lun, with an influence from Ouyang's script, are from the museum entry; it also quotes Wang Shizhen's account that Wen wrote a page of small regular script every morning and evening as daily practice. [Palace Museum, Wen Zhengming's Return Home leaf](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230983.html)

### Works in this library

The museum records the leaf of the Return Home in regular script, ink on paper, 13.7 by 16.1 cm, signed "on the eleventh day of the ninth month of the xinhai year, written in a boat at Hengtan, Zhengming, aged eighty-two"; the museum dates xinhai to 1551. The leaf entered the Yuan-Ming album with works by Zhao Mengfu and Deng Wenyuan and was collected by An Qi, who recorded it in the Motuan huiguan. This library holds no separate work record; only the museum's account is given. [Palace Museum, Wen Zhengming's Return Home leaf](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/handwriting/230983.html)
