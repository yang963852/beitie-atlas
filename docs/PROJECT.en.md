# Between Steles · 碑帖之间

> This file and the [Chinese original](PROJECT.md) together form the single authoritative statement of the project's purpose. Other documents give a short introduction and link here instead of reproducing the whole text. Changes must be made in both languages, with the English preserving the meaning of the Chinese.
>
> The philosophy stands apart from website features, technical architecture and release versions.

## Why we began

We began with one stele or modelbook on the copying desk, and wanted to trace its history, its transmission and the beauty of its writing, and to meet the calligraphers, periods and culture behind the work.
We came together through calligraphy, and we hope to share what we learn in studying, practising, reflecting and talking with more people.

## Mission

To bring calligraphy to a wider public.
Calligraphy need not be an art reserved for a small professional few. It can also be a way for ordinary people to understand history, feel beauty, express themselves and enrich their inner life.
We believe an understanding of beauty is not innate; it grows through learning, practice and exchange. Even when artistic achievement is not the goal, the acts of writing and looking have value in themselves.

## What this project is

Between Steles is an open, shared knowledge project built by calligraphy enthusiasts, primarily for beginners and fellow enthusiasts.
Starting from classical steles and modelbooks, we bring together works, calligraphers, surviving objects, histories of transmission, research literature and experience of copying, so that reliable, readable and connected public knowledge is available for learning.
We offer knowledge and guidance for study, but we do not try to replace education between people.
A stele or modelbook is where we begin to understand calligraphy; it is not a permanent boundary for the project.

## Principles

**Everyone contributes for the benefit of others, and everyone benefits from what others share.**

- Open and shared: let knowledge be freely read, learned, used and built upon.
- Respect for facts: attend to sources and evidence, allow conflicting accounts to stand side by side, and acknowledge what is unknown.
- Respect for individuals: value each person's real experience of learning, aesthetic judgement and independent thought.
- Learning together: record not only the results of study but the processes of learning, discussion and exploration.
- People first: technology and artificial intelligence serve human learning, expression and collaboration; they do not replace human experience and judgement.
- Keep the love of it: scale, traffic and worldly success are not why this project is built.

## Building together

We welcome anyone who loves calligraphy and wants to share, whatever their stage of learning, professional background or technical ability.
The maintainers carry responsibility for building, managing and publishing the project, while respecting contributors' autonomy and supporting them in using the project's resources for study, research, exchange and related cultural activities.

## What we hope for

A public cultural project for calligraphy that can accumulate over time, be shared freely and continue to be built together.
May everyone interested in calligraphy find something here, and have the chance to leave what they have learned for those who come later.
We are in no hurry to reach a destination. We simply hope to walk this road we love alongside more people.
