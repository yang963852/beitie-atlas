Task 037 fills the largest remaining gaps on the 帖 side and the early medieval 碑 side: 平复帖、十七帖、洛神赋十三行、好太王碑、始平公造像记、虞恭公碑、颜氏家庙碑、万岁通天帖、胆巴碑、三门记 and 三希堂法帖. Where a work survives only as a later carrier — a Southern Song carving, a Tang tracing, a paper draft, a set of Qing blocks — the record describes that carrier and does not imply the lost original survives.

## Bronze inscriptions and bamboo slips (043)

The owner extended the corpus beyond steles and modelbooks. Bronze inscriptions are filed as `bei` because they reached calligraphers as rubbings; bamboo slips are filed as `tie` because they are ink writing. A new object type `bronze-vessel` records the vessel itself, never a rubbing of it.

## Open questions from the witness audit

- **张黑女墓志 (张玄墓志).** Now recorded as a draft (041) from a [Yongji municipal government feature](https://www.yongji.gov.cn/doc/2024/09/02/472291.shtml), not from the Shanghai Museum entry. The album's line count (twenty lines or twelve), the character count and the accession year remain unverified. Match the He Shaoji album against a catalogue or plate before asserting the holding.
- **郑文公碑.** Compare measured surveys of the two cliffs before citing disputed dimensions or counts. Check the Palace Museum album separately for trimming and retouching.
- **化度寺塔铭.** Obtain the Si’ou album shelfmark and a page-level catalogue of its colophons. Research the Dunhuang leaves under their Paris and London identifiers separately. Their dating and recut status require comparison.
- **淳化阁帖.** Check the contents and supplied leaves of volume six against another identified volume. Keep the location of disputed matrices outside the album history.
- **兰亭序 and 礼器碑.** Match the Dingwu album against individually catalogued impressions. For 礼器碑, compare the two characters in 新00044512 with 新00045759 before publishing a visual comparison.
- **雁塔圣教序 and 书谱.** An accession register for 新00045571 could distinguish the reported 1952 gift from a 1954 registration. For the manuscript 故書000058, consult the 2008 optical study and an object-specific transport inventory before adding movements. Keep the engraved copy and other Ouzhai rubbings separate.

Other leads include 张黑女墓志, individual 龙门 inscriptions and 大观帖. The task 023 format backlog is historical; the opening paragraph lists manuscripts already researched in task 024.

## Image leads and why none shipped in task 037

Every candidate image was checked for two separate things: a stated licence, and a file that could actually be fetched and inspected. The two accessible open-access catalogues were:

- **The Metropolitan Museum of Art**, whose Asian Art department holds twentieth-century rubbings of 孔子庙堂碑 (1977.375.12), 多宝塔碑 (1977.375.13), 颜氏家庙碑 (1977.375.14a–d), 玄秘塔碑 (1977.375.17) and 峄山碑 (1977.375.5), plus a thirteenth-century 十七帖 album (1991.380). The collection API reports `isPublicDomain: true` and supplies credit lines and accession numbers.
- **Wikimedia Commons**, the source of the project's existing 神龙本 crop.

Neither could supply a usable file from the working network on 2026-11-23: Commons and `upload.wikimedia.org` timed out, `images.metmuseum.org` returned a bot-protection page instead of image bytes, and `www.metmuseum.org` did not load. A licence that cannot be read and a file that cannot be fetched are not evidence, so no `media` record was created and no image was bundled. The Met records remain valid leads: a later editor who can open the object page should confirm the licence there, download the file, and add one `media` item per object with the credit line and the object URL as `rightsEvidence`.

Downloadability is not a reuse licence, and the museum's own photography of a public-domain stele is not automatically public domain. Do not substitute a museum JPEG for a PD claim.

For every candidate, record the institutional item URL or a consulted print locator, the supported facts, and a separate per-image licence if an image is later used. Downloadability or login access is not a reuse licence.
