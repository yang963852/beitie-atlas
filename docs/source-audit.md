# Source audit

Research date: 2026-11-17. These are research notes, not a completed release dataset. Source access dates do not certify an object's physical location on that date. Coordinates and image reuse permissions still require their own evidence.

## Seed family: Yan Qinli Stele / 颜勤礼碑

### Original stone

- [Palace Museum catalog entry](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234175.html): identifies the inscription and states the stone is held at the Xi'an Beilin Museum. The cataloged object on this page is a separate rubbing.
- [Xi'an Beilin Museum historical article](https://www.beilin-museum.com/index.php?a=index&aid=2440&c=View&m=home), published 2007-08-07: describes discovery in 1922, subsequent relocation to the provincial governor's compound courtyard, movement to the Xincheng small stele collection in 1928, and transfer to Beilin in 1948.
- Suitable use: one work, one original-stone object, separately sourced historical events.
- Gaps: original erection location, exact historical-site coordinates, all intervening custody, present-day display status. Do not upgrade the 2007 publication date to a 2026 physical verification.
- Precision: the historical descriptions support named areas/sites; they do not by themselves establish exact GPS points.

### Palace Museum Republican-era rubbing

- [Official object record](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/impres/234175.html).
- Catalog number: `新00045406`; identified as a Republican-era rubbing.
- This is an independent object linked to the same work and, where supported, to the source stone. Its collection assertion differs from the original stone's.
- Gaps: exact rubbing year, full custody history, current exhibition or storage location. Do not copy the stone's timeline into this rubbing's history.
- Images: not cleared for repository redistribution. Use original text summaries and a source link pending permission.

## Additional stele candidates

### Shimen Song / 石门颂

- [Hanzhong government exhibition account](https://jhj.hanzhong.gov.cn/hzjhjwz/lswh/202412/3274960269104c93abe79bc646dc2dae.shtml), 2024: identifies the stone inscription in Hanzhong Museum's exhibition. This supports a collection lead, not guaranteed exhibition availability today. Retrieved during research; a later fetch timed out.
- Prefer this as the third real object for M1, after verifying the collection assertion and geographic evidence.
- Do not assign a relocation year yet: references to cutting, removal, transport, and accession may describe different milestones. The complete primary-source chronology remains to be checked.
- The Shaanxi cultural heritage authority's 2025 inventory appeared in search results, but its PDF did not load reliably. Do not import a truncated search-result line as a verified catalog record.
- [Zhongxiang Museum's Qing rubbing](https://www.zxbwg.cn/jpww/zh/119.html) is a separate potential derivative object for later inclusion, not a second location for the original stone. Image redistribution has not been cleared.

### Jiucheng Palace Liquan Inscription / 九成宫醴泉铭

- [Baoji government introduction](https://www.baoji.gov.cn/col26/col95/201705/t20170525_10385.html), published 2017-05-25: dates erection to 632 and describes the then-current location as the Jiucheng Palace site in Linyou.
- [Hunan culture and tourism article](https://whhlyt.hunan.gov.cn/whhlyt/news/wlyw/201911/t20191128_10780883.html), 2019: identifies the original with Linyou County Museum while discussing a distinct reproduction project.
- Treat these as historical and custodial evidence. The holding institution's name does not prove the original is located in a particular museum building today. Verify the current pavilion/site before adding a current marker.
- Do not claim the stone has remained on its exact original spot since 632.

## Rights and editorial use

- [Palace Museum website terms](https://www.dpm.org.cn/bottom/privacy/254.html) and [image authorization page](https://www.dpm.org.cn/bottom/apply_image): do not treat public web display as blanket permission to redistribute museum images in an open repository.
- [National Palace Museum image/text reuse rules](https://theme.npm.edu.tw/opendata/%E6%95%85%E5%AE%AEOpen%20Data%E5%B0%88%E5%8D%80%E5%9C%96%E5%83%8F%E8%88%87%E6%96%87%E5%AD%97%E6%8E%88%E6%AC%8A%E8%A6%8F%E7%AF%84.pdf): different download variants have different stated licenses. Verify the actual asset and credit before use; a catalog-wide assumption is insufficient.
- Museum prose is not automatically reusable wholesale. Write concise original factual summaries, preserve bibliographic references, and keep any quotations short and justified.
- The opening desk displays a modern study plate cropped from the opening of the Shenlong manuscript (witness-lanting). The source JPEG measures 4513 × 1480. The crop retains columns in their original order and proportions, from x=2590 through the right edge, at full source height. The two static desk posters also depict the crop. [Image and PD-Art declaration](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:LantingXu.jpg&oldid=1087138769).
- The wood diffuse and OpenGL normal maps are WebP derivatives of Wood Table 001 by Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers. [Poly Haven asset](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_001) and [CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license).
